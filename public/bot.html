KidPoshanBot

KidPoshanBot is the reader used by KidPoshan, a site that helps Indian parents decide what to cook for their children by age, meal and season.

What it does

  • Reads public recipe pages, mainly the recipe card (title, ingredients, steps), so we can review them privately.
  • Follows your robots.txt and waits at least 10 seconds between requests to your site, longer if you set a Crawl-delay.
  • Never publishes your method text or photos on KidPoshan without your permission. Without it, we show the dish name and link parents to your page.

Stop KidPoshanBot

Add this to your robots.txt. We re-read it every day.

User-agent: KidPoshanBot
Disallow: /
