Product Intelligence Lab

Trial the live AI product-research layer before connecting commerce. Gemini researches current product information; KidPoshan's scoring engine remains separate.

How this trial works

  1. Gemini searches current web sources.
  2. It extracts product identity, ingredients and nutrition into structured data.
  3. Only verified facts are passed forward.
  4. The deterministic KidPoshan Score engine will score the verified facts once its rules are connected.
  5. Swiggy will be the next live-availability layer.
No KidPoshan score is invented during research. Missing facts stay missing.
