Privacy Policy

KidPoshan · www.kidposhan.in · Last updated 10 October 2026

KidPoshan helps parents decide what to cook for their children by age, meal and season, and gives every recipe a Poshan Score. This page says what we collect, why, who sees it, and how to have it removed. For anything here, write to kidposhan@gmail.com.

What we collect from parents who use the site

  • Name, mobile number and email address, only if you give them (when you rate a recipe, send a remark, or fill in the small "Join KidPoshan" box on a recipe page). We use them to know who rated a recipe or wrote a remark. We do not check that a number or address is yours. Your mobile number and email are never shown on the site. Your name is shown only next to a remark that our team has approved.
  • Ratings and remarks you submit. A remark appears on the site only after our team approves it, and you can ask us to remove it.
  • Optional ₹5 support. You pay in your own UPI app; we never see your bank or card details. If you tell us you have paid, we keep the amount, the time and the payment reference you typed, so we can confirm it.
  • A random id kept in your browser (no name, number or IP address is stored with it). It lets us count how many different browsers visit and show you recipes you have not seen yet when you repeat a search. Your browser also keeps your chosen filters, shopping ticks and login token on your device. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

What we collect about recipe creators

KidPoshan features recipes from public recipe sites, with the creator's name, photo credit and a link back, and steps written in our own words. To ask permission we email the creator at the contact address published on their own site, once. We keep that address, our email, their reply, and a phone number if they choose to send one, only to record their permission and to reach them about sharing. We stop and remove their recipes and photos if they tell us to. Our reader for public pages is described at KidPoshanBot.

How we use Google (Gmail) data

To send those permission requests and read the replies, KidPoshan connects to the Google account kidposhan@gmail.com through the Gmail API, with permission only to send email and to read the replies to the emails we sent. We do not read any other mail, we do not share Gmail data with anyone, we do not use it for advertising, and it is not used to train any AI model. A reply is read by our systems to record whether the creator agreed and any phone number given, and by the owner of the site. KidPoshan's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Who else is involved

The site runs on Cloudflare (hosting and database). We use Cloudflare's AI service to rewrite recipe steps and Tavily to search the web for recipe pages; neither receives your name, number or email. Gmail (Google) is used only as described above. We do not sell or rent your information.

How long we keep it, and your choices

We keep what you give us for as long as the site runs, or until you ask us to delete it. You can ask us to show you, correct or delete your name, number, email, ratings or remarks, or a creator's details, by writing to kidposhan@gmail.com. We will deal with it promptly.

Children

KidPoshan is written for parents. Children should not give us their details.

Changes

If we change what we collect we will update this page and its date.
