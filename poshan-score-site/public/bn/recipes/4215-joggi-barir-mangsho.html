Joggi Barir Mangsho

70মোটামুটি

Joggi Barir Mangsho-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য এক সার্ভিং হিসেবে 100-এর মধ্যে 70 (মোটামুটি)। একই খাবারের স্কোর বয়সভেদে আলাদা হয়, কারণ ছোট শিশুর চিনি, নুন ও স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাটের দৈনিক সীমা কম।

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

উপকরণ

  • 1 kg Mutton (cut into big chunks)
  • 100 g Mutton fat
  • 400 g Onion
  • 2 Tbsp Ginger Paste
  • 2 Tbsp Garlic Paste
  • 200 g Hung Curd
  • 1 Tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 Tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 Tsp Bengali Garam Masala Powder
  • 1 Tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 Tsp Sugar
  • 1 Tsp Salt
  • 2 Tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 2 Tbsp Poppy Seed/ Posto
  • 1 Tbsp Melon Seed / Charmagaj
  • 1 Tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 6 Tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 Tbsp Ghee
  • 6 Dry Red Chilli
  • 4 Bay leaf
  • 3 Black Cardamom
  • 3 Green Cardamom
  • 2 Cinnamon Stick

পদ্ধতি

  • Wash and pat dry Mutton chunks and chunks of mutton fat.
  • I do not marinate the mutton for a longer period for this recipe, considering that this is how it has been cooked by professional catering cooks, aka the Rannar Thakur.
  • Marinate the mutton for around 30 minutes before cooking with 1 Tsp Coriander Powder, 1 Tsp Cumin Powder, 1 Tsp Bengali Garam Masala Powder, 1 Tsp Red Chilli Powder, 1 Tsp Sugar, 1 Tsp Salt, and also 2 Tbsp Mustard Oil.
  • Mix the curd as well.
  • Soak poppy seeds and melon seeds separately in hot water for 15 minutes, then blend them into a paste.
  • Finely chop the onion.
  • While cooking, heat the oil.
  • Sprinkle 1/4 tsp each of turmeric powder and red chili powder and also salt.
  • Temper the oil with Dry Red Chilli, bay leaf, Black Cardamom, Green Cardamom, and cinnamon stick.
  • Once the spices are fragrant, add the chopped onion and cook until translucent.
  • Add both ginger and garlic paste and cook till the mixture leaves oil from the edges.
  • Now add both the pastes followed by the spices which are 1 Tsp Coriander Powder, 1 Tsp Cumin Powder, remaining Red Chilli Powder, turmeric powder, and also 1 Tsp Sugar.
  • Cook for around 2 minutes at this stage and add marinated mutton.
  • Mix using a spatula and cook on low flame for around 30 minutes.
  • Stir in between,
  • Adjust the salt if needed.
  • Meanwhile, boil around 5 cups of water.
  • Add the boiling water to the mutton after 30 minutes of cooking.
  • Mix properly and cover with a lid.
  • Cook for around 1.5 hours or more or till the mutton is soft.
  • Add boiling water in between while cooking.
  • The gravy should not be runny but not thick as well.
  • Once the mutton is well-cooked add Ghee and the remaining Bengali Garam Masala and mix well.
  • Serve it hot with steamed rice.

    Joggi Barir Mangsho-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য 100-এর মধ্যে 70 (মোটামুটি)। 10টি পরীক্ষার মধ্যে 7টিতে পাস করে এবং সোডিয়াম, স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাট, বেশি ফ্যাট, চিনি বা নুন-এ নম্বর হারায়।
