উপকরণ

  • 4 large Eggs
  • 3 cups Water
  • 2 tablespoons Oil
  • 1 tablespoon Coriander leaves (chopped, for garnish)
  • 1 large Egg (beaten)
  • 1 teaspoon Gram flour / Kadalai maavu / Besan flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon Red Chilli powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon Turmeric powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon Black pepper powder (coarsely crushed)
  • 1 tablespoon Onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 each Green Chilli (chopped)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Ginger Garlic paste or finely minced
  • 2 leaves Curry leaves (chopped)
  • 1/8 teaspoon Salt

পদ্ধতি

  • Boil eggs in enough water, peel and cut them into half and keep them aside.
  • Beat an egg, whisk in along with gram flour, red chilli powder, turmeric powder, black pepper powder and salt.
  • Beat until well blend to remove any lumps from the flour.
  • Add in chopped onions, green chillies, ginger garlic, coriander and curry leaves.
  • Heat oil in a frying pan and dip each halved egg in this mixture to coat it all over and place it on the tawa.
  • Cook one side until it firms up a bit and then flip to the other side and cook until coating becomes crisp. Garnish with coriander leaves.
  • Remove and serve as an evening snack or as a side dish with any meal.

    Mutta Bajji কি বাচ্চাদের জন্য স্বাস্থ্যকর?

    Mutta Bajji-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য 100-এর মধ্যে 90 (ভালো)। 10টি পরীক্ষার মধ্যে 9টিতে পাস করে এবং ফাইবার-এ নম্বর হারায়।
