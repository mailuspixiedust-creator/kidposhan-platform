উপকরণ

  • 5 cups basil (5 packed cups, fresh)
  • 3/4 cup parmesan cheese (finely grated)
  • 1/2 cup pine nuts
  • 3 garlic cloves (peeled and smashed)
  • 3/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tsp salt (plus more, to taste)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice (from 1/2 of a small lemon, optional)
  • 3 bell peppers (about 3 cups, mix of colors of choice, thinly sliced)
  • 1/2 cup olive oil
  • 1/2 white onion (medium in size, sliced thin)
  • 4 garlic cloves (peeled, smashed and sliced very thin)
  • 5 sprigs fresh thyme
  • 2 1/2 tbsp red wine vinegar
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1 flatbread (homemade or a mix like Bob's Redmill or an already prepared flatbread of choice, naan would work great too)
  • 1 lb cooked ground sausage
  • 1/2 cup parmesan cheese (grated)
  • basil or parsley (chopped, optional)

পদ্ধতি

Pesto

  1. Heat your oven to 325F and spread your pine nuts on a parchment lined baking sheet.
  2. Toast for 6 minutes.
  3. Add pine nuts and all pesto recipe ingredients, except the oil, (including lemon, if using - this will add a touch of brightness) to a food processor or high powered blender.
  4. Blend or process on high while slowly streaming in the oil until the pesto is smooth.
  5. Add more salt as needed.
  6. Set pesto aside until you're ready to assemble the flatbread.
