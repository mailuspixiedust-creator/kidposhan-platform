উপকরণ

  • 1 pound pasta
  • ½ teaspoon table salt, plus salt for cooking pasta
  • 1 (15-ounce) can cannellini beans, rinsed
  • 1 ½ cups half-and-half
  • 1 teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 teaspoon onion powder
  • ½ teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • ½ teaspoon pepper
  • 12 ounces extra-sharp cheddar cheese, shredded (3 cups)

পদ্ধতি

  • Bring 4 quarts water to boil in large pot.
  • Add 1 pound pasta and 1 tablespoon table salt and cook, stirring often, until al dente.
  • Reserve ¾ cup pasta cooking water, then drain pasta and return it to pot.
  • Meanwhile, process 1 rinsed can cannellini beans, 1½ cups half-and-half, 1 teaspoon garlic powder, 1 teaspoon onion powder, ½ teaspoon smoked paprika, and ½ teaspoon salt in blender until smooth, about 30 seconds.
  • Melt 2 tablespoons unsalted butter in large saucepan over medium heat.
  • Add bean puree, reserved pasta water, and ½ teaspoon pepper and whisk to combine.
  • Cook until starting to bubble around edges, about 5 minutes.
  • Whisk in 3 cups shredded extra-sharp cheddar, 1 handful at a time, until melted.
  • Stir pasta into cheese sauce and season with salt and pepper to taste. Stir until sauce thickens, about 1 minute. Serve.
