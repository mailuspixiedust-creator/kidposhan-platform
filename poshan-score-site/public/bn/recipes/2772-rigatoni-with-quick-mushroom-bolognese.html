উপকরণ

  • 1 pound rigatoni
  • 3/4 teaspoon table salt, plus salt for cooking pasta
  • 1 pound cremini mushrooms, trimmed and quartered
  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 small onion, chopped fine
  • 1 carrot, peeled and chopped fine
  • 1/4 cup tomato paste
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 cup dry white wine
  • 1/4 cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese, plus extra for serving

পদ্ধতি

  • Bring 4 quarts water to boil in Dutch oven.
  • Add 1 pound rigatoni and 1 tablespoon table salt and cook, stirring occasionally, until al dente.
  • Reserve 1 cup cooking water, then drain pasta and return it to pot.
  • Meanwhile, pulse 1 pound trimmed and quartered cremini mushrooms in food processor until finely chopped, about 10 pulses.
  • Heat 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil in 12-inch skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking.
  • Add mushrooms, 1 finely chopped small onion, 1 finely chopped peeled carrot, and ¾ teaspoon table salt and cook until mushrooms appear dry and begin to stick to bottom of skillet, about 14 minutes.
  • Stir in ¼ cup tomato paste and 3 minced garlic cloves and cook until fond forms on bottom of skillet, about 1 minute.
  • Stir in ¼ cup white wine, scraping up any browned bits, and cook until evaporated, about 2 minutes.
  • Stir in reserved cooking water and bring to boil.
  • Add sauce and ¼ cup grated Pecorino Romano cheese to pasta in pot and stir to combine.
  • Serve with extra grated Pecorino Romano cheese.
