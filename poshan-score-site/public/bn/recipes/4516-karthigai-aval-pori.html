Karthigai Aval Pori

80মোটামুটি, একটি ঝুঁকির পরীক্ষায় আটকানো

Karthigai Aval Pori-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য এক সার্ভিং হিসেবে 100-এর মধ্যে 80 (মোটামুটি, একটি ঝুঁকির পরীক্ষায় আটকানো)। একই খাবারের স্কোর বয়সভেদে আলাদা হয়, কারণ ছোট শিশুর চিনি, নুন ও স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাটের দৈনিক সীমা কম।

উপকরণ

  • 1/2 heaped cup puffed rice
  • 1/2 cup thick poha
  • 2 tablespoon raw peanuts
  • 2 tablespoon fried gram dal
  • 2 tablespoon black sesame seeds
  • 2 tablespoon coconut
  • 1/8 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon dry ginger powder
  • 1/2 cup jaggery
  • 1/4 cup water

পদ্ধতি

  • Take 1/2 heaped cup pori in a sieve and sieve it. Hand pick specks or impurities if any, set aside.
  • Take 1/2 cup thick aval in a sieve and sieve it. Hand pick specks or impurities if any, set aside.
  • To a heavy bottomed kadai add pori and roast for few seconds to make it crisp. Remove to a plate and set aside.
  • We need 2 tablespoon ghee in total. Now add little ghee and add half of aval first.
  • Roast it until crisp and remove to the plate. Add little more ghee add remaining aval and roast until crisp and remove to a plate.
  • Add 2 tablespoon sesame seeds, roast until golden and remove to a plate and set aside.
  • Add 2 tablespoon fried gram dal, roast until golden and remove to a plate.
  • Add 2 tablespoon raw peanuts and roast until crisp. Remove to a plate.
  • Remove the skin and set aside.
  • Add remaining ghee and add 2 tablespoon coconut chopped and fry until golden brown. Remove to a plate.
  • To a pan add 1/2 cup jaggery along with 1/4 cup water.
  • Heat it up and let jaggery dissolve no need to check for any consistency.
  • Strain and add the jaggery syrup to the kadai.
  • Cook for few minutes in low flame, keep stirring.
  • Keep cooking and keep checking by adding a drop in water. It should not dissolve and stand still.
  • When you try you should be able to form a loose ball like this, when you press it should be soft. This is the right consistency.
  • Add all the fried items along with 1/8 teaspoon dry ginger powder and 1/8 teaspoon cardamom powder.
  • Mix it well and switch off.
  • Spread to a plate and cool down. Roughly break it.
  • Store in a dry airtight container and enjoy!
