Oats Avocado Toast (No Bread)

উপকরণ

  • 1 cup quick cooking oats
  • salt (namak) to taste, restricted salt for a health
  • 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch)
  • 3/4 cup water (approx., adjust as required)
  • 2 tsp ghee
  • 1 ripe avocado
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp crushed garlic (lehsun)
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped onions
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped tomatoes (seeds removed)
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander
  • 2 tsp finely chopped green chillies
  • salt (namak) to taste, restricted salt for health
  • 1 tsp white sesame seeds (til)
  • 1 tsp black sesame seeds (kala til)

পদ্ধতি

  • To make the Oats Avocado Toast, Take 1 cup quick cooking oats in deep bowl.
  • Add salt to taste.
  • Add 1/4 tsp freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch).
  • Gradually add water.
  • Whisk to form a smooth, lump-free batter. Let it rest for 5 minutes.
  • Heat a non-stick pan or tawa and lightly grease it with ½ tsp ghee.
  • Pour half the batter onto the pan and spread it into a thick round, similar to a toast or pancake.
  • Grease some ghee on the top.
  • Cook on a medium flame for 3 to 4 minutes until the bottom is crisp and golden.
  • Flip carefully.
  • Cook the other side for another 2 to 3 minutes until firm and crisp.
  • Remove them in a plate and repeat with the remaining batter.
  • In a bowl, put 1 ripe avocado.
  • Mash well using a fork.
  • Add 1 tsp crushed garlic (lehsun).
  • Add 2 tbsp finely chopped onions.
  • Add 2 tbsp finely chopped tomatoes (seeds removed).
  • Add 2 tsp finely chopped green chillies.
  • Add 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander.
  • Add salt to taste, restricted salt for health.
  • Mix gently until well combined. Avoid over-mashing to retain some texture.
  • Place the crispy oats base on a serving plate.
  • Spread a generous layer of guacamole evenly over it.
  • Sprinkle black sesame seeds (kala til) and white sesame seeds (til) seeds.
  • Cut into 2 pieces.
  • Serve Healthy Oats Avocado Toast immediately while the oats base is still crisp.
