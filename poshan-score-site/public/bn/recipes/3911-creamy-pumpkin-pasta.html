Creamy Pumpkin Pasta

উপকরণ

  • 1 1/2 to 2 cups Pumpkin puree from roasted pumpkin (or canned puree)
  • 1 1/2 cups Pasta of your choice. I have used macaroni
  • 1 tbsp Oil (olive or coconut)
  • 1 Onion (chopped)
  • 4 to 5 Garlic cloves (chopped)
  • 1 tsp Rosemary (fresh or dried, optional)
  • 1 tsp Pepper flakes
  • 1 tsp Italian pasta seasoning
  • 1/3 cup Heavy cream
  • 1/2 to 3/4 cup Pasta water or broth
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1/4 cup Parmesan cheese (grated)

পদ্ধতি

  • Puree the homemade roasted pumpkin with little water, or season the canned pumpkin puree.
  • Cook the pasta in salted boiling water according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside. If possible, reserve the pasta water.
  • Heat oil in a pan. Add onion and garlic and cook until the onion becomes light golden, 6 to 7 minutes, on a medium flame.
  • Add the chile flakes, Italian pasta seasoning, and rosemary, and saute for a few seconds.
  • Add pumpkin puree, cream, and reserved pasta water.
  • Stir and let the sauce simmer for 5 to 8 minutes so the flavors meld together.
  • At this stage, taste and adjust the salt and seasoning.
  • Add the cooked pasta and toss until combined and heated through.
  • Add the parmesan cheese and mix. Serve and enjoy.
