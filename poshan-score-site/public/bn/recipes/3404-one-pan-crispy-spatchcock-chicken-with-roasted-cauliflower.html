উপকরণ

  • 1 head cauliflower
  • 1 onion, thinly sliced
  • 8 cloves garlic – whole
  • small handful savory herbs- sage, thyme or rosemary
  • salt & pepper to taste
  • olive oil for drizzling
  • 1 whole Chicken- Spatchcocked (butterflied)
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • ½ cup whole grain mustard or a mix of whole grain and dijon
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • pinch salt

পদ্ধতি

  • Preheat oven to 400F
  • Slice cauliflower into “steaks” about ½ inch -¾ inch thick, green parts and all.
  • Place them on a rimmed parchment-lined baking sheet.
  • Scatter sliced onions, whole garlic cloves and sprigs of herbs over top.
  • Sprinkle with salt and pepper, then drizzle with a very light drizzle of olive oil.
  • In a small bowl, mix mustard, oil, and a pinch of salt.
  • (See the video).
  • Place the chicken on a cutting board, backside up, breastbone down.
  • Using sturdy kitchen scissors, cut along each side of the backbone, removing it, saving it for stock.
  • Open the chicken like a book, find the lower tip of the breastbone, and slice the flesh open at that point so the tip can protrude.
  • Turn the chicken over, spread apart, and press down hard on the breastbone with the heel of your hand to flatten.
  • Sprinkle the inside and outside generously with salt and pepper. Place the chicken, breast side up, over the cauliflower, pressing down to flatten it.
  • Brush or rub the skin side of the chicken with the mustard marinade ( saving 1-2 tablespoons for basting), getting all the nooks and crannies.
  • Place it as flat as you can, opened up, over the cauliflower, pressing the breast down one more time.
  • Place in the middle of the oven for 40 minutes.
  • Baste with the remaining mustard, and if there are pan juices (there may not be yet), spoon some over the cauliflower, then bake for another 10-20 minutes, until cooked through.
  • Bake until the thigh, at its thickest part, reaches 170°F or the juices run clear.
  • Pull it out of the oven, and when you tilt the pan, you will see clear pan juices- spoon this flavorful juice over the exposed cauliflower.
  • Let rest 10 minutes before cutting.
