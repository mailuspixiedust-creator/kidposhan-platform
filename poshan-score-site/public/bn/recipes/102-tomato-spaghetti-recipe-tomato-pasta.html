উপকরণ

  • 150 grams Spaghetti (use any pasta)
  • 4 large Tomatoes (crushed)
  • 2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 6 cloves Garlic (peeled & sliced thinly)
  • 1 tsp Sugar (or to taste)
  • 1 tsp Dried Red Chilli Flakes (or to taste)
  • 1 tsp Dried Oregano
  • ¼ cup Parmesan Cheese (grated)
  • Salt to taste

পদ্ধতি

  • Bring lots of water to boil, add in salt and drop in the pasta. Cook as per package directions.
  • When the pasta is cooking, heat olive oil in a pan.
  • Add in garlic and fry till golden.
  • Now add in tomatoes, salt and sugar.
  • Mix well.
  • Cook this till the raw smell leaves from tomatoes.
  • Add in chilli flakes and oregano.
  • Mix well and cook for few more mins.
  • Sauce done.
  • Now Strain the pasta and add it to sauce along with cheese. Toss well and serve immediately.
