  • ¼ teaspoon garlic (– finely chopped, 2 small cloves)
  • 4 to 5 basil leaves (– chopped, about 1 tablespoon)
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper powder (– optional)

  • Pizza Sauce (– as required)

পদ্ধতি

Assembling & Making Tawa Pizza

  1. Divide the dough into 5 equal parts.
  2. Roll one part into a ball between your palms. Flatten it and place it on a floured rolling board.
  3. Roll it into a thin round of 0.3 to 0.5 cm (about ⅛ inch) thickness. Dust with more flour if the dough sticks.
  4. Heat a 28 to 30 cm (11 to 12 inch) tawa or pan on low heat until it is hot. Brush some olive oil on it.
  5. Gently place the rolled pizza base on the tawa.
  6. Cook the base on low heat or sim.
  7. When bubbles appear on top and the underside has golden specks, flip the base.
  8. Quickly spread pizza sauce on the cooked side.
  9. Sprinkle grated mozzarella or pizza cheese.
  10. Top with the marinated veggies and drizzle a little of the oil and juices from the bowl all over.
  11. Cover with a lid and cook on low heat or sim. A glass lid works best as you can see the cheese melting.
  12. Cook until the base is golden and crisp and the cheese has melted. Lift the pizza lightly with a spatula to check for golden specks on the underside.
  13. Serve tawa pizza hot with tomato ketchup. Sprinkle pizza seasoning or red chili flakes on top if you like.
