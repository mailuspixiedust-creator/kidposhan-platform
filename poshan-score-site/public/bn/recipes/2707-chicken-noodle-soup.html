উপকরণ

  • 125 gms , soaked Dry Cellophane Noodles
  • 3 cups Chicken Stock
  • 2 cups Water
  • 2 , skinless Chicken Breasts
  • 2 stalks , medium, inner bottom part only, bruised, cut into 3 parts, Lemongrass
  • 8 cloves Garlic
  • 1 1/2 tblsp, minced Ginger
  • 1 1/2 tblsp, minced Galangal
  • 1 1/2 tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tblsp Oil
  • Coriander Leaves - few, chopped, for garnish
  • 2 , sliced, for garnish Spring Onions
  • 3 to 4 , hardboiled Egg

পদ্ধতি

  • Pour the chicken stock and water into a large pot.
  • Place it over high heat and bring to a boil.
  • After 2 to 3 minutes, add the chicken and lemongrass.
  • Bring to a boil again.
  • Reduce flame to medium and simmer for 30 to 45 minutes.
  • Grind together the garlic, ginger and galangal to a smooth paste.
  • Heat little oil in a pan over high flame.
  • Saute the ground paste for a minute or till it starts to turn golden.
  • Switch off the flame and add the turmeric powder.
  • Mix well and keep aside.
  • Remove and transfer the chicken and lemongrass to a plate.
  • Add the garlic-ginger paste, coriander powder and salt.
  • Stir well until the paste is thoroughly mixed.
  • Allow it to simmer.
  • Meanwhile, remove the meat off the chicken bones and finely shred them.
  • Add the shredded chicken meat to the soup and bring the soup to a boil.
  • Transfer the noodles into individual serving bowls.
  • Place one hardboiled egg into each bowl and ladle the soup over.
  • Garnish with the coriander leaves and spring onions.
  • Serve warm.
