Butternut Squash Galette with Gruyère

উপকরণ

  • 1 1/4 cups (6 1/4 ounces/177 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup (2¾ ounces/78 grams) whole-wheat flour
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 3/4 teaspoon salt
  • 10 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into 1/2-inch pieces and chilled
  • 7 tablespoons ice water
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar
  • 6 ounces (170 grams) baby spinach
  • 1 1/4 pounds butternut squash, peeled and cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 5 teaspoons olive oil
  • 1 red onion, sliced thin
  • 1/2 teaspoon minced fresh oregano
  • 3 ounces (85 grams) gruyère cheese, shredded (3/4 cup)
  • 2 tablespoons crème fraîche
  • 1 teaspoon sherry vinegar
  • Salt and pepper
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons minced fresh parsley

পদ্ধতি

FOR THE DOUGH:

  1. Pulse flours, sugar, and salt in food processor until combined, 2 to 3 pulses.
  2. Add butter and pulse until butter is cut into pea-sized pieces, about 10 pulses.
  3. Transfer mixture to medium bowl.
  4. Sprinkle water and vinegar over mixture.
  5. With rubber spatula, folding mixture until loose, shaggy mass forms with some dry flour remaining (do not overwork).
  6. Transfer mixture to center of large sheet of plastic wrap, press gently into rough 4-inch square and wrap tightly.
  7. Refrigerate for at least 45 minutes.
  8. Transfer dough to lightly floured work surface.
  9. Roll into 11- by 8- inch rectangle with short side of rectangle parallel to work surface.
  10. Using bench scraper, bring bottom third of dough up, then fold upper third over it, folding like business letter into 8- by 4-inch rectangle.
  11. Turn dough counterclockwise 90 degrees.
  12. Roll out dough again, perpendicular to edge of work surface, into 11- by 8-inch rectangle and fold into thirds.
  13. Turn dough 90 degrees counterclockwise and repeat rolling and folding into thirds again.
  14. After last fold, fold dough in half to create 4-inch square.
  15. Press top of dough gently to seal.
  16. Wrap in plastic wrap and refrigerate for at least 45 minutes or up to two days.
