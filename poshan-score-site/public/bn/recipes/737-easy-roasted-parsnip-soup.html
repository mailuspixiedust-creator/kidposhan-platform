Easy Roasted Parsnip Soup

উপকরণ

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 kg parsnips (peeled and cut into small chunks, roughly 2cm / ¾inch cubes - See Note 1)
  • Salt and pepper to taste (See Note 2)
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 large onion (sliced)
  • 2 cloves garlic (grated or crushed)
  • ¼ teaspoon chilli flakes (optional – or add more if you like things hot!)
  • 1 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon cumin
  • 2 teaspoons garam masala
  • 500 ml vegetable stock (from a cube is fine, I used 1 Kallo Organic Vegetable Stock Cube)

পদ্ধতি

For the roast parsnips (See Note 3)

  1. Preheat your oven to 220C / 200C fan / gas mark 7 / 425F.
  2. Place the chopped parsnips and 2 tablespoons olive oil on a large baking sheet (or 2 small ones) – it should be large enough for the parsnip pieces to spread out in 1 layer (if they are not spread out in a single layer they will steam, not roast!).
  3. Sprinkle over a little salt and pepper (see Note 2), then turn the parsnip pieces in the oil until they are evenly coated with oil, salt and pepper.
  4. Roast the parsnips for 10 minutes
  5. Remove the tray from the oven and turn the parsnip pieces again, then return the parsnips to the oven for a further 10 minutes.
  6. While the parsnips are roasting, you can get on with the rest of the recipe…
