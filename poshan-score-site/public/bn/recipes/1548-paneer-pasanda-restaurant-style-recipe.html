পদ্ধতি

Assembling and stuffing paneer sandwiches

  1. In a bowl or plate, take the 2 to 2.5 tablespoons of grated paneer, 1 tablespoon raisins (chopped), 2 tablespoons finely chopped cashews, 1 teaspoon chopped green chilies, 1 tablespoon chopped coriander leaves and ½ tablespoon chopped mint leaves.
  2. Now add ¼ teaspoon red chili powder, ½ teaspoon cumin powder and ¼ teaspoon salt or add as required.
  3. Mix everything evenly and set aside.
  4. Slice the paneer gently in triangles. Now slice each triangle into two halves gently.
  5. Now place some of the stuffing on one paneer triangle halve.
  6. Cover it with its top halve gently. Don't press. One paneer sandwich is ready.
  7. Make all paneer sandwiches stuffed with the filling this way.
