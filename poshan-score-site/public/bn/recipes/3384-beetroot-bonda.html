Beetroot Bonda

Beetroot Bonda-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য এক সার্ভিং হিসেবে 100-এর মধ্যে 100 (ভালো)। একই খাবারের স্কোর বয়সভেদে আলাদা হয়, কারণ ছোট শিশুর চিনি, নুন ও স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাটের দৈনিক সীমা কম।

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

উপকরণ

  • 500 grams potatoes (about 2 medium-big)
  • 125 grams (1/2 of a medium) sized beetroot
  • 80-100 grams carrots (about 1 medium sized * see note#1)
  • 80-100 grams green peas (approx 1/2 cup - frozen ones will do * see note#2)
  • salt to taste
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard
  • 1 tablespoon minced ginger
  • 2 small green chillies minced (de seed them to tone down the spice or adjust the quantity to taste)
  • 20-25 curry leaves (about 3 sprigs, finely shredded)
  • 1 cup loosely packed fresh coriander leaves, finely chopped
  • 1-2 tablespoons oil
  • 2 cups gram flour/chickpea flour (besan * see note#3)
  • 1/2 tablespoon rice flour * see note#4
  • 1 teaspoon plain red chilli powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 pinch soda bi carb (baking soda)
  • 1 pinch carom seeds (ajwain/omam)
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
  • approx 1-3/4 cups water (or as required to make the batter - see notes before proceeding)
  • Oil for deep frying * see note#6

পদ্ধতি

There are two ways to cook the vegetables:

  1. A) You can pressure cook the whole potatoes (washed but not peeled or cut) in some water (enough to cover them) and salt for about 6-7 whistles.
  2. Then turn off the heat and let the cooker cool down to room temperature and then open the lid.
  3. Don't leave the potatoes after this point as they will begin to absorb the water and turn soggy when mashed.
  4. Remove, peel and mash.
  5. In another pan add the peeled and finely chopped carrots and beetroots, salt and cook till tender.
  6. Mash them well and add the potato mash and peas and mash everything well
  7. B) You can cook everything in one go by peeling the potatoes, beetroots and carrots and chopping them into tiny cubes.
  8. Transfer to a pressure cooker and add just enough water to prevent scorching.
  9. I used approx (little less than) 3/4th cup of water and salt to taste.
  10. Pressure cook for 2-3 whistles and turn off the heat.
  11. Let the cooker cool down before opening the lid.
  12. Mash well, add the frozen peas and mash coarsely.
  13. Heat the oil in a wok/kadhai and add the mustard seeds.
  14. When the stop spluttering add the shredded curry leaves, minced ginger and green chillies and reduce the heat.
  15. Fry for half a minute and then add the prepared vegetable mash.
  16. Stir in the chopped coriander and let the mixture cool completely.
  17. Form lemon sized balls and keep aside (*see notes).
  18. Sift in the gram flour, rice flour, salt, hing and chilli powder into a bowl.
  19. Stir in the carom seeds and add the water 1/4 cup at a time and mix until you get a loose batter - it should neither be too thick nor too thin.
  20. Try dropping a little batter and it should fall into droplets.
  21. Heat oil for deep frying - to test readiness, pour 1-2 drops of the batter into it and if the droplets come to the surface immediately with bubbles around them, the oil is ready.
  22. Maintain the heat of the oil on a medium high - too hot and the bonda will burn.
  23. Not hot enough and they will absorb too much oil.
  24. When the oil is ready, dip the prepared balls into the batter, roll well to coat evenly and release into the oil one by one.
  25. Do not over crowd the pan.
  26. Use a slotted spoon to flip them over and fry evenly till golden.
  27. Drain and remove onto an absorbent kitchen tissue.
  28. Serve hot with chutney or ketchup.
