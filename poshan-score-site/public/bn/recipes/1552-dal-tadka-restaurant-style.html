উপকরণ

  • 1 cup or (200 grams) tuvar dal
  • 1 to 2 green chilies (or serrano peppers - chopped or slit lengthwise)
  • ½ cup onions (- chopped)
  • 1 cup tomatoes (- finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (- finely chopped)
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder (ground turmeric)
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing, optional)
  • 2.5 cups water (- for pressure cooking)
  • 1 tablespoon light cream (or low fat cream, optional)
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves (- chopped, cilantro)
  • salt (as required)
  • 3 tablespoon oil (or 3 tablespoon ghee, clarified butter)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 5 to 6 garlic cloves (- finely chopped, small to medium-sized)
  • 2 to 3 dry red chilies (- broken and seeds removed)
  • 1 generous pinch of asafoetida (hing, optional)
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi (- crushed, dry fenugreek leaves, optional)
  • ½ teaspoon kashmiri chilli powder (or cayenne pepper or paprika)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons coriander leaves (- chopped, cilantro)
  • 1 small piece of natural charcoal
  • ¼ teaspoon oil (or ghee, clarified butter)

পদ্ধতি

Cook lentils

  1. Thoroughly rinse tuvar dal (tur dal) in water.
  2. Add them to a 3-litre stovetop pressure cooker.You can also cook the lentils in a pot.
  3. Soak the lentils for an hour or two before you cook them in a pot adding water as needed.If cooking in a pot, you could add about 3 to 3.5 cups of water.
  4. Add the chopped onions, finely chopped tomatoes, chopped green chillies and finely chopped ginger.
  5. Pour 2.5 cups of water into the pressure cooker.
  6. Add turmeric powder and asafoetida (hing). Mix well.
  7. Pressure cook the lentils on medium to medium-high heat for about 7 to 8 whistles (about 8 to 10 minutes), or until the lentils become soft and creamy.
  8. Let the pressure drop naturally in the cooker and then only open the lid. Check the lentils to see if they have cooked thoroughly and softened.
  9. Mash the cooked lentils with a wired whisk or with a spoon and set aside.
  10. If the consistency looks thick, add about ½ to 1 cup water (depending on the thickness) to get a medium consistency.
  11. Mix again and on a low to medium-low heat simmer dal for 3 to 4 minutes.
  12. Once the desired consistency is reached, add the light cream or low-fat cream (optional), garam masala powder, chopped coriander leaves (cilantro) and salt to taste.
  13. Turn off the the heat.
  14. Mix well so that everything combines to an even consistency. Taste and adjust salt before adding tadka.
