  • For the onion mixture, add 1 cupthinlysliced onions in a deep bowl.
  • Add 2 tspfinelychopped green chillies.
  • Add 2 tsplemon juice.
  • Add saltto taste.
  • Mix well and keep aside.
  • For the rotis, add 3/4 cupplain flour (maida) in a deep bowl.
  • Add 1 tbspsoft butter.
  • Add 1/2 tspsugar.
  • Add saltto taste.
  • Mix well.
  • Knead into a soft dough using enough water.
  • Cover the dough with a lid and keep aside for 15 minutes.
  • Divide the dough into 4 equal portions.
  • Roll a portion of the dough into a 175 mm. (7&rdquo;) diameter circle without using any flour for rolling.
  • Heat a non-stick tava (griddle), grease it with 1 tsp of oil.
  • Place the rolled circle on it and cook using 1 tsp of oil till it turns brown spots appear from both the sides.
  • Do not cook it till it is crisp otherwise it will be difficult to roll.
  • Repeat steps 8 to 10 to make 3 more rotis. Keep aside.
  • Break an egg in a small bowl.
  • Add a little salt.
  • Also add freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch)to taste.
  • Whisk well using a fork.
  • Heat a non-stick tava (griddle) and grease it with &frac12; tsp of oil.
  • Pour the egg mixture on it and immediately place the cooked roti over it.
  • Cook on a medium flame for 2 minutes, turn over and cook on a medium flame for 1 more minute.
  • Transfer on a clean, dry surface with the egg side facing upwards.
  • Spread &frac14;th of the onion mixture horizontally and spread 10 cucumber strips over it.
  • Spread 2 tsp tomato ketchupover it.
  • Also spread 1 tsp ofchilli sauce evenly over it.
  • Roll it tightly from both the sides.
  • Place the egg roll over a rectangular shaped butter paper and wrap one side with it. Repeat to make 3 more egg rolls.
  • Instead of roti, you can also use whole wheat chapati or laccha paratha.
  • For the stuffing, along with onions and cucumber you can also spread some coloured capsicum strips.
