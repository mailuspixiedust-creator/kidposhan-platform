উপকরণ

  • 500 g baby potatoes
  • 1/2 bunch parsley
  • 1/2 bunch rosemary
  • 2 twigs thyme
  • 1.5 tbsp salt
  • 1 tsp black pepper powder
  • 4 tbsp water

পদ্ধতি

  • Baby potatoes are ideal for this recipe; I used 500g. First, wash the potatoes very well to remove any dirt.
  • Pierce a few holes in the potatoes with a fork.
  • Next, place the potatoes into a microwave-safe bowl with a lid.
  • I like to use a glass bowl for this.
  • Add 3-4 tablespoons of water.
  • Also, add 1/2 teaspoon of salt and mix everything thoroughly.
  • Cover the bowl with its lid and microwave on high (900 watts) for 5 minutes.
  • After microwaving, you'll find the potatoes are soft and have absorbed the water.
  • Now, add fresh herbs; I used parsley, rosemary, and thyme from my kitchen. Roughly chop the herbs before adding those.
  • Finally, add 50g of butter, 1/2 teaspoon of salt, 1 teaspoon of black pepper powder, and 2 tablespoons of chopped garlic.
  • Mix everything together very well.
  • Microwave the potatoes uncovered on medium heat (600 watts) for 10 minutes.
  • After the first 5 minutes, remove the bowl and stir the potatoes well to ensure even cooking, then return them to the microwave for the remaining 5 minutes.
  • In just 15 minutes, you'll have perfectly soft potatoes, full of herb flavor, with delightfully crispy and crunchy skins, all from your microwave!
  • Carefully take the herb potatoes out of the microwave, give them another stir, and serve them hot alongside baked chicken, roasted chicken, and rice.
