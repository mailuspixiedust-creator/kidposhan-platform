উপকরণ

  • 75 gms boneless chicken breast (cut into ½ inch cubes)
  • ¼ cup milk
  • 3 dry shiitake mushrooms (hydrated)
  • ½ cup shimeji mushrooms
  • 1 carrot cut into semi-roundels
  • 3 slices ginger
  • 1 teaspoon chicken broth powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ⅛ teaspoon white pepper powder
  • 2 ½ cups water
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • ½ teaspoon cornflour mixed in 2 tablespoons water
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil

পদ্ধতি

  • Add chicken cubes to a bowl, add milk and rub the chicken gently with milk to remove the odor.
  • Discard the milk, rinse with water, drain the water and set aside the chicken cubes.
  • Slice the shiitake mushrooms into thin slices.
  • Heat a pressure cooker, add chicken, shiitake & shimeji mushrooms, carrots, ginger, broth powder, salt, pepper, water, rice, mix well, close the lid and pressure cook until 2 whistles, approx.
  • 7 minutes.
  • Let the cooker de-pressurise by itself, open the lid and set the cooker to heat on medium.
  • Add cornflour slurry, mix continuously till it boils and cook for 2-3 minutes.
  • Top with toasted sesame oil and serve hot.
