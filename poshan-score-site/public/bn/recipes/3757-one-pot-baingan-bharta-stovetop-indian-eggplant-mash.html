One-Pot Baingan Bharta (Stovetop Indian Eggplant Mash)

One-Pot Baingan Bharta (Stovetop Indian Eggplant Mash)-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য এক সার্ভিং হিসেবে 100-এর মধ্যে 80 (মোটামুটি, একটি ঝুঁকির পরীক্ষায় আটকানো)। একই খাবারের স্কোর বয়সভেদে আলাদা হয়, কারণ ছোট শিশুর চিনি, নুন ও স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাটের দৈনিক সীমা কম।

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

উপকরণ

  • 1 Eggplant (about 500 gms)
  • 2 medium ripe tomato
  • 1-2 green chili
  • 4 medium garlic
  • ½ cup onion, finely chopped
  • 2 teaspoons ginger, finely chopped
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • ¼ teaspoon spicy red chili powder (optional)
  • 2 teaspoons coriander powder
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1.5 teaspoon tomato paste
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ¼ teaspoon sugar
  • 1-2 teaspoon lime juice (optional)
  • 1 ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 3 tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon coriander leaves

পদ্ধতি

  • Peel the eggplant and cut it in half lengthwise. Cut each half into long wedges of approximately equal size for even cooking.
  • Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a wide pan and arrange the eggplant wedges in a single layer.
  • You can use more or less oil depending on your preference.
  • If using less oil, occasionally splash a little water into the pan to prevent the eggplant from sticking or burning.Cook, turning the wedges as needed, until golden and tender on all sides.
  • Make some space in the pan and add the halved tomatoes, whole garlic, green chili, and chopped onion.
  • Add 2 teaspoons of oil, cover the pan, and cook undisturbed until the vegetables are soft and lightly charred.
  • Check that the eggplant is completely soft and that the tomato skin comes off easily.
  • Remove the tomato skin, then use a potato masher to mash everything together until well combined and slightly chunky.
  • Scoot the mashed eggplant mixture to one side of the pan.
  • Add a teaspoon of oil and cumin seeds to the empty side.
  • Let the cumin seeds sizzle, then add the chopped ginger and cook for about 30 seconds.
  • Add red chili powder, coriander powder, and turmeric. Cook for about 30 seconds, then add the tomato paste and cook for another minute.
  • Mix everything together and add salt, garam masala and sugar.
  • Cook on low heat for 7-8 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  • Taste and adjust the seasoning as needed.
  • Finally, add a dash of lime juice, and lots of fresh coriander leaves.
  • Enjoy this stovetop Baingan Bharta with roti or rice.
