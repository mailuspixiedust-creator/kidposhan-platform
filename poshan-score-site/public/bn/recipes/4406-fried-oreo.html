উপকরণ

  • 20 Oreo Cookies (I use the signature cookie with vanilla stuffing)
  • 150 ml Milk (1 cup)
  • 1 Egg
  • 4 drops Vanilla essence
  • 1 Tsp. Vinegar
  • 2 Tbsp. Melted Butter
  • 350 g Plain Flour (2 cups)
  • 2 Tsp. Baking Powder
  • 1 Tsp. Baking Soda
  • 1/2 Tsp. Salt
  • 4 Tbsp. Powdered Sugar
  • 300 ml White Oil/ Vegetable Oil
  • 2 Tbsp. Powdered Sugar

পদ্ধতি

  • Keep Oreos in the refrigerator for one hour before frying.
  • Take 350g Plain Flour in a bowl.
  • Now add 2 Tsp. Baking Powder.
  • Add 1 Tsp. Baking Soda as well.
  • Add 1/2 Tsp. Salt to the mixture as well.
  • Now add 3Tbsp. Powdered Sugar to it.
  • Mix properly and strain through a strainer.
  • Take 150ml Milk at room temperature in a bowl.
  • Add 2 Tbsp. Melted Butter to it.
  • Add 1 egg (at room temperature) as well,
  • Now add a few drops of Vanilla or any other essence of your preference.
  • At this point, add 1/2 Tsp. plain Vinegar and mix properly.
  • There should not be any lump in the mixture.
  • Now add the flour mixture in small batches to the wet mixture.
  • After adding half of the flour mixture blend using a whisk.
  • Once mixed properly, add the remaining flour mixture and mix to make a semi-runny batter. This is basically Pancake batter.
  • You can add little milk to get the right consistency, which I have shown in the video for your reference.
  • Once done, keep the mixture for 10 minutes before using it as the batter.
  • Meanwhile, heat sufficient Oil (preferably White vegetable oil without any smell).
  • The pan should be half-filled with the oil as deep-fried Oreo calls for deep-frying!
  • Keep the flame medium all through.
  • Now take an Oreo cookie and coat it with the batter. I use a fork to do this.
  • Transfer the coated Oreo to the pan having hot oil.
  • You can fry around 3-5 cookies at one go depending on the size of the pan you are using. I prefer to fry 3-4 at one go.
  • You will have to fry both sides 1 Minute each.
  • The coating should turn golden brown in color.
  • If needed fry for a few more seconds.
  • Once done strain from the pan and keep the fried Oreos aside.
  • Now fry the remaining Orio cookies.
  • Once done transfer fried Oreos to a plate and sprinkle some powdered sugar before serving hot!
  • You can the deep-fried Oreos with a dollop of ice cream or your choice of sauce such as caramel sauce or coulee etc.
