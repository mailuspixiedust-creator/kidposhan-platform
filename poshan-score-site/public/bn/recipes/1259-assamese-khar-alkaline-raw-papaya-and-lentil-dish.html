Assamese Khar (Alkaline Raw Papaya & Lentil Dish)

রেসিপি: NNWA

ছবি: NNWA

Assamese Khar (Alkaline Raw Papaya & Lentil Dish)-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য এক সার্ভিং হিসেবে 100-এর মধ্যে 80 (মোটামুটি, একটি ঝুঁকির পরীক্ষায় আটকানো)। একই খাবারের স্কোর বয়সভেদে আলাদা হয়, কারণ ছোট শিশুর চিনি, নুন ও স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাটের দৈনিক সীমা কম।

উপকরণ

  • 250g raw (green) papaya, peeled, deseeded and cubed
  • 100g split yellow moong dal, lightly dry-roasted
  • 1/4 teaspoon food-grade sodium bicarbonate dissolved in 500ml water (a safe modern substitute for filtered banana-peel-ash water, or kolakhar)
  • 2 tablespoons mustard oil
  • 1 small onion, thinly sliced
  • 4 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 1 -inch piece ginger, crushed
  • 2-3 green chillies, slit lengthways
  • Salt, to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped, to finish

পদ্ধতি

  • Heat the mustard oil in a heavy-bottomed kadhai until it just begins to smoke, then lower the heat.
  • Add the sliced onion and sauté for 2-3 minutes until softened and translucent.
  • Stir in the crushed garlic, ginger and slit green chillies, and cook for a minute until fragrant.
  • Add the roasted moong dal and stir for two minutes to coat it in the oil.
  • Add the papaya cubes and stir well to combine.
  • Pour in the bicarbonate solution (or filtered kolakhar), adding a little plain water if needed to just cover the vegetables.
  • Cover and simmer on low heat for 20-25 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the papaya and dal are completely soft.
  • Season with salt, then lightly mash with the back of the ladle so the dish takes on a loose, semi-thick texture.
  • Simmer uncovered for a further 5 minutes, scatter over the coriander, and serve hot as the first course of the meal.
