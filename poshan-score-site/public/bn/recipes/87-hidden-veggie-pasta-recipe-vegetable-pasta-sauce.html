উপকরণ

  • Macaroni - 1 cup
  • Olive Oil - 1 tblsp
  • Garlic - 4 to 5 cloves
  • Carrot - 1 chopped
  • Capsicum - 1 chopped
  • Zucchini - 1/2 chopped
  • Cauliflower - 1/2 cup chopped
  • Tomato - 4 to 5 chopped
  • Oregano - 1 tsp
  • Salt to taste
  • Sugar a pinch
  • Tomato Ketchup - 2 to 3 tblsp
  • Butter - 1 tblsp

পদ্ধতি

  • Cook pasta in salted boiling water till it is done. Drain and set aside.
  • Heat oil in a pan, add in garlic and saute for a min.
  • Add in veggies and saute for 5 to 6 mins till cooked. Take it in a blender and puree till smooth.
  • Now heat butter in a pan, add oregano and mix well.
  • Add the pureed sauce and mix well.
  • Add in salt, sugar, ketchup and mix well.
  • Simmer for 5 mins.
  • Add cooked pasta and mix well.
  • Serve.
