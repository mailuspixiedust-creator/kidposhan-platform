Oatmeal Creme Pies

উপকরণ

  • 1 cup (5 ounces/142 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1 teaspoon table salt
  • ¾ teaspoon baking soda
  • 3 cups (9 ounces/255 grams) old-fashioned rolled oats
  • ½ cup (3 ounces/85 grams) raisins
  • ¾ cup packed (5¼ ounces/149 grams) dark brown sugar
  • ½ cup (3½ ounces/99 grams) granulated sugar
  • ½ cup (105 grams) vegetable oil
  • 4 tablespoons (57 grams) unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 teaspoons molasses
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1 large egg plus 1 large yolk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 5 ounces (142 grams/1¼ cups) Fluff brand marshmallow crème
  • ¾ cup (3 ounces/85 grams) confectioners' sugar
  • 6 tablespoons (86 grams) unsalted butter, softened
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ¼ teaspoon table salt

পদ্ধতি

FOR THE COOKIES:

  1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 400 degrees.
  2. Line 2 rimmed baking sheets with parchment paper.
  3. Whisk flour, salt, and baking soda together in medium bowl; set aside.
  4. Process oats and raisins in food processor until very finely ground, about 1 minute.
  5. Whisk brown sugar, granulated sugar, oil, melted butter, molasses, and cinnamon in large bowl until smooth.
  6. Whisk in egg and yolk and vanilla until smooth.
  7. Using wooden spoon or spatula, stir in flour mixture until evenly combined and no dry flour remains.
  8. Stir in oat-raisin mixture until evenly distributed. (Mixture will be stiff; once dough begins to stiffen, you can mix with your hands until dough is evenly combined.)
  9. Divide dough into twenty-four 1⅓-ounce (38-gram) portions, about 1½ tablespoons each.
  10. Roll dough portions between your hands to form smooth balls.
  11. Evenly space dough balls on prepared sheets, 12 balls per sheet.
  12. Using your hand or bottom of dry measuring cup, flatten balls to 2½-inch-wide disks.
  13. Bake cookies, 1 sheet at a time, until tops are beginning to crack at edges, 5 to 7 minutes.
  14. Let cookies cool on sheet for 5 minutes.
  15. Using spatula, transfer cookies to wire rack and let cool completely, about 45 minutes.
