One-Pot Pork and Cabbage Stew with Cheddar Dumplings

উপকরণ

  • 1 (12- to 16-ounce/340- to 454-gram) pork tenderloin, trimmed, halved lengthwise, and sliced crosswise ¼ inch thick
  • 1 ¼ teaspoons table salt, divided
  • 1 ¼ teaspoons pepper, divided
  • ½ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 5 tablespoons all-purpose flour, divided
  • 6 slices bacon, chopped
  • ½ head green cabbage, halved, cored, and cut into 1-inch pieces (8 cups)
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 carrots, peeled and chopped
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 teaspoons chopped fresh rosemary
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 ½ tablespoons Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley
  • 1 ½ cups (7½ ounces/213 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1 ½ teaspoons sugar
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon table salt
  • 4 ounces (113 grams) extra-sharp cheddar cheese, shredded (1 cup), divided
  • 1 ¼ cups heavy cream

পদ্ধতি

FOR THE STEW:

  1. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 425 degrees.
  2. Sprinkle pork with ¾ teaspoon salt, ¾ teaspoon pepper, and cayenne.
  3. Toss pork with 1 tablespoon flour; set aside.
  4. Cook bacon in Dutch oven over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned, 8 to 10 minutes. Using slotted spoon, transfer bacon to plate.
  5. Add pork in single layer to fat left in pot, breaking up any clumps.
  6. Cook, without stirring, until well browned on bottom, 3 to 5 minutes.
  7. Using slotted spoon, transfer pork to plate with bacon.
  8. Add cabbage, onion, carrots, garlic, rosemary, remaining ½ teaspoon salt, and remaining ½ teaspoon pepper to fat left in pot (if very little fat remains, add 1 tablespoon vegetable oil).
  9. Cook, stirring occasionally, until cabbage is wilted, 5 to 7 minutes.
  10. Stir in remaining ¼ cup flour to coat vegetables.
  11. Slowly stir in broth and mustard, scraping up any browned bits and smoothing out any lumps with wooden spoon.
  12. Bring to simmer.
  13. Cover; reduce heat to medium-low; and simmer until flavors meld, about 7 minutes.
  14. Off heat, stir in pork, bacon, and any accumulated juices.
