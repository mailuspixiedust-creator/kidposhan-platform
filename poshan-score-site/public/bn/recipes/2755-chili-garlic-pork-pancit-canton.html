Chili Garlic Pork Pancit Canton

উপকরণ

  • 500 grams flour stick noodles (pancit canton)
  • 3/4 lbs pork belly (thinly sliced)
  • 12 pieces long green beans (sliced diagonally)
  • 2 pieces carrots (medium, julienned)
  • 1 pieces cabbage (1 head, chopped)
  • 1/2 cup flat-leaf parsley (chopped)
  • 1/2 cup onion leeks (thinly sliced, white and green parts separated)
  • 1 pieces red bell pepper (large, julienned)
  • 2 pieces onions (sliced)
  • 1/4 cup garlic (minced)
  • 3 tablespoons chili garlic oil (including the chili solids)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 8 grams MAGGI Magic Sarap
  • 3 cups chicken broth
  • 1 1/2 cups water
  • 1/2 cup cooking oil (for toasting the garlic)
  • ground black pepper (to taste)

পদ্ধতি

  • Heat 1/2 cup cooking oil in a pan over medium-low heat.
  • Add the minced garlic and cook, stirring frequently, until golden brown.
  • Immediately strain the garlic from the oil.
  • Set the toasted garlic and the garlic-infused oil aside separately.
  • Prepare an ice bath by combining 4 cups of ice and 6 cups of water in a large bowl. Set aside.
  • Bring 12 cups of water to a boil in a large cooking pot.
  • Blanch the long green beans, carrots, and cabbage in batches for 35 to 50 seconds.
  • Immediately transfer the vegetables to the ice bath to stop the cooking.
  • After 2 minutes, drain thoroughly and set aside.
  • Heat 3 tablespoons of the reserved garlic-infused oil in a large wok or cooking pot.
  • Add the pork belly and sauté for 4 to 5 minutes, or until lightly browned.
  • Pour in 1 1/2 cups water and bring to a boil.
  • Continue simmering until the water evaporates completely and some of the pork fat renders.
  • Add the sliced onions and the white parts of the onion leeks. Sauté until softened.
  • Add the chili garlic oil, including the chili solids. Stir-fry over medium heat for 30 seconds to release its aroma.
  • Pour in the soy sauce and oyster sauce. Stir until well combined with the pork.
  • Add the chicken broth and bring to a boil. Continue cooking for 5 to 10 minutes, or until the pork belly is tender.
  • Add the flour stick noodles.
  • Gently toss until they soften and absorb the seasoned broth.
  • Add a little hot water or additional chicken broth as needed so the noodles cook evenly without drying out.
  • Sprinkle in the MAGGI Magic Sarap. Toss until evenly distributed throughout the noodles.
  • Add the chopped parsley, the green parts of the onion leeks, the julienned red bell pepper, and about three-fourths of the toasted garlic.
  • Toss gently to combine.
  • Season with ground black pepper.
  • Return the blanched vegetables to the wok and toss everything together.
  • Continue cooking for 1 to 2 minutes, or until the vegetables are heated through and evenly distributed.
  • Transfer the pancit canton to a serving platter.
  • Sprinkle the remaining toasted garlic on top.
  • Drizzle with a little of the reserved garlic-infused oil or additional chili garlic oil, if desired.
  • Share and enjoy!
