Methi Thepla Recipe Without Curd

উপকরণ

  • 3 cups whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)
  • 1 cup finely chopped fenugreek leaves (methi)
  • 1 tsp green chilli paste
  • 2 tsp garlic (lehsun) paste
  • 1/2 tsp ginger (adrak) paste
  • 1 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp coriander-cumin seeds (dhania-jeera) powder
  • 1/2 tsp haldi powder (turmeric powder)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds (til)
  • 2 tbsp besan (gram flour)
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta) for rolling
  • oil for cooking

পদ্ধতি

  • To make the dough for thethepla take a deep bowl and add the 3 cups whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta) into it.
  • Add the 1 cup finely chopped fenugreek leaves (methi). You can also add kasoori methi or dreid fenugreek leaves if you do not have fresh fenugreek leaves.
  • Add the 1 tsp green chilli paste for the spice.
  • Add the 2 tsp garlic (lehsun) paste.
  • Also add the 1/2 tsp ginger (adrak) paste. Both ginger and garlic paste are added to flavour the dough of the Methi Thepla.
  • Now add the 1 1/2 tsp chilli powder. You can increase or decrease the quantity of the chili powder according to your spice tolerance level.
  • Add the 1 tbsp sugar. It balances the flavours of the masalas that we are adding.
  • Add the 1 tsp coriander-cumin seeds (dhania-jeera) powder.
  • Add the 1/2 tsp turmeric powder (haldi) for the colour.
  • Add the 1 tsp cumin seeds (jeera).
  • Add the 1/4 tsp asafoetida (hing) for the desired astringent flavour.
  • Also 3 tbsp oil. This will make the Methi Theplas soft.
  • Add the 1 tbsp sesame seeds (til). It gives a mild nutty flavour to the Methi Theplas.
  • Furthermore, add the 2 tbsp besan (Bengal gram flour). You can also add other flours like bajra or jowar flour to make it more nutritious.
  • Finally, add the salt to taste.
  • Mix well.
  • Add about 1 cup of water. You may require more water depending upon the quality of the flour.
  • Divide the dough into 24 equal portions.
  • Sprinkle a little whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)r on the rolling surface.
  • Take a portion of the dough and flatten it.
  • Roll out each portion into a 150 mm. (6") diameter circle using a rolling pin.
  • Heat a non-stick tava (griddle) and cook the Methi Thepla, on a medium flame.
  • Apply a little oil, and let it turn golden brown on one side.
  • Flip and cook the other side in the same manner. Repeat the steps 2 to 7 with the remaining dough to make 23 more theplas.
  • Serve immediately or cool the thepla for travel and wrap in an aluminium foil.
  • Pack them in an air-tight container and store in your refrigerator.
  • Theplas will last for 15 days and perfect to carry when you travel by plane or train and then store in the refrigerator.
