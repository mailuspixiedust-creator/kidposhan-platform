Matar Paneer (Instant Pot & Stovetop)

উপকরণ

  • 1 tablespoon Oil
  • 1 teaspoon Cumin seeds (Jeera)
  • 1 Green Chili Pepper (finely diced)
  • 1 1/4 cup Onion (diced)
  • 1 teaspoon Ginger (grated)
  • 1 teaspoon Garlic (minced)
  • 2 Tomato (large, pureed, about 1 1/4 cup)
  • 10 Cashews
  • 10 ounces Paneer or Cottage cheese (cubed)
  • 1 cup Green peas (I used frozen)
  • 3/4 cup Water
  • 1 teaspoon Garam Masala (adjust to taste)
  • 3 tablespoon Cream
  • 2 tablespoon Dried Fenugreek leaves (Kasoori Methi) (Optional)
  • Cilantro (to garnish)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Ground Turmeric (Haldi powder)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon Coriander powder (Dhaniya powder)
  • 1 teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)

পদ্ধতি

  • Start the Instant Pot in sauté mode. Add oil and cumin seeds.
  • Once the cumin seeds start to splutter, add diced green chili, onion, ginger and garlic. Let this cook for 4 mins until the onions turn light brown.
  • On the side, blend tomatoes with the cashews in a blender to a smooth paste.
  • Once the onion is cooked, add the tomatoes paste and spices to the instant pot and cook for another 3-4 mins.
  • Add the cubed paneer, green peas and water to the instant pot.
  • If anything is stuck to the bottom, deglaze the pot.
  • Press cancel and close lid with vent in sealing position.
  • Change the instant pot setting to manual or pressure cook mode on high pressure for 2 minutes.
  • Once the instant pot beeps, quick release the pressure manually.
  • Add the garam masala and cream.
  • Roll the kasuri methi with your hand to make it a fine powder and add to the curry.
  • Stir it all gently.
  • Garnish with cilantro. Mutter paneer is ready to serve.

    • Stovetop Method

    1. When cooking on the stovetop, use a heavy bottom pan or dutch oven.
    2. The initial steps to make matar paneer are same as above.
    3. Make sure to saute the onions well for 4-5 minutes until they are golden brown in color.
    4. When sautéing the tomatoes, make sure all the liquid is dried and the tomatoes are completely softened.
    5. Then add the green peas, paneer and water. Stir gently, and let it simmer for about 6-8 minutes, until the paneer and peas soften. Stir at regular intervals.
    6. Add the garam masala and cream.
    7. Roll the kasuri methi with your hand to make it a fine powder and add to the curry.
    8. Stir it all gently.
    9. Garnish with cilantro.
    10. Mutter paneer is ready to serve.
