Rajgira Curd Rice

উপকরণ

  • 1/4 cup amaranth (rajgira)
  • 3/4 cup water (for cooking)
  • 1 cup yogurt (whisked)
  • 1/4 cup carrot (shredded)
  • 1/4 cup cucumber (shredded)
  • salt and pepper (to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons pomegranate
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves
  • 1 teaspoon ghee (or oil)
  • 1 tablespoon peanuts
  • 6-8 cashew nuts
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 stalk curry leaves

পদ্ধতি

  • Rinse ¼ cup of amaranth (rajgira) thoroughly.
  • Add it to a saucepan with ¾ cup of water and cook over medium-low heat for about 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the amaranth is tender and the water is absorbed.
  • Add 1 cup whisked homemade yogurt, ¼ cup shredded cucumber, ¼ cup shredded carrot, and ½ cup cooked amaranth (rajgira), salt, and black pepper to a bowl.
  • Mix well until everything is evenly combined.
  • Next, prepare the tempering.
  • Heat 1 teaspoon of homemade ghee (or oil) in a tadka pan.
  • Add ½ teaspoon cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  • Then, add the raw peanuts, cashews, and curry leaves.
  • Sauté until the nuts turn lightly golden and aromatic.
  • Pour the prepared tempering over the yogurt mixture. Add 2 tablespoons of pomegranate seeds and 2 tablespoons of chopped coriander leaves. Mix gently.
  • Serve the amaranth curd rice fresh as a main dish or a side dish with pickle, papad, potato fry, or beetroot poriyal.
