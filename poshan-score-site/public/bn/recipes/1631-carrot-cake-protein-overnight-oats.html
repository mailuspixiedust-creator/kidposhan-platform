উপকরণ

  • 1 ripe banana
  • 1/2 large carrot
  • 90 g Dunnes Stores Oats
  • 250 ml Dunnes Stores Greek Yogurt
  • 60 ml Dunnes Stores High Protein Cottage Cheese
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 2 tablespoons raisins
  • Extra Dunnes Stores Greek Yogurt
  • Finely ground walnuts

পদ্ধতি

  • Mash the banana until it turns into a smooth puree, then place into a bowl.
  • Finely grate the carrot and add to the bowl along with the oats, yogurt, cottage cheese, cinnamon and raisins.
  • Give the mixture a really good stir until it is really smooth and totally combined, then spoon into a serving jar.
  • Top with the extra Greek Yogurt then sprinkle over the ground walnuts.
  • Leave in the fridge overnight before serving.
