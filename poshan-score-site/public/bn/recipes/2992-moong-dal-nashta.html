70মোটামুটি

Moong dal nashta-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য এক সার্ভিং হিসেবে 100-এর মধ্যে 70 (মোটামুটি)। একই খাবারের স্কোর বয়সভেদে আলাদা হয়, কারণ ছোট শিশুর চিনি, নুন ও স্যাচুরেটেড ফ্যাটের দৈনিক সীমা কম।

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

উপকরণ

  • ½ cup wheat flour
  • ¼ cup coarse wheat flour or rava
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ¼ cup water or as required
  • 1 cup soaked green moong dal
  • 1 tbsp crushed coriander seeds
  • 1 tsp fennel seeds
  • 1 tsp ajwain
  • ½ tsp red chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ¾ cup chopped onion
  • 2 chopped green chilli
  • ½ tsp ginger paste
  • ¼ cup coriander leaves
  • ½ cup soaked dates
  • ½ cup soaked tamarind
  • 1 tbsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp black salt
  • 1 tbsp ginger powder
  • 1 tbsp jeera powder
  • 2 tbsp sugar or jaggery
  • 2 tbsp chopped onion
  • Some coriander leaves
  • Water as required

পদ্ধতি

Making moong dal puri

  1. In a mixing bowl, add wheat flour, coarse wheat flour, turmeric powder, red chilli powder, salt and oil. Mix well.
  2. Add water gradually and knead a tight and smooth dough.
  3. Cover and rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Then divide the dough into two equal parts and roll it into an oval shape sheet.
  5. Prick it with a fork and cut it into smaller size squares or circles.
  6. Make a square from all the dough. Cover and keep it aside.
  7. Now discard water from 1 cup soaked green moong dal.
  8. Grind dal into the coarse batter without adding water to it.
  9. Take out dal batter into mixing bowl, add crushed coriander seeds, fennel seeds, ajwain, red chilli flakes, garam masala, salt, chopped onion, chopped green chilli, ginger paste and coriander leaves.
  10. Mix well
  11. Then spread a teaspoon of batter on rolled square puri.
  12. Heat oil in a pan, add batter-coated puri and fry till it becomes crispy from both sides.
  13. Crispy moong dal puri is ready to serve with chutney.
  14. In another batter make a bhajiya from the batter and fry it on medium heat till it becomes crispy on both sides.
  15. Serve moong dal bhajiya with chutney.

Moong dal nashta কি বাচ্চাদের জন্য স্বাস্থ্যকর?

Moong dal nashta-এর স্কোর 4–6 বছর বয়সের জন্য 100-এর মধ্যে 70 (মোটামুটি)। 10টি পরীক্ষার মধ্যে 7টিতে পাস করে এবং অ্যাডেড সুগার, সোডিয়াম, বেশি ফ্যাট, চিনি বা নুন-এ নম্বর হারায়।
