উপকরণ

  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 6 stalks scallions (thinly sliced, separate white and green parts)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger grated
  • 1 teaspoon garlic minced
  • 2 cups frozen sweet corn kernels (divided)
  • 4 cups low sodium vegetable broth (Made with Better Than Bouillon)
  • 1 tablespoon corn starch or arrowroot flour
  • ¼ cup water
  • ½ cup carrots julienned
  • ½ cup green cabbage (thinly sliced)
  • black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar

পদ্ধতি

Stove Top Recipe

  1. Heat oil in a 5-Qt pot over medium heat and add scallions. Saute for a minute.
  2. Add ginger and garlic and saute for another 30 seconds.
  3. Stir in half of the corn and the vegetable broth.
  4. Bring to a full boil and then lower the heat.
  5. Cook on low heat for 10 to 15 minutes.
  6. Using a hand blender, blend the soup for a minute. Stir in arrowroot powder mixed with water and cook on medium heat.
  7. Add carrots, cabbage, and the remaining corn. Add vinegar and bring the soup to a boil.
  8. Taste and adjust the seasoning with salt and season with black pepper. Ladle the soup in bowls garnish with green onions and serve hot.
