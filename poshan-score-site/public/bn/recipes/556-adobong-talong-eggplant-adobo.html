উপকরণ

  • 3 pieces Chinese eggplant (medium size)
  • 5 tablespoons cooking oil
  • 5 cloves garlic (chopped)
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 3 tablespoons cane vinegar
  • 1/4 cup water
  • 1 teaspoon brown sugar
  • 2 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon cracked black pepper

পদ্ধতি

  • Remove the stems from the eggplants. Slice each eggplant into 3-inch pieces, then cut each piece in half lengthwise.
  • Heat 4 tablespoons of cooking oil in a pan over medium heat.
  • Fry the eggplant in batches for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until lightly browned and tender. Transfer to a plate.
  • Heat the remaining cooking oil in the same pan. Add the garlic and saut&eacute; for 30 seconds.
  • Pour in the soy sauce and water. Add the cracked black pepper and dried bay leaves and let the mixture boil.
  • Pour in the cane vinegar. Let it boil for 1 minute without stirring.
  • Add the brown sugar . Stir and simmer for 2 minutes.
  • Return the fried eggplant to the pan. Gently toss until each piece is coated with the adobo sauce.
  • Simmer for 2 minutes, until the eggplant absorbs some of the sauce. Keep the stirring light so the eggplant stays whole.
  • Transfer to a serving plate. Serve with warm rice. Share and enjoy!

    • পদ্ধতি: Panlasang Pinoy. মূল রেসিপি দেখুন.
