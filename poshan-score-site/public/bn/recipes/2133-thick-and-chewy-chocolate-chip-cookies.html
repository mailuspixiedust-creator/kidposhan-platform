উপকরণ

  • 2 cups plus 2 tablespoons (10 2/3 ounces/302 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 12 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • 1 cup packed (7 ounces/198 grams) light brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup (3 1/2 ounces/99 grams) granulated sugar
  • 1 large egg plus 1 large yolk
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1 1/2 cups (9 ounces/255 grams) semisweet chocolate chips

পদ্ধতি

  • Adjust oven rack to lower-middle position and heat oven to 325 degrees.
  • Line 2 baking sheets with parchment paper.
  • Combine 2 cups plus 2 tablespoons (10⅔ ounces) all-purpose flour, ½ teaspoon baking soda, and ½ teaspoon table salt in bowl.
  • Using stand mixer fitted with paddle, beat melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar on medium speed until smooth, about 2 minutes.
  • Add egg and yolk and vanilla and beat until combined.
  • Reduce speed to low and add flour mixture in 3 additions until just combined, scraping down bowl as needed.
  • Stir in chocolate chips by hand.
  • Working with 2 tablespoons dough at a time, roll into balls and space them 2 inches apart on prepared sheets.
  • Bake cookies, 1 sheet at a time, until edges are set and beginning to brown but centers are still soft and puffy, 15 to 20 minutes, rotating sheet halfway through baking.
  • Let cookies cool on sheets for 10 minutes before serving.
