উপকরণ

  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 onion, chopped fine
  • 1 red bell pepper, seeded and chopped fine
  • 1 jalapeño chile, seeded and chopped fine
  • 1 tablespoon chili powder
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 quarts low-sodium chicken broth
  • 1 1/2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken breast
  • 1 can diced tomatoes (14.5-ounce), drained
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh cilantro leaves
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • Salt and pepper

পদ্ধতি

  • Heat oil in large Dutch oven over medium heat.
  • Add onion, bell pepper, and jalapeno and cook, stirring frequently, until softened, about 8 minutes.
  • Stir in chili powder and garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  • Add broth and chicken and bring to boil.
  • Reduce heat and simmer until chicken is cooked through, about 20 minutes.
  • With slotted spoon, remove chicken; cool and shred into small pieces.
  • Return shredded chicken to pot along with tomatoes, cilantro, and lime juice. Season with salt and pepper. Serve with recommended garnishes.
