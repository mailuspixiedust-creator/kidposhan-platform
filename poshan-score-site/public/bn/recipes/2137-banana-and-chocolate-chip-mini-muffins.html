উপকরণ

  • Vegetable oil spray
  • 1 ⅓ cups (6⅔ ounces/189 grams) all-purpose flour
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon table salt
  • 2 very ripe bananas (skins should be speckled black)
  • ½ cup (3½ ounces/99 grams) sugar
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, melted and cooled
  • ¼ cup plain yogurt
  • 1 large egg
  • ½ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cup (2 ounces/57 grams) mini chocolate chips

পদ্ধতি

  • Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 375 degrees.
  • Spray 24-­cup mini muffin tin well with vegetable oil spray (make sure to get inside each mini cup!).
  • In medium bowl, whisk together flour, baking soda, and salt.
  • Peel bananas and place in large bowl. Use large fork or potato masher to mash bananas until broken down but still chunky.
  • Add sugar, melted butter, yogurt, egg, and vanilla to bowl with bananas and whisk until combined.
  • Add flour mixture and chocolate chips and use rubber spatula to gently stir until just combined and no dry flour is visible.
  • Batter should look thick and chunky—­do not overmix.
  • Spray 1-­tablespoon measuring spoon with vegetable oil spray. Use greased measuring spoon to scoop 1 heaping tablespoon batter into each muffin tin cup.
  • Place muffin tin in oven. Bake until muffins are golden brown and toothpick inserted in center of 1 muffin comes out clean, 13 to 15 minutes.
  • Use oven mitts to remove muffin tin from oven and place on cooling rack (ask an adult for help). Let muffins cool in muffin tin for 15 minutes.
  • Using your fingertips, gently wiggle muffins to loosen from muffin tin and transfer directly to cooling rack.
  • Let muffins cool for at least 5 minutes before serving.
