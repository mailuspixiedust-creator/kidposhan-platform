উপকরণ

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 2 tablespoons semolina (sooji/rava)
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • 2 teaspoons oil (optional)
  • 3/4 cup water (or as needed, to knead)
  • oil (for frying)

পদ্ধতি

Kneading The Dough

  1. Take a large mixing bowl, and add 2 cups of wheat flour, 2 tablespoons of fine rava (sooji or semolina), salt to taste, sugar, and 2 teaspoons of oil.
  2. Mix well.
  3. Rava is added to make the poori crispier.
  4. Next, Add the water little by little and knead the dough using your fingers and hands, or use a standing mixer.
  5. The dough must be smooth, and tight (Not too soft like roti and not too stiff like mathri).
  6. Grease the dough with a dash of oil to prevent it from drying.
  7. Cover the bowl with a plate or damp cloth.
  8. Let it rest for at least 15 minutes at room temperature.
