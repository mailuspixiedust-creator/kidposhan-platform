Ingredient-first · Scored for kids

Packaged foods, scored

Every product below gets a Poshan Score out of 100, from the nutrition label printed on the pack, worked out for one realistic serving (the same way recipes are scored, so the two can be compared). The score is made of ten yes-or-no checks, each worth 10 points, and it is worked out for the age you pick. A low score is not a warning. It is a reason to keep it occasional. We search for new products every few hours and read their labels, and a product appears here once its label has been read and checked.
