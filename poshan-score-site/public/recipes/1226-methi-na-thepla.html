Methi na thepla

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 35 minServes 6VegetarianBreakfastLunchSchool lunchboxFrom 3 years

Methi na thepla scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Pinch of hing
  • 1 tsp ajwain
  • 1 tbsp white sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp green chilli paste
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 cup chopped methi leaves
  • 1 cup coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ½ cup grated dudhi (lauki)
  • 2 tbsp curd
  • 2 tbsp fresh malai
  • 2 cup wheat flour
  • ¼ cup gram flour (besan)
  • 4 tbsp water or as requied
  • Oil for roasting thepla
  • 4 medium size tomatoes or 1 cup grated tomatoes
  • 1 cup sugar
  • ¼ tsp black salt
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp achar masala
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice

Method

Making Methi Thepla

  1. In a pan, add 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp cumin seeds, a pinch of hing, 1 tsp ajwain, and 1 tbsp white sesame seeds. Sauté.
  2. Add 1 tbsp green chili paste and 1 tsp ginger paste. Sauté.
  3. Add 1 tsp turmeric powder and mix well. Add 1 cup chopped methi leaves, 1 cup chopped coriander leaves, and salt. Sauté.
  4. Add 1 tsp coriander powder, 1 tsp sugar, and 1 tsp lemon juice. Mix well, turn off the heat, and let the mixture cool.
  5. In a mixing bowl, grate ½ cup dudhi (lauki). Add the methi mixture, 2 tbsp curd, and 2 tbsp malai. Mix well.
  6. Add 2 cups wheat flour and ¼ cup besan. Mix well.
  7. Add some water as needed and knead a medium-soft dough for the thepla.
  8. Pinch a medium-sized ball of dough, roll, and flatten it.
  9. On a rolling board, roll the ball into a thin thepla.
  10. Place the rolled thepla on a hot tawa and cook for a minute.
  11. When the base is partly cooked, apply some oil or ghee and roast the thepla on medium flame. Repeat for all theplas.
  12. Serve the methi thepla with sweet tomato chutney.
