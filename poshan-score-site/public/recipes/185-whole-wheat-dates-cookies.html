Whole Wheat Dates Cookies

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup dates (khajur)
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)
  • 1/2 cup butter
  • 4 tbsp sugar

Method

  • To make whole wheat date cookies, take 1/4 cupdates (khajur).
  • Chop the dates into small pieces and discard the seeds.
  • Transfer them into a saucepan.
  • Add &frac12; cup of water.
  • Bring it to boil, then simmer for 10 to 12 minutes or till the dates are well cooked. Cool it completely.
  • Transfer it into a blender jar.
  • Blend to a smooth puree. This yields approx. &frac14; cup of date puree. Keep aside.
  • In a deep bowl, add 1 cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta).
  • Add 1/2cup butter.
  • Addsugar.
  • Mix well using fingertips till it resembles the texture of breadcrumbs.
  • Add the cooled date puree.
  • Knead it into a semi-soft dough.
  • Cover the dough with a plastic film and refrigerate for 15 minutes.
  • Place the dough on the rolling board.
  • Roll out into a sheet of 10&rdquo; (250 mm.) diameter.
  • Cut out 7 cookies using a cookie cutter.
  • These are the scrapings of the dough.
  • Re-roll the scrapings and make 7 more cookies. You will get 14 cookies in all.
  • Place all the cookies on a greased baking tray.
  • Bake in a preheated oven at 180&deg;C (360&deg;F) for 25 minutes or till the cookies are golden brown in colour.
  • This is how the cookies look after baking. Cool completely.
  • Health conscious people can avoid adding sugar. However the sweetness will be less.
  • Remember to cool the date mixture completely before blending.
  • Keep enough distance between each cookie while placing them on the baking tray.
  • Also ensure to place the baking tray in the middle rack of the oven, else they tend to burn easily.
