Chickpea Salad Recipe (No Oil, Vegan)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup cooked chickpeas
  • 1 English Cucumber or Hothouse Cucumber (medium size, finely chopped)
  • ½ cup Cherry Tomatoes (halved)
  • 1 Onion (medium size, finely chopped)
  • ¾ cup Green Bell Pepper (finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup Cilantro (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Mint (finely chopped)
  • 1 ½ tsp Za'atar Spice
  • ½ tsp Red Chilli Flakes (or use 1/4 tsp paprika)
  • ¼ tsp Black Pepper Powder
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • Juice of 2 limes

Method

  • In a wide salad bowl, add cooked chickpeas, chopped vegetables (cucumber, peppers, onion and cherry tomatoes).
  • In another bowl add (za'atar, salt, pepper, red chilli flakes) and squeeze juice from 2 limes.
  • Add half the quantity of cilantro and mint and reserve the rest for garnish.
  • Add the dressing to the chickpeas mix and toss well. Garnish with more cilantro and mint. Refrigerate for 30 minutes before serving.
