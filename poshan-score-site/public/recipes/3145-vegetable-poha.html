Vegetable Poha

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 25 minServes 4VegetarianBreakfastLunchSchool lunchboxFrom 3 years

Vegetable Poha scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup poha
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 3 cup hot water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 3 tbsp peanuts
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 6-7 curry leaves
  • 2-3 chopped green chilli
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 3 tbsp chopped carrot
  • 3 tbsp chopped capsicum
  • 3 tbsp green peas
  • 1 medium size or 3/4 cup chopped potatoes
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ½ cup chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ cup chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • Ratlami sev
  • Pomegranate seeds

Method

  • sieve poha and soak it only for 2 minutes in hot water with turmeric powder.
  • Sieve water from poha and dry them completely.
  • Then add salt and sugar into soaked poha and mix well.
  • Now in a pan, add oil and peanuts. Fry peanuts till it slightly brown. Keep it aside.
  • In the same pan, add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, curry leaves, chopped green chilli and ginger paste. Sauté for a minute.
  • Then add chopped carrot, capsicum, green peas and chopped potatoes. add salt and mix vegetable into oil.
  • Now sprinkle some water on vegetable. Cover and cook vegetable on low flame till it become slightly soft.
  • Then add chopped onion and chopped tomates. Mix well and cook them on high flame till it become slightly soft.
  • Now add red chilli powder garam masala. mix well.
  • Add soaked poha, some coriander leaves, lemon juice and roasted peanuts. mix well. Poha is ready
  • To serve vegetable poha and top it with ratlami sev and pomegranate seeds.

    Common questions

    Is Vegetable Poha healthy for kids?

    Vegetable Poha scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

    What age is Vegetable Poha best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
