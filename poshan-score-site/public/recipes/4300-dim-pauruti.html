Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 Double Roti / Quarter Bread
  • 1 Onion (small)
  • 2 Green Chilli
  • 1 Tbsp. Butter
  • 1 Tsp Mustard Oil
  • 1/4 Tsp Salt (or to taste)

Method

  • Roughly chop the onion as well as the chilies.
  • In a bowl take two eggs along with the chopped onion and also chilles.
  • Add salt to it and beat for a while to make the mixture frothy.
  • Meanwhile, cut the bread lengthwise if not purchased in pieces.
  • Heat a pan and lightly toast the bread.
  • Add butter and toast again for 1 minute.
  • With the help of a spatula pick up the bread slices and add the mustard oil to the pan.
  • Spread the oil and then pour the egg mixture and spread it across.
  • place the bread slices and lightly fold the omelet over the bread.
  • Take the egg-covered bread out on a plate and sprinkle some rock salt and serve it hot.

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
