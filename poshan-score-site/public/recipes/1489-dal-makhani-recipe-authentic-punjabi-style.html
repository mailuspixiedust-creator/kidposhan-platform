Method

Preparation & Cooking Lentils

  1. Soak both the whole urad dal and rajma overnight in enough water for 8 to 9 hours or overnight. Later drain them well.
  2. Rinse both the lentils a couple of times in water.
  3. Drain again and then add them in a 3 litre pressure cooker. Add water and stir well.
  4. Pressure cook for 18 to 20 whistles on a high flame, till both the urad dal and rajma have cooked thoroughly and softened.
  5. If they are undercooked, then add about ½ cup water again and pressure cook for 4 to 5 whistles more.
  6. The urad dal should melt in the mouth and should not give any bite or resistance when eaten.
  7. You can also just mash the urad dal with a spoon or with your fingers to check the doneness.
  8. The same rule applies for kidney beans too.
  9. Keep the cooked lentils and beans aside.
  10. In a blender or mixer jar, take chopped tomatoes and blend to a fine and smooth puree. Set aside.
  11. You can also add ready 1 cup tomato puree instead of blending the tomatoes. No need to blanch the tomatoes while pureeing.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
