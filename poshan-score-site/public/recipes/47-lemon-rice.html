Lemon Rice

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 25 minServes 6VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

How often: Often A few times a week. The Poshan Score shows how one serving does against a child's daily limits; this line is about how the food is made.

Lemon Rice scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • ¼ cup Oil
  • 2 tbsp Split Urad dal
  • 2 tbsp Split Channa Dal
  • 15 no Cashews
  • 1 tsp Mustard seeds
  • 3 no Dry Red Chillies (broken into small pieces)
  • 2 sprig Curry leaves
  • ½ tsp Asafoetida
  • 1 tbsp Ginger (peeled & chopped finely)
  • 2 no Green chillies (chopped finely)
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tbsp Salt
  • ½ cup Lemon juice
  • 2 cups Rice (I used ponni) (for basmati rice, check notes)
  • 5 cups Water

Method

  • Cooking Rice - I am using ponni rice in this recipe.
  • Wash and soak rice for 15 minutes.
  • Take rice in a pressure cooker.
  • For 1 cup of rice, I use 21/2 cup to 3 cups of water.
  • Pressure cook for 3 whistle.
  • Take it off the heat and let the steam escape by itself.
  • Open the cooker and gently fluff the rice.
  • Tempering Spices - Heat oil in a kadai.
  • Add channa dal, urad dal and let it turn golden.
  • Add in cashews, mustard, dry red chillies, curry leaves and fry for 30 seconds.
  • Add in asafoetida, ginger, green chillies and mix well.
  • Add turmeric powder and mix.
  • Now take it off the heat.
  • Making Lemon Rice - Add lemon juice, salt and mix well.
  • This is your lemon rice mix.
  • Now add cooked rice in and mix well.
  • You can taste and add more lemon juice and salt to taste.
  • Serve hot or put in lunch box along with potato poriyal.

    Common questions

    Is Lemon Rice healthy for kids?

    Lemon Rice scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, fibre, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is Lemon Rice best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years, and lowest, 60, for 16–18 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
