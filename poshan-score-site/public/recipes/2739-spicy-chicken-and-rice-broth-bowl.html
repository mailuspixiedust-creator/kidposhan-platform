Spicy Chicken and Rice Broth Bowl

Ingredients

  • 250 grams minced chicken
  • 2 teaspoons light soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon grated garlic
  • 1 tablespoon sesame or any neutral cooking oil (divided)
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped ginger
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped garlic
  • 1 tablespoon red thai curry paste
  • 1 teaspoon light soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon peanut butter
  • ½ teaspoon sugar
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 cup thin coconut milk
  • ½ cup water
  • 1 cup cooked rice
  • ⅓ cup cooked edamame beans
  • ½ lemon juice
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped coriander

Method

  • Add minced chicken to a bowl, add soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, mix well and set aside to marinate for 20-25 minutes.
  • Heat sesame oil in a flat bottomed pan, add marinated chicken and roast on medium heat till they turn golden from the bottom Flip and roast on the other side till they turn golden.
  • Break them into bite sized pieces and transfer to a plate.
  • Heat the same pan, add the remaining 1 teaspoon of oil.
  • Add ginger, garlic and saute for a few seconds till fragrant.
  • Add thai curry paste, soy sauce, peanut butter, sugar, salt and saute for a few seconds till they mix well.
  • Add coconut milk, water and boil for 2-3 minutes and take off the heat.
  • To serve, add rice to a large bowl, pour the hot broth on the rice, top with roasted chicken mince, edamame beans, lemon juice, garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.
