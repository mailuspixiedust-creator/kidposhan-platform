Rustic Vegetable Barley Soup

Recipe by bowlofdelicious.com

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter (or olive oil)
  • 1 onion (diced, about 1 ½ cups)
  • 2 stalks celery (diced, about 1 cup)
  • 2 carrots (diced, about 1 cup)
  • 1 lb. leeks (trimmed and halved lengthwise, sliced, and washed well, see notes, about 3-4 cups)
  • salt and pepper (to taste)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves (or 1 teaspoon dried, plus more for serving)
  • 6 cups broth (any kind will do - chicken, vegetable, beef, or a combination.)
  • 2 medium potatoes (diced, about 2-3 cups)
  • 2 parsnips (diced, about 2-3 cups, or use more potatoes)
  • 2 cups chopped cabbage
  • ½ cup pearl barley (rinsed, see notes)
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley (plus more for serving)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon butter (or olive oil)
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice

Method

  • Melt the 2 tablespoons butter in a Dutch oven or large pot over medium heat.
  • Add the diced 1 onion, diced 2 stalks celery, diced 2 carrots, and sliced 1 lb. leeks, along with a generous pinch of salt and pepper.
  • Sauté until softened and much of the vegetables' water has evaporated, about 10 minutes.
  • Add the minced 2 cloves garlic and 1 tablespoon fresh thyme leaves.
  • Sauté until fragrant, then pour in the 6 cups broth.
  • Add the diced 2 medium potatoes, diced 2 parsnips, 2 cups chopped cabbage, and rinsed ½ cup pearl barley.
  • Bring to a boil, cover, and simmer on low for 30 minutes, or until barley and all the vegetables are fully cooked.
  • If soup is too thick after everything cooks, feel free to add more broth or some water.
  • Use an immersion blender to puree the soup no more than halfway right in the pot.
  • You want there to still be plenty of texture, so don't go overboard with blending!
  • Alternatively, you can use a standing blender to blend about 4-6 cups of the soup and add it back to the pot.
  • Turn off heat.
  • Stir in the 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, 1 tablespoon soy sauce, remaining 1 tablespoon butter, and 2 tablespoons lemon juice.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper as needed.
  • Serve, garnished with a bit more fresh parsley, thyme, and fresh cracked black pepper if desired.
