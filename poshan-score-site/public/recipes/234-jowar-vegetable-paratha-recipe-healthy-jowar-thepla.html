Jowar Vegetable Paratha Recipe (Healthy Jowar Thepla)

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup jowar atta (jowar flour)
  • 3/4 cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)
  • 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup shredded spinach (palak)
  • 1/4 tsp haldi powder (turmeric powder)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp roasted cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 tsp oil
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta) for rolling
  • oil for cooking

Method

  • To make dough for Jowar Vegetable Paratha, in a bowl put1/4 cupjowar (white millet) flour.
  • Add3/4 cupwhole wheat flour (gehun ka atta).
  • Add1/4 cupgrated carrot.
  • Add1/4 cupshredded spinach (palak).
  • Add1/4 tspturmeric powder (haldi).
  • Add1/2 tspchilli powder.
  • Add1/2 tsproastedcumin seeds (jeera).
  • Add 1 tsp oil.
  • Add salt to taste. We used 1/2 tsp salt.
  • Add enough water to make a soft dough. We added 1/4 cup water and then later 1 tablespoon of water.
  • Knead into a soft dough.
  • Divide the dough into 12 equal portions.
  • Dust a rolling board with whole wheat flour.
  • Flatten dough and put some whole wheat flour on it.
  • Roll outdough into a 125 mm. (5") diameter circle.
  • Heat a non stick pan and grease it with oil. You need to do that only once.
  • Place uncooked paratha on non stick pan.
  • Let the paratha cook on medium flame till small bubbles appear on the paratha which should take about 20 seconds.
  • Now grease the top of theparatha with oil.
  • Flip over and cook the other side of theparatha the same way.
  • Our paratha is now ready.
  • Place the cooked jowar vegetable paratha in a serving plate.
  • Then pack them in an aluminium foil.
  • Cool the parathas after they have been cooked. If you pack hot parathas they will harden.
  • As a variation, you can substitute jowar flour with bajra flour or ragi flour.
