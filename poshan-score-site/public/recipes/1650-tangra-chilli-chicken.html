Ingredients

  • 500 grams boneless chicken thighs (cut into ¾ inch cubes)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ cup all purpose flour / maida
  • ¼ + ⅛ cups cornflour
  • 2 teaspoons grated garlic
  • 1 teaspoon vinegar
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 ½ tablespoons dark soy sauce
  • 3 ¾ tablespoons tomato ketchup
  • 3 tablespoons red chilli sauce
  • 1 ½ tablespoons oyster sauce
  • 1 ½ teaspoons chicken broth powder
  • ¾ teaspoon cornflour
  • ¾ cup water
  • 1 ½ tablespoons finely chopped garlic
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped ginger
  • 8-9 green chillies (sliced diagonally)
  • 1 medium onion (cut into petals)
  • ½ green capsicum (cut into ¾inch cubes)
  • ½ red capsicum (cut into ¾inch cubes)
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped spring onion greens

Method

Marinating chicken

  1. Add chicken to a bowl, add all the marination ingredients, give a good mix and set aside to marinate at least for 30 minutes

Sauce mix

  1. Add all ingredients for sauce mix to a bowl, whisk well and set aside.

Air frying chicken

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 180C and brush the basket with oil.
  2. Arrange the marinated chicken with some gap between each other.
  3. Brush with oil.
  4. Air Fry at 180C for 7 minutes, flip once, air fry at 180C for 5 minutes and for 2-3 minutes at 200C, till they turn golden from outside.

Making tangra chicken

  1. Heat oil in a wok or pan, add ginger, garlic and green chillies and saute on high for 30 seconds.
  2. Add onions, capsicums and saute on high for a minute or 2 till they get roasted. add air fried chicken and saute for a minute on high.
  3. Add sauce mix and cook on medium for 2 minutes while stirring constantly.
  4. Garnish with spring onion greens and serve.
