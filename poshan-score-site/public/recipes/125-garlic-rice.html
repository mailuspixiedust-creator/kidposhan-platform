Garlic Rice

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 20 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Garlic Rice scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • Rice-2 cup
  • Garlic-10 cloves chopped
  • Green chilli-2 chopped
  • Cinnamon / Pattai-1 inch piece
  • Cumin seeds / Jeerakam-1 tsp
  • Raisins / kishmish-1 tblpsn
  • Peanuts / Verkadalai-1 tblpsn
  • Cashews-1 tblspn
  • Almonds / Badam-1 tblspn
  • Pepper-1 tblspn
  • Coriander leaves for garnishing
  • Oil-1 tblspn
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Soak rice for 30 min.Cook rice by adding some salt and oil in it.
  • In a kadai heat oil and fry garlic..Add almonds,peanuts and cashews and fry till golden.
  • Throw in cinnamon,cumin seeds and mix well.
  • Add green chilli, raisins, salt and mix well.
  • Now add in cooked rice,pepper and toss well.
  • Garnish with coriander leaves and serve hot.

    Is Garlic Rice healthy for kids?

    Garlic Rice scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

    What age is Garlic Rice best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 70, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
