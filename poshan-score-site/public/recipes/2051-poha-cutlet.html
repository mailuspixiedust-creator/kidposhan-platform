Poha cutlet

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 25 minServes 15VegetarianBreakfastLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 5 years

Poha cutlet scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup poha
  • 1 cup boiled and grated potatoes
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • ¼ cup coriander leaves
  • 2 chopped green chilli
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp kasuri methi
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • Pinch of black salt
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp crushed peanut powder
  • 4 tbsp besan
  • Oil for frying
  • ½ cup coriander leaves
  • ½ cup mint leaves
  • 2-3 green chilli
  • ½ inch ginger
  • 2-3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp roasted chana dal
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • ½ tsp chaat masala
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp lemon juice
  • 4 tbsp + 1 cup curd
  • 1 ice cubes

Method

Making Poha Cutlet

  1. Take 2 medium-sized potatoes and pressure cook them over medium-high heat for 4 whistles, or until soft.
  2. Peel the boiled potatoes and allow them to cool completely.
  3. In a bowl, take 1 cup poha. Add water and wash it gently. Drain the water using a sieve and spread the poha to dry completely.
  4. In a mixing bowl, add 1 cup soaked poha, 1 cup grated boiled potatoes, ¼ cup chopped onion, ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves, 2 finely chopped green chilies, 1 tsp cumin powder, 1 tbsp red chili powder, 1 tbsp coriander powder, 1 tsp garam masala, ½ tsp kasuri methi, ¼ tsp turmeric powder, salt to taste, a pinch of black salt, 1 tsp lemon juice, 1 tsp sugar, and 2 tbsp peanut powder.
  5. Mix everything well.
  6. Add 4 tbsp besan and mix again until the mixture binds together.
  7. Grease your hands with a little oil. Take a portion of the mixture, roll it between your palms, and shape it into a heart. Repeat with the remaining mixture.
  8. Heat oil in a pan. Carefully add the cutlets and fry them over medium-high heat until golden brown and crispy on both sides.
  9. Crispy Poha Cutlets are ready. Serve hot with tomato ketchup.
