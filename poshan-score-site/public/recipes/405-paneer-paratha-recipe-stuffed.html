Paneer Paratha Recipe (Stuffed)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt (to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon oil
  • 3/4 -1 cup water (for kneading the dough)
  • 2 cups paneer (Indian Cottage Cheese) (about 200 grams)
  • 2-3 tablespoons onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (grated)
  • 1-2 green chilies (finely chopped)
  • 2-3 tablespoons coriander leaves
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon roasted cumin powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon amchur powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • salt (to taste)
  • ghee (for roasting parathas)

Method

Make Paneer Stuffing

  1. To make the paneer filling, use either homemade or store-bought paneer.
  2. Grate the paneer using a box grater for even-sized paneer.
  3. You can also crumble it into small pieces.Then add onions, ginger, green chilies, coriander leaves, red chili powder, roasted cumin powder, amchur powder, garam masala powder, and salt.
  4. Mix everything well.
  5. Ps: If you are making it for kids, you can reduce the spice level.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
