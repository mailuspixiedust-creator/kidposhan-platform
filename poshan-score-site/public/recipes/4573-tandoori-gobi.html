Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 15 to 20 even sized florets cauliflower
  • 1 cup curd
  • 1 tablespoon gram flour / besan flour
  • 1 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 3/4 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
  • 1 pinch chat masala powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon jeera powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon ajwain
  • 1/2 teaspoon kasoori methi
  • 1/2 teaspoon lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoon oil
  • salt to taste

Method

  • Clean cauliflower and cut into small even sized florets.
  • Parboil cauliflower with salt.
  • It should be soft yet crunchy enough to bite, Drain and Set aside.
  • Dry roast besan flour till slightly browned and nice aroma rises, keep aside.
  • Add a cup of curd to a muslin cloth and hang it for 30 minutes.Then add the thick hung curd and mix it with all other ingredients except the veggie.
  • Mix well to form a smooth thick batter.
  • Now add the cauliflower pieces and mix well.
  • Marinate it for an hour.
  • Meanwhile lay silver foil on the wire rack and keep it ready.
  • Preheat oven at 200 deg C for 10 minutes.
  • After an hour if you see it would be slightly dried.
  • Now arrange the pieces on the wire rack covered with silver foil.
  • Bake it for a good 30mins and grill it for 5mins until it turns golden brown.
  • Turn over in between for even baking/grilling.
  • Brush with more oil if you want.
  • Sprinkle chat masala powder and serve it as a starter or as a snack with green chutney.
