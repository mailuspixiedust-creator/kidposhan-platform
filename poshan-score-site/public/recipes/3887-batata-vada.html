Method

Making besan batter

  1. In a mixing bowl, add the besan, turmeric powder, ajwain, hing, salt, and baking soda and mix well.
  2. add water gradually to make a smooth and thin coating consistency of batter also make sure there should not be any lumps.
  3. Use a whisk to whisk the batter nicely, whisk for at least 4-5 minutes, and make sure the consistency of the batter should not be too thick or too thin, it should be in flowing consistency.
  4. Once whisked well, let the batter rest for a minimum time of 15 minutes.

Common questions

Is Batata vada healthy for kids?

Batata vada scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on added sugar, sodium, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Batata vada best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 7–9 years and 10–12 years and adults, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
