Murmura chilla

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 20 minServes 3VegetarianBreakfastLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Murmura chilla scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup soaked murmura
  • ½ cup coarse rava (semolina)
  • ¼ cup curd
  • 3 tbsp + ½ cup water
  • ½ tsp ginger paste
  • 2 chopped green chilli
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • ¼ cup chopped tomatoes
  • ¼ cup grated carrot
  • 4-5 chopped curry leaves
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • ½ tsp lemon juice
  • Oil

Method

  • Soaked 2 cups of murmura with some water.
  • Also soak semolina with curd and some water.
  • After 20 minutes, Drain water from the murmura and take it into a mixture jar.
  • Now add soaked semolina and ½ cup water and grind it into a paste.
  • Take out the batter into a mixing bowl, then add ginger paste, chopped green chilli, chopped onion, chopped tomatoes, grated carrot, chopped curry leaves, some coriander leaves and salt.
  • Mix well.
  • Add baking soda and lemon juice. Mix well.

    • making chilla

    1. heat a pan, and spread some oil on it.
    2. add a ladleful of batter and spread it evenly.
    3. cook for 5 minutes on medium flame or until the base is cooked well.
    4. flip over and cook both sides.
    5. enjoy murmura chilla with tomato sauce or chutney.
