Methi Pulao (No Onion No Garlic)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati or long-grain rice
  • 1 tsp oil
  • Salt to taste
  • 4-5 cups water for cooking the rice
  • 1 ½ cups tightly packed methi leaves
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp green chili paste or add to taste
  • 1 tbsp ginger grated or paste
  • ⅓ cup finely chopped celery
  • ½ cup finely chopped green peppers
  • ½ cup finely chopped tomatoes
  • ½ cup boiled green peas
  • ¼ cup finely chopped coriander leaves
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp pav bhaji masala or garam masala

Method

Cooking The Rice

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly until the water runs clear. Then, soak the rice in cold water for 20-30 minutes. Drain the rice and set it aside.
  2. Boil 4-5 cups of water in a big pot.
  3. Add drained rice, salt, and oil to boiling water.
  4. Do not cover the pot with the lid; let the rice cook for about 10-12 minutes or until well cooked.
  5. Keep an eye on the rice while cooking; we should not overcook the rice.
  6. If overcooked, then rice will be mushy and sticky.
  7. So make sure to boil rice just enough so each grain stays separate.
  8. Once the rice is cooked, pour it on the strainer and remove all the water from the rice. Then, spread the rice on the big plate and let it cool completely.
