Ingredients

  • 1 pound pasta (such as elbow or other mini pasta shape)
  • ¼ cup butter
  • 4 cups baby spinach
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 ½ cups shredded cheddar cheese
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (optional)
  • pinch garlic powder (optional)
  • Parmesan cheese, cracked black pepper (optional)

Method

  • Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Cook the pasta according to package directions.
  • Drain and toss with the butter.
  • Meanwhile, add the spinach, milk, cheddar cheese, salt, and garlic powder (if using) to the blender. Blend until smooth.
  • Stir the cheese sauce into the pasta and serve warm, topped with Parmesan and cracked black pepper if desired.

    Common questions

    Is Green Mac and Cheese healthy for kids?

    Green Mac and Cheese scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, fibre, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is Green Mac and Cheese best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
