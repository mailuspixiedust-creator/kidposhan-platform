Mediterranean Baked Cod

Recipe by feastingathome.com

Photo: feastingathome.com
⏱ 30 minServes 4Non-vegetarianDinnerLunchSchool lunchboxFrom 3 years

Ingredients

  • 2 medium zucchini (or other veggies, see notes), sliced into ½ inch pieces
  • 2 bell peppers, cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (optional)
  • 1 red onion, cut into ½ inch wedges
  • ⅓ cup kalamata olives, halved
  • 6 garlic cloves, roughly chopped
  • lemon zest from one medium lemon, divided
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons salt, divided, more to taste
  • 1 teaspoon black pepper, divided
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1 ¼ – 1 ½ lbs white fish- cod or sub black cod, mahi mahi, sea bass, halibut, red snapper, or haddock
  • 1 tablespoon fresh oregano (or thyme)- or substitute 1 ½ teaspoons dried oregano
  • optional- red pepper flakes to taste, cubes of feta (½-1 cup)
  • Garnish– lemon juice, lemon slices, and fresh herbs like chopped Italian parsley, basil ribbons, mint, dill or make gremolata for drizzling.

Method

  • oven to 425°F and place parchment on a large sheet pan.
  • Place the sliced veggies in a large bowl with cherry tomatoes, sliced red onion, olives, half of the chopped garlic, half of the lemon zest, half of the salt, half of the black pepper, and half of the oregano.
  • Toss well with half of the olive oil, and place on a large parchment-lined sheet pan.
  • Bake for 10-15 minutes while you prep the rest.
  • Pat the cod dry with a paper towel, and place it in the same bowl.
  • Sprinkle with the remaining salt, pepper, garlic, lemon zest, oregano, and olive oil, and toss to coat all sides well.
  • Nestle the fish into the par-cooked veggies on the sheet pan.
  • Cut the lemon in half and add it to the pan.
  • Sprinkle the pan with chili flakes if you like.
  • Feta chunks are nice here, too!
  • Place the sheet pan on the middle rack in the oven for 12-15 minutes, depending on the thickness and size of the fish.
  • Large or thick filets may take longer.
  • Fish should flake with a fork, turn opaque, and have an internal temp of 130- 135°F. (145°F is considered well done.)
  • Once done, squeeze with lemon juice, sprinkle with fresh herbs, and drizzle with optional gremolata sauce. Adjust salt and pepper to taste.

    Common questions

    Is Mediterranean Baked Cod healthy for kids?

    Mediterranean Baked Cod scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

    What age is Mediterranean Baked Cod best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 70, for 13–15 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
