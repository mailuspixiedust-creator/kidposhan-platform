Method

  • Firstly, in a large kadai heat 1 tbsp butter and add vegetables. cook and mash well.
  • Add 1 tsp chilli powder, ¼ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp pav bhaji masala, 1 tsp kasuri methi and 2 tbsp coriander leaves.
  • heat a tbsp of butter and add ¼ tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp pav bhaji masala, 1 tsp kasuri methi.
  • also add 1 tbsp coriander leaves, 1 tsp ginger garlic paste, 1 onion and ½ lemon juice. saute well.
  • now add 3 drops of red food colour and mix well.
  • boil and mash for 5 minutes adjusting consistency.
  • Finally, serve pav and bhaji as pav bhaji.

    Common questions

    Is Pav Bhaji healthy for kids?

    Pav Bhaji scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is Pav Bhaji best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 7–9 years and 10–12 years and adults, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
