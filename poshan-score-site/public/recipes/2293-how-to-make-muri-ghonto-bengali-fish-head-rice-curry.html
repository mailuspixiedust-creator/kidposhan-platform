How to make Muri Ghonto-Bengali fish head rice curry

Recipe by nupursindiankitchen.com

Photo: nupursindiankitchen.com
⏱ 1 h 50 minServes 100Non-vegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • Turmeric powder (for coating fish head)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 400g Rui Macher Muro (Rohu Fish Head)
  • 250g Gobindo Bhog Rice
  • 250g potatoes (cut in 1 inch pieces)
  • 5 tbsp. Mustard oil
  • 3/4 tsp. turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 2 tsp. Cumin powder
  • 1 tsp. red chilli powder
  • 3 green chillies (slit)
  • 1 tsp. Garam Masala powder
  • 1 tbsp. Ghee
  • 1.5 tbsp. Ginger paste
  • 4-5 garlic cloves
  • Sugar (1/2 tsp. or to taste)

Method

  • Soak rice in water for 20 min at least
  • Clean the fish heads well. Coat well with salt and turmeric powder
  • Set a pot on medium heat and add 5 tbsp. mustard oil
  • Stir fry the potatoes till they are golden brown in color and set aside
  • Fry the fish heads till they are crisp
  • You may need to cover the fish heads if the oil splatters too much
  • Break the fish heads into smaller pieces and make sure it’s fried well
  • Take out the fried fish in another bowl
  • To the same oil add cumin seeds, bay leaf and slit green chillies
  • Stir fry for 30 sec
  • Turn down the heat and add turmeric powder, cumin powder, red chilli powder, ginger paste and salt (to taste)
  • Stir fry the masala for a few seconds
  • Add potatoes and fish in and stir
  • Drain off the water from the rice and add the rice in
  • Add ghee and stir fry everything for 30 sec
  • Add garlic cloves (whole or chopped) and add stir them in
  • Add enough hot water to cook the rice through
  • Taste the water, check for salt and add more if needed
  • Add sugar to taste
  • Place the lid on, turn down the heat and let the rice and potatoes cook through
  • Add more hot water to let the rice cook fully (if needed)
  • Add garam masala powder
  • Stir well
