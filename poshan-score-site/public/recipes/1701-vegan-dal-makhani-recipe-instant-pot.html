Ingredients

  • ¾ cup Black Urad Dal
  • ¼ cup Rajma or Kidney Beans
  • 3 ½ cups Water (for cooking the beans)
  • 3 Onions (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 4 Tomatoes (pureed) (read notes below)
  • 12 Cashew (to be soaked in ½ cup of hot water for 15 mins)
  • 2 ½ tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds or Jeera
  • 1 Bay Leaf or Tej Patta
  • ½ tsp Turmeric
  • ½ tsp Kashmiri Red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 tsp Kasuri Methi
  • Salt to taste

Method

Prepping the Lentils and Beans

  1. Measure and add the whole urad dal (black gram) and Rajma or kidney beans in a bowl.
  2. Wash them couple of times, drain and soak with 4 cups of water overnight or 8 hours.
  3. The next morning, drain the water, rinse the lentils and beans. Keep aside.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
