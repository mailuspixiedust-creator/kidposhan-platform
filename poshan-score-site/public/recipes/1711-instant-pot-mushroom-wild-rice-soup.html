Ingredients

  • 1 cup Wild rice (uncooked)
  • 3 Carrots (a bit more than 1 cup, diced)
  • 3 stalks Celery
  • 1 cup Onion
  • 4 cloves Garlic (minced)
  • 8 ounces Cremini Mushrooms (sliced)
  • 4 cups Broth (vegetable or chicken)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Thyme (dried)
  • 2 Bay leaf
  • 1/2 teaspoon Rosemary (I used dried)
  • 2 large handfuls Kale leaves (roughly chopped with thick stems removed)
  • 1.5 teaspoon Salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon Black Pepper (freshly cracked)
  • 4 tablespoon Butter
  • 1/4 cup All purpose flour
  • 1.5 cups Milk (I used whole milk)

Method

On the stovetop:

  1. When the pressure cooking is done, melt the butter in a pan on the stovetop.
  2. Add the flour and saute for a minute until the raw smell is gone (about 1-2 minutes).
  3. Whisk in the milk, a little at a time, until you have a smooth, thick consistency.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
