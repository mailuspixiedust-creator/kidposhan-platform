Mini Mixed Moong Dal Chila ( Baby And Toddler)

Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp yellow moong dal (split yellow gram) , soaked for 1 hour and drained
  • 2 tbsp green moong dal (split green gram) , soaked for 1 hour and drained
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 1/4 tsp oil for greasing and cooking

Method

  • If you like Mixed Moong Dal Chila, then also try other healthy recipes for your growing kids like green pea and paneer tikki jowar golpapdi for kids oats bhel for kids
  • Mini Mixed Moong Dal Chila is a protein rich snack for kids and toddlers. Each chila provides around 2 g of protein.
  • These colourful chilas are a finger food which kids find most tempting. Let them pick and enjoy them own their own.
  • Add a handful of veggies to increase the fibre count of these chilas.
  • Avoid serving these chilas with tomato ketchup.
  • To that add green moong dal.
  • Wash both the dals with clean water.
  • Strain the dals using a strainer.
  • Add more water for soaking the dals.
  • Cover with a lid and and allow them to soak for 1 hour.
  • After 1 hour, again strain the dals of mixed dal pancake for kids. Add this strained dals to a mixer jar.
  • Add 3 tbsp of water to it for blending.
  • Blend in a mixer to a smooth paste. This is how the mixture of mini mixed moong dal chila looks like after blending.
  • Transfer the mixture into a deep bowl.
  • Add salt as per taste to it.
  • Add a pinch of asafoetida also.
  • Mix well using a spoon. The batter for moong dal pancake for toddlers is ready.
  • Now to make a healthy snack for kids, heat a mini uttapa pan and grease it using &frac14; tsp of oil.
  • Pour a spoonful of the batter in each uttapa mould to make a 75 mm. (3&quot;) diameter round.
  • Cook all the chilas, using 1 tsp of oil, till they turn golden brown in colour from one side.
  • Turn them around and cook them till they turn golden brown in colour from other side also.
  • Serve mini mixed moong dal chila recipe immediately.
