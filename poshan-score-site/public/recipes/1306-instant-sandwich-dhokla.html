Instant sandwich dhokla

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book

Instant sandwich dhokla scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup rava
  • Salt to taste
  • 3 cup buttermilk or 1 cup curd
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 tsp eno fruit salt
  • Some red chill powder
  • Some balck pepper powder
  • 12-13 garlic cloves
  • 2 tbsp kashmiri red chilli powder
  • 1 tomato
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • Pinch of hing
  • Some curry leaves
  • 2 chopped green chilli

Method

For the Dhokla Batter

  1. In a mixing bowl, add 2 cups of rava, salt to taste, and 3 cups of buttermilk.
  2. Mix well and prepare a medium-thick batter with a ribbon-like pouring consistency.
  3. Cover and let the batter rest for 15 minutes.

What age is Instant sandwich dhokla best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 7–9 years, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
