Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1.5 cups idli rice (- 330 grams, parboiled rice, ukda chawal, sela chawal, or 1.5 cups regular rice)
  • ½ cup urad dal (- 125 grams, husked whole or split black gram)
  • ¼ cup thick poha (- 35 grams, flattened rice)
  • 1 tablespoon chana dal (- husked and split bengal gram)
  • 20 fenugreek seeds (methi seeds)
  • 1 cup water (for soaking urad dal)
  • 2 cups water (for soaking rice)
  • ½ cup water (for grinding dal)
  • ½ teaspoon rock salt (edible and food grade, add as required or sea salt or pink salt)
  • 4 potatoes (medium-sized potatoes - 300 grams or 2 cups boiled, chopped or crumbled potatoes)
  • 2 tablespoons oil (- any neutral oil)
  • ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1.25 cups onions (thinly sliced, or 2 medium to large - 150 grams)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (- finely chopped, or 1 inch peeled ginger)
  • 8 to 10 curry leaves
  • 1 teaspoon green chillies (- chopped, or 1 to 2 green chillies)
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing, - optional)
  • 3 tablespoons coriander leaves (- chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons cashews (- optional)
  • ¼ to ½ teaspoon sugar (- optional)
  • salt (as required)

Method

Soaking rice and lentils

  1. First take all the ingredients in bowls. Take rice and flattened rice in separate bowls. Take the urad dal, chana dal and fenugreek seeds in one bowl.
  2. Rinse the urad dal, chana dal and fenugreek seeds a couple of times. Then soak them in 1 cup water for 4 to 5 hours.
  3. Rinse the rice a couple of times and keep aside.
  4. Rinse flattened rice once or twice and then add to the rice.
  5. Pour 2 cups water. Stir and soak both rice and flattened rice together for 4 to 5 hours.

Common questions

Is Masala Dosa healthy for kids?

Masala Dosa scores 90 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years. It passes 9 of the 10 checks and loses points on fibre.

What age is Masala Dosa best for?

It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 80, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
