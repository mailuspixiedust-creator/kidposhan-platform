Indian Vegetable Quesadillas

Recipe by Madhu's Everyday Indian / Madhavi Kadaba

Ingredients

  • 1 small yellow bell pepper, sliced
  • 1 small red bell pepper, sliced
  • 1/2 cup frozen corn, rinsed
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 2 to 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jalapeno, finely chopped
  • 1.5 tbsp oil (more to pan fry the quesadillas)
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 to 2 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 to 2 tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp red chili powder or to taste
  • salt to taste
  • Shredded mexican style cheese
  • 5-6 Flour tortillas
  • a handful of chopped coriander leaves
  • lime wedges

Method

Pan frying the quesadillas

  1. Heat a large pan and drizzle some oil.
  2. Gently place the tortilla in to the pan and press down and cook on medium flame till light brown.
  3. Flip on other side and cook till golden brown and crunchy.
  4. Slice and veggie quesadillas are ready to be served.
