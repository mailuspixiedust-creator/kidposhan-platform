Matar Paneer Pulao

Recipe by shwetainthekitchen.com

Photo: shwetainthekitchen.com
⏱ 30 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Matar Paneer Pulao scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 cup paneer cubes (approx 200 gms or 7oz)
  • 1 cup green peas
  • 2 tablespoons ghee (or oil)
  • 1 medium yellow onion (sliced)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (minced)
  • 1-2 green chilies (finely chopped)
  • ½ cup tomato puree (store bought or homemade)
  • 1 tablespoon Lemon juice (adjust to taste)
  • 2 tablespoon cilantro leaves (chopped)
  • 1 ½ teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 4 cloves
  • 1 nutmeg shell
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 small piece cinnamon
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon coriander powder

Method

Using Stove top

  1. Follow the exact same steps as mentioned above for cooking using pressure cooker.
  2. Just add more water i.e.
  3. 2 to 2.5 cups (varies based on quality of rice) of water for 1 cup of basmati rice.
  4. Cook on medium flame and get it to a boil.
  5. Then cover and cook on low flame till water is absorbed.
  6. Check in between if more water is required.
  7. Fluff with a fork and see if the rice is cooked.
  8. Once done, switch off the flame and let it sit covered for 5 mins.
  9. Then fluff with fork.
  10. Add lemon juice and fluff it up again. Be careful to not break the paneer (as much as possible). Finish off with some cilantro.
  11. Serve warm with some cucumber raita.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
