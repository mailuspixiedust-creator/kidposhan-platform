Corn Fried Rice

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

How often: Everyday Fine most days, with variety. The Poshan Score shows how one serving does against a child's daily limits; this line is about how the food is made.

Corn Fried Rice scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons sesame (or olive oil)
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 green chilies (slit lengthwise)
  • 1 medium onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1/4 cup white onions
  • 1 cup sweet corn (frozen or fresh, or 1 medium corn cob)
  • 3/4-1 cup colored bell pepper (diced)
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon white vinegar (optional, or apple cider vinegar)
  • 3 cups cooked basmati rice (or 1 cup uncooked basmati rice)
  • salt (to taste)
  • black pepper powder (to taste)
  • 2-3 tablespoons green onions

Method

Prep :

  1. Cook the basmati or any kind of rice and allow it to cool completely. If you want you can keep the rice in the refrigerator to get extra firm.
  2. Also dice the colored bell pepper.

Common questions

Is Corn Fried Rice healthy for kids?

Corn Fried Rice scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years. It passes 10 of the 10 checks.

What age is Corn Fried Rice best for?

It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 90, for 13–15 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
