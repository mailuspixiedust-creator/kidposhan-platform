Sweet Corn Masala

Recipe by SouthIndianRecipes.in

Photo: SouthIndianRecipes.in
⏱ 30 minServes 2Jain4 PM snackFrom 1 years

Sweet Corn Masala scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Sweet Corn Kernels
  • 2 tsp Butter
  • 1/2 tsp Dry Mango Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Pepper Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Red Chillies Powder
  • Salt
  • Lemon Juice

Method

  • Steam the sweetcorn for 25 mins or else cook the corn in a little water.
  • Now take a pan and melt the butter, then add steamed sweet corn and fry them for a minute.
  • Now add dry mango powder, salt, red chilli powder, pepper powder and mix well.
  • Finally add lemon juice and serve hot.
