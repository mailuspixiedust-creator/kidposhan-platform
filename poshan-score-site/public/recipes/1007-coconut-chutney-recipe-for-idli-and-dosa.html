Coconut Chutney Recipe for Idli & Dosa

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Ingredients

  • 1 cup grated coconut
  • 2 chopped green chillies
  • 1 tsp grated ginger (adrak)
  • 1 tbsp roasted chana dal (daria)
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • 1/2 tsp mustard seeds (rai / sarson)
  • 1 pandi chillies , broken into pieces
  • 2 to 3 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 1 tsp oil

Method

  • To make coconut chutney, In a big mixer jar, add the 1 cup fresh grated coconut.
  • You can use fresh or frozen coconut.
  • Also, for easy grinding we have taken grated coconut but, if you are lazy then you can put roughly chopped coconut and pulse it more.
  • Many people even use coconut powder or dessicated coconut, if doing so then make use of warm water while grinding.
  • Add roughly chopped 2 small green chilli. If you like your South-Indian coconut chutney spicy, then simply increase the quantity of chilies.
  • Add 1 tsp grated ginger (adrak) to the mixer jar. Adrak can be skipped or substituted with garlic.
  • Now add 1 tbsp roasted chana dal (daria).
  • If chana dal is not available, then you can skip adding it.
  • The nariyal ki chatni will taste equally delicious without it.
  • Add salt to taste.
  • Add approximately 1/2 cup of water and blend. We have added a little more then half cup.
  • Grind to make a fine paste. It looks like this! If you like a liquidy coconut chutney then increase the quantity of water.
  • Transfer the chutney into a bowl.
  • For the tempering of nariyal ki chutney, heat 1 tsp oil in a non-stick tadka pan. Make use of any neutral flavored oil.
  • Once the oil is hot, add the 1/2 tsp mustard seeds ( rai / sarson).
  • When the seeds crackle, add 1 pandi chillies , broken into pieces.
  • Finally, add 2 to 3 curry leaves (kadi patta).
  • Kadi patta is one of the most important ingredient of an authentic South-Indian tempering.
  • Sauté on a medium flame for a minute.
  • Pour this tempering over the chutney.
  • Gently mix and your coconut chutney for idli, dosa and vada is ready!
  • Refrigerate and use coconut chutney as required. It is best to consume it fresh.
