Spanakopita Beans

Recipe by americastestkitchen.com

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces feta cheese, crumbled (2 cups)
  • 2 ounces Pecorino Romano cheese, grated (1 cup)
  • 1 cup plain whole-milk Greek yogurt
  • 4 scallions, sliced thin
  • ¼ cup chopped fresh mint
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh dill
  • 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest plus 1 tablespoon juice
  • 1 teaspoon ground nutmeg
  • ⅛ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 shallot, sliced thin
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 (15-ounce) cans butter beans or gigante beans, rinsed
  • 10 ounces frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • ½ teaspoon table salt
  • ½ teaspoon pepper
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 8 ounces (14 by 9-inch) phyllo, thawed

Method

  • Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 375 degrees.
  • Combine 2 cups crumbled feta, 1 cup grated Pecorino, 1 cup Greek yogurt, 4 thinly sliced scallions, ¼ cup chopped mint, 2 tablespoons chopped dill, 1 teaspoon grated lemon zest, 1 lemon tablespoon juice, 1 teaspoon nutmeg, and ⅛ teaspoon cayenne in large bowl; set aside.
  • Cook 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, 1 thinly sliced shallot, and 4 minced garlic cloves in 10-inch skillet over medium heat until shallot is just softened, about 2 minutes.
  • Stir in 2 rinsed cans butter beans; 10 ounces frozen spinach, thawed and squeezed dry; ½ teaspoon table salt; and ½ teaspoon pepper and cook until warmed through and excess moisture has evaporated, about 2 minutes.
  • Stir bean mixture into feta mixture until thoroughly combined.
  • Return bean-feta mixture to now-empty skillet and spread into even layer.
  • Melt 4 tablespoons unsalted butter.
  • Working with 1 phyllo sheet from 8 ounces phyllo, thawed, at a time, lay flat on counter and brush lightly with melted butter.
  • Crumple phyllo into 2-inch ball and place on top of bean mixture in skillet.
  • Repeat until top of bean mixture is covered.
  • Brush phyllo with remaining melted butter.
  • Transfer skillet to oven and bake until phyllo is golden brown and crisp, about 25 minutes. Serve.
