Masala Rice

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 20 minServes 2VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Masala Rice scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked basmati rice (or any rice)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrot, beans, peas, bell pepper)
  • 1/2 cup onions (finely chopped)
  • 1/2 inch ginger (minced)
  • 2 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 2 green chilies (finely chopped)
  • few curry leaves
  • 2 tablespoon coriander leaves
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (or ghee)
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (jeera) seeds
  • a pinch hing (asafetida)
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
  • 1/2 inch cinnamon stick
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 cardamom

Method

  • Firstly take whole spices like cinnamon, cloves, and cardamom in a mortar pestle and crush till you get a fine powder. Keep aside.
  • Heat 1 tablespoon of oil/ghee in a Kadai. Add mustard and cumin seeds.
  • Once the mustard seeds start spluttering, add green chilies, curry leaves, hing (asafoetida), minced ginger-garlic, and ground whole spice powder.
  • Saute for a few seconds till the raw smell of the ginger-garlic goes away.
  • Next, add onions. Saute till onions are light brown in color.
  • Then add the mixed vegetables and cook for 2-3 minutes till the vegetables are tender.
  • Next, add the cooked rice and spices like turmeric, red chili powder, coriander powder, garam masala, and salt.
  • Mix it well.
  • Cover and simmer for 2-3 minutes, and then turn off the gas.
  • Lastly, squeeze some fresh lime juice over it. Garnish with coriander leaves.
  • Serve vegetable spiced rice hot with raita.
