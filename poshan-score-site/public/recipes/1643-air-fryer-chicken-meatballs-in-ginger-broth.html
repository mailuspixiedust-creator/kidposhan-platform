Air Fryer Chicken Meatballs in Ginger Broth

Ingredients

  • 250 grams chicken thighs (cut into large strips)
  • ¼ cup + 1 tablespoon cubed onions
  • 2 sticks celery cut into 3 inch pieces
  • ½ + ⅛ cup roughly chopped spring onion greens
  • ¾ inch ginger (peeled)
  • 4-5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
  • 1 teaspoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon cornflour
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon pepper (crushed)
  • 1 tablespoon sesame oil
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped coriander leaves
  • 2 teaspoons oil
  • ½ teaspoon finely chopped garlic
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped ginger
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped spring onion whites
  • 2 ½ cups chicken stock / broth
  • ½ teaspoon cornflour mixed in 2 tablespoons water
  • ½ teaspoon sugar
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon sunflower or any neutral cooking oil
  • 4 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped spring onion greens

Method

Meatball mix

  1. Add chicken thighs, onions, celery sticks, spring onion greens, ginger, garlic cloves to a chopper or mixer and pulse to get coarse mixture.
  2. Add rest of the ingredients - oyster sauce, soy sauce, cornflour, salt, pepper, sesame oil, coriander leaves & pulse once or twice just until it gets mixed well.
  3. Transfer to a bowl, make lemon sized meatballs & place on a plate.

Air frying meatballs

  1. Preheat the air fryer to 200C and lightly brush the basket with oil.
  2. Place the meatballs in a single layer in the air fryer basket, making sure they don't touch each other and drizzle a few drops of oil.
  3. Airfry the meatballs at 200C for 6-8 minutes till they turn light golden from the outside and cooked from the inside.
  4. Remove from the air fryer and transfer to a plate.

Chicken broth

  1. Heat oil in a pan, add garlic, ginger and saute on high for a few seconds till fragrant.
  2. Add spring onion whites and saute for a few seconds till the rawness goes.
  3. Add chicken broth, soy sauce, pepper, conflour slurry, sugar, give a good mix and bring to boil while stirring.
  4. Adjust the seasoning by adding salt if needed.
  5. Simmer for 2-3 minutes and take off the heat.

For each serving

  1. Add 1 cup of rice to a bowl, place 2-3 meatballs on top, pour 1-2 ladles of broth, garnish with spring onion greens and serve immediately
