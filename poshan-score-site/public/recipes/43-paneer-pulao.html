Paneer Pulao

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 40 minServes 3VegetarianLunchFrom 2 years

Paneer Pulao scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • Paneer - 200 grams cubed
  • Basmati Rice - 1 cup
  • Oil - 1 tblsp
  • Ghee - 2 tblsp
  • Cumin Seeds / Jeerakam - 1 tsp
  • Cinnamon / Pattai - 1 small piece
  • Cardamom / Yelakai - 4
  • Cloves / Krambu - 4
  • Bay Leaf - 1
  • Onion - 2 large sliced thinly
  • Ginger Garlic Paste - 1 tblsp
  • Green Chillies - 2 slit
  • Carrot - 1 chopped finely
  • Coriander leaves - 3 tblsp
  • Mint Leaves - 3 tblsp
  • Water - 2 cup
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Wash and soak basmati rice for 30 mins.
  • Heat oil and ghee in a pan, add paneer and fry till golden brown. Drain and set aside.
  • In the same pot, add whole spices and let them sizzle.
  • Add onions and cook till golden.
  • Add green chillies and ginger garlic paste and saute well.
  • Add in water, salt, and bring it to a boil.
  • Add in rice and cook till most of the rice is absorbed.
  • Now add in paneer cubes and coriander leaves.
  • Cover and cook on a low heat for 10 mins.
  • Remove and set aside for 10 mins.
  • Fluff up the rice and serve.

    What age is Paneer Pulao best for?

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
