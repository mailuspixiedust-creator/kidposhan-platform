Method

Making Gravy Base:

  1. In a pressure cooker, add 2 onions, 2 tomatoes, 6 garlic cloves, 1 inch of ginger, 3 dry Kashmiri red chillies, and ½ cup cashew pieces.
  2. Add ½ cup water and cover the lid.
  3. Pressure cook it on medium-high flame for 3 whistles.
  4. When the pressure cooker releases its temperature naturally, remove the skin from the boiled tomatoes. Drain the water and let the mixture cool down.
  5. Transfer the mixture into a jar and grind it into a smooth paste with very little water. Keep it aside.

Common questions

Is Kaju lasan nu shaak healthy for kids?

Kaju lasan nu shaak scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Kaju lasan nu shaak best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 10–12 years and adults, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
