Rava Dosa

Recipe by SouthIndianRecipes.in

Photo: SouthIndianRecipes.in

Rava Dosa scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup Rava /Sooji
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 tsp Ajwain
  • 1 Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Finely chop the onions and green chilly.
  • Mix and make batter of rava by adding water, salt, onions green chilly and ajwain into it.
  • Neither add so less water that it gets difficult to spread like dosa nor make it too easy flowing.
  • Keep it two minutes.
  • Now take a non stick tava and put oil in for shallow frying. Spread it evenly on the tava.
  • Now take the rava batter in a serving spoon or a small steel bowl and spread it on the tava surface.
  • Cook it one side for 2 minutes and see if the color changes to brown turn the dosa and cook it from the other side now.
  • When it is brown from both the sides, considered it cooked. Serve it hot with coconut chutney, rasam or sambhar.
