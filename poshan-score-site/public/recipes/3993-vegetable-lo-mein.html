Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1/2 cup vegetable broth
  • 3 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons vegetarian oyster sauce
  • 2 tablespoons hoisin sauce
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon cornstarch
  • 1/4 teaspoon five-spice powder
  • 3 tablespoons vegetable oil
  • 4 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 pound shiitake mushrooms, stemmed and halved if small or quartered if large
  • 10 scallions, white parts sliced thin and green parts cut into 1-inch pieces
  • 2 red bell peppers, stemmed, seeded, and sliced into ¼-inch-wide strips
  • 1/2 small head napa cabbage, cored and sliced crosswise into ½-inch-thick pieces (4 cups)
  • 1/4 cup Chinese rice wine or dry sherry
  • 1 pound fresh Chinese noodles
  • 1 tablespoon Asian chili-garlic sauce

Method

FOR THE SAUCE:

  1. Whisk all ingredients together in bowl.

FOR THE NOODLES AND VEGETABLES:

  1. Combine 1 tablespoon oil, ginger, and garlic in bowl.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon oil in 12-inch nonstick skillet over high heat until just smoking.
  3. Add mushrooms and cook until lightly browned, 6 to 8 minutes.
  4. Stir in scallions and cook until wilted, 2 to 3 minutes; transfer to bowl.
  5. Add remaining 1 tablespoon oil, bell peppers, and cabbage to now-empty skillet, and cook until spotty brown, about 8 minutes.
  6. Clear center of skillet, add garlic-ginger mixture, and cook, mashing mixture into skillet until fragrant, about 30 seconds; stir into vegetables.
  7. Stir in wine and cook until liquid is nearly evaporated, 30 to 60 seconds.
  8. Stir in mushroom mixture and sauce and simmer until thickened, 1 to 2 minutes; cover and set aside.
  9. Meanwhile, bring 4 quarts water to boil in large pot.
  10. Add noodles and cook, stirring often, until tender.
  11. Drain noodles and return them to pot.
  12. Add cabbage mixture and chili-garlic sauce and toss to combine.
  13. Serve.

What age is Vegetable Lo Mein best for?

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
