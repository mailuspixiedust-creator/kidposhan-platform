Palak Biryani (Spinach Biryani)

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Palak Biryani (Spinach Biryani) scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice (rinsed)
  • 1 medium onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 medium tomato (finely chopped)
  • 2 teaspoons ginger garlic paste
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons oil (or ghee)
  • juice of half lemon
  • 2-3 cups baby spinach (or 1 medium bunch of spinach)
  • 1/4 cup mint (pudina)
  • 1/4 cup coriander leaves
  • 3 green chilies
  • 1 inch cinnamon
  • 2 cloves
  • 2 green cardamom
  • 1 small star anise (optional)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon fennel seeds
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder (haldi)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin (jeera) powder
  • 1 teaspoon biryani powder (or garam masala powder)
  • 8-10 fried cashews (for garnishing)
  • 2 tablespoons fried onions (optional)

Method

Stovetop Pressure Cooker Method:

  1. Heat a pressure cooker with oil on medium flame, Add oil and once it’s hot, add whole spices and saute for 30 secs or till aromatic.
  2. Then add sliced onions, ginger-garlic paste.
  3. Saute for 2-3 minutes until they turn soft and golden brown in color – stir regularly.
  4. Add tomatoes, dry spices like turmeric powder, coriander powder, jeera powder, biryani powder and cook for 2 minutes or till tomatoes turn soft.
  5. Next, add spinach puree, let it boil for 1-2 minutes.
  6. Then add rinsed basmati rice, water, salt, and give a stir. Adjust water appropriately.
  7. Close the lid place a whistle and cook for 2 whistles on medium flame.
  8. After 10 mins add lime juice, garnish with fried onion and cashews, and mix well.
  9. Spinach Biryani is ready. Serve with Raita or plain yogurt.
