Air Fryer Sweet Potato Fries

Recipe by masalachilli.com

Ingredients

  • 500 grams Sweet Potatoes
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (or use paprika)
  • ½ teaspoon Garlic Powder
  • ½ teaspoon Turmeric Powder
  • ¼ teaspoon Black Pepper Powder
  • ¾ teaspoon Salt (or use salt to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon Extra Virgin Olive Oil (+ extra, read notes below)

Method

  • Prepping Sweet Potatoes – Scrub the sweet potatoes to remove any residual dirt or mud particles. Pat dry using a kitchen towel.
  • Peel the skin, cut the pointy ends of the sweet potatoes and further, cut in half through the center.
  • Each half then can be sliced into 3-4 slices depending on the size of the sweet potato. Cut each slice lengthwise with ¼ or ½ inch thickness for fries.
  • Seasoning – Add the prepped fries into a bowl followed by spices and oil. Toss well ensuring each fry is coated with spices and oil.
  • Pre-heat the Air Fryer for 2-3 minutes at 180 degrees Celsius. Brush or spray oil in the basket.
  • Once pre-heated, place the fries in a single line and air fry for 12-15 minutes or until crispy.
  • Shake the basket once - twice in between for crispy texture and even cooking.
  • Once done, remove onto a serving bowl and serve with a dip of your choice.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?
