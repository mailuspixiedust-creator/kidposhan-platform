Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1/3 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tbsp baking powder
  • 1 1/2 tsp ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
  • 1/8 tsp ground cloves
  • 1/2 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup cold unsalted butter (cut into small cubes)
  • 1/2 cup pumpkin puree
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 3 tbsp milk (plus more if needed)
  • 1 tbsp milk or cream (for brushing)
  • 3/4 cup powdered sugar
  • 2 tbsp pure maple syrup
  • 1-2 tbsp milk
  • 1/4 tsp vanilla extract
  • pinch of salt

Method

  • Preheat the oven. Preheat oven to 400°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  • Combine the dry ingredients. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, brown sugar, baking powder, cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, cloves, and salt.
  • Cut in the butter.
  • Add the cold cubed butter.
  • Use a pastry cutter, fork, or your fingertips to work the butter into the flour mixture until you have pea-sized pieces.
  • Mix the wet ingredients. In a separate bowl, whisk together the pumpkin puree, egg, vanilla, and 3 tablespoons milk.
  • Make the dough.
  • Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and gently stir until the dough just begins to come together.
  • If it seems too dry, add an additional splash of milk.
  • Shape the scones. Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface. Gently press it into a round about 1-inch thick. Cut into 8 wedges.
  • Chill if needed. If the dough has become warm or soft, place the shaped scones in the refrigerator for 10–15 minutes before baking.
  • Brush and bake.
  • Transfer the scones to the prepared baking sheet, leaving a little space between each one.
  • Brush the tops lightly with milk or cream.
  • Bake for 18–22 minutes, or until the edges and bottoms are lightly golden.
  • Cool. Transfer to a cooling rack and allow the scones to cool for at least 10 minutes before adding the glaze.
  • Make the maple drizzle.
  • Whisk together the powdered sugar, maple syrup, milk, vanilla, and salt until smooth.
  • Add more milk as needed to reach a drizzleable consistency.
  • Drizzle and serve. Spoon or drizzle the maple glaze over the cooled pumpkin scones.
