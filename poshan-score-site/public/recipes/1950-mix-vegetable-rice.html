Mix Vegetable Rice

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 cup Rice
  • 2 tsp Green Peas
  • 1 Carrot
  • 1/2 cup French Beans
  • 1 Potato
  • 1 Tomato
  • 2 tsp Sambhar Masala
  • 2 cup Thick Coconut Milk
  • 1 Onion
  • 5 Garlic Cloves
  • 1/2 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • Few Curry Leaves
  • Coriander Leaves
  • 1 tbsp Refined Oil

Method

  • Wash and soak rice for 15 minutes. Heat oil in a pressure cooker. Crackle mustard and add onions, garlic and curry leaves. Fry for sometime.
  • Add vegetables one at a time and mix well. Add tomato and sambhar powder. Fry for 3 minutes till the masala is coated to the vegetables.
  • Now vegetables are half cooked.
  • Add rice and mix.
  • Prepare coconut milk by grinding fresh coconut with very little water.
  • Strain the milk and discard the pulp.
  • Add coconut milk and salt.
  • Pressure-cook the rice till it is done (for about 20 min).
  • Garnish with chopped coriander and serve.
