Kali Dal

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole black lentils (sabut urad dal or black gram)
  • ⅓ cup or 1 medium-sized onion (– chopped)
  • 2 medium tomato (pureed – yields about 1 cup tomato puree)
  • 2 to 3 small to medium garlic cloves (– crushed in a mortar-pestle)
  • 1 inch ginger (– crushed in a mortar-pestle)
  • ½ to 1 teaspoon or 1 green chili (– finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder (ground coriander)
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ teaspoon turmeric powder (ground turmeric)
  • ½ teaspoon red chili powder (or cayenne pepper or paprika)
  • ¼ teaspoon garam masala
  • 2 tablespoons oil (or ghee or butter)
  • 3 to 3 ½ cups water (or add as required)
  • salt (add as required)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons coriander leaves (– for garnish, optional)

Method

Soaking Lentils

  1. Pick the sabut urad dal and rinse in water for 2 to 3 times and soak it in water for 30 to 40 minutes.
  2. You can also soak the lentils for 4 to 5 hours or overnight, depending on the quality of dal.
  3. If the lentils are old or near expiry date, then soak overnight.
  4. Later drain the soaked sabut urad dal.
