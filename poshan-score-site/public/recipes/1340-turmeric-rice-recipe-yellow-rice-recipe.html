Ingredients

  • 3 cups Cooked Rice
  • 2 tbsp Oil or Butter
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Salt (to taste)
  • 3 tbsp Coriander leaves (finely chopped)

Method

  • Heat oil or butter in a pan, add cumin and let them sizzle in the oil for 30 seconds for the flavour to release in the oil. .
  • Now reduce the heat to low and add in turmeric powder and mix well.
  • Add in cooked rice and mix well with the oil and turmeric so each rice grains gets coated with the turmeric oil.
  • Now add in salt to taste and mix well.
  • Taste and adjust the salt.
  • Finally add in finely chopped coriander leaves and take it off the heat.
  • Toss to combine.
  • Serve hot.
