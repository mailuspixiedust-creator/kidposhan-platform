Vermicelli Upma (Semiya Upma)

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon jeera (cumin) seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon spilt urad dal
  • 1 teaspoon chana dal (optional)
  • 6-8 cashews (optional)
  • 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (minced)
  • 1 stalk curry leaves
  • 1 pinch hing (asafetida) (skip for gluten-free)
  • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 cup mix veggies (carrot, beans, peas, capsicum, sweet corn)
  • 1 cup Bambino vermicelli (semiya) (roasted)
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder (haldi) (optional)
  • salt (to taste)
  • 2 cups water (1 1/4 cups of water for instant pot version)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons coriander (cilantro) (for garnish)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh grated coconut (optional)

Method

Stovetop Method

  1. Dry roast the vermicelli in a pan over medium-low flame until it turns light golden brown. I have used roasted Bambino vermicelli for this recipe.
  2. Heat oil in a Kadai, add mustard seeds, jeera seeds. Allow them to crackle.
  3. Add urad dal, chana dal, and cashew nuts. Saute until the dal turns to light golden color.
  4. Next, add green chilies, ginger, curry leaves, and hing (asafoetida). Saute for a few seconds until aromatic.
  5. Add chopped onion and cook until the onion becomes light brown.
  6. Next, add the chopped mixed vegetables and sauté for around 2 minutes until the veggies become soft and tender.
  7. Add turmeric and salt, and cook for another minute.
  8. Next, add 2 cups of water and bring it to a boil. Check for salt at this stage and adjust seasoning if needed.
  9. Add the roasted vermicelli to the kadai and mix well. Cover and cook over medium-low heat until the vermicelli absorbs all the water completely.
  10. Squeeze fresh lime juice over the vermicelli upma, garnish with coriander leaves, and freshly grated coconut if desired. Gently mix and serve hot.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?
