Instant Pot Chicken Biryani

Recipe by spiceindiaonline.com

Photo: spiceindiaonline.com
⏱ 1 hServes 4Non-vegetarianLunchFrom 2 years

Instant Pot Chicken Biryani scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basamati rice
  • 4 cups Water (to soak)
  • 1/2 kg Chicken (whole cut with bones)
  • 3 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 2 each Bay leaf
  • 3 small Cinnamon stick
  • 1 whole Star anise
  • 1 each Black Cardamom
  • 2 each Green Cardamom
  • 6 each Cloves
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 6 each Green Chilles (hot variety, slit open)
  • 1.5 cups Onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic paste
  • 1/2 cup Tomatoes (chopped)
  • 1/4 cup Plain yogurt
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli powder
  • 2.5 tsp Salt (or to taste)
  • 1/4 cup Coriander leaves (tightly packed, to saute)
  • 1/4 cup Mint leaves (tightly packed, to saute)
  • 3 cups Water

Method

Prep Work

  1. Soak basmati rice in enough water for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Chicken does not need any marination but marinating ahead time will enhance flavor.
  3. If you decide to marinate, use the yogurt along with turmeric, coriander, red chilli powder and little salt from the list and coat the chicken and refrigerate.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
