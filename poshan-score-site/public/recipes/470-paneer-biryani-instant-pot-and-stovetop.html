Paneer Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop)

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 20 minServes 3VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Paneer Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1 - 1.25 cups paneer (Indian cottage cheese) (cubed and air-fried)
  • 2 tablespoons ghee (or oil)
  • 1 large onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger and garlic (minced)
  • 2 green chilies (slit lengthwise)
  • 1/4 cup mint leaves (chopped)
  • 1/4 cup coriander leaves (chopped)
  • 1/4 cup curd (yogurt) (vegans can use dairy free)
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 inch cinnamon stick
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 2 cardamoms
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 2-3 teaspoons biryani masala powder
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves

Method

Stovetop Pressure Cooker

  1. Follow the same steps as Instant Pot pressure cooker. Cover the cooker with a lid and cook for one whistle, followed by natural pressure release.

Common questions

Is Paneer Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) healthy for kids?

Paneer Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, hfss status.

What age is Paneer Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 70, for 10–12 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
