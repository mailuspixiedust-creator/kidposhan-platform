Common questions

Is Maa Chole Ki Dal (No Onion No Garlic) healthy for kids?

Maa Chole Ki Dal (No Onion No Garlic) scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Maa Chole Ki Dal (No Onion No Garlic) best for?

It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 7–9 years and 10–12 years and 13–15 years, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
