Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 potato (boiled and peeled)
  • ¼ onion (finely chopped)
  • ½ -1 inch ginger (finely chopped)
  • few coriander leaves (finely chopped)
  • salt to taste
  • ¼ cup mozzarella cheese (shredded)
  • ½-1 tsp kashmiri chilli powder
  • ¼ tsp chaat masala
  • ¼ tsp dry mango powder / amchur powder
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour / atta
  • 1 tsp oil or ghee
  • water as required for kneading
  • ¼ cup wheat flour for dusting
  • ghee or oil as required

Method

cheesy stuffing recipe:

  1. firstly, in a large mixing bowl, smash 1 boiled potato.
  2. also add finely chopped onion, ginger, coriander and grated cheese.
  3. furthermore, add all the spices and mix well.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
