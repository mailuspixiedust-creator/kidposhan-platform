Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 500 g Ground Pork
  • 3 Eggs
  • 2 cups Bread Crumbs
  • 1 Onion
  • 10 cloves Garlic
  • 25 g Rosemary
  • 25 g Thyme
  • 25 g Dill Leaves
  • 25 g Parsley
  • 1 Tsp. Salt
  • 1 Tsp. Cinnamon Powder
  • 1 Tsp. Paprika
  • 1.5 Tsp. Black Pepper
  • 1 Tsp. Onion Powder
  • 1 Tsp. Garlic Powder
  • 2 Tbsp. Tomato Ketchup
  • 2 Tbsp. BBQ Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp. Mustard Sauce
  • 2 Tbsp. Brown Sugar

Method

Process of making easy pork meatloaf at home

  1. In a mixing bowl, mix ground pork with three fresh eggs.
  2. Next, add a blend of seasonings as mentioned in the ingredients list - black pepper, salt, paprika, cinnamon powder, onion powder, and garlic powder.
  3. Now remove the leaves from the thyme and rosemary sprigs.
  4. Finely chop dill and parsley if using.
  5. Peel and roughly chop the garlic cloves.
  6. Grate the onion.
  7. Add both onion and garlic to the bowl.
  8. Thoroughly mix the pork with the spices and herbs until well incorporated.
  9. Now add breadcrumbs to bind the mixture into a dough-like consistency, as shown.
  10. You may refer to the video shown from my channel here to have an idea about the consistency.
  11. Meanwhile, Preheat the oven to 160 degrees Celsius
  12. Line a baking tray with parchment paper, lightly oiled, and transfer the prepared meat mixture, shaping it into a loaf.
  13. Ensure the sides of the tray remain clear.
  14. Bake the meatloaf for 15 minutes.
  15. Take it out for the next step.
  16. While it's in the oven, prepare a simple glaze of your choice to complement the dish.
  17. Mix the glaze ingredients as mentioned in the ingredients list to make the glaze.
  18. Generously apply the prepared glaze, ensuring it fully coats the loaf.
  19. Then, bake it at 160 degrees Celsius for 40 minutes.
  20. Once the baking is complete, take the meatloaf out of the oven and cover it with the juices.
  21. Finally, slice the meatloaf and serve it hot, either as part of a sandwich or as a generous slice accompanied by mashed potatoes or vegetables – enjoy the delicious meal!
