Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 kg Chicken (curry cut with bone)
  • 2 Gondhoraj Lebu / lime
  • 10 Lebu pata or lime leaves
  • 10 Green Chilies
  • 1 tbsp Ginger paste
  • 1 tbsp Garlic paste
  • 200 ml Coconut milk
  • 1 Mace
  • 2 Green Cardamom
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 1.5 tbsp Salt
  • 3 tbsp Vegetable oil (unflavored)

Method

  • To begin, you'll need bone-in chicken for this recipe, preferably curry-cut chicken without the skin. Wash and pat the chicken pieces dry before cooking.

    • Marination

    1. First, marinate the chicken. Place the chicken in a bowl.
    2. Next, using a grater or peeler, carefully remove only the green outer peel of a Gondhoraj Lebu (lime), making sure to avoid the bitter white part underneath.
    3. Add this lemon zest to the chicken.
    4. For one kilogram of chicken, you'll need the zest from at least one large lime.
    5. Also, add the juice from one Gondhoraj Lebu.
    6. Add 1 tbsp ginger paste and 1 tbsp garlic paste to it.
    7. Finally, add one teaspoon of salt and mix all the ingredients thoroughly with the chicken. Let the chicken marinate for 30 minutes.
