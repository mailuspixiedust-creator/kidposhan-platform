Potato Fritters

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 20 minServes 2Vegetarian4 PM snackFrom 1 years

Potato Fritters scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • Potato - 2
  • Spring Onion - 2
  • Sweetcorn - 1/4 cup
  • Salt to taste
  • Red Chilli Flakes - 1 tsp
  • Oregano - 1 tsp
  • All Purpose flour / Maida - 1/2 cup or more
  • Water as needed
  • Oil for pan frying

Method

  • Peel and grate potatoes.
  • Take all ingredients in a bowl, except oil and mix well.
  • Heat oil in a nonstick pan, spoon some potato mix and let it cook till golden in the bottom.
  • Flip over and cook.
  • Remove and serve.

    Common questions

    Is Potato Fritters healthy for kids?

    Potato Fritters scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

    What age is Potato Fritters best for?

    It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and adults, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
