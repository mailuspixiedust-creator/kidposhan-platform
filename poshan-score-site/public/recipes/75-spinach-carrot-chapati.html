Spinach Carrot Chapati

Ingredients

  • Wheat Flour - 1 cup
  • Spinach - 1/2 cup chopped finely
  • Carrot - 1/4 grated finely
  • Salt a pinch
  • Oil or ghee for making chapati

Method

  • Take wheat flour, carrot, spinach and salt in a bowl. Mix well.
  • Add in water and knead to a soft dough.
  • Divide it into equal balls.
  • Roll it into chapati.
  • Cook on tawa, apply ghee and cook.
  • Serve.
