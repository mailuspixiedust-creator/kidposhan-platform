Raw Banana Crispies Recipe (Raw Banana Fritters)

Ingredients

  • 1 1/4 cups parboiled and diagonally cut raw banana
  • 2 cups corn flakes for rolling
  • oil for deep-frying
  • 3/4 cup maida (plain flour)
  • 2 tbsp cornflour
  • 1/2 tsp green chilli paste
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • salt (namak) to taste

Method

  • what is raw banana crispies made of? See below image of list of ingredients for raw banana crispies.
  • Raw bananas ( Plantains ) belong to the banana family but tend to be firmer and cannot be eaten raw.
  • They require cooking and are used when green or under ripe.
  • They are a staple food in tropical regions of the world and are used in varied ways like steamed, fried etc.
  • They are referred to as "potatoes of the Caribbean" since they resemble in flavour.
  • To parboil raw bananas, cut each banana with a sharp knife into 2 pieces along with its skin and discard the ends.
  • Wash well.
  • You will require 3 raw bananas to make 1 1/4 cups parboiled and diagonally cut raw bananas.
  • Add cut raw bananas to a deep saucepan of boiling water and cook for 8 minutes, or until they are 80% cooked.
  • Raw bananas are 80% cooked.
  • Drain and cool. While the parboiled bananas cool we will make the batter.
  • Peel the parboiled raw bananas by hand.
  • Diagonally cut parboiled raw bananas or cut to desired shape. You can cut them into thick or thin diagonal slices as per the recipe requirement.
  • In a bowl put 3/4 cup plain flour (maida).
  • Add 2 tbsp cornflour. Cornstarch is a key ingredient in making banana crispies crisp.
  • Add 1/2 tsp green chilli paste. Adjust the spice of the dish by adding more or less green chilli paste.
  • Add 1/2 tsp chilli powder.
  • Add salt to taste. We added 1/4th tsp salt.
  • Add 1 cup of water.
  • Whisk the batter until smooth, breaking up any lumps that form.
  • Batter is of pouring consistency. It should not be too thick or too runny.
  • Crush some cornflakes by hand and place them on a plate. We will add more cornflakes as we coat the parboiled raw bananas.
  • Coat the parboiled raw banana pieces in the prepared batter.
  • Roll the batter-dipped raw bananas in the cornflakes until they are evenly coated.
  • Heat the oil in a deep non-stick kadhai.
  • Deep-fry small batches of raw banana pieces at a time over low to medium heat.
  • Use a slotted spoon to flip the crispies over while deep frying so that they cook evenly on all sides.
  • Cook till raw banana crispies turn golden brown in colour from all the sides.
  • Drain on an absorbent paper.
  • Add 2 tbsp cornflour. Cornstarch is a key ingredient in making banana crispies crisp.
  • Add 1/2 tsp green chilli paste. Adjust the spice of the dish by adding more or less green chilli paste.
  • Batter is of pouring consistency. It should not be too thick or too runny.
  • Raw bananas are 80% cooked.
  • Roll the batter-dipped raw bananas in the cornflakes until they are evenly coated.
