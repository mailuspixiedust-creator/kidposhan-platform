Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup grated sweet corn cob
  • 1/2 tsp ghee
  • 1/4 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 tbsp finely chopped onion
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped tomato
  • a pinch of sambar powder
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)

Method

  • Its smooth texture makes it easily acceptable by most babies and toddlers.
  • Begin with a small quantity since it&rsquo;s a new flavor and gradually increase the quantity.
  • Always serve a new food in the morning, as babies are fresh and more acceptable to newer textures and taste.
  • Feed your baby only in upright position and not while running around in the house or playing.
  • Avoid using salt completely if advised by your pediatrician.
  • Grate the sweet corn using a grater. Grated sweet corn will be nice and juicy. Keep aside.
  • Next for the tempering of the Corn Sambhar for Kids, heat &frac12; tsp of ghee in a deep non-stick pan. Allow the ghee to heat well.
  • Add cumin seeds to the hot oil. Cumin seeds aid in digestion. Allow the cumin seeds to crackle.
  • Then add a pinch of asafoetida.
  • Add finely chopped onions with it. Some babies don&rsquo;t like the texture and flavor of chopped onions. In that case you can avoid it.
  • Saut&eacute; the onions on a medium flame for 30 seconds.
  • Then add finely chopped tomatoes. They give slight tang to the Sweet Corn Sambar for Kids. This gets balanced with the sweetness of corn.
  • Add sweet corn to it.
  • Also add a pinch of sambar powder to it.
  • You can also add sambar powder if you wish to.
  • Ensure to add only a pinch as excess would make it unpalatable for tiny tots.
  • Add salt to taste if advised by your pediatrician.
  • This is optional.
  • For babies below 1 year salt can be avoided and for kids above 1 year, minimum quantity of salt can be added.
  • Mix all the ingredients very well using a flat ladle.Cook on a medium flame for 2 to 3 minutes. Keep stirring it occasionally.
  • Add 1 cup of water to adjust the consistency of Corn Sambar.
  • Mix well with a ladle and cook on a medium flame for 2 minutes. Remember to stir it in between so that the sambar doesn&rsquo;t stick to the pan.
  • Cool the Corn Sambar slightly. Serve the Sweet Corn Sambar with Vegetable Idli for Babies and Toddlers lukewarm.
