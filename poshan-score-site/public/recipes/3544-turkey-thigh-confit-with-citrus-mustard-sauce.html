Method

To Cure

  1. Process 3 coarsely chopped large onions, 12 sprigs thyme, 2½ tablespoons table salt, 4½ teaspoons sugar, and 1½ teaspoons pepper in food processor until finely chopped, about 20 seconds, scraping down sides of bowl as needed.
  2. Spread one-third of mixture evenly in bottom of 13 by 9-inch baking dish.
  3. Arrange 4 pounds bone-in turkey thighs, skin side up, in single layer in dish.
  4. Spread remaining onion mixture evenly over thighs.
  5. Wrap dish tightly with plastic wrap and refrigerate for 4 to 6 days (whatever is most convenient).
