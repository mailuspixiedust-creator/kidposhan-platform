Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon (15 ml) oil
  • 2 green cardamoms (or ¼ to ⅓ tsp ground cardamom, elaichi)
  • 1 cup (130 grams) onions (sliced or diced from 1 medium yellow or 2 small red onions)
  • 1 inch (10 grams) ginger (peeled & sliced or use 1½ to 2 teaspoon ginger garlic paste, read notes)
  • 5 to 6 (12 grams) large garlic cloves (peeled, omit to use gg paste)
  • 2 cups (350 grams) tomatoes (sliced, use up to 420 grams from 2 large or 4 medium)
  • 15 (30 grams) whole cashew nuts (use up to 18 nuts/ 36 g if using more tomatoes or use almonds)
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon jaggery/ sugar (optional)
  • ¾ to 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder ((or paprika) (adjust to heat & color preference))
  • ¾ to 1 teaspoon garam masala (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder ((daniya powder) (optional))
  • 1 cup (240 ml) cold water (to blend)
  • 2 tablespoons (30 grams) unsalted butter
  • 1 small bay leaf (tej patta, optional)
  • 2 inch Ceylon cinnamon piece (or 1 inch dalchini, optional)
  • 3 cloves (laung, optional)
  • 250 to 300 grams (9 to 10 oz) paneer (I cut mine to ¾ inch)
  • ½ tablespoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves, crushed)
  • 3 to 4 tablespoons (45 to 60 ml) heavy cream (sub with fresh cream, cooking cream or thickened cream)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoon coriander leaves (cilantro finely chopped, to garnish)

Method

Make Paneer Butter Masala

  1. On a low heat, melt butter in the same pan and add the optional whole spices if using – bay leaf, cinnamon and cloves.
  2. When they begin to sizzle, stir in the optional red chili powder. Hold a strainer over the pan and pour the onion tomato masala. Stir with a spoon to strain.
  3. Pour ½ cup more water & mix well.
  4. Cook covered on a medium heat until slightly thick, yet flowy.
  5. It will thicken upon cooling. (Takes about 10 to 12 mins, keep stirring every 3 to 4 mins).
  6. You will see traces of fat on the butter masala when it is ready.
  7. Remove the whole spices to discard and taste test to adjust salt and garam masala.
  8. Stir in the jaggery, kasuri methi & paneer.
  9. Cook covered for 2 to 3 mins on a low heat and turn off.
  10. Stir in the cream (reserve a tablespoon to garnish).
  11. Garnish paneer butter masala with cream and chopped coriander leaves. Best served with Basmati Rice, Jeera Rice, Butter Naan, Tandoori Roti or plain parathas.

Common questions

Is Paneer Butter Masala healthy for kids?

Paneer Butter Masala scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, fibre, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Paneer Butter Masala best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
