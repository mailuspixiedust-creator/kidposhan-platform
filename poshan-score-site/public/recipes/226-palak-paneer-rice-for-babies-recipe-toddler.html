Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup chopped spinach (palak) , washed and drained
  • 1/4 cup chopped paneer (cottage cheese) , preferably made with cow's milk
  • 1/4 cup rice (chawal) , washed and drained
  • 1/2 tsp ghee
  • a pinch of cumin seeds (jeera)
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)
  • fresh dahi (curd) , preferably made with cow?s

  • Palak Paneer Rice for Babies and Toddlersis a healthy fare for babies above 1 year of age who have started eating homely food cooked for all.
  • You need to remember is to cut down on the spice while cooking for babies.
  • This Spinach and Paneer Rice for Babies and Toddlersis rich in protein, vitamin A, folic acid, calcium and a few micronutrients too.
  • Feed Palak Paneer Rice to your toddler in an upright position, while sitting down on the table. Do not form a habit of playing around the house and eating.
  • Be innovative and occasionally feed your child in plates of different sizes, shapes and colours.
  • Motivate your child to hold small bites of food and eat on his/her own.
  • For Palak Paneer Rice for Babies, and Toddlers first choose spinach that has vibrant deep green leaves and stems with no signs of yellowing.
  • The leaves should look fresh and tender, and not be wilted or bruised.
  • Avoid those that have a slimy coating as this is an indication of decay.
  • Spinach is rich in thiamin, folic acid, magnesium, fibers, etc.
  • To read in detail about the nutritional benefits of spinach, refer our article about Health Benefits of Spinach.
  • Then wash the spinach leaves with water to get rid of any dirt.
  • Chop the spinach leaves with the help of a sharp knife.
  • Do not chop them roughly as that may be difficult for the babies to chew on.
  • Keep the chopped palak aside.
  • Chop the paneer into small pieces and keep aside.
  • Next we need to prepare the rice for Spinach and Paneer Rice for Babies and Toddlers.For that first clean and wash the rice with clean water.
  • Drain the water well and discard it. Keep the washed rice aside.
  • Now take a clean pressure cooker and add &frac12; tsp of ghee to it. Allow the ghee to heat.
  • Once the ghee melts, add the cumin seeds. Allow the cumin seeds to crackle.
  • Add the washed and drained rice to it to make sumptuous Palak Paneer Pulao for Toddlers.
  • Saut&eacute; the rice in ghee on a medium flame for 1 minute. This enhances the flavour of rice.
  • Add the chopped spinach.
  • Add the chopped paneer.
  • Add salt.
  • Since this Palak Paneer Chawal is for toddlers, add restricted amounts of salt.
  • Use of excessive salt in cooking is a habit which is developed since childhood.
  • Avoid it.
  • Add 1 cup of water for cooking and mix well using a ladle.
  • Close the lid of the pressure cooker and pressure cook for 2 whistles. Let the steam escape before opening the lid.
  • This is how the Spinach and Paneer Chawal for babieslooks after cooking.
  • Serve the Palak Paneer Rice for Babeis and Toddlers lukewarm to your growing baby.
