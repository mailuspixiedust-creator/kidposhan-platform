How to make Cucumber yogurt curry

Recipe by nupursindiankitchen.com

Photo: nupursindiankitchen.com
⏱ 20 minServes 5VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

How to make Cucumber yogurt curry scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 500g cucumber
  • 2 cups plain unsweetened yogurt
  • 2 tbsp. oil
  • 1/2 tsp. cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp. mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp. urad dal
  • 2 dried red chillies
  • 1.5 inch piece of ginger
  • Hing or asafoetida (a pinch)
  • 1 green chilli
  • 1/2 large onion (chopped)
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 3 tbsp. coriander leaves (chopped)
  • lime juice (to taste)
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1/4 tsp. black pepper powder

Method

  • Cut cucumber in small pieces (discard the ends of the cucumber)
  • Crush ginger and green chillies in a mortar and pestle
  • Set a pan on medium heat and add oil
  • Add cumin seeds, mustard seeds, dried red chillies, curry leaves, Hing or asafoetida
  • Once the seeds start to splutter add the ginger/green chilli paste
  • Give it a quick stir
  • Add cucumber and salt to taste
  • Place the lid on till the cucumber softens slightly (~5 to 10 min)
  • Turn off the heat and let the cucumber cool down
  • Add yogurt, chopped onion, black pepper powder, coriander leaves and some salt and lime juice (to taste)
  • Have this with rice or roti
