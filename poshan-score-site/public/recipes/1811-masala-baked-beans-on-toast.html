Masala Baked Beans on Toast

Recipe by shwetainthekitchen.com

Photo: shwetainthekitchen.com
⏱ 30 minServes 6VegetarianBreakfast4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Masala Baked Beans on Toast scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon oil or butter
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ½ cup red onions (finely chopped)
  • ½ cup green bell pepper (finely chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (minced)
  • 1-2 green chili (chopped, adjust to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons Maggi hot and sweet chili sauce (or tomato ketchup or chili sauce of your choice)
  • 1 teaspoon dry oregano
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri chili powder (or use red chili flakes)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½-1 teaspoon Garam Masala
  • 1 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 12 oz can Baked beans (Heinz)
  • 1-2 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1 teaspoon sugar (optional only if needed)
  • 2 tablespoon cilantro (finely chopped)
  • 2 tablespoons Spring onions green (chopped)
  • 4 slices bread (Sourdough or bread of choice)
  • 1 garlic clove (to rub on toasted bread - optional)
  • 1 cup cheese (cheddar, mozzarella, or pepper jack)
  • 2 tablespoons Red onions (finely chopped, as needed)
  • 2 tablespoons Cilantro (finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon Chaat masala

Method

For Masala Baked Beans

  1. Heat butter (or oil) in a pan on medium heat.
  2. Add cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.
  3. Add chopped onions and green bell pepper and sauté until translucent
  4. Stir in garlic and green chilli. Cook for another 30 seconds.
  5. Add spices: Cumin powder, coriander powder, Kashmiri red chilli powder,oregano, garam masala, and salt.
  6. Mix well and cook the masala for 30 seconds.
  7. Optional: Add a splash of water if the masala starts to stick.
  8. Add Chili sauce followed by baked beans (with sauce - see notes)
  9. Stir everything well so the beans are fully coated in the masala mixture.
  10. Lower the heat and let it cook for 3–5 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  11. The sauce will thicken slightly and absorb all the masala flavors.
  12. Turn off the heat and add lemon juice and mix in chopped cilantro leaves. Taste and adjust salt or spice if needed.
