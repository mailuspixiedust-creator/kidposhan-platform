Poha Dosa (Atukula Dosa)

Ingredients

  • 3 cups rice (I used carolina rice) (do not use basmati rice)
  • ¾ cup thick poha
  • ¼ cup whole urad dal
  • salt to taste
  • oil to make the dosa

Method

Soaking-

  1. In a large bowl add the rice and rinse it with water 3-4 times.
  2. Soak the rice in enough water for atleast 6 hours.
  3. In a different bowl add the urad dal and poha and rinse it.
  4. Soak them in just enough water for 6 hours.

Poha Dosa (Atukula Dosa) scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

What age is Poha Dosa (Atukula Dosa) best for?

It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for adults, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
