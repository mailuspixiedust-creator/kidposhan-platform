Method

Making dosa batter

  1. In a mixing bowl, add 2 cup dosa rice and 1 cup small grain rice, 1 cup urad dal and 1 tsp methi dana. Wash and soak it for 4-5 hrs.
  2. Now remove water from the soaked grain and keep it aside. Use water while grinding the batter.
  3. Then in a mixture jar, add soaked grains and some water. Grind batter into a smooth and ribbon-like consistency.
  4. Take out batter into mixing bowl and whisk batter in one direction.
  5. Cover and ferment batter to keep it in warm place. You can cover the batter with a thick blanket or keep it in oven or instant pot.
  6. After 6-7 hrs, the batter is properly fermented.
  7. Now add ¼ cup idli rava and mix well.
  8. The Dosa batter is ready.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
