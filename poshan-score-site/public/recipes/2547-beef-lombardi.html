Beef Lombardi

Recipe by americastestkitchen.com

Photo: americastestkitchen.com
⏱ 1 h 45 minServes Serves 6 to 8Non-vegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Beef Lombardi scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 pound 90-percent lean ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • Salt and pepper
  • 3 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3 (10-ounce) cans Ro-Tel Original Diced Tomatoes & Green Chilies, drained
  • 12 ounces (7 3/4 cups) wide egg noodles
  • 1 1/4 cups sour cream
  • 4 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 tablespoon cornstarch
  • 4 scallions, sliced thin
  • 8 ounces Colby Jack cheese, ­shredded (2 cups)

Method

  • Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 350 degrees.
  • Grease 13 by 9-inch baking dish.
  • Heat oil in 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until just smoking.
  • Add beef, onion, 1 teaspoon salt, and 1 teaspoon pepper and cook, breaking up pieces with spoon, until beef is cooked through, about 8 minutes.
  • Stir in tomato paste and garlic and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  • Stir in tomatoes and cook until most liquid has evaporated, 3 to 5 minutes.
  • Remove from heat and set aside.
  • Meanwhile, bring 4 quarts water to boil in large pot.
  • Add noodles and 1 tablespoon salt and cook, stirring often, for 3 minutes.
  • Reserve 2 cups cooking water, then drain noodles.
  • Whisk sour cream, cream cheese, cornstarch, and 1/2 cup reserved ­cooking water together in now-empty pot until smooth.
  • Stir in scallions, noodles, remaining 1 1/2 cups cooking water, and 3/4 teaspoon salt.
  • Spread noodle mixture in even layer in prepared baking dish.
  • Spread beef mixture evenly over noodles.
  • Sprinkle Colby Jack evenly over top.
  • Bake until bubbling around edges and cheese is spotty brown, 25 to 30 minutes.
  • Let cool for 15 minutes.
  • Serve.

    • TO MAKE AHEAD:

    1. The casserole can be fully assembled minus the Colby Jack, cooled completely, covered, and refrigerated for up to 24 hours.
    2. To serve, sprinkle with Colby Jack and increase baking time to 45 to 50 minutes.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
