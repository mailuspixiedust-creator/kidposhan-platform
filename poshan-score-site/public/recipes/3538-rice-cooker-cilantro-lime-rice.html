Ingredients

  • 1 cup long grain rice
  • 1 ¾ cups water
  • ½ teaspoon chicken bouillon powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 2-2 ½ tablespoons melted butter
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons chopped cilantro

Method

  • Rinse the rice 3–4 times, changing the water each time, until it runs mostly clear and drain well.
  • Add the water, chicken bouillon powder, and salt. Stir to combine, transfer everything to the rice cooker, and cook using the regular rice setting.
  • Once the rice is cooked, add the melted butter and lime juice.
  • Fluff and mix well, then stir in the chopped cilantro.
  • Serve warm with my fresh Tomato Salsa and Chipotle Chicken.
