Vegetarian Cajun Pasta

Recipe by shwetainthekitchen.com

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 9 oz mini penne pasta (uncooked)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (or butter)
  • 1 small red onion (sliced or diced)
  • 1 bell pepper (sliced or diced, using mix of red, green and orange)
  • 1 Roma tomato (chopped)
  • ¼ cup green peas (using frozen, thawed)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (minced)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 1 ½ cup heavy cream
  • ½ cup parmesan cheese (or Parmigiano Reggiano, freshly grated, plus more for serving)
  • 1 teaspoon dry parsley (or fresh parsley for serving)
  • 1 ½ - 2 tablespoons Cajun seasoning (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • ½ teaspoon paprika (or smoked paprika or red chili powder)
  • ½ teaspoon red chili flakes (adjust to taste)
  • ¼ teaspoon ground black pepper (adjust to taste)
  • ¼-½ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)

Method

  • Bring a pot of water to a boil (I add 2 teaspoons of salt to the water), then cook the pasta according to the directions on the package.
  • When it's done cooking, drain and set aside.
  • Heat olive oil in a large, deep skillet over medium heat. Add onion and bell pepper; sauté for about 2-3 minutes or until onion begins to soften.
  • Add garlic, green peas, and sauté again for 2 minutes or until the raw garlic smell disappears.
  • Turn heat to medium-low and stir in the spices and tomato paste. Mix well and cook for a minute. Deglaze with 1/4 cup water (or pasta water) (if needed).
  • Add cream and mix until everything is well combined. Bring the sauce to a boil and then reduce to a simmer.
  • Add cheese and cook over medium-low heat, stirring occasionally until the sauce thickens, forming a creamy Cajun pasta sauce.
  • Then add the tomatoes and cooked pasta.
  • Mix well to coat the pasta evenly with the sauce.
  • Cook for 2-3 minutes.
  • Taste and adjust any seasoning if needed.
  • Turn off the heat.
  • Creamy Vegetarian Cajun Pasta is ready to serve!
  • Serve Cajun pasta garnished with more parmesan cheese and parsley. Enjoy!

    Common questions

    Is Vegetarian Cajun Pasta healthy for kids?

    What age is Vegetarian Cajun Pasta best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 70, for 10–12 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
