Best Prawn Biryani Recipe Kerala Style

Ingredients

  • 250 g Prawns
  • 250 g Vermicelli
  • 150 g Ghee
  • 100 g Cashews
  • 4-5 Cloves
  • 5 g Cinnamon
  • 5 Cardamom
  • 1 cup Spring Onions
  • 2 Onions
  • 2 tsp Green Chillies
  • 2 tsp Lime Juice
  • Salt
  • 1 Bunch Mint and Coriander Leaves
  • 1 cup Curd
  • 2 tsp Ginger garlic Paste
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • Chilli Powder

Method

  • Boil a glass of water in a pan. Add vermicelli and cook. Drain the water and keep it aside.
  • Heat ghee in a Kadai and fry onions, cashews till brown.
  • Then add ginger-garlic paste, cinnamon, cloves, cardamoms, spring onions, green chillies and fry.
  • Add these ingredients one at a time and fry each of them for 2 minutes.
  • Add raw prawns, beaten curd, salt, turmeric, chilli powder and mix well.
  • Boil till prawns are done and water evaporates.
  • Then mix vermicelli and sauté for a minute.
  • Mix lime juice and remove from flame.
  • Garnish with chopped mint, coriander leaves and cashew nuts. Serve hot with onion raita.
