Method

  • Pat 1 pound shrimp, peeled, deveined, and tails removed, dry with paper towels and sprinkle all over with ½ teaspoon table salt and ½ teaspoon pepper.
  • Heat 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil in large Dutch oven over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  • Add 12 ounces andouille sausage, sliced ¼ inch thick, and cook until crisp and brown, about 5 minutes.
  • Using slotted spoon, transfer sausage to paper towel–lined plate.
  • Add shrimp to fat remaining in pot and cook until browned and cooked through, about 2 minutes per side.
  • Using slotted spoon, transfer shrimp to plate with the sausage.
  • Add 1 chopped onion, 1 chopped green bell pepper, and remaining ½ teaspoon salt to fat remaining in pot. Cook, stirring, until softened, about 5 minutes.
  • Stir in 6 minced garlic cloves, 2 tablespoons tomato paste, 1 teaspoon dried thyme, and 1 teaspoon Tony Chachere’s Original Creole Seasoning.
  • Cook until fragrant and tomato paste caramelizes, 1 to 2 minutes.
  • Add 1½ cups orzo, stirring to coat evenly.
  • Pour in 4 cups chicken broth, scraping up browned bits.
  • Add 1 cup canned diced tomatoes and their liquid, bring to boil, then reduce heat to simmer.
  • Cook, stirring often, until orzo is al denteand liquid has thickened, about 15 minutes.
  • Stir in ¼ cup heavy cream, sausage, and shrimp and cook until warmed through, 1 to 2 minutes.
  • Off heat, stir in ½ cup grated Parmesan.
  • Serve, garnishing with 2 thinly sliced scallions.
