Fried Fish with Chili Shrimp Paste Sauce

Recipe by Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar

Photo: Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar
⏱ 45 minNon-vegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 1 years

Fried Fish with Chili Shrimp Paste Sauce scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 , about 1 kg, scaled and cleaned Whole Fish
  • Oil for deep frying
  • For Chili Shrimp Paste
  • 7 to 10 , soaked in water, deseeded and finely chopped Dried Red Chillies
  • 100 gms , minced Garlic
  • 100 gms , sliced Small Onions
  • 1/2 tblsp Dried Shrimp Paste
  • 3 tblsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Fish Sauce
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 5 , finely sliced Kaffir Lime Leaves

Method

  • Make incisions about 1/2 inch apart along the back of the fish.
  • Heat 3 tbslp oil in a pan over high heat.
  • Fry the dried red chillies, garlic, onions and shrimp paste until fragrant.
  • Add the fish sauce and sugar.
  • Fry for 2 minutes and keep aside.
  • Dry the fish and deep fry in hot oil until cooked.
  • Serve the fish topped with the chilli shrimp paste.
  • Garnish with lime leaves.
