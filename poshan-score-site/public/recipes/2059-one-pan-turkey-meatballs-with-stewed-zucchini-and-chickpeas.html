One-Pan Turkey Meatballs with Stewed Zucchini and Chickpeas

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup panko bread crumbs
  • 1 large egg, lightly beaten
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 3/4 teaspoon table salt, divided
  • 1 pound 93 percent lean ground turkey or chicken
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, plus extra for drizzling
  • 1 teaspoon ground coriander
  • 1 teaspoon ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • 1/2 teaspoon pepper
  • 1 (28-ounce) can whole peeled tomatoes, drained with juice reserved, chopped coarse
  • 1 (15-ounce) can chickpeas, rinsed
  • 2 zucchini (8 ounces each), quartered lengthwise, seeded, and sliced 1 inch thick
  • 1/4 cup raisins
  • 1/2 cup chopped fresh parsley, cilantro, and/or mint, divided
  • 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest

Method

  • Combine ¼ cup panko, 1 lightly beaten egg, 2 minced garlic cloves, and ¼ teaspoon salt in large bowl.
  • Add 1 pound ground turkey and mix with your hands until thoroughly combined.
  • Using moistened hands, pinch off and roll mixture into 16 meatballs, about 1¼ ounces each.
  • Heat 2 tablespoons oil in Dutch oven over medium-high heat until just smoking.
  • Add meatballs and cook until well browned on all sides, about 5 minutes.
  • Using slotted spoon, transfer meatballs to plate.
  • Add 1 teaspoon coriander, 1 teaspoon cumin, ¾ teaspoon cinnamon, and ½ teaspoon pepper to fat left in pot and cook until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  • Stir in 1 (28-ounce) can coarsely chopped tomatoes and reserved juice, scraping up any browned bits, then stir in 1 (15-ounce) can rinsed chickpeas; 2 zucchini, quartered lengthwise, seeded, and sliced 1 inch thick; ¼ cup raisins, and remaining ½ teaspoon salt.
  • Bring to simmer and cook until zucchini has softened, 6 to 8 minutes.
  • Reduce heat to medium-low and nestle meatballs into pot.
  • Cover and simmer gently until zucchini is very tender and meatballs register 160 degrees, 6 to 8 minutes.
  • Off heat, stir in ¼ cup chopped parsley and season with salt and pepper to taste.
  • Sprinkle with remaining ¼ cup parsley and 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest and drizzle with extra oil.
  • Serve.
