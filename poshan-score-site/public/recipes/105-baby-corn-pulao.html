Baby Corn Pulao

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 50 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Baby Corn Pulao scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Basmati Rice
  • 2 cups Baby Corn (cut lengthwise)
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Fennel Seeds
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 3 cm Cinnamon
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 1 large Onion (sliced thinly)
  • 3 to 4 slit Green Chilli
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • Sat to taste
  • Coriander leaves a handful finely chopped
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 1/2 cup Water

Method

  • Wash and soak rice for 30 mins. Drain it and set aside.
  • Heat ghee and oil in a pressure cooker, add in whole garam masala and let them sizzle.
  • Add in onion, chillies and saute till golden brown.
  • Add in ginger garlic paste and saute for a min.
  • Add in babycorn and saute for 5 mins or so.
  • Add in all turmeric and salt and mix well.
  • Now add in water, milk, coriander leaves and rice and mix well.
  • Bring it to a boil and cover and pressure cook for 1 whistle, simmer for 5 mins. Turn off the heat and let the steam go all by itself.
  • Now open the lid and fluff the rice with fork.
  • Cover again and set aside for 10 mins.
  • Now serve.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
