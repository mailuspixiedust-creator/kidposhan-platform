Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon oil (or ghee)
  • 1 medium onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste
  • 1 cup mixed veggies (carrot, beans, peas, sweet corn)
  • 2 tablespoons mint (pudina) (optional)
  • 1 cup quinoa (rinsed)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1.25 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 tablespoon cilantro (coriander leaves)
  • 1 teaspoon jeera (cumin) seeds
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 inch cinnamon
  • 2 cardamom
  • 2 cloves

Method

Stovetop Pressure Cooker Method:

  1. Follow the process mentioned above on a pot. Add mixed vegetables, mint, and cook for a minute.
  2. After pressure has released naturally, Fluff up the quinoa gently.
  3. Lastly, Squeeze some lime juice and garnish with cilantro.
  4. Quinoa pulao in a pressure cooker is ready to serve.
