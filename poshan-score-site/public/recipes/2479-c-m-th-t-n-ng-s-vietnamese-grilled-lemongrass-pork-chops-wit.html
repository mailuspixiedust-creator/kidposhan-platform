Cơm Thịt Nướng Sả (Vietnamese Grilled Lemongrass Pork Chops with Broken Rice)

Ingredients

  • ½ cup packed brown sugar
  • ½ cup fish sauce
  • ½ cup vegetable oil
  • 4 lemongrass stalks, trimmed to bottom 8 inches, bruised, and minced
  • 1 large shallot, minced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 teaspoons pepper
  • 2 Thai chiles, stemmed, seeded, and sliced thin
  • 8 (4- to 6-ounce) bone-in blade-cut pork chops, ½ inch thick, trimmed
  • 1 ½ tablespoons granulated sugar, divided
  • 1 small Thai chile, stemmed and minced
  • 1 small garlic clove, minced
  • ⅓ cup hot water
  • 2 ½ tablespoons fish sauce
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 6 scallions, sliced thin
  • Pinch table salt
  • 3 cups broken rice
  • 6 tomatoes, cored, halved, and sliced ¼-inch thick
  • ½ cup chopped dry-roasted peanuts (optional)
  • 2 English cucumbers, sliced into ⅛-inch-thick rounds

Method

FOR THE PORK CHOPS:

  1. Combine sugar, fish sauce, oil, lemongrass, shallot, garlic, pepper, and Thai chiles in large bowl and stir until sugar is dissolved.
  2. Add pork chops and toss until evenly coated.
  3. Arrange chops evenly in large baking dish, pouring any excess marinade from bowl over top.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or up to 24 hours.
  5. FOR THE NƯỚC CHẤM: Meanwhile, using mortar and pestle, mash 1 tablespoon sugar, Thai chile, and garlic to fine paste (or mash to paste on cutting board using flat side of chef’s knife).
  6. Transfer to medium bowl and add hot water and remaining 1½ teaspoons sugar.
  7. Stir until sugar is dissolved.
  8. Stir in fish sauce and lime juice and set aside (nước chấm can be refrigerated for up to 2 days.)
