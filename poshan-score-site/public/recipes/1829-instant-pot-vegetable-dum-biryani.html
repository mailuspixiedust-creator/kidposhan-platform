Instant Pot Vegetable Dum Biryani

Recipe by spiceindiaonline.com

Photo: spiceindiaonline.com
⏱ 1 h 15 minServes 2VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Instant Pot Vegetable Dum Biryani scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Basmati
  • 3 cup Water
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 3 each Bay leaf
  • 1 each Star anise
  • 3 sticks Cinnamon
  • 2 each Green cardamom
  • 5 each Cloves
  • 1/2 tsp Fennel seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 4 each Green Chili (1/2)
  • 1/2 cup Onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic paste
  • 1/4 cup Tomato
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Red Chili powder
  • 1 tbsp Coriander powder
  • 1.5 tsp Salt (add 1/4 tsp salt or as per taste)
  • 1 tbsp Mint leaves
  • 1 Tbsp Coriander leaves
  • 1/4 cup Plain Yogurt
  • 2 cup Mixed Vegetables (carrots, beans, peas, potato & cauliflower)
  • 1/4 cup Mint leaves
  • 1/4 cup Coriander leaves
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • 3 strands Saffron
  • 1/4 cup Warm milk
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves
  • 10 each Cashews
  • 10 pieces Fried Bread pieces (fry white bread in some ghee until golden)

Method

Prep work

  1. Soak basmati rice in enough water for at least 45 minutes.
  2. Vegetables does not need any marination but marinating ahead time will enhance flavor.
  3. If you decide to marinate, use the yogurt along with turmeric, coriander, red chili powder and little salt from the list and coat the veggies and refrigerate.
