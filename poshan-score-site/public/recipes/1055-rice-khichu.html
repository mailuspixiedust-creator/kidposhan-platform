Rice Khichu

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Photo: Tarla Dalal
⏱ 15 minServes 4 servingsJainLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

How often: Everyday · whole grain Fine most days, with variety. The Poshan Score shows how one serving does against a child's daily limits; this line is about how the food is made.

Rice Khichu scores 80 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice flour (chawal ka atta)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera) , roughly ground
  • 1 tsp finely chopped green chillies
  • a pinch of baking soda
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • 2 tsp oil
  • 8 tsp oil
  • red chilli powder for sprinkling

Method

  • Like Rice Khichu ( gujarati rice khichu, chawal ka atta khichu, rice flour khichu) then check our collection of Gujarati snacks.
  • We have an amazing collection of all the popular Gujarati Snack Recipes from dhoklas to khakras to patras. moong dal dhokla poha dhokla masasla khakhra
  • For making the rice khichu, in a deep non-stick pan, take 1/2 tsp cumin seeds (jeera). Also, you can toss some sesame seeds for an added flavour.
  • Add 1 tsp finely chopped green chillies.
  • You can add more or less chillies as per the spice level you can handle.
  • Alternatively, you can add green chilli paste or even put some ginger to make the khichu recipe more delightful.
  • Add a pinch of baking soda and salt to taste.
  • My grandmother makes use of papad khar while preparing the khichu.
  • Also, be cautious while adding salt as soda will also make khichu a bit salty.
  • Pour 4 cups of water and mix well. You can even put some fresh coriander leaves to elevate the flavour of rice khichu.
  • Boil on a high flame for 3 to 4 minutes.
  • Once the water starts boiling, add the 2 tsp oil.
  • Add the 1 cup rice flour (chawal ka atta) and mix vigorously using a whisk or a flat ladle.
  • You can even make use of a wooden rolling pin to mix the khichu mixture.
  • While you are stirring, within a minute, the rice flour will absorb most of the water and becomes a thick, sticky dough.
  • Khichu can be prepared with other flour like bajra flour, jowar flour, nachni flour or whole wheat flour.
  • For variation, you can even use a combination of flour.
  • Cook on a low flame for another 3 to 4 minutes or till the mixture leaves the sides of the pan, while stirring it continuously to ensure that it is not sticking to the bottom of the pan.
  • Switch off the flame and it is ready to serve!
  • Divide the gujarati rice khichu into 4 equal portions. Put a portion of the khichu in an individual cup.
  • Pour 2 tsp of oil evenly over it.
  • Finaly sprinkle little chilli powder or koro sambhar. Here is the perfect recipe to make koro sambar it at home.
  • Serve the rice khichu | chawal ka atta khichu immediately.

    Is Rice Khichu healthy for kids?

    What age is Rice Khichu best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 1–3 years, and lowest, 80, for 4–6 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
