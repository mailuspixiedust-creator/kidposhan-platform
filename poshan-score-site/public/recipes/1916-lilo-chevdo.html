Lilo Chevdo

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 30 minServes 1VegetarianLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Lilo Chevdo scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 4 medium sized potatoes
  • Soaked and dried chana dal
  • ¼ cup cashew
  • ¼ cup raisin
  • Oil for frying
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp white sesame seeds
  • 2 chopped green chilli
  • 6-7 curry leaves
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • ¼ cup water
  • 3 tbsp sugar
  • ½ cup chana dal
  • ¾ cup water
  • Pinch of baking soda

Method

  • In a bowl, soak chana dal with hot water and a pinch of baking soda for 3-4 hours.
  • Drain the water from dal and spread them on a kitchen towel or cloth.
  • Grate the potatoes with a wafer slicer or the thicker side of the grater.
  • Wash 3-4 times, so that all starch is removed.
  • Drain the water from the potatoes and spread them on a thick towel.
  • All you need to do is air dry these grated potatoes and soaked dal.
  • Once dry deep fry the potatoes, while they are frying add salt to 1 tbsp water, dissolve and add it to the oil.
  • By adding salt solution the potatoes will naturally become salted.
  • Remove the crisp potatoes on a kitchen towel.
  • Then fry dal with the help of a sieve and added with potatoes.
  • Lastly, fry cashews and raisins and added with potatoes.

    • Tempering

    1. Heat a tbsp of oil.
    2. Add green chilies, curry leaves, and finally sesame seeds, and sauté for 1 minute.
    3. Add turmeric powder and mix well.
    4. Then add ¼ cup water and sugar. Mix well and cook till sugar reaches one-string consistency.
    5. Switch off the flame and add this syrup to chevda mixture.
    6. Mix well so that the mixture is well coated with syrup.
    7. Lilo Chevdo is ready to serve. This can be stored for a maximum of up to a week and Refrigerate for longer storage.
