Instant Pot Thai Peanut Noodles

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 20 minServes 3VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 5 years

Instant Pot Thai Peanut Noodles scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 8 oz spaghetti
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 inch ginger (minced)
  • 2 cups vegetable broth (or water)
  • salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 medium carrot (cut into julienne)
  • 1/2 red bell pepper (cut into julienne)
  • 1/2 green bell pepper (cut into julienne)
  • 1 cup red cabbage (shredded)
  • 3 stalks green onions (chopped)
  • 1/4 cup peanut butter (creamy or crunchy)
  • 2 tablespoons dark soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon rice vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup (Or honey or 1 tablespoon brown sugar, optional)
  • 2 tablespoons sriracha (Add more or less according to your taste, or red chilli sauce)
  • 1/4 cup crushed roasted peanuts
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tablespoon sesame seeds (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons cilantro (coriander leaves)

Method

Stovetop Pot Method:

  1. Cook the noodles in a large pot according to package directions. Drain the noodles.
  2. In a small bowl, combine creamy peanut butter, soy sauce, vinegar, red chili sauce or Sriracha, maple syrup or honey.
  3. Stir well with a whisker until well blended and keep aside.If sauce it too thick add 2 tablespoon of water.
  4. In a medium pan, heat oil, once hot add minced ginger and garlic and saute for few seconds. Then toss the veggies on high heat for few seconds.
  5. Add the cooked noodles and peanut sauce, Toss gently until everything is coated evenly.
  6. Lastly squeeze some lime juice and garnish with chopped roasted peanuts, cilantro and sesame seeds. Enjoy hot!
