Method

Making the Potato Fry

  1. Boil potato till fork tender and keep on the side.
  2. Take a pan add 4 teaspoons of oil.
  3. When the oil gets hot add mustard seeds.
  4. When the mustard starts popping add chana dal, urad dal and cumin seeds.
  5. Fry for few seconds.
  6. Add the green chili and curry leaves. Then add the chopped onion, turmeric and some salt. Cook for about five minutes till onion gets soft.
  7. Add the bell pepper, corn and green peas. Mix and close the lid. Cook for few more minutes till all the veggies get soft.
  8. Add the cooked cubed potato with the skin on.
  9. Slightly mash with back of the spoon.
  10. Finally add the lemon juice, coriander leaves and salt.
  11. Cook for another five minutes and turn off the heat.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
