Gobi 65

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 20 minServes 4Vegetarian4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Gobi 65 scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 medium Cauliflower (You can use broccoli as well | cut into bite size florets)
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 2 tsp Salt to taste
  • Oil for deep frying
  • Curry leaves (for garnishing)
  • 2 tbsp Kashmiri Chilli powder
  • 3 tbsp Lemon Juice or Vinegar
  • 4 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 2 tsp Garam masala powder
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup All Purpose Flour | Plain Flour
  • ½ cup Rice Flour
  • ¼ cup Cornstarch
  • ½ tsp Baking Soda
  • ¼ cup Water
  • 1 tsp Oil
  • 6 cloves Garlic (chopped finely)
  • 1 medium Onion (sliced thinly)
  • 1 no Green Chilli (chopped finely)
  • ¼ cup Bellpepper | Capsicum (chopped finely)
  • 1 sprig Curry leaves

Method

How to Make Gobi 65

  1. Bring water to a full boil.
  2. Add in salt and turmeric powder.
  3. Add chopped gobi in a sauce pan.
  4. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Strain using slotted spoon and take it in a large mixing bowl.
  6. Add in marination ingredients and mix well.
  7. Let it marinate for 10minutes.
  8. Just before frying add in flour, cornstarch, rice flour, baking soda and water.
  9. Mix well so the cauliflower is coated with the flour and masala.
  10. Heat oil for deep frying.
  11. Drop in the cauliflower pieces in oil and fry till golden.
  12. Just before you serve, add a handful of curry leaves into the oil and fry for 10 seconds.
  13. Now strain the fried gobi with slotted spoon and serve hot.

For Making Gobi 65 with Seasoning

  1. Now heat 1 tsp of oil in a kadai.
  2. Add in garlic and curry leaves.
  3. Fry for 30 seconds.
  4. Add in green chillies and onions and saute for a min.
  5. Now add in bell peppers and salt and mix well.
  6. Now add in fried gobi and mix well.
  7. Serve hot.
