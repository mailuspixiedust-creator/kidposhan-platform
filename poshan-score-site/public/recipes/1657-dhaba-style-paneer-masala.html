Ingredients

  • 2 tomatoes chopped
  • ½ cup coriander leaves
  • ¼ cup mint leaves (pudina leaves)
  • 2 green chillies
  • 1 teaspoon kashmiri chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½ teaspoon cumin powder
  • ⅛ teaspoon pepper powder
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • ½ teaspoon chat masala
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 1 ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • ¼ teaspoon garam masala
  • 250 grams paneer cut into cubes
  • 1 ½ tablespoons oil
  • 1 tablespoon ghee
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds (whole jeera)
  • 2 cardamoms
  • 3 cloves
  • 1 inch cinnamon
  • ½ cup chopped onions (60 gms)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste
  • ¼ cup curds (whisked)
  • ¼ teaspoon kasuri methi (dry fenugreek leaves)
  • ¾ cup hot water

Common questions

Is Dhaba Style Paneer Masala healthy for kids?

Dhaba Style Paneer Masala scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Dhaba Style Paneer Masala best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 60, for 13–15 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
