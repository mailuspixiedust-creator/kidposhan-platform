Ingredients

  • ½ a red onion, thinly sliced, with the grain.
  • 1- 1 ½ teaspoon kosher salt, start with 1, add more to taste
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • ¾ cup fresh lime juice (4-6 limes) freshly squeezed (try to use ripe limes)
  • 1-2 garlic cloves very finely minced (use a garlic press)
  • 1 fresh serrano or jalapeño chili pepper seeded and very finely chopped. Start conservatively, more to taste.
  • 1 pound fresh fish- sea bass, red snapper, corvina, halibut, dorado, escolar, mahi-mahi, tilapia, or hamachi – diced into 1/2 inch cubes.
  • ¼-½ cup fresh cilantro, chopped
  • 1 cup grape or cherry tomatoes, sliced or cut in half (or 1 cup diced tomatoes)
  • 1 cup diced cucumber
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil (optional)
  • optional: 1 semi-firm Avocado, diced, or make the Avocado Sauce
  • ⅔ cup Avocado
  • ⅓ cup cilantro
  • 2/3 cup water- plus more as needed
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1 teaspoon coriander
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove
  • cracked pepper to taste

Method

  • Slice the red onion thinly with the grain, and toss in a bowl with salt, pepper and lime juice, coating well.
  • Add the fish, garlic and fresh chilies, and gently mix.
  • Add the tomatoes, cucumber, cilantro and olive oil, and give a stir and marinate in the refrigerator for at least 30 minutes before serving (45-60 minutes is ideal).
  • The longer you marinate the firmer and more “cooked” the fish will become.
  • Before serving, taste and adjust the salt and heat.
  • Add more salt or chilies if you like.
  • If adding avocado, gently fold it in right before serving- you may need to add a pinch more salt.
  • To make the optional Avocado Sauce, blend all ingredients in a blender until smooth, adding a bit more water or oil, if needed to get the blades going.

    Common questions

    Is The Best Ceviche Recipe! healthy for kids?

    The Best Ceviche Recipe! scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is The Best Ceviche Recipe! best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 70, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
