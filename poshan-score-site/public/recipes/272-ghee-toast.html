Ghee Toast

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Ingredients

  • 4 whole wheat bread slices or bread
  • 2 tbsp ghee
  • 2 tsp sugar , optional

Method

  • To make the Ghee Toast, Spread ½ tbsp pure ghee evenly on one side of each bread slice.
  • Sprinkle ½ tsp sugar , optional evenly over the ghee-coated side of each bread slice.
  • Spread evenly over the ghee-coated side.
  • Place the bread slices on the hot tawa with the ghee- and sugar-coated side facing down and Cook on a medium flame until the underside turns crisp and golden brown.
  • Apply a little more ghee to the other side.
  • Flip the bread, and toast until both sides are evenly golden and crisp.
  • Transfer the ghee toast to a serving plate.
  • Cut each toasts diagonally into 2 equal pieces.
  • Cool completely and pack in a tiffin box.
  • Serve Ghee Toast immediately with a glass of warm milk.
