Instant Pot Jeera Rice (Indian Cumin Rice)

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 15 minServes 2JainDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Instant Pot Jeera Rice (Indian Cumin Rice) scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 teaspoon cumin (jeera) Seeds
  • 1 inch cinnamon
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 green cardamom
  • 1-2 tablespoons oil or ghee
  • 1 cup basmati rice (rinsed)
  • 2 green chilies (split lengthwise)
  • 1.5 cups water
  • salt (to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons coriander (cilantro)

Method

Stovetop Pot Method:

  1. To make jeera rice recipe or pulao on a stovetop pot, follow the steps as above until adding water and then cook covered on a low heat until the water is absorbed and then turn off the heat.
  2. Allow it to rest for some time and then open the lid and fluff the rice.
