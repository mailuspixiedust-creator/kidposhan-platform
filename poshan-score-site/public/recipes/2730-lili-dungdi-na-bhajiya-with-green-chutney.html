Lili dungdi na bhajiya with green chutney

Ingredients

  • 6 stalks green onion or 2 cups chopped green onion
  • 4 finely chopped green chili
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp ajwain
  • 1 tsp dry mango powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp black salt
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tsp white sesame seeds
  • ¾ cup besan (gram flour)
  • 1 tbsp rice flour
  • Pinch of turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp water
  • 1 tbsp hot oil
  • Oil for deep frying
  • ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves
  • 3 green chili
  • 5 garlic cloves
  • 1 medium size tomato

Method

Making onion bhajiya

  1. Rinse and then chop spring onions with their green leaves.
  2. In a mixing bowl, add chopped spring onions, chopped green chili, grated ginger, red chili powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, ajwain, amchur powder, garam masala, black salt, salt, and white sesame seeds.
  3. Mix it well using your hand, and rub it with your fingers so flour and spices are coated with onions.
  4. Cover and rest it for 5 minutes.
  5. Now add besan, rice flour, and turmeric powder. Mix well.
  6. Also, add a tablespoon of hot oil to a batter and mix well.
  7. By adding a tablespoon of water at a time, make a very thick batter.
  8. Heat the oil in a pan or Kadai on medium heat for frying. When the oil is hot add a spoonful of batter to the oil.
  9. Fry a few pakoras at a time. Keep moving in between for even browning.
  10. Once get crispy and golden brown from all sides remove it onto the paper towel-lined plate using a slotted spoon.
  11. Serve hot with spicy green chutney.
