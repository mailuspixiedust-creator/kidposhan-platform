Suji kachori

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 35 minServes 15VegetarianLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Suji kachori scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 300 grm or 4 small size boiled potatoes
  • 1 tsp green chilli paste
  • ½ tsp ginger paste
  • ¼ cup chopped capsicum
  • ¼ cup chopped carrot
  • ¼ cup boiled and slightly mashed green peas
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp black pepper powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp dry mango powder
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • ¼ tsp black salt
  • ½ tsp kasuri methi
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ½ cup soaked poha
  • ¼ cup coriander leaves
  • 1 cup fine rava
  • ½ cup curd
  • 1.5 cup water
  • Oil for frying

Method

Making aloo stuffing

  1. In a mixing bowl, add boiled potatoes and mash them completely.
  2. Then add green chilli paste, ginger paste, chopped capsicum, carrot, green peas, onion, salt, black pepper powder, red chilli powder, coriander powder, cumin powder, garam masala, dry mango powder, chaat masala, black salt, kasuri methi, turmeric powder, sugar, lemon juice, soaked poha and coriander leaves.
  3. Mix well.
  4. The stuffing is ready. Make a small size ball from it. keep it aside.

Common questions

Is Suji kachori healthy for kids?

Suji kachori scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

What age is Suji kachori best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
