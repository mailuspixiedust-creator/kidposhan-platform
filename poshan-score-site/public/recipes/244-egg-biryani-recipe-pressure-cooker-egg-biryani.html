  • 2 slit green chilli
  • 3/4 cup sliced onions

  • 1/2 cup tomato pulp
  • 2 tsp red chilli powder

  • 1 1/2 tbsp biryani masala powder
  • 1/4 cup dahi (curd)
  • 1/4 cup chopped mint leaves (phudina)
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped coriander (dhania)
  • 1/2 cup deep fried onions

Method

  • Heat 2 tbsp oil in a small broad non-stick pan.
  • Add 6 boiled eggs.
  • Add &frac14; tsp turmeric powder.
  • Add &frac14; tsp chili powder.
  • Add salt to taste.
  • Saut&eacute; on a medium flame for 2 to 3 minutes until golden brown and keep aside.
  • To make egg biryani, in a deep bowl, add 1 cup long grain basmati rice.
  • Add enough water and soak for 1 hour.
  • Drain well and keep aside.
  • Heat 2 tbsp oil in a pressure cooker.
  • Add 1 tbsp ghee.
  • Add 1 small cinnamon stick.
  • Add 1 cardamom.
  • Add 2 cloves.
  • Add 1 tsp cumin seeds.
  • Add 2 slit green chillies.
  • Saut&eacute; on a medium flame for 30 seconds.
  • Add &frac34; cup sliced onions.
  • Saut&eacute; on a medium flame for 2 to 3 minutes.
  • Add &frac12; tbsp ginger garlic paste.
  • Add &frac12; cup tomato pulp.
  • Add &frac14; tsp turmeric powder.
  • Add 2 tsp chili powder.
  • Add 1 tsp coriander cumin seed powder.
  • Add 1&frac12; tbsp biryani masala.
  • Mix well.
  • Saut&eacute; on a medium flame for 30 seconds.
  • Add &frac14; cup curd.
  • Add &frac14; cup chopped mint leaves (phudina).
  • Add &frac14; cup finely chopped coriander.
  • Mix well and cook on a medium flame for 2 minutes, while stirring occasionally.
  • Add the soaked rice.
  • Add the masala eggs.
  • Add &frac12; cup fried onions.
  • Add salt to taste.
  • Add 1&frac12; cups water and mix well.
  • Pressure cook for 3 whistles.
  • Allow the steam to escape then open the lid.
  • Serve egg biryani hot.
  • We suggest you to use basmati rice to make this recipe for the best results.
  • Instead of curd you can also add 1 tbsp of lemon juice.
  • Do not open the lid of the pressure cooker until the steam is escaped naturally.
