Dal Makhani

Recipe by shwetainthekitchen.com

Photo: shwetainthekitchen.com
⏱ 9 h 30 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup whole black lentils (sabut urad dal or black gram)
  • ¼ cup red kidney beans (rajma)
  • 2 tbsp butter (+ dollop more for finishing, optional, . Use oil or vegan butter to make it vegan)
  • 1-2 black cardamoms
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 2 green cardamoms
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds (zeera)
  • ¼ tsp asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 medium yellow onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 inch ginger piece (grated or 1 tbsp minced)
  • 6 garlic cloves (minced or 1 tbsp minced)
  • 1-2 green chillies (finely chopped optional, adjust to taste)
  • 1 cup tomato puree (store bought) (or, use 2-3 tomatoes pureed approx 1 cup pureed + 1 tbsp tomato paste)
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ¼ - ½ tsp garam masala
  • pinch black pepper (adjust to taste)
  • 2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tbsp kasuri methi (dry fenugreek leaves)
  • ¼ cup cream (skip for making it vegan or add cashew cream)
  • 1 smalll piece of charcoal
  • 1 tsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tbsp butter (or as needed)
  • 1-2 tbsp cream (or as needed)
  • few ginger slices (thin julienne, optional)
  • 2-4 tbsp cilantro leaves (optional)

Method

To make Dal Makhani in traditional Pressure Cooker

  1. Drain the water off the soaked dal and add it to pressure cooker with 4 cups water, whole spices bay leaf, black and green cardamom cardamom, asafoetida and 1 tsp salt.
  2. Pressure cook for 4-5 whistles on high and then turn the heat to low and cook for another 30 mins.
  3. Switch off the heat and allow the steam to escape completely before opening the lid.
  4. Now in a pan melt the butter (or oil), to this add the cumin seeds.
  5. Once they splutter add the onions and cook until golden brown.
  6. Then add ginger garlic green chillies and cook until the raw garlic smell goes away.
  7. Stir in the tomato puree and cook till the water evaporates.
  8. At this point add in spices - salt, pepper, red chilli powder, coriander powder and garam masala.
  9. Mix well and cook till the masala starts oozing oil from sides.
  10. Add this tempering to the cooked dal and mix.
  11. Crush the kasuri methi (dry fenugreek leaves) between your palms and add to the dal.
  12. On low flame simmer the dal for 30mins -2 hours.
  13. Longer the better.
  14. Mash the dal with back of spoon for a few times and keep stirring intermittently and scraping the sides.
  15. Add water 1/4 cup at a time to adjust consistency in between.
  16. Lastly add cream and dollop of butter and give it a good stir.
  17. Simmer for another 15-20 mins.
  18. Our delicious Dal Makhani is ready to be served.
  19. Serve it topped with some more butter, drizzle of cream, some cilantro and ginger slices (optional) with side of rice or roti/naan and some salad and chutney.
  20. Optional - Follow the steps for Dungar Method below to get that restaurant style dal Makhani with smoky flavor.
