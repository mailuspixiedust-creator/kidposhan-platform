Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoon Ghee
  • 10 Cashews
  • 12 Raisins

  • 2 tablespoon Milk (warm)
  • 10 strands Saffron (Kesar)
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1/2 cup Plain Yogurt (I used full-fat)
  • 1 teaspoon Garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1/2 tablespoon Ginger (paste)
  • 1/2 tablespoon Garlic (paste)
  • 1 teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)

  • 1/2 teaspoon Black Peppercorns

Common questions

Is Instant Pot Chicken Biryani healthy for kids?

Instant Pot Chicken Biryani scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Instant Pot Chicken Biryani best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 60, for adults. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
