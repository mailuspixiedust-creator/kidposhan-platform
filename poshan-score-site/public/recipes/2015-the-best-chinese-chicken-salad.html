The Best Chinese Chicken Salad

Recipe by feastingathome.com

Photo: feastingathome.com
⏱ 1 h 25 minServes 8Non-vegetarianLunchSchool lunchboxFrom 3 years

The Best Chinese Chicken Salad scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1- 1 ½ lb chicken breasts
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon sesame oil
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup sliced almonds
  • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
  • 1 tablespoon maple syrup
  • ¼ cup olive oil
  • 5 tablespoons rice wine vinegar
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce, or gluten-free liquid aminos
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon ginger
  • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
  • 1 tablespoon orange zest, more for garnish
  • 3 tablespoons orange juice
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper
  • pinch cayenne
  • 4-5 cups napa cabbage, shredded
  • 1 red bell pepper, thinly sliced
  • 1 cup carrots, matchsticks or ribbons
  • 1 cup cucumber, sliced, (sub or snow peas)
  • ½ – 1 cup chopped cilantro
  • 1/2 cup green onions, sliced
  • optional- microgreens (broccoli sprouts are nice!)

Method

  • Place chicken breasts in a bowl and toss with the soy sauce, sesame oil and salt, place on a parchment-lined sheet pan.
  • Bake until cooked through (165F), roughly 20-28 minutes, depending on size.
  • Let cool and shred with two forks, adjust salt to taste. ( You can do this up to 2 days ahead, storing in the fridge).
  • In a non-stick medium skillet, toast the almonds over medium heat until golden and lightly toasted, stirring often.
  • Add the sesame seeds and toast, stirring just until they begin to pop.
  • Pour in the maple syrup, stir for 30 seconds, and remove from the heat.
  • Fluff them up a bit, and let them completely cool.
  • They will harden as they cool. (You could do this up to 3 days ahead, storing at room temperature.)
  • Whisk all the ingredeints together in a small bowl. ( You can do this up to 3 days ahead and store it in a jar in the fridge.)
  • Place the cabbage, bell pepper, carrots, cucumber, cilantro, green onions, and chicken in a large bowl.
  • Stir the dressing, then add enough to coat, tossing as you go lightly.
  • You may not need it all, depending on how much cabbage you use.
  • Season to taste with salt and pepper.
