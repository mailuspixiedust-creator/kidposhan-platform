Palak Paratha (Spinach Paratha)

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 20 minServes 2VegetarianBreakfastDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Palak Paratha (Spinach Paratha) scores 90 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 2.5-3 cups palak (spinach) (tightly packed)
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 1 green chili
  • 1/2 cup water (for grinding)
  • 1 cup whole wheat (or multigrain flour)
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala
  • salt (to taste)
  • oil (or ghee for cooking)

Method

  • Add the washed spinach, ginger, and green chilies to a blender.
  • Puree them to a smooth paste with ½ cup of water.
  • This yields little more than ½ cup of spinach puree.
  • Add wheat flour, palak puree, garam masala, and salt in a deep bowl. Mix well.
  • Add very little water and make a smooth (but not sticky) dough. Smear little oil on the dough and let it rest for at-least 10-15 mins.
  • Divide the dough into balls. I was able to get six balls.
  • Roll the parathas to 3-inch diameter using a chapati roller.
  • Cook it on both sides on a hot tawa and spread some ghee on top.
  • Serve palak ke parathe with curd, pickle, raita, ketchup, or any gravy of your choice.
