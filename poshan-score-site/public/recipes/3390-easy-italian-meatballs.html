Easy Italian Meatballs

Ingredients

  • 1/4 cup walnuts lightly toasted and ground (or use a 1/2 cup bread crumbs. See notes.)
  • 1/4 cup fresh parsley, finely chopped
  • 2-3 teaspoons mixed fresh rosemary, thyme, and oregano, minced (or 1 teaspoon dried)
  • 3/4 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 2 large cloves fresh garlic, minced or pressed
  • 1 egg
  • 1 teaspoon dijon
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 batch of Fast & Easy Marinara or Simple Oven Roasted Tomato Sauce (or sub 1 jar of store-bought Marinara Sauce)

Method

  • Prepare the sauce- Fast & Easy Marinara or Simple Oven Roasted Tomato Sauce.
  • In a bowl mix together walnuts, parsley, rosemary, thyme, salt, pepper, garlic, egg, and dijon.
  • Gently mix in ground beef until just combined. Do not over mix, this can toughen the meat.
  • Roll into balls, a generous 1 oz meatball -yields about 13-16.
  • Pan sear in a preheated skillet over medium-high heat, with just a light coating of oil, until most sides are browned.
  • The goal is just to brown the outside of the meatballs they will finish cooking as they simmer in the sauce.
  • Place the browned meatballs in the sauce and simmer for about 5 minutes.
  • An instant-read thermometer is helpful here.
  • Internal temperature should be 160 to assure they are done but not overcooked.

    Common questions

    What age is Easy Italian Meatballs best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 7–9 years and 10–12 years and 13–15 years, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
