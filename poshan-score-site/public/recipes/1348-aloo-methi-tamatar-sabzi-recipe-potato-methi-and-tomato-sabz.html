Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 large Potatoes (peeled and cubed)
  • Water (as needed)
  • 1 tsp Salt (to taste)
  • 2 tbsp Peanut Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 2 large Tomatoes (chopped finely)
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Roasted Cumin Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala powder
  • 2 tbsp Kasuri Methi Leaves (Dried Fenugreek Leaves)
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup Water

Method

  • Start by cooking the potatoes. Take potatoes in a pan, cover with water, add salt and cook till potatoes are cooked. Strain through a colander and set aside.
  • Heat oil in a pan, crackle cumin seeds. Add tomatoes and saute that for 2 to 3 minutes until tomatoes start to wilt a little.
  • Now Add in 1 cup water, cumin, chilli, salt and turmeric powder and mix well.
  • Cook till oil separates.
  • It will take around 4 to 5 minutes.
  • The oil will separate from the tomato mixture.
  • Now add in the boiled potatoes and mix through the tomatoes.
  • Season with more salt if required.
  • Finally add in kasuri methi leaves and garam masala powder and mix well.
  • Serve with roti or rice.
