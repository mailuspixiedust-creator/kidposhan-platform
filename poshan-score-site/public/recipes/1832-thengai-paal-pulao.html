Is Thengai Paal Pulao healthy for kids?

Thengai Paal Pulao scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, hfss status.

What age is Thengai Paal Pulao best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 60, for 13–15 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
