Kothmir marcha vada

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 20 minServes 4VegetarianLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Kothmir marcha vada scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice flour
  • Pinch of hing
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ cup chopped green chillies
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 cup curd
  • 1 cup water
  • Oil for deep frying

Method

  • In a pan, add 1 cup rice flour, pinch of hing, 1 tsp cumin seeds, salt to taste, ¼ cup chopped green chilli, 1 tsp ginger paste and ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves.
  • Then add 1 cup curd and 1 cup water in it. Mix well and make a free flowing and lump free batter.
  • Now place pan on gas and start cooking the batter.
  • Stir continuously and Cook batter until it reaches the consistency of dough.
  • Take out dough in bowl and slightly cool down.
  • Apply some oil on your palms, take lemon sized dough and roll it. Flatten it softly and make a hole in its centre using your fingers.
  • Now heat oil in a pan, once oil is heated add vada for frying to it on medium flame.
  • Flip and fry vada from both sides until it becomes golden brown.
  • Drain out the fried vadas and fry rest of vada likewise.
  • Serve hot vada with chutney, tomato sauce and slices of onion.
