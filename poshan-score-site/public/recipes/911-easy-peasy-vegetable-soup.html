Easy Peasy Vegetable Soup

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 onion (finely diced)
  • 1 leek (sliced thinly)
  • 3 carrots (sliced, no need to peel!)
  • 2 sticks celery (sliced)
  • 1 small parsnip (peeled and sliced)
  • 1 medium potato (peeled and chopped into 1cm / ½inch cubes)
  • 500 ml vegetable stock (made with a cube is fine, I use 1 Kallo Organic vegetable stock cube)
  • Salt and pepper (to taste)

Method

  • Place the olive oil, onion and leek in a wide, deep saucepan. Cook on a low heat with the lid on for 5 minutes until softened. Stir occasionally.
  • Add the prepared carrot, celery, parsnip and potato, and stir.
  • Add all the stock, plus some salt and pepper to taste.
  • Bring to the boil and then simmer on a low heat, with the lid on, for 15 minutes - or until all the vegetables are completely soft.
  • Use a hand blender to blitz the soup to a smooth puree and then add a little extra boiling water from the kettle if you think the soup is too thick.
  • Taste the soup and add more salt and or pepper if you think it needs it.
  • Ladle into bowls and serve with some crusty soda bread and a glass of chilled white wine.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
