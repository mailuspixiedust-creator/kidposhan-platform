Zucchini Chips with Tzatziki

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup plain Greek yogurt
  • ⅓ cup shredded English cucumber
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh dill
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 ½ teaspoons lemon juice
  • ½ teaspoon pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon table salt
  • 2 (8-ounce) zucchini, sliced ⅛ inch thick
  • 2 teaspoons table salt
  • 2 quarts vegetable oil for frying
  • 1 ¼ cups water
  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup cornstarch
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder

Method

FOR THE TZATZIKI:

  1. Stir all ingredients together in bowl.
  2. Cover and refrigerate until ready to serve. (Tzatziki can be refrigerated for up to 4 days; stir to recombine and season with salt and pepper to taste before serving.)

FOR THE ZUCCHINI CHIPS:

  1. Toss zucchini with salt in large bowl to combine. Transfer to colander and set colander over now-empty bowl. Let sit for 30 minutes.
  2. Spread zucchini over clean dish towel. Cover with second clean dish towel and press to remove as much moisture as possible. Sprinkle with pepper.
  3. Add oil to Dutch oven and heat over medium-high heat to 375 degrees.
  4. Line large platter with triple layer of paper towels.
  5. Set wire rack in rimmed baking sheet.
  6. Whisk water, flour, cornstarch, and baking powder in large bowl until smooth.
  7. Add one-third of zucchini to batter.
  8. Using rubber spatula, stir to evenly coat.
  9. Using both your hands and working quickly, transfer batch of coated zucchini to hot oil 2 pieces at a time.
  10. Fry until deep golden brown, stirring occasionally with spider skimmer or slotted spoon, 6 to 8 minutes.
  11. Adjust burner, if necessary, to maintain oil temperature between 350 and 375 degrees.
  12. Transfer zucchini to prepared platter and let drain for about 1 minute.
  13. Transfer drained zucchini to wire rack.
  14. Return oil to 375 degrees and repeat with remaining zucchini in 2 batches.
  15. Serve warm or at room temperature, with tzatziki.
