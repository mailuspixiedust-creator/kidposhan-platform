Mini matar kachori

Mini matar kachori scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Method

For Outer Layer Dough

  1. In a mixing bowl, add 1.5 cups of wheat flour, ½ cup coarse wheat flour, salt to taste, ½ tsp ajwain, and 4 tbsp ghee. Mix well.
  2. Gradually add water and knead into a tight, smooth dough.
  3. Cover and let the dough rest for 15 minutes.

Common questions

Is Mini matar kachori healthy for kids?

Mini matar kachori scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 6 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, fibre, protein, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Mini matar kachori best for?

It scores highest, 60 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
