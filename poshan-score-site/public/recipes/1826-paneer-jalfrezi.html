Paneer Jalfrezi

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds (zeera)
  • ¼ teaspoon asafoetida (hing)
  • ½ tablespoon ginger (grated or minced)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (minced)
  • 1 red onion (cut in julienne or cubes)
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste (or ¼ cup of tomato puree or canned tomato sauce.)
  • 1 tablespoon tomato ketchup (or Hot and Sweet sauce, for spicy)
  • 320 grams paneer (fresh or frozen, cut in strips or cubes)
  • 15oz can baby corn (or 10-12 fresh baby corns)
  • 2 bell peppers (cut in julienne or cubes, I've used 1/2 each pepper, red, green, yellow and orange)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice (adjust to taste)
  • 2-3 tablespoon cilantro (finely chopped)
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon kashmiri red chilli powder (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon ground cumin
  • ½ tablespoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)

Method

To Make Paneer Jalfrezi

  1. Heat oil in a pan on medium high heat. Add the cumin seeds and asafoetida (hing), and let the seeds sizzle.
  2. Now add ginger, garlic, green chili (optional) followed by onions. Saute for 2-3 mins on medium heat until soft.
  3. Stir in the bell peppers. Stir fry until the bell peppers are tender yet crunchy.
  4. Add the tomato paste and spices (turmeric, red chili, cumin powder, coriander powder, and salt). Mix well.
  5. Add the baby corn, cover, and cook until baby corn is done (approximately 5 mins).
  6. Note: If you are using fresh baby corn (it may take longer to cook in which case add them with the bell peppers.)
  7. Finally, add the ketchup and paneer and toss gently so that the paneer gets coated evenly. Cover and cook for about a 1-2 minutes.
  8. Last but not least, add the lemon juice, cilantro leaves and mix well. Easy Vegetable Paneer Jalfrezi is ready! Serve with Naan, roti, paratha or rice.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
