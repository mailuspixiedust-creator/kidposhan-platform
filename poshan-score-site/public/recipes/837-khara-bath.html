Khara Bath

Khara Bath scores 50 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Sooji
  • 3 cup Water
  • 1 Carrot
  • 1 Capsicum
  • 2 Tomato
  • 1 Cauliflower
  • 1/4 cup Oil
  • 2 Onions
  • Salt
  • 3 Green Chillies
  • 2 Red Chillies
  • Cashews
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tsp Ginger garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Urad Dal
  • 1 pinch Asafoetida
  • Coriander Leaves

Method

  • Heat oil in a heavy bottom pan. Add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, and hing into it.
  • After they start spluttering, add chopped onions and green chillies. Let them fry for some time.
  • Then add ginger and garlic paste and saute it for 1 minute. And then add all the vegetables except tomatoes.
  • Fry the vegetables until half did. Add tomatoes and fry till the oil separates from it.
  • Now add water and let them boil. Then add salt and suji into the water and keep stirring in medium flame.
  • Finally add coriander leaves, cashew nuts and serve hot.
  • Serve with coconut chutney.

    Common questions

    Is Khara Bath healthy for kids?

    Khara Bath scores 50 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 5 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, fibre, protein.

    What age is Khara Bath best for?

    It scores highest, 60 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 50, for 4–6 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
