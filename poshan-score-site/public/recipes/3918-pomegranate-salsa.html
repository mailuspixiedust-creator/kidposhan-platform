Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 cups Pomegranate Seeds (arils)
  • 1/4 cup Cilantro (coriander leaves, chopped)
  • 1/4 cup Red Onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 Jalapeño (Green chile, chopped)
  • 1/2 tsp Lemon or Lime Zest
  • 2 tbsp Lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
  • Salt (pinch, optional)

Method

  • In a bowl, add pomegranate seeds (arils), cilantro, red onion, jalapeño, lemon zest, and juice, and mix well.
  • Taste and add salt only if needed. Let this sit for 10 minutes so that the flavors blend.
  • Serve this salsa as is or with pita chips, tortillas, crostinis, or with Thanksgiving turkey or a whole roasted chicken.
