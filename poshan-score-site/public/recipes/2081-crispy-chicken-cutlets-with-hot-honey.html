Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup honey (preferably raw and organic)
  • 1 jalapeno (sliced into thin rounds)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp crushed red pepper
  • 1 tsp red wine vinegar
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 1 cup mini sweet peppers (sliced into thin rounds)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/4 salt (I always use pink Himalayan)
  • 2 chicken breasts (each one split to make 4 total chicken breast cutlets)
  • 1/2 cup olive oil (or other oil of choice)
  • 1 cup all purpose flour
  • 1 cup panko breadcrumbs
  • 1/3 cup parmesan cheese (finely grated)
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 tsp salt (plus more for seasoning)
  • pepper
  • 1/4 cup chives (minced)

Method

Hot Honey

  1. Combine honey, jalapeno and crushed red pepper in a small pot.
  2. Place the pot over medium heat.
  3. As soon as the pot comes to a gentle simmer, remove the pot from the heat and allow the chilis to infuse the honey for 15 minutes.
  4. Strain the honey mixture through a fine mesh sieve.
  5. Set aside until chicken is complete.
  6. Leftover honey can be stored in a container in the refrigerator for one week.

What age is Crispy Chicken Cutlets with Hot Honey best for?

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
