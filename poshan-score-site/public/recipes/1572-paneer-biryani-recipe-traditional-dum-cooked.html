Method

Dum cooking paneer biryani

  1. Cover with a aluminium foil or with a moist kitchen cotton towel or napkin.
  2. Place the lid tightly on the pan and place it on a tava or griddle. For the first 5 minutes dum cook the biryani on a medium heat.
  3. Then lower the flame and cook the biryani.
  4. On dum for 12 to 15 minutes.
  5. You can also cook the biryani in a preheated oven for 20 to 25 minutes at 180 degrees C (350 degrees F).
  6. Use an oven proof pot or pan.
  7. Serve the Paneer Biryani hot with boondi raita or cucumber raita or onion raita or kuchumber salad.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
