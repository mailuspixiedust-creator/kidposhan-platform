Nylon Khaman Dhokla

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Photo: Tarla Dalal
⏱ 20 minServes 4 servingsJainBreakfastLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Nylon Khaman Dhokla scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cups besan (gram flour)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp rava (suji)
  • 4 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • 1 tsp fruit salt
  • 3 tsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai / sarson)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (til)
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 2 to 3 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 1 tsp chopped green chillies
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander (dhania)

Method

  • To make Nylon Khaman Dhokla batter, first take 1 1/2 cups besan (Bengal gram flour) in a large mixing bowl. This will come out to approximately 250 gms.
  • Now add the 1 1/2 tbsp semolina (rava / sooji).
  • Now add the 4 tsp sugar to the bowl.
  • There are two ways to make khaman dhokla ? the first one is adding the sugar to the batter and the second one is adding sugar to the tempering which is poured over the dhokla.
  • If you don?t want to add sugar to the batter, you can add it to the oil-water tempering, dissolve it and then pour it over the dhokla.
  • Now add the 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste.
  • Now add the 1 tsp lemon juice and salt to taste.
  • As this is a sweet and slightly sour dhokla, we add lemon juice.
  • Alternately you can add citric acid (nimbu ka phool).
  • The lemon juice (or citric acid) and fruit salt create a fizzy reaction which results in soft, spongy khaman.
  • Now add approx. ¾ cup of water to the bowl.
  • Mix well using a whisk to get a smooth Nylon Khaman Dhokla batter.
  • The batter will have to mixed very well as the sugar needs to dissolve properly.
  • If you want you can keep the batter aside for 10-15 minutes till the sugar has properly dissolved.
  • Grease 175 mm. (7") diameter thali with a little oil.
  • This is an important step because greasing helps the dhokla come out of the plate without breaking or sticking.
  • Just before steaming, add the 1 tsp fruit salt. This will react with the lemon juice to give a fluffy batter.
  • Pour 1 tsp water over the fruit salt to activate it.
  • Gently mix the fruit salt in the batter till just mixed. Do not over mix the batter, it should be airy and fluffy like this!
  • Pour the Nylon Khaman Dhokla mixture immediately to the thali.
  • Spread the nylon khamman dhokla batter evenly by rotating the thali clockwise.
  • Remove the Nylon Khaman Dhokla from the steamer.
  • Heat the oil in a small non-stick pan and add the 1 tsp mustard seeds ( rai / sarson).
  • When the seeds crackle, add the 1 tsp sesame seeds (til) to the pan.
  • Then add the a pinch of asafoetida (hing).
  • Add 2 to 3 curry leaves (kadi patta).
  • Now add the 1 tsp chopped green chillies. Sauté on a medium flame for 30 seconds
  • Remove from the flame, add ½ cup of water and mix well.
  • Pour the tempering over the prepared dhoklas and spread it evenly.
  • Make sure the dhoklas are warm when you pour the tempering or else they will not soak the water in.
  • Let the dhoklas sit for 10-15 minutes till soft and spongy before serving.
  • Cut into even pieces.
  • Garnish with 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander (dhania).
  • Insert a toothpick or knife in the centre. If the toothpick comes out clean, the Nylon khaman dhokla are cooked. If not, it needs more time. Keep aside.
  • Serve the soft and spongy Gujarati nylon khamman dhokla with green chutney.
