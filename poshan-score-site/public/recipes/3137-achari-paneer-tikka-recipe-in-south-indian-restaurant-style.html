Achari Paneer Tikka Recipe In South Indian Restaurant Style

Ingredients

  • 2 tsp Hung Curd
  • Salt
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tsp Garam Masala
  • 1/2 tsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Pickle Gravy
  • 1 tsp Gram Flour
  • 1 cup Mushroom
  • 6 Paneer Cubes
  • 1/4 cup Bell Pepper

Method

  • For pickle gravy, grind a small amount of any pickle in a mixer with very little water. This is used to give achar (pickle) flavor to the dish.
  • In a mixing bowl take hung curd, salt, chilli powder, garam masala, ginger-garlic paste, pickle gravy and besan.
  • Mix all these ingredients well without adding water.
  • Add mushrooms and mix so that they are well coated with the paste.
  • Take the mushrooms on a plate.
  • Add paneer cubes to the same paste and mix again.
  • Take them separately on a plate.
  • Leave it for 15 minutes.
  • Heat a tava and add 1 tsp oil and spread it. Place mushrooms and paneer on the tava. Fry over a low flame till golden and reverse. Fry and take them on a plate.
  • Now mix the bell peppers with the leftover paste and fry them on the tava for few minutes on both sides.
  • Take a toothpick and prick one diced bell pepper, one paneer cube and one mushroom. Repeat the same for all pieces. Serve hot.
