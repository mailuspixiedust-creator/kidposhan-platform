Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 cup (150 grams) sabudana (- sago or tapioca pearls)
  • water (as required, to soak sabudana)
  • 2 potatoes (- medium-sized)
  • ½ cup peanuts (- roasted)
  • 8 to 10 curry leaves (- optional)
  • 1 teaspoon grated ginger (- optional)
  • ½ to 1 teaspoon or 1 green chili (- chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • ¼ cup coconut (- grated, fresh - optional)
  • ½ to 1 teaspoon sugar (or as required)
  • ½ to 1 teaspoon lemon juice (optional, or as required)
  • 3 tablespoons peanut oil (or ghee)
  • rock salt (- edible and food grade, sendha namak, as required)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons coriander leaves (- chopped, optional)

Method

Preparation

  1. Rinse sabudana very well in water. Then soak sabudana overnight or for 3 to 5 hours.
  2. After the sabudana has soaked well enough, check if they have become soft or not.
  3. To do this take a few pearls and press them with your fingers.
  4. They should get mashed easily.
  5. If you feel some hardness in the center of the sabudana pearls, then add a few tablespoons of water in the bowl.
  6. Cover and leave to soak for 30 minutes more.
  7. Drain the soaked sabudana very well of all the water and set aside.
  8. Boil the potatoes and when warm peel and chop them.
  9. In a pan, dry roast the peanuts till browned and when cooled make a coarse powder in a mortar-pestle or in a dry grinder.
  10. Mix the coarsely powdered peanuts, salt and sugar with the drained sabudana.

Is Sabudana Khichdi healthy for kids?

What age is Sabudana Khichdi best for?

It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
