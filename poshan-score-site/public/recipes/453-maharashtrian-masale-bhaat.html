Maharashtrian Masale Bhaat

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 20 minServes 3VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 5 years

Maharashtrian Masale Bhaat scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons oil (or ghee)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 stalk curry leaves
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (grated)
  • 1 green chili (thinly sliced)
  • 1 pinch hing
  • 1 small onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 cups mixed veggies (carrot, potato, green peas)
  • 2-2.5 teaspoons goda masala (I like Kpra brand)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon ghee
  • 8-10 roasted cashews
  • 2 tablespoons fresh coconut (shredded)
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves

Method

Stovetop Pressure Cooker

  1. Follow the same steps as the Instant Pot, close the pressure cooker lid, and place a whistle.
  2. Cook for two whistles on medium flame.
  3. All other steps remain the same.
