Method

Sweet potato halwa

  1. Boil the sweet potato in a steamer. When it cooldown peels and mash using a potato masher.
  2. Heat the ghee in a pan on medium heat. Once hot add dry fruit and roast it slightly. Keep it aside.
  3. Add mashed sweet potatoes. Mix and cook with stirring constantly until it becomes thick and leaves the sides of the pan.
  4. Then add kesar milk and sugar. Mix, as sugar melts, it becomes loose and runny again. Cook until it becomes thick again.
  5. Add cardamom powder and roasted dry fruits. Mix well.
  6. Sweet potato halwa is ready.

What age is Mahashivratri special thali best for?

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
