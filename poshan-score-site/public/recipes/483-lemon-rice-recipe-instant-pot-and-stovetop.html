Lemon Rice Recipe - Instant Pot & Stovetop

Ingredients

  • 2-3 tablespoons oil (or ghee)
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 teaspoon split urad dal
  • 1/2 tablespoon split chana dal
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (minced)
  • 1-2 dry red chili (optional)
  • 2 green chili (finely chopped)
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing) (skip for gluten-free)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder (haldi)
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1.5 cups water
  • salt (to taste)
  • juice of one lemon
  • 2 tablespoon roasted peanuts
  • 2 tablespoon roasted cashews (optional)
  • 2 tablespoon coriander leaves (cilantro) (finely chopped)

Method

Stovetop Method

  1. Rinse the rice and cook the Basmati Rice Instant Pot Electric Pressure or stovetop pressure cooker.
  2. Once cooked, spread it out on a wide plate and let it cool completely, this step helps to achieve separate and non-mushy rice.
  3. In a Kadai, Heat oil. Add in the raw peanuts and fry them until they turn slightly golden brown. Take them out and keep them aside.
  4. In the same Kadai, add mustard seeds and let them crackle. Then add chana dal, and urad dal. Sauté for about a minute until they are light brown.
  5. Then add ginger, green chilies, red chili, and curry leaves, and saute for a few seconds.
  6. Turn off the gas and then add turmeric powder and asafoetida. Stir quickly.
  7. Add the cooked rice, roasted peanuts, lemon juice and salt. Mix well.
  8. Sprinkle coriander leaves and serve immediately.
