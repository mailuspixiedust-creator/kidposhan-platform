Marry Me Chicken

Recipe by Greedy Gourmet

Photo: Greedy Gourmet
⏱ 30 minServes 6Non-vegetarianDinnerFrom 2 years

Marry Me Chicken scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 6 boneless, skinless chicken breasts, sliced into thin cutlets
  • ½ teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 6 tablespoons all-purpose flour (or gluten-free flour)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 3 cloves fresh garlic, minced
  • 1 cup chicken stock
  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • ½ teaspoon dried oregano
  • ½ teaspoon dried thyme
  • 1 teaspoon crushed red pepper flakes (optional)
  • 1 cup oil-packed sundried tomatoes, drained and chopped
  • Fresh basil leaves, chopped, for garnish

Method

  • Season the chicken breasts evenly with kosher salt and black pepper. Lightly coat each piece in flour, shaking off any excess.
  • Heat the olive oil and butter together in a large skillet over medium heat.
  • Once the butter is melted and foamy, add the chicken in a single layer.
  • Cook for 4–5 minutes per side, until golden brown and cooked through.
  • Transfer the chicken to a plate and set aside.
  • Reduce to a medium low heat. Add the garlic to the same skillet and cook for about 30–60 seconds, stirring constantly, until fragrant.
  • Pour in the chicken broth and scrape up any browned bits from the bottom of the pan. Stir in the heavy cream and bring the sauce to a gentle simmer.
  • Add the Parmesan cheese, oregano, thyme, and crushed red pepper flakes (if using). Stir until the cheese melts and the sauce begins to thicken.
  • Stir in the sun-dried tomatoes, then return the chicken to the skillet.
  • Spoon the cream sauce over the chicken and let everything simmer together for 3–5 minutes, until the sauce has thickened slightly, reaching the desired consistency, with the chicken heated through.
  • Remove from the heat and sprinkle with fresh basil before serving.

    Common questions

    Is Marry Me Chicken healthy for kids?

    Marry Me Chicken scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 6 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, fibre, hfss status.

    What age is Marry Me Chicken best for?

    It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 60, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
