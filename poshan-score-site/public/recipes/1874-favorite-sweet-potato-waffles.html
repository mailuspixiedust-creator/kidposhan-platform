Favorite Sweet Potato Waffles

Ingredients

  • 1 cup sweet potato puree
  • 2 large eggs
  • ½ cup milk
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter (melted and slightly cooled)
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1 cup whole wheat flour
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • ½ teaspoon salt

Method

  • Stir together the sweet potato puree, eggs, milk, butter, and vanilla in a medium bowl.
  • Add the flour, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt. Stir gently to make a thick batter.
  • Warm the waffle maker and coat with nonstick spray.
  • Use about ¼ cup batter per waffle, cooking them according to the instructions on your waffle maker. (I cook them on the second to highest heat setting, or a 4 out of 5.)
  • Continue to prepare the rest of the batter, keeping the finished waffles warm in a 275 degree oven if desired.
  • Serve warm with fruit, maple syrup, or another favorite topping.
