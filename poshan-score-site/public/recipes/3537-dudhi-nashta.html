Method

Making lauki nashta

  1. In a mixture jar, add lauki pieces, lemon juice, ginger and coriander leaves. grind into a smooth paste.
  2. Now in a mixing bowl, add semolina, besan, curd, and lauki paste. Mix well. Add the required water and make a thick batter.
  3. rest for 10 minutes or until the rava absorbs water well.
  4. Meanwhile in a pan, add oil, mustard seeds, hing, chana dal, and urad dal. Sauté for a minute.
  5. Then add chopped green chilli and onion. Sauté till onion become transparent.
  6. Now add the tempering to the batter, also add red chili flakes, garam masala, cumin powder, salt, and coriander leaves. mix well.
  7. Add the required water and make a thick but flowing consistency of the batter.
  8. Heat oil in a pan, add batter, and spread it evenly.
  9. Cover and cook
  10. cover and simmer for 5 minutes or until the base gets cooked well.
  11. now flip over and cook base for 3-4 minutes.
  12. Enjoy lauki ka nashta with peanut chutney

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
