Dhal Curry

Recipe by Nyonya Cooking

Photo: Nyonya Cooking
⏱ 1 hServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 1 years

Dhal Curry scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 yellow onion(s)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 2 shallot(s)
  • 1 tsp ginger
  • 1 tomato(es)
  • 1 garlic clove(s)
  • 2 curry leaves stalk(s)
  • 0.5 tbsp turmeric powder
  • 1 carrot(s)
  • 1 potatoes
  • 1 aubergine (Indian)
  • 250 g chana dhal
  • 1.5 l water
  • 2 chilli peppers (dried)
  • 0.5 tbsp salt

Method

  • Heat oil over medium heat and sauté sliced onions until golden brown. Remove onions from pan and leave aside.
  • Using the same pan, fry chopped shallots, minced garlic, minced ginger and curry leaves until fragrant.
  • Add tomato slices.
  • Cover pan with the lid to allow tomatoes to soften.
  • Chop carrots, potatoes, aubergine and carrots into bite-size pieces.
  • Add them into the pot along with turmeric powder, dried chillies and dhal.
  • Add water and cook over high heat.
  • Once it boils, lower heat to the minimum and simmer for about 30 minutes or until dhal softens.
  • Keep stirring from time to time to prevent curry from being burnt.
  • Add salt to taste.
  • Finally, stir in fried onions.
