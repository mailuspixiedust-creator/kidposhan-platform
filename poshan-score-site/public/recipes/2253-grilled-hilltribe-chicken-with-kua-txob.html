Method

FOR THE CHICKEN:

  1. Place chicken breast side down on cutting board.
  2. Using kitchen shears, cut along length of 1 side of backbone.
  3. Flip chicken breast side up and open chicken on board.
  4. Using palm of your hand, press firmly on center of breast to flatten chicken.
  5. Tuck wingtips under breasts.
  6. Combine salt, granulated garlic, pepper, and paprika in bowl. Season chicken all over with spice mixture.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
