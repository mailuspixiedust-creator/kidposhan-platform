Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons sesame or any neutral cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped ginger
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped garlic
  • 2 tablespoons red thai curry paste
  • 2 tablespoons peanut butter
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon sugar
  • 2 cups water
  • 4 -5 bok choy leaves (cut into ½ inch pieces)
  • 10-12 edamame pods
  • 1 teaspoon light soy sauce
  • 200 ml coconut milk
  • 6 frozen chicken momos / dumplings
  • 4 teaspoons chilli oil

Method

  • Heat oil in a pan, add curry paste and saute for a few seconds, add ginger, garlic and saute for a few seconds till fragrant.
  • Add peanut butter, salt, sugar, water and mix well to incorporate all the ingredients.
  • Bring to a boil and cook for 2-3 minutes till the water absorbs the flavours.
  • Add bok choy, edamame pods, soy sauce, mix well and cook covered for 2-3 minutes.
  • Add the dumplings and cook for 2-3 minutes - they’ll continue cooking even when you turn off the heat.
  • Do not over cook at this stage as dumplings tend to disintegrate.
  • Add coconut milk, mix and take it off the heat.
  • Add dumpling soup to a bowl, drizzle with chilli oil and serve
