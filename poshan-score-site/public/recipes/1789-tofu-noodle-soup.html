Tofu Noodle Soup

Recipe by shwetainthekitchen.com

Photo: shwetainthekitchen.com
⏱ 30 minServes 4EggDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Ingredients

  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • ½ tablespoon ginger (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (minced)
  • 1 small yellow onion (chopped)
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 2 carrots (peeled and sliced, approximately 1 cup)
  • 2 celery stalks (chopped, approximately 1 cup)
  • ½ red bell pepper (chopped, approximately ½ cup)
  • 2 tablespoon green peas (using frozen)
  • 2 tablespoon corn kernels (using frozen)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • ½ teaspoon dry oregano
  • ½ teaspoon thyme leaves (dry)
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes (optional, adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper (freshly ground, adjust to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 2 cups egg noodles (or pasta of choice)
  • 5 cups vegetable broth (or water)
  • 7 ounces Tofu (extra firm)
  • 1-2 tablespoon cilantro (chopped)
  • 1-2 tablespoon lemon juice (or to taste)
  • hot sauce of choice for serving (optional, to taste)

Method

Stove Top Method:

  1. Heat a dutch oven or a soup pot with heavy base and once hot add oil followed by ginger garlic.
  2. Give it a quick stir and add the onions.
  3. Cook till the onions are slightly translucent.
  4. Add turmeric and give it a mix till all is well combined.
  5. Now add all the veggies (carrot, celery, green peas, corn and red bell pepper), tomato sauce, bay leaf, spices - salt, pepper, organo, thyme.
  6. Mix, cover and cook for 5 mins or until the veggies are half done.
  7. They will finish cooking with the noodles so do not overcook.
  8. Add vegetable stock or water and bring it to a boil.
  9. Add the noodles, cover and cook until they are done or al dente. Add more liquid if you think its reduced.
  10. Finally, add the tofu pieces during the last few minutes of the cooking.
  11. Finish off with some lemon juice and chopped cilantro.
  12. Give it a taste and adjust the seasoning if needed.
  13. Tofu Noodle Soup is ready to be served!!
