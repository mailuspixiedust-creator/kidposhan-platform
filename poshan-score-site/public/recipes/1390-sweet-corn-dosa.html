Sweet Corn Dosa

Recipe by SouthIndianRecipes.in

Photo: SouthIndianRecipes.in
⏱ 25 minServes 4VegetarianBreakfastDinnerLunch4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Sweet Corn Dosa scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Sweet Corn Kernels
  • 1/4 cup Split Moong Dal
  • 2 Green Chillies
  • 1 tsp Ginger Paste
  • Salt
  • 1/4 tsp Black Pepper
  • 3 tbsp Rice Flour
  • 3-4 tbsp Water
  • 2 tbsp Cooking Oil

Method

  • Seperate the corn seeds from the cob .
  • Grind corn ,chilli ,jeera and salt along with rice.
  • Grind them to a nice smooth batter. Take the ground batter to a vessel.
  • Add water generously such that the batter attains running consistency ( like milk ).
  • Heat a tawa or nonstick pan. When the tawa is hot , rub it with a drop of oil using an onion piece.
  • To the hot tawa pour a ladle full of corn dosa batter in circular motion.
  • Start pouring the batter from the outside rim of tawa to inwards center of tawa.
  • As the batter is thin ,it will become lacy .( The method of pouring this dosa is similar to rava dosa) .Smear few drops oil around the dosa.
  • Lid the tawa and cook it on a medium flame. Cook till bottom side starts to turn golden brown. Once it becomes crisp ,just fold and serve . No need to flip .
  • Serve hot Corn dosa with coconut chutney / sambar / chutney powder.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
