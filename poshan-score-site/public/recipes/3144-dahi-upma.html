Method

Making Upma

  1. In a pan, add 2 tbsp ghee and 1 tbsp oil.
  2. Then add ½ tsp mustard seeds, ½ tsp cumin seeds, 1 tsp chana dal, and some curry leaves. Sauté it.
  3. Then add 2 tbsp peanuts and sauté till they become slightly golden brown.
  4. Now add 1 cup chopped onion and sauté till it becomes slightly transparent.
  5. Then add 2 tbsp green peas, 3 tbsp chopped carrot, and 3 tbsp chopped capsicum. Sauté it, cover, and cook the vegetables till they become slightly soft.
  6. Now add 1 cup rava and sauté it on low flame till the granules puff up. It takes 7–8 minutes to roast rava on low flame.
  7. Then add 1 small chopped tomato and mix well.
  8. Now in a mixing bowl, add 3 cups buttermilk, 2 chopped green chillies, ½ tsp grated ginger, and ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves. Mix well.
  9. Then gradually add the buttermilk mixture into the roasted rava and mix well.
  10. Now add ½ tsp turmeric powder, salt to taste, and 1 tsp sugar. Mix well.
  11. As the buttermilk gradually gets absorbed into the rava, add 1 tsp sambar masala and some coriander leaves. Mix well.
  12. Then add 1 tbsp ghee, cover the pan, and cook the upma on low flame for 3 minutes.
  13. Now the upma is ready. Add 1 tsp lemon juice and some coriander leaves. Mix well.
  14. Serve the upma with neer chutney.

What age is Dahi upma best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
