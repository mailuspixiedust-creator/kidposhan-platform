Chicken Curry

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • ½ kg (1.1 lbs.) chicken (preferably bone-in, boneless is okay)
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons oil
  • 1 cup (3 medium) onions (fine chopped)
  • 1 to 2 green chilies (slit, omit for less spicy, Thai or Indian chili peppers or serrano peppers)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste (or ¾ inch ginger, 3 cloves garlic minced)
  • ½ cup (2 medium) tomatoes (pureed or finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup yogurt (or 1½ tbsp cashew butter or 12 cashews powdered & pureed with ¼ cup water, refer notes)
  • ½ to ¾ teaspoon salt (adjust as needed)
  • ½ to 1 cup hot water (or light coconut milk)
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves (or mint leaves finely chopped)
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (or smoked paprika, ½ tsp for less spicy)
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala (½ tsp more if needed, adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 bay leaf (or 1 sprig curry leaves)
  • 4 cloves
  • 2 inch cinnamon piece
  • 3 green cardamom

Method

Preparation

  1. On a medium flame, heat oil in a deep pan. Add the whole spices - bay leaf, cinnamon, cloves & green cardamoms.
  2. Stir in the chopped onions and chilies. Saute until deep golden in color, for 7 to 8 mins.
  3. Add ginger garlic paste and saute till you get a nice aroma, for about a minute.
  4. Then add tomatoes, turmeric and salt. Mix well and cook until the tomatoes turn soft & mushy.
  5. Turn down the heat to low, Stir in the yogurt (or cashew paste), red chili powder, garam masala & coriander powder.
  6. Cook on low heat, until the mixture smells good and aromatic.
  7. Add chicken and coriander leaves. Fry on a medium heat till the chicken turns white or pale in color. This takes around 3 mins.
  8. Cover and cook on a low heat for 3 to 4 minutes. This step is very important as the chicken absorbs all the flavors of the spices.
  9. Meanwhile in a separate pot, heat up 1 cup water. Using cold water most times toughens the chicken so it is good to use hot water.

Common questions

Is Chicken Curry healthy for kids?

Chicken Curry scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Chicken Curry best for?

It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 70, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
