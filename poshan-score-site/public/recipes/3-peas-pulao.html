Peas Pulao

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi
⏱ 40 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Peas Pulao scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 inch Cinnamon
  • 3 no Cardamom
  • 2 no Cloves
  • 1 large Onion (sliced thinly)
  • 3 no Green Chillies (slit)
  • 2 tbsp Ginger Garlic paste
  • 1 cup Basmati Rice
  • 1 tsp Chilli Powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala Powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp Cashews
  • 2 tbsp Raisins
  • 1 cup Coconut Milk
  • ½ cup Water
  • 2 cup Green peas

Method

  • Heat oil and ghee in a pressure cooker. Add in whole spices and let it sizzle.
  • Add in onion, green chillies and saute till golden.
  • Add in ginger garlic paste and saute for a min.
  • Add in spice powders and salt.
  • Add in peas, cashews and kishmish and mix well.
  • Now add in rice, water, coconut milk.
  • Bring it to a boil. Cover it and simmer for 10 mins. Turn off the heat and Let the steam go all by itself. Fluff the rice and serve.

    What age is Peas Pulao best for?

    It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 70, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
