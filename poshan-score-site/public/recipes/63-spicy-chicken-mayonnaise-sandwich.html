Spicy Chicken Mayonnaise Sandwich

Ingredients

  • 500 grams Boneless chicken
  • 2 tsp Garlic Powder
  • 2 tsp Onion Powder
  • 2 tsp Red chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp Black pepper powder
  • 1 tsp Dried Parsley
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • Salt to taste
  • 8 Bread Slices
  • 1/2 cup Mayonnaise
  • 1/2 cup Cabbage (shredded)
  • 1 Carrot (grated)
  • 1 Onion (sliced)

Method

  • Take all the ingredients given for cooking chicken in a pressure cooker.
  • Add in 1/4 cup of water and pressure cook for 1 whistle.
  • Let the steam escape by itself.
  • Open the cooker and chop the meat into small pieces.
  • Set aside to cool.
  • Take chicken, mayo, cabbage, onions, carrots in a bowl. Mix well.
  • Take bread slice, stuff with the filling. Cover with other slice.
  • Wrap in plastic wrap and pop it into the fridge until serving.
  • Slice in half and enjoy.
