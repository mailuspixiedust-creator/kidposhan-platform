Perfect Basmati Rice - Instant Pot Pressure Cooker

Ingredients

  • 1 cup basmati rice (rinsed)
  • 1 1/4 cups water
  • 1/2 teaspoon oil (or ghee, optional)
  • salt (to taste, optional)

Method

Instant Pot Method

  1. Rinse the basmati rice 2-3 times until the water runs clear (removes extra starch) and drain the remaining water.Note: This is a no-soak method and my rice always turns out soft, fluffy, and separated.
  2. Add the rinsed rice, water, oil, and salt to the Instant Pot steel insert.
  3. Give a quick stir.For softer rice: add 1 ¼ cups water.For al dente rice: add 1 cup water.
  4. Close the Instant Pot with pressure valve to Sealing. Cook on High Pressure for 4 minutes.
  5. When the instant pot beeps, let the pressure release naturally for 5 minutes and then Quick Release the remaining pressure manually.
  6. Open the lid and use a fork or ladle to fluff up the rice and serve hot with curry of your choice.
