Eggless Strawberry Waffles Video

Ingredients

  • All Purpose Flour - 3/4 Cup
  • Whole Wheat Flour - 1/4 Cup
  • Fresh Strawberries - 1 Cup (From 8 - 10 berries, Chopped)
  • Regular Milk / Almond Milk (For Vegan) - 1 Cup
  • Vegetable or any flavorless Oil - 1/4 Cup
  • Brown or Turbinado Sugar - 1.5 Tbsp
  • Baking Powder - 2 tsp
  • Cinnamon Powder - 1/2 tsp
  • Salt - 1/4 tsp
  • Vanilla Extract - 1/2 tsp

Method

  • Soak fresh strawberries in water for a while.
  • Meanwhile add flours, brown sugar, baking powder, cinnamon powder and salt in a mixing bowl.
  • Mix the dry ingredients well and add milk to the flour mixture.
  • Add oil to the waffle mixture.
  • Now wipe the excess water and hull the berries, chop them as shown in the video.
  • Add to the waffle batter and do mix gently.
  • Mine is a non stick if not do grease the waffle maker with oil.
  • In preheated waffle maker, pour waffle batter (refer video and i always keep medium brown setting in the waffle maker).
  • Once poured do turn it upside down for the waffle to cook.
  • If you see the bottom green light down turn it upside.
  • Now wait for the green light to turn on.
  • Even if the lights are on I leave it for 30 more seconds before removing it for more crispiness.
  • It takes hardly 4.5 to 4.75 minutes for each waffle to done perfectly.
  • In case the waffle sticks do leave few more seconds and don't reduce oil in the batter this helps to prevent sticking.
  • This measurement yields 4 waffles for the family.
