Ingredients

  • 1 kg Chicken (12-14 Pieces- curry -cut)
  • 4 Potato (each cut into 4 pieces)
  • 200 g Plain Curd/ Yogurt
  • 5 Onion (roughly chopped)
  • 1 Tbsp Ginger Paste
  • 1 Tbsp Garlic Paste
  • 3 Tbsp Tomato Puree
  • 1/2 Tsp Cumin Seed
  • 1 Tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 1 Tsp Cumin Powder
  • 1 Tsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 Tsp Bengali Garam Masala Powder
  • 2 Tsp Red Chili Powder
  • 2 Bay Leaf
  • 2 Dry Red Chilli
  • 5 Green cardamom
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick
  • 2 Green Chilies
  • 1 Tbsp Sugar
  • 1.5 Tsp Salt (or to Taste)
  • 4 Tbsp Mustard Oil

Method

Prep Work

  1. Thoroughly wash the chicken pieces and gently pat them dry.
  2. For a minimum of 3-4 hours or overnight, marinate the chicken with half of the ginger paste, garlic paste, cumin powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, a pinch of salt, yogurt, and one tablespoon of mustard oil.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
