Method

Soaking Beans

  1. Rinse and soak the rajma (kidney beans) in enough water overnight or for 8 to 9 hours.
  2. Next day, discard the water and rinse the beans again in fresh water for a couple of times.
  3. Drain all the water.

Common questions

Is Rajma healthy for kids?

Rajma scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years. It passes 10 of the 10 checks.

What age is Rajma best for?

It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 4–6 years and 7–9 years and 10–12 years, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
