Method

Preparation

  1. Rinse rice till the water runs clear of starch and become transparent while rinsing.
  2. Soak the rice in enough water for 20 to 30 minutes. Drain all the water and keep the soaked rice aside.
  3. Rinse, peel and chop the vegetables.
  4. Crush chopped ginger, garlic and green chillies to a paste in a mortar-pestle or grind them in a small mixer or grinder with a bit of water.

Is Vegetable Pulao Recipe (Veg Pulav) healthy for kids?

Vegetable Pulao Recipe (Veg Pulav) scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Vegetable Pulao Recipe (Veg Pulav) best for?

It scores highest, 100 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and adults, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
