Method

Making vegetable balls

  1. Take the finely chopped or grated veggies in a bowl.
  2. Next add the dry ingredients - cornstarch, all-purpose flour, black pepper and salt.
  3. Mix and gather the whole mixture together.
  4. Then kind of mix and knead so that the veggies leave water and you get a dough-like mixture.
  5. But do not knead like that a bread or roti dough.
  6. Gluten strands can form that will give a dense chewy texture in the fried veggie balls.
  7. So just mix very well and press so that the veggies release their juices.
  8. Then take a small portion of the mixture in your hands.
  9. Press and roll it in your palm and make a round veggie ball. You can spread some oil on your palms while making the vegetable balls.
  10. Make all veggie balls this way and set aside.

Common questions

Is Manchurian healthy for kids?

Manchurian scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, protein, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Manchurian best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years, and lowest, 60, for 10–12 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
