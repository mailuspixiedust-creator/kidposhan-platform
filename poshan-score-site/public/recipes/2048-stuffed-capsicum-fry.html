Stuffed Capsicum Fry

Ingredients

  • 4 Capsicum
  • 200 g Gram Flour
  • 100 g Corn Flour
  • 50 g All Purpose Flour
  • 100 g Green Peas
  • 2 Potato
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil

Method

  • In a Bowl, Mix 100 Gms Gram Flour, Potatoes, Green Peas and Salt.
  • Take a Capsicum, Remove seeds if it is in excess, then Stuff the above mix into it.
  • Mix remaining gram flour, Corn flour, All purpose flour, salt and water in a bowl.
  • Dip the Stuffed Capsicum in the Flour.
  • Deep fry till golden brown.
  • Serve it hot with Tomato Chutney or Tomato Sauce.
