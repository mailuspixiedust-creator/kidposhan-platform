Air Fryer General Tso's Chicken

Recipe by myfoodstory.com

Photo: myfoodstory.com
⏱ 40 minServes 4Non-vegetarian4 PM snackFrom 1 years

Air Fryer General Tso's Chicken scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 500 gms boneless chicken thigh / cut into ¾ inch cubes
  • 2 teaspoons grated ginger
  • 1 tablespoon grated garlic
  • 2 tablespoons cornflour
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 egg whites
  • 1 ½ cups all purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 ½ teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 tablespoon water
  • ¼ cup light soy sauce
  • 2 teaspoons dark soy sauce
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 2 tablespoons vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons ketchup
  • 1 teaspoon white pepper
  • 2 teaspoons cornstarch
  • ½ cup water
  • 2 teaspoons toasted sesame oil
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (finely chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger (finely chopped)
  • 6 red chillies (whole)
  • ½ red capsicum (cut into ½ inch squares)
  • 1-2 tablespoons cooking oil for brushing (additional oil if deep frying)

Method

Marinating chicken

  1. Take all ingredients for marination in a bowl, mix to combine well. Set aside for 30 minutes.
