Paneer Ki Sabji

Recipe by hebbarskitchen.com

Paneer Ki Sabji scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 15 cubes paneer / cottage cheese
  • ½ capsicum (cubed)
  • 1 tsp cumin
  • 1 onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp gigner & garlic (chopped)
  • ½ tsp turmeric
  • 1 tsp chilli powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 3 tomato (crushed)
  • 1 cup hot water
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 2 tbsp kasuri methi (crushed)
  • 2 tbsp coriander (chopped)

Method

  • Firstly, in a wok, heat 3 tbsp oil. Fry 15 cubes paneer, ½ capsicum till they turn golden brown.
  • Once the paneer turns golden brown, keep it aside.
  • In the same oil, add 1 tsp cumin and saute it until it crackles.
  • Add 1 onion, 2 tbsp ginger & garlic, and sauté until the onions turn golden brown.
  • Keeping the flame on low, add ½ tsp turmeric, 1 tsp chilli powder, ½ tsp cumin powder, ½ tsp coriander powder and 1 tsp salt.
  • Sauté on low flame till the spices turn aromatic.
  • Now add 3 tomatoes and sauté until the tomatoes soften and the oil separates.
  • Add 1 cup of hot water and cook, adjusting the consistency as required.
  • Also, add fried paneer, capsicum and 2 tbsp cream.
  • Mix well and cook for 5 minutes.
  • Further add ½ tsp garam masala, 2 tbsp kasuri methi, and 2 tbsp coriander. Mix well.
  • Finally, enjoy the Paneer Sabji Recipe, which tastes great with roti or rice.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
