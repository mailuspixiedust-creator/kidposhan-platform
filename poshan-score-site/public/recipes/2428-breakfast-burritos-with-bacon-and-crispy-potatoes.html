Breakfast Burritos with Bacon and Crispy Potatoes

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • ¼ cup sour cream
  • 2 tablespoons minced canned chipotle chile in adobo sauce
  • 2 teaspoons lime juice
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper
  • ¼ teaspoon table salt
  • ¼ cup vegetable oil, divided
  • 2 cups frozen tater tots, thawed and patted dry
  • 8 slices bacon, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • 8 large eggs, beaten
  • 3 ounces pepper Jack cheese, shredded (¾ cup)
  • 2 scallions, sliced thin
  • 4 (10-inch) flour tortillas

Method

FOR THE CHIPOTLE SOUR CREAM:

  1. Stir all ingredients together in bowl; set aside.

FOR THE BURRITOS:

  1. Heat 3 tablespoons oil in 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  2. Add tater tots to skillet and press with spatula or underside of dry measuring cup to flatten slightly.
  3. Cook until crispy and deep golden brown, about 4 minutes per side.
  4. Transfer tater tots to paper towel–lined plate and set aside.
  5. Wipe skillet clean with paper towels.
  6. Cook bacon in now-empty skillet over medium heat until crispy, 8 to 10 minutes.
  7. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons fat (leaving bacon in skillet).
  8. Add eggs and, using heat-resistant rubber spatula, constantly and firmly scrape along bottom and sides of skillet until eggs begin to clump and spatula leaves trail on bottom of skillet, about 2 minutes.
  9. Reduce heat to low and add pepper Jack and scallions.
  10. Gently but constantly fold eggs until clumped and slightly wet, 30 to 60 seconds.
  11. Remove from heat and cover to keep warm.
  12. Wrap tortillas in damp dish towel and microwave until warm and pliable, about 1 minute.
  13. Arrange tortillas on counter.
  14. Spread about 1½ tablespoons chipotle sour cream across bottom third of each tortilla, leaving 1-inch border.
  15. Divide tater tots and eggs evenly over chipotle sour cream.
  16. Working with 1 burrito at a time, fold sides of tortilla over filling, then fold up bottom of tortilla and roll tightly around filling.
  17. Wipe skillet clean with paper towels.
  18. Heat remaining 1 tablespoon oil in now-empty skillet over medium heat until shimmering.
  19. Arrange burritos in skillet seam side down and cook until crisp and golden, about 1 minute per side.
  20. Serve.
