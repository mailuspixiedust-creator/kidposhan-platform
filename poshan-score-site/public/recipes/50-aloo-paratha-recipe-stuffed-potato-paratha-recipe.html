Aloo Paratha Recipe (Stuffed Potato Paratha Recipe)

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 cups Wheat Flour (or as needed)
  • 1 tsp Salt (to taste)
  • ¾ to 1 cup Water (or as needed)
  • 2 large Potatoes
  • 2 tsp Kashmiri Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Chaat Masala Powder
  • 2 tsp Roasted Cumin Powder
  • ½ tsp Garam Masala Powder
  • 1 no Green Chillies (chopped finely)
  • 1 tsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • ½ cup Coriander leaves (chopped finely)
  • ¼ cup Whole Wheat Flour
  • 12 tsp Oil or Ghee

Method

  • Start by cooking potatoes.
  • Take whole potatoes with skin on in a pressure cooker.
  • Cover the pressure cooker and cook for 2 to 3 whistle on medium high heat.
  • Turn off the heat and let the steam release by itself.
  • Open the cooker, strain the potatoes through a colander and let it sit in the colander and cool completely.
  • Make the paratha dough.
  • Take wheat flour, salt in a bowl and mix well.
  • Now add in water little at a time and knead to a soft dough.
  • Cover the dough with a plate and let it rest for 30 minute.
  • Now make the Potato filling or aloo paratha filling.
  • Take your cooled potatoes and peel of the skin.
  • Use a grater to grate the boiled potatoes.
  • Take the grated potatoes in a bowl and add all the ingredients mentioned for filling into it.
  • Mix it gently and set aside.
  • Now lets stuff and roll the paratha. Take the dough and knead it once to combine. Divide the paratha dough into 6 equal portions.
  • Take a portion of dough and flatten it using your hands. The sides of the dough should be thinner than the centre of the dough.
  • Place 1/4 cup of potato filling inside paratha dough. Sprinkle some wheat flour on top of the filling, this removes any moisture from the filling.
  • Bring the sides of the dough to the centre. Seal the edges and shape into smooth ball. Dust the dough with some flour on both sides. Flatten it gently,
  • Dust both sides with wheat flour. Now without giving much pressure, Roll it into thick paratha. Don't give too much pressure when rolling.
  • Place paratha on a hot tawa.
  • Let it cook for 30 seconds, flip over and apply 1 tsp of ghee or oil.
  • Flip over and apply 1 tsp more ghee or oil on other side too.
  • Cook on medium high heat for few seconds on each sides till the paratha gets golden brown.
  • Remove the paratha and serve with curd, pickle.
