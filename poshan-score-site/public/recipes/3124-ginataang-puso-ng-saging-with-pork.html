Ginataang Puso ng Saging with Pork

Recipe by Panlasang Pinoy

Ingredients

  • 1 medium banana blossom (puso ng saging) (tough outer layers removed and hard core trimmed)
  • 1/2 lb pork belly (liyempo) (sliced into bite-sized pieces)
  • 2 cups coconut milk (gata)
  • 1 medium red onion (chopped)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 thumb ginger (minced)
  • 1 1/2 to 2 tablespoons shrimp paste (bagoong alamang)
  • 3 to 5 pieces Thai chili peppers (siling labuyo) (chopped)
  • 2 pieces long green chili peppers (siling haba) (sliced diagonally)
  • 3/4 cup vinegar (divided)
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons coarse sea salt (for preparing the banana blossom)
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground black pepper (or white pepper)
  • 1 to 1 1/2 cups water (divided)

Method

  • Remove the tough outer layers of the banana blossom.
  • Quarter it lengthwise and trim away the hard inner core.
  • Thinly slice the remaining portions crosswise into shreds.
  • Transfer the shredded banana blossom to a large bowl.
  • Add the coarse salt, half of the vinegar, and about 3/4 cup of water.
  • Mix and massage gently, then soak for 15 minutes to draw out the sap and keep the blossom from browning.
  • Squeeze the banana blossom firmly to remove as much liquid and sap as possible.
  • Rinse thoroughly with clean water, then squeeze again to remove the excess moisture.
  • Transfer the banana blossom to a clean bowl. Add the remaining vinegar and toss to combine. Squeeze out the excess liquid once more and set aside.
  • Heat a cooking pan over medium heat. Add the pork belly and cook without additional oil until lightly browned and some of the fat has rendered.
  • Pour in about 3/4 cup of water.
  • Cover, reduce the heat, and simmer for 15 to 18 minutes, or until the pork becomes fork-tender.
  • Allow the water to evaporate, leaving the rendered pork fat in the pan.
  • Add the garlic to the same pan and sauté for 30 seconds, or until fragrant.
  • Add the onion and cook for 1 minute, or until softened.
  • Stir in the ginger, bagoong alamang, and chopped Thai chili peppers.
  • Continue sautéing for another minute.
  • Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer. Cook for 2 minutes.
  • Add the prepared banana blossom and toss until evenly coated with the coconut milk mixture.
  • Pour in about 1/4 cup of water, cover, and simmer over low heat for 5 to 8 minutes, or until the banana blossom becomes tender.
  • Season with ground black pepper to taste. Add the sliced long green chili peppers and simmer uncovered for another minute, or until the sauce thickens slightly.
  • Transfer to a serving dish and serve warm with steamed white rice. Share and enjoy!
