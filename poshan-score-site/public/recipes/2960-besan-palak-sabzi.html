Besan palak sabzi

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Besan palak sabzi scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 300 grm palak bhaji
  • 4 tbsp besan/gram flour
  • 7-8 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 2 tsp green chilli
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • ¼ cup oil
  • ½ cup mustard seeds
  • Pinch of hing
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ cup water
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 300 grm tandalji bhaji amaranth bhaji
  • 7-8 finely chopped garlic
  • 1 inch ginger paste
  • 2 green chilli paste
  • 1 chopped tomatoes
  • ¾ cup water or as required
  • 1 tbsp coriander powder
  • 1 tbsp red chilli powder

Method

Making Besan palak sabzi

  1. Wash palak leaves properly and dry it. Then cut leaves into pieces.
  2. In a pan, add gram flour and roast till it changes its color.
  3. Now in a mortal-pestal, add garlic cloves, ginger, green chilli and red chilli powder. Crushed it into paste.
  4. Heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds, hing and crushed garlic-red chilli paste. Sauté it.
  5. Then add turmeric powder and coriander powder. Mix well.
  6. Add chopped palak leaves and mix well.
  7. Now add sugar, salt and water. Mix well and cook palak till it become soft.
  8. When palak is properly cooked, add roasted besan gradually and mix well.
  9. Then add lemon juice and garam masala. Mix well.
  10. Besan palak sabzi is ready to serve with khichdi or bhakhari.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?
