Garlic Cheese Naan

Ingredients

  • 1 1/2 cup Whole milk (, warm)
  • 2 tsp Sugar
  • 1 packet Active dry yeast (2 1/4 tsp)
  • 4 tbsp Sour cream (or yogurt)
  • 3 tbsp Butter (or Ghee or Brown butter, Melted)
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 4 cups All purpose flour (or Maida, add more or less as needed to form dough)
  • 2 1/2 to 3 cups Mozzarella cheese (, grated)
  • 10 to 12 cloves Garlic (, minced)
  • 4 tbsp Butter (melted)
  • 3 tbsp Cilantro (or Coriander leaves, chopped)

Method

Step 1

  1. In a large bowl, add whole milk, sugar and sprinkle yeast.
  2. Cover it and keep it undisturbed for about 10 minutes or until the yeast activates.
