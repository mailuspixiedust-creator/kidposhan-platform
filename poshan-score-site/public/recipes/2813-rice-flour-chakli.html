Rice flour chakli

Ingredients

  • 2 cup or 250 grm rice flour
  • 1 cup or 100 grm besan
  • 1.5 cup water or as required
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp green chilli paste
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chilli powder
  • 2 tbsp sesame seeds
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • Oil for frying

Method

  • In a mixing bowl, add 2 cups of rice flour and 1 cup of besan. Mix well.
  • Now, in a kadai, add 1½ cups of water and bring it to a boil.
  • Add salt, 1 tbsp green chilli paste, ¼ tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp red chilli powder, 2 tbsp white sesame seeds, and 3 tbsp oil.
  • Mix well.
  • When the water starts boiling, lower the flame, add the flour mixture, and mix well.
  • Switch off the flame and cover the kadai for 2 minutes.
  • Transfer the flour mixture to a mixing bowl. When the mixture is slightly warm, knead it well. This process helps bind the flour mixture together.
  • Now, add 1–2 tbsp of water and knead into a semi-soft dough for chakli.
  • Take a chakli maker fitted with a star-shaped disc. Lightly grease the chakli maker with oil, add the dough, and close the lid.
  • On a greased steel plate, rotate the chakli maker in a circular motion while twisting the handle to form spiral chaklis.
  • Join the ends of each chakli to prevent it from opening while frying.
  • Heat oil over a medium flame. Test the temperature by adding a small piece of dough. If it rises to the surface without browning immediately, the oil is ready.
  • Add 3–4 chaklis at a time to the hot oil and deep-fry them over medium heat until they are crispy and golden brown.
  • Fry evenly on both sides, then remove them and place them on a wire rack.
  • Allow the chaklis to cool to room temperature. They are now ready to serve or store in an airtight container.

    What age is Rice flour chakli best for?

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
