Ingredients

  • 1 pound bread loaf (brioche, french or sourdough)
  • 7 large egg
  • 2 cups milk
  • 1/4 cup brown sugar
  • 1 tablespoon vanilla extract
  • 2 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 cup flour
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1/4 cup butter (room temperature)
  • 2 medium bananas (optional)

Method

  • Preheat oven to 350 degrees and grease a 9x13 inch pan.
  • Cut the bread into 1 inch cubes and place in a large bowl.
  • In a separate bowl, whisk together the eggs, milk, brown sugar, vanilla, cinnamon and salt.
  • Pour over your bread cubes and gently toss to coat.
  • Spread the mixture evenly into the prepared baking dish.
  • For the crumb topping, add the flour, brown sugar and cinnamon to a small bowl.
  • Stir to combine, then cut in butter using a fork or your fingers until the mixture resembles coarse crumbs.
  • Sprinkle over the casserole.
  • Bake for 30-40 minutes or until lightly browned and cooked through.
  • Serve warm with fresh banana slices and maple syrup!
