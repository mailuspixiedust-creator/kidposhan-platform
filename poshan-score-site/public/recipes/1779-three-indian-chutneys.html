Three Indian Chutneys

Recipe by pipingpotcurry.com

Three Indian Chutneys scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Tamarind (Imli seedless)
  • 1 cup Dates (pitted, ~10 if using Medjool dates)
  • 1 cup Jaggery (powdered)
  • 3 cup Water
  • 1 teaspoon Ground Cumin (Jeera powder) (Jeera powder)
  • 1 teaspoon Ginger powder
  • 1 teaspoon Red Chili powder (Mirchi powder) (Mirchi powder adjust to taste, optional)
  • 1 teaspoon Black salt (Kala namak) (or Salt)
  • 2 cups Cilantro leaves (Dhaniya packed, hard stems removed, washed and roughly chopped)
  • 1 cup Mint leaves (Pudhina packed, stems removed, washed)
  • 2 Green Chili Pepper (I used thai, adjust to taste)
  • 1 " inch Ginger (grated)
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 teaspoon Roasted Cumin Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Salt (you can also use black salt, adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon Sugar (optional, adjust to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon Lime juice (or Tamarind juice)
  • ½ cup Water
  • 10 Dried Red Chili Whole (Kashmiri chili prefered)
  • 1 cup Water (for soaking chili)
  • 10 cloves Garlic (peeled)
  • ¼ tablespoon Salt

Method

Tamarind Chutney

  1. Add tamarind, dates, jaggery and water to a pot.
  2. Bring to a boil and cook on medium heat for 15-20 minutes until the tamarind and dates have softened.
  3. You can also pressure cook on high pressure for 10 minutes.
  4. Let the mixture cool a bit. Then stir well and mash using a masher. You can also blend if the tamarind is completely seedless.
  5. Place a mesh strainer over a bowl.
  6. Work in batches to stain the tamarind date pulp.
  7. Press with the back of a spoon or with clean hands (if it is cool enough to handle).
  8. Add the ginger, cumin, red chili powder and black salt to the strained chutney. Stir it well.
  9. Transfer chutney to a clean glass air tight jar and store in the refrigerator. This chutney stays well in refrigerator for up to a month.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?
