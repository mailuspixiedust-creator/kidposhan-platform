Ingredients

  • 6 slice bread, whole wheat
  • 3 large egg
  • 1/4 cup milk
  • 3 tablespoon flaxseed, ground
  • 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon

Method

  • In a medium bowl, combine eggs, milk, flax meal and cinnamon.
  • Whisk together until well combined.
  • Dunk each piece of bread into the mixture and immediately transfers to a hot pan.
  • Cook on each side until golden brown.
  • Server with your favorite fruit and maple syrup.
