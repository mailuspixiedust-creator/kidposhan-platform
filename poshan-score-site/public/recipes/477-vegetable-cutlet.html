Vegetable Cutlet

Recipe by spiceindiaonline.com

Photo: spiceindiaonline.com
⏱ 35 minServes 6VegetarianSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 5 years

Vegetable Cutlet scores 80 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 small potato (about 3 inch long)
  • 1 each carrot
  • 1/4 cup green peas (quick frozen)
  • 1/2 cup onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 1 inch ginger (minced)
  • 1 long green chilli (minced)
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon garam masala powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1 tablespoon coriander leaves (minced)
  • 1 cup bread crumbs (for coating)
  • 3 cups oil (for deep frying)
  • 1 tablespoon maida
  • 1/4 cup water

Method

  • Wash the veggies, cube the potatoes and carrots.
  • Boil cubed potatoes, carrots and green peas until thoroughly cooked.
  • Drain water from the steamed vegetables and keep aside.
  • Heat a tsp of oil in a small saute pan and fry the minced onions, green chilli, ginger and garlic until slightly browned. Switch off!
  • Add this to the steamed veggies along with turmeric powder, red chilli powder, garam masala and salt. Let this cool completely.
  • Mix (mash gently) to make a thick mixture.
  • Take a small portion and roll them between your palms to make a ball, then press to flatten or shape the cutlet according to your wish.
  • Meanwhile make a thin paste using maida and water.
  • Spread a layer of bread crumbs on a plate for coating.
  • Heat the oil in a pan and keep it ready on medium flame.
  • Now take each patty, first dip it in the maida paste and then place it on the bread crumbs to coat all sides.
  • Drop that in the oil and fry until its golden on all sides.
  • Serve with ketchup or sauce of your choice.

    Common questions

    Is Vegetable Cutlet healthy for kids?

    Vegetable Cutlet scores 80 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on fibre, protein.

    What age is Vegetable Cutlet best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 1–3 years, and lowest, 80, for 4–6 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
