Method

Stovetop Pressure Cooker Method:

  1. Follow the same steps as the Instant Pot.
  2. Close the lid and put the whistle weight on.
  3. Cook for 2 whistles on medium flame and turn off the gas.
  4. All the other steps remain the same.

Common questions

Is Tomato Rice Recipe - Instant Pot & Stovetop healthy for kids?

Tomato Rice Recipe - Instant Pot & Stovetop scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Tomato Rice Recipe - Instant Pot & Stovetop best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 60, for 16–18 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
