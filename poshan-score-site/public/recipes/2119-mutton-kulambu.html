Mutton Kulambu

Mutton Kulambu scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Poppy Seeds or Cashews
  • 6-8 Kashmiri Dry Red Chilies
  • 1 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 tsp Fennel Seeds
  • 2-3 Cloves
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 2 tsp Whole Black Peppercorns
  • 1 tsp Chopped Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ginger
  • 1/2 cup Onions
  • 2 tbsp Fresh Coconut
  • 4 tbsp Vegetable Oil
  • 1 cup Onions
  • 10-12 Curry Leaves
  • 3-4 Green chilies
  • 1 cup Tomatoes
  • Salt
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 2 tsp Red Chili Powder
  • 500 g Mutton

Method

  • Add all of the ingredients for the paste to a blender.
  • Add ¼ cup of water and blend to make a smooth paste. Set aside.
  • Heat vegetable oil in a pressure cooker over high heat.
  • Once it is hot, add onion and green chili peppers. Cook until onions are softened (4-5 minutes).
  • Keep stirring at regular intervals.
  • Add tomatoes and salt, cook for 2-3 minutes.
  • Add turmeric powder and Kashmiri red chili powder. Cook for one more minute.
  • Add the ground masala paste with the mutton pieces. Cook for two minutes with 2 cups of water.
  • Cook the mutton for one whistle on high heat.
  • Reduce the heat to low and cook it for 20-25 minutes or until it is tender.
  • After that, take the cooker off of the heat and wait for all of the pressure to release.
  • Once it's released, open up the lid and add in some chopped cilantro. Serve hot with Roti or Rice.
