Potato Masala for Dosa

Recipe by masalachilli.com

Photo: masalachilli.com
⏱ 45 minServes 4VegetarianBreakfastFrom 2 years

Potato Masala for Dosa scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • ½ kg Potatoes (7 medium potatoes, cooked)
  • 3 Onions (medium, sliced into half moons)
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped ginger
  • 2-3 green chilies (finely chopped)
  • curry leaves
  • ½ teaspoon Turmeric powder
  • 1/8 teaspoon Hing or Asafoetida
  • ½ teaspoon Mustard Seeds
  • 1 teaspoon Chana Dal or Bengal Gram Dal
  • 1 ½ tablespoon Oil
  • ¼ cup Water (read notes)
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Pressure cook Potatoes. Once cool, peel the skin and roughly mash with a fork. Keep aside.
  • Heat a pan, add oil. Next add chana dal, mustard seeds.
  • Once mustard crackles and chana dal appears roasted, add turmeric, curry leaves, hing, chopped ginger and green chilies. Cook this for 1-2 mins.
  • Next, add onions (sliced half moon).
  • Saute onions on low heat until it cooks and reduces in volume. Splash some water to deglaze the pan and prevent the onions from burning. Cover and cook.
  • Once the onions are partially cooked, add some salt. Mix and let it cook for 1-2 mins.
  • Next, add the mashed potatoes.
  • Add salt to taste and some water. Mix until potatoes coat the spices, turmeric. Switch off heat and serve.
