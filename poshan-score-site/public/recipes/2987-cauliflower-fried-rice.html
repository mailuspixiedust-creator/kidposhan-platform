Cauliflower Fried Rice

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Cauliflower
  • 1/4 cup Carrots
  • 3 Green Chillies
  • 1/2 tsp Pepper Powder
  • Salt
  • 2 tsp Corn Flour
  • 1 tsp Maida
  • 1/2 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • Oil
  • 3 tbsp Spring Onion
  • 1 cup Cooked Rice
  • 1/2 tsp Soya Sauce
  • 1/2 tsp Chilli Sauce

Method

  • In a mixing bowl mix together cauliflower, corn flour, maida, red chilli powder and salt with some water.
  • Drop these florets in hot oil and fry till crisp.
  • Take them on a plate and keep aside.
  • In another pan heat 4 tsp oil and fry carrots, 2 tbsp spring onions and green chillies for a while.
  • Then add rice, pepper powder and salt. Mix well. Stir in chilli sauce, soy sauce and fried cauliflower and mix well. Sauté on a low flame for 2-3 minutes
  • Garnish with the remaining spring onions and serve hot.
