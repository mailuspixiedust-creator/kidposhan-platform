Corn and Saffron Rice

Recipe by Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar

Photo: Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar
⏱ 40 minVegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 5 years

Corn and Saffron Rice scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Basmati Rice (soaked for 10 to 15 minutes)
  • 3 cups Sweet Corn Kernels (pressure-cooked)
  • 2 Onions (sliced)
  • 1 Bay Leaf
  • 1 inch Cinnamon
  • 2 Cardamoms -
  • 10 Cashewnuts (fried in a bit of ghee)
  • 1/4 tsp Saffron Strands (dissolved in 2 tbsp warm milk)
  • Ghee as required
  • Salt as per taste
  • Onion - 1
  • Garlic - 4 cloves
  • Ginger - 1/2-inch piece
  • Turmeric Powder - 1 tbsp
  • Coriander Powder - 1 tbsp
  • Garam Masala Powder - 1/4 tsp
  • Red Chilli Powder - 1 tsp
  • Almonds - 8 to 10 (blanched)

Method

  • Drain the soaked rice and fry in 1/2 to 1 tsp of ghee until moisture evaporates.
  • In a pan, heat ghee and add bay leaf, cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves.
  • Add sliced onions and fry until golden.
  • Mix in corn and rice.
  • Pour 2 2 1/2 cups of hot water, add salt, and pressure cook for 10 minutes.
  • After cooking, add the saffron mixture and stir well.
  • Garnish with fried cashew nuts.
  • Serve the flavorful Corn and Saffron Rice.
