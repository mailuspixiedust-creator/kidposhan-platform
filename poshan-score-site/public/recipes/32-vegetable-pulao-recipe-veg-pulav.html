Ingredients

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 1 large Onion (Peeled & Sliced)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • ¼ cup Coriander leaves (chopped)
  • ¼ cup Mint Leaves (chopped)
  • ½ cup Yoghurt
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 3 tbsp Oil
  • 1 cup Milk
  • 2 cups Water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 large Carrot (chopped)
  • 10 no Green Beans (chopped)
  • 1 cup Green Peas
  • 1 large Potato (peeled & chopped)
  • 1 tsp Coriander powder
  • 1 tsp Chilli powder
  • 1 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala powder
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 4 no Cardamom
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds

Method

  • Soak Basmati rice for 30 mins. Strain in a colander and set aside.
  • Heat ghee or oil in a heavy bottom pan. Add in whole spices and sauté for 1 min.
  • Now add in onions and fry until the onions get slightly golden.
  • Add in ginger garlic paste and cook for couple of minutes.
  • Now add in all the spice powders and mix well.
  • Now add in all the vegetables and mix well.
  • Saute all the vegetables with the onions for 4 to 5 minutes.
  • Add in coriander leaves and mint leaves and mix well.
  • Pour in curd and give a stir.
  • The curd will combine with the vegetables.
  • Now add in the drained rice and mix well.
  • Pour in 2 cup of water and 1 cup of milk (measure water and milk from the same cup you measured the rice)and mix well.
  • Season with salt.
  • Bring this to a full boil.
  • Cover the pan with a lid and simmer it for 10 to 12 minutes.
  • Now switch off the flame and let it rest for 5 more mins.
  • Fluff up with a fork and let it sit covered for 10 more minutes.
  • Now enjoy with raita.
