Methi Pulao (Menthya Bath Karnataka Style)

Recipe by Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil

Photo: Indian Veggie Delight / Bhavana Patil
⏱ 25 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Methi Pulao (Menthya Bath Karnataka Style) scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 3 tablespoons grated coconut (fresh/frozen)
  • 2 tablespoons onion (optional)
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 3-4 green chilies
  • 2 tablespoons mint/pudina
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves
  • Spices (2 cloves, 1/2 inch cinnamon, 2 cardamom) (optional)
  • 1/4 cup water (or more) (for grinding)
  • 2 tablespoons oil (or ghee)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 inch cinammon
  • 2 cloves
  • 2 cardamoms
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/2 teaspoon saunf (optional)
  • 1 star anise (optional)
  • 1 large onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 big bunch fresh methi leaves (about 2 cups tightly packed)
  • 1/2 cup green peas (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 medium tomato (finely chopped)
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1 cup sona masuri rice
  • 2 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice

Method

Stovetop Pressure Cooker Method

  1. Close the lid, place a whistle, and cook for 2 whistles on medium flame. The remaining steps are the same.
