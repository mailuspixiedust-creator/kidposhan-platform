Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp mixed sprouts (moong , chana , chawli etc.)
  • 2 tbsp rice (chawal) , soaked for 15 minutes and drained
  • 1/2 tsp ghee
  • 1/4 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic (lehsun) paste
  • 1 tsp finely chopped onion
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)
  • fresh dahi (curd)

  • The process of sprouting makes it easier to digest as well.

  • Drain using a strainer. Keep the washed rice aside.
  • Now to make mixed sprouts khichdi for babies begin by tempering the khichdi. For that heat ghee in a pressure cooker.
  • Add cumin seeds to it and allow them to crackle.
  • Add a pinch of asafoetida. This aids in digestion.
  • Add some garlic paste if your baby likes it. Avoid this paste if your baby dislikes strong flavours.
  • Saut&eacute;sprouts khichdi recipe for kidsfor a few seconds.
  • Add about a tsp of finely chopped onions.
  • Now saut&eacute; onions for about 30 seconds.
  • Add the washed rice to the pressure cooker.
  • Along with this also add mixed sprouts to make healthy sprouts khichdi for toddlers. You can also make use of any one variety of sprouts.
  • Saut&eacute; the rice and sprouts for 30 seconds as well.
  • Add around &frac34; cup of water for cooking.
  • Add salt at this stage before cooking itself.
  • Mix all the ingredients for mixed sprouts khichdi for babies with a ladle.
  • Cover it with a ladle and pressure cook for 3 whistles.
  • Once the cooker has been cooled down, open the lid. This is how sprouts khichdi recipe for kids looks lie after cooking.
  • Mash the healthy sprouts khichdi for toddlers slightly with the back of a spoon or a potato masher.
  • Adjust to get the consistency of the khichdi your baby likes.
