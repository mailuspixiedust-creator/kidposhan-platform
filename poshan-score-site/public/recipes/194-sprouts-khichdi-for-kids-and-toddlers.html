Ingredients

  • 2 tbsp mixed sprouts (moong , chana , chawli etc.)
  • 2 tbsp rice (chawal) , soaked for 15 minutes and drained
  • 1/2 tsp ghee
  • 1/4 tsp cumin seeds (jeera)
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic (lehsun) paste
  • 1 tsp finely chopped onion
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)
  • fresh dahi (curd)

  • Add salt at this stage before cooking itself.
  • Mix all the ingredients for mixed sprouts khichdi for babies with a ladle.
