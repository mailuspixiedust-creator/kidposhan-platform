Cabbage Peas Stir Fry (Patta Gobi Matar)

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 4 cups cabbage (slice into fine long shreds, discard the stalk and thick stems)
  • 1/2 cup green peas (fresh/frozen)
  • 1 tablespoon coconut oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 2 green chilies (finely chopped)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (minced)
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
  • 1/2 teaspoon ground turmeric (haldi)
  • 1/4 teaspoon red chilli powder (or cayenne pepper, lal mirch)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala
  • 1/4 teaspoon sugar (optional)
  • 2-3 tablespoons water (for instant pot only)
  • 1 teaspoon lemon juice (or [1/4 teaspoon amchur powder, dry mango powder, ])
  • 2 tablespoons cilantro/coriander leaves (hara dhania) (chopped, optional)
  • salt (to taste)

Method

Stovetop Pot Method:

  1. Heat a pan/kadai on medium heat. Add oil to the pan. Once hot, add cumin seeds, and let them splutter.
  2. Next, add green chilies, minced ginger, and hing. Saute for a few seconds.
  3. Add green peas and stir fry for a minute.
  4. Then add spices like turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder, sugar, and salt, and stir well to combine.
  5. Add shredded cabbage and mix well. Close the lid, and cook on medium-low heat for 6-8 minutes until the cabbage and peas are tender. Stir occasionally.
  6. Once done, sprinkle garam masala and lemon juice, Mix well.
  7. Switch off the flame when there is still some crunch to the cabbage.
  8. You dont want them to be too mushy or overcooked.
  9. Garnish with coriander leaves, Cabbage Peas Stir Fry is ready, serve hot with chapatis, roti & parathas.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt.
