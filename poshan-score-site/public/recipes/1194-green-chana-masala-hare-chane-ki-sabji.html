Ingredients

  • 2 cups (300g) fresh green chickpeas (Choliya)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt

  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds

  • 2 green chilis slitted
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • ½ cup finely chopped tomatoes
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ cup whisked yogurt
  • Boiled green chickpeas
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp garam masala or chana masala
  • ½ tsp Kasoori methi
  • ½ cup water or as required
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander leaves

Method

Cooking The Green Chickpeas

  1. Add water to a medium saucepan and bring to a boil.
  2. Add green chickpeas, salt, and sugar, and stir well.
  3. Boil the chickpeas until they are thoroughly cooked.
  4. It will take about 10-12 minutes.
  5. Once they are cooked, drain the water and set the boiled chickpeas aside.

What age is Green Chana Masala / Hare Chane Ki Sabji best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 60, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
