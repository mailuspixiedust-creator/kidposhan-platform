Khud Pukht Qureshi

Recipe by Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar

Photo: Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar
⏱ 50 minVegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Khud Pukht Qureshi scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Lentil
  • 4 cups Water
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp Yellow Chilli Powder
  • 1 tbsp Garlic Paste
  • 1/2 cup Cream
  • 1 cup Yoghurt
  • 1/2 cup Butter
  • 1 tbsp , Chopped Garlic
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1/2 cup , Chopped Onions
  • 4 Dry Red Chillies

Method

  • Boil the lentil with water, salt and yellow chilli powder till soft.
  • Add the garlic paste and cook for 10 minutes.
  • Pour in the cream and yoghurt and 1/4 cup butter.
  • Cook again for 10 minutes, stirring frequently so that the fat is incorporated into the lentil mixture.
  • Heat the remaining butter in a pan.
  • Add the garlic, cumin seeds and onions.
  • Saute for 2 minutes.
  • Add the dry red chillies and saute till brown.
  • Pour this mixture into the boiled lentil.
  • Serve hot.
