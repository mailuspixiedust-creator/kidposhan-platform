Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp Butter
  • 1 Onion (finely chopped)
  • 6 to 8 Garlic cloves (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp Spring onions (or Chives, finely chopped)
  • 1/2 tsp dried Thyme
  • 1 tbsp All-purpose flour
  • 3/4 cup Milk
  • 3 Sweet corn ears (or 1.5 cups of frozen corn kernels)
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley (or herbs of your choice)
  • 150 + 50 grams Cheddar cheese (or any melting cheese, 2 cups)
  • 150 grams Cream cheese (softened - 3/4 cup)
  • 1/2 tsp Salt

Method

How to make BAKED CORN CHEESE CASSEROLE | CHEESY CREAMED CORN

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F. Grease your baking pan and keep it aside.
  2. In a pan on medium heat, add butter.
  3. To this, add finely chopped onion and saute until translucent.
  4. Now add garlic cloves and spring onion and continue sauteing for 2 minutes.
  5. Next, add dried thyme and flour, and saute for a minute or two.
  6. Add milk and mix well.
  7. Add sweet corn kernels and continue to cook for 2 minutes.
  8. Check out the video here for more details.
  9. Finally, add fresh herbs, grated cheddar cheese, cream cheese, salt, and mix until well incorporated.
  10. Transfer this to a greased baking dish, top it with the remaining cheese and bake for 20 minutes or until bubbly and golden brown on top.
