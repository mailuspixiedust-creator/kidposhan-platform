Aloo Paratha

Recipe by pipingpotcurry.com

Aloo Paratha scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups Whole Wheat Flour (use indian style aata)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Salt (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon Oil (plus more for roasting parathas)
  • 3/4 cup Water (plus few tablespoons more as required)
  • 3 Potatoes (medium)
  • 2 Green Chili Pepper (finely chopped, optional)
  • 1 teaspoon Coriander powder (Dhaniya powder)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Cumin Powder
  • 1 teaspoon Red Chilli Powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Garam Masala (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Pomegranate powder (Anardana) (or Amchur Powder, dry mango powder)
  • 1 teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)
  • 3 tablespoons Cilantro (finely chopped)
  • 4 tablespoons Ghee (can also use oil)

Method

Make the Potato Filling/ stuffing

  1. Boil the potatoes first, and prepare the dough while they are boiling.
  2. In a stovetop pressure cooker, cook for 4-5 whistles.
  3. In an instant pot, pressure cook for 12-18 minutes depending on size of potatoes.
  4. Remove potatoes from pressure cooker, and let them cool a bit.
  5. Then peel the potatoes.
  6. Cut the potatoes roughly and mash them with a potato masher in a bowl.
  7. You can also grate them (I recommend this for potatoes that were pre-boiled and stored).
  8. Make sure there are no lumps.
  9. Add the finely chopped green chillies and cilantro.
  10. Add all the spices - coriander powder, cumin powder, red chilli powder, garam masala, pomegranate (or amchur) powder and salt.
  11. Combine everything and make a smooth mixture.
  12. Taste and adjust salt or spices if required.
