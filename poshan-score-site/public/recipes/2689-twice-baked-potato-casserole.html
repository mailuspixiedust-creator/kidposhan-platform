Twice Baked Potato Casserole

Ingredients

  • 3 lbs russet potatoes
  • 6 slices bacon
  • 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 4 tbsp butter
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp kosher salt
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • 1 1/2 cups shredded cheddar cheese (divided)
  • 3 green onions, sliced (divided)

Method

  • Bake the potatoes.
  • Preheat the oven to 400°F.
  • Scrub the potatoes and prick them several times with a fork.
  • Place directly on the oven rack or on a baking sheet and bake for 50–60 minutes, or until very tender.
  • Cook the bacon. While the potatoes are baking, cook the bacon until crisp. Transfer to a paper towel-lined plate, then crumble or chop into small pieces.
  • Cool the potatoes slightly. Let the baked potatoes cool just until they're easy to handle.
  • Scoop out the potato. Slice the potatoes in half and scoop the flesh into a large mixing bowl. Discard the skins or save them for another use.
  • Make the filling.
  • Add the Greek yogurt, milk, butter, garlic powder, salt, and pepper to the potatoes.
  • Mash until creamy, leaving a little texture if you prefer.
  • Add the mix-ins. Fold in 1 cup of the cheddar cheese, about two-thirds of the bacon, and half of the sliced green onions.
  • Transfer to a baking dish. Spread the potato mixture into a lightly greased 9x13-inch baking dish.
  • Add the toppings. Sprinkle the remaining cheddar cheese, bacon, and Parmesan over the top.
  • Bake again. Reduce the oven temperature to 375°F and bake for 20–25 minutes, or until heated through and the cheese is melted and bubbly.
  • Garnish and serve. Sprinkle with the remaining green onions before serving.
