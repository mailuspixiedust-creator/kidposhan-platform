Moong Dal Tadka (Yellow Moong Dal Recipe)

Ingredients

  • ½ cup moong dal (split and hulled mung lentils)
  • ⅓ cup onions (- finely chopped)
  • ½ cup tomatoes (- chopped)
  • 1 inch ginger (- finely chopped or minced)
  • ¼ teaspoon red chili powder (or ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper)
  • ⅓ teaspoon turmeric powder (ground turmeric)
  • 1.5 cups water (- for pressure cooking)
  • salt (as required)
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons ghee (or oil or butter)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 4 to 5 garlic cloves (- small to medium-sized, crushed lightly)
  • ¼ or ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • ¼ teaspoon red chilli powder (or ¼ teaspoon cayenne pepper)
  • 1 or 2 green chilli (- slit or ½ teaspoon chopped)
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing, optional)

Method

Cooking Lentils

  1. First rinse the moong lentils a couple of times in water. Drain the water and set the lentils aside.
  2. In a 3 litre stove-top pressure cooker take the rinsed lentils, chopped onions, chopped tomatoes and finely chopped ginger.
  3. Also add the turmeric powder, red chili powder and water to the cooker.
  4. Mix well.
  5. Pressure cook for 5 to 6 whistles on medium flame till the moong lentils are softened well. Once the pressure settles down, remove the lid and stir the dal.
  6. If the dal looks thick, then add some water and simmer for 1 to 2 minutes.
  7. Add salt. Mix very well and keep aside. Check the taste and if required you can add more salt.
