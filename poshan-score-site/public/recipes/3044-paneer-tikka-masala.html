Paneer tikka masala

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 40 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerLunchSchool lunchboxFrom 3 years

Paneer tikka masala scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 200 grms or 1 cup paneer (cottage cheese cubes)
  • 1 cup green capsicum
  • 1 cup onion
  • ¾ cup thick or hung curd
  • 1 tbsp besan (gram flour)
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala powder
  • 1 tsp kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ½ tsp ginger paste
  • ½ tsp garlic paste
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 to 3 tsp oil for roasting
  • 3 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 4-5 cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 dry red chilli
  • 2 cardamoms
  • 3 chopped onion
  • 1 tbsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • 4 crushed tomatoes
  • 3 tsp hung curd
  • ½ cup water
  • Marinated paneer and vegetables
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi
  • Coriander leaves

Method

For paneer tikka

  1. In a mixing bowl add 3/4 cup thick or hung curd.
  2. Then add roasted besan and all spices like ginger garlic paste, red chilli powder, garam masala, turmeric powder, lime juice and salt to taste. Mix well.
  3. Add 200 grms or 1 cup paneer cubes, diced ½ cup onion and ½ cup capsicum into it.
  4. Mix well so the marinade coats the paneer and veggies evenly.
  5. Cover the bowl and keep the marinade in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
  6. After 1 hour, Heat 2 tsp oil on tawa. The gas flame should be low to medium and arrange paneer and veggies.
  7. When one side gets browned then turns over to the other sides.
  8. Cook all sides evenly browned.
