Method

For paneer tikka

  1. In a mixing bowl add 3/4 cup thick or hung curd.
  2. Then add roasted besan and all spices like ginger garlic paste, red chilli powder, garam masala, turmeric powder, lime juice and salt to taste. Mix well.
  3. Add 200 grms or 1 cup paneer cubes, diced ½ cup onion and ½ cup capsicum into it.
  4. Mix well so the marinade coats the paneer and veggies evenly.
  5. Cover the bowl and keep the marinade in the fridge for at least 1 hour.
  6. After 1 hour, Heat 2 tsp oil on tawa. The gas flame should be low to medium and arrange paneer and veggies.
  7. When one side gets browned then turns over to the other sides.
  8. Cook all sides evenly browned.

What age is Paneer tikka masala best for?

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
