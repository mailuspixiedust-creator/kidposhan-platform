Instant Pot Broccoli Cheddar Soup

Recipe by shwetainthekitchen.com

Photo: shwetainthekitchen.com
⏱ 30 minServes 4VegetarianDinnerFrom 2 years

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 1 small yellow onion (diced)
  • 1 tablespoon garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup carrots (shredded)
  • 3 cups broccoli florets
  • 1 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper
  • ½ teaspoon dijon mustard
  • ¼ teaspoon paprika
  • ¼ teaspoon ground cayenne pepper (or red pepper flakes)
  • 2 cups shredded cheddar cheese (medium or sharp)
  • 2 cups heavy cream (or half and half)
  • ⅛ cup cornstarch
  • ¼ cup cold water (for cornstarch slurry)
  • 3 cups veggie broth (or water)
  • ¼ teaspoon sugar

Method

Stovetop Method

  1. Melt butter in a large dutch oven or pot over medium-high heat.
  2. Add the onion and saute till the onions turn translucent.
  3. Stir in the garlic and saute for another minute.Add the broccoli florets, carrots, spices, and broth.
  4. Mix well.
  5. Bring to a simmer then reduce heat to medium-low and cover and cook for 15-20 minutes or until the broccoli and carrots are cooked through.
  6. Keep stirring intermittently.
  7. Stir in cream and cheddar cheese and simmer for 2-3 minutes until the cheese is melted.
  8. Make the cornstarch slurry by mixing cornstarch in cold water. And add it to the soup. Mix to combine.
  9. Let the soup simmer for a couple of minutes or till it thickens. Taste and adjust seasoning if needed. Add sugar (if needed to balance the flavors)
  10. Broccoli Cheddar Soup is ready to serve. Garnish with some cheddar cheese and croutons.
