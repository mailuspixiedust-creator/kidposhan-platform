Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 200 grams paneer (sliced into 4 pieces)
  • ½ cup mustard oil
  • 2 cups warm water
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 cardamom
  • 2 cloves
  • ½ inch cinnamon stick
  • 200 grams Dabur homemade tomato puree (approx.¾ cup)
  • 3 teaspoons kashmiri chilli powder
  • 2 teaspoons coriander powder
  • ¼ teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 ½ teaspoons dry ginger powder
  • ¾ teaspoon fennel seeds powder
  • ¾ teaspoon salt
  • A pinch sugar
  • ½ teaspoon dry mint (crushed)
  • ⅛ teaspoon cardamom powder

Method

  • Heat oil in a kadai or deep pan till hot, add 2 paneer slices and fry on low to medium heat for 3-4 minutes till they are golden and crisp on the edges.
  • Transfer to a bowl filled with 2 cups of warm water.
  • Follow the same process to fry the remaining paneer slices.
  • Remove the oil from the kadai while retaining 1 ½ tablespoons of oil.
  • Add whole spices - bay leaf, cardamom, cloves, cinnamon and saute them for a few seconds till fragrant.
  • Add tomato puree and cook on medium to low for 2-3 minutes till oil separates from the side.
  • Add spice powders - kashmiri chilli, coriander, cumin, dry ginger, fennel seeds, salt, sugar, ½ cup paneer soaked water, mix well and cook for 3-4 minutes on low till the puree absorbs the spices.
  • Drain the fried paneer slices and add to the gravy in the kadai. cook for 3-4 minutes on low till the paneer is lightly coated with the gravy.
  • Add dry mint, cardamom powder, mix well, take off the heat and serve.
