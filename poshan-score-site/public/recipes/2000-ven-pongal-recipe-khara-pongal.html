Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • ½ cup rice (100 grams, any short rice or millets, oats, quinoa)
  • ½ cup moong dal (100 grams, skinned split mung beans)
  • 3 ¼ to 3½ cups water (800 to 840 ml, more if needed)
  • ½ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons ghee (½ tbsp more for serving)
  • 10 cashew nuts (or as needed, optional)
  • ¾ teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • ½ teaspoon black pepper corn (crushed lightly)
  • 1 inch ginger (peeled, grated or sliced or chopped)
  • 1 sprig curry leaves
  • ⅛ teaspoon asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 green chili (slit, optional, skip if offering to god)

Method

Preparation

  1. Add ½ cup dal to a pot or pressure cooker. (If you prefer to rinse the lentils before roasting, follow my step by step photo instructions in the post).
  2. Dry roast on a low to medium flame till it begin to smell nice & aromatic.
  3. Do not brown or discolor the moong dal.
  4. This step is optional however pongal gets a real aroma from the roasted dal.
  5. Add ½ cup rice to the roasted dal.
  6. Wash them at least three times.
  7. Drain the water completely.
  8. Pour 3¼ to 3½ cups water.
  9. Add ½ teaspoon salt.
  10. Cook the rice & dal to a soft texture.
  11. For pressure cooking: If cooking directly in the pressure cooker, then cook for 2 to 3 whistles on a medium flame.
  12. If cooking in a bowl, then place it in the cooker and pressure cook for 4 whistles.
  13. When the pressure releases naturally, open the lid.
  14. To cook in pot: You can also cook the rice & dal in a pot until soft & mushy. Add more water if needed as it cooks.
  15. Open the cooker when the pressure drops. If you prefer, you can mash up the cooked rice & dal a bit to get creamy consistency. It should be mushy and soggy.
  16. If it is too dry, add some boiling water to bring it to a consistency you like.
  17. Boil It for a few minutes.
  18. It should not be very runny or dry.
  19. Add more salt if needed and stir.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
