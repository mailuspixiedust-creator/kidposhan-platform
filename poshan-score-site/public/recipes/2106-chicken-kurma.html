Chicken Kurma

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1/2 kg Chicken (whole cut or boneless, whole cut country chicken preferred)
  • 1 cup Onion (small onion / pearl onion / sambar onion)
  • 2 Green chili (slit open, add dry red chilies instead)
  • 1/2 cup Tomato
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 1/2 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp Fennel seeds
  • 1 small Bay leaf
  • 4 each Cloves
  • 2 1 inch Cinnamon
  • 2 Cardamom
  • 2 petals Black stone flower / kalpasi (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 Red Chili powder (hot variety)
  • 1 tbsp Coriander powder or All purpose curry powder (kuzhambu milagai thool) (*** refer notes)
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 1/4 cup Coriander leaves (for garnish)
  • 4 leaves Curry leaves
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1/2 cup Fresh grated coconut
  • 4 whole Cashew nut
  • 1 tsp Poppy seeds
  • 1/2 cup Water

Method

  • To start with first grind coconut, fennel, poppy seeds and cashew with some water and make a thick paste.
  • Chop all the veggies and keep them ready.
  • Clean and chicken, wash and keep it ready.
  • Chicken can be marinated with little turmeric and salt but not really necessary.
  • Heat oil and ghee in a sauce pan and splutter cumin and fennel seeds.
  • Also add the whole dry garam masalas like bay leaf, cloves, cardamom, black stone flower, cinnamon & curry leaves.
  • Saute onions & green chilli until golden brown. I have used combination if sambar onions and regular onions.
  • Add ginger garlic paste and saute till it cooks a bit.
  • Now add tomatoes along with turmeric powder, red chili powder, coriander powder and salt.
  • Bring the flame to medium, cover and cook for 3-4 minutes.
  • The chicken will release water and cook along with the onion mixture.
  • Saute until the masala powder cooks.
  • Add water at this stage.
  • Now bring the flame to medium low setting, cover and cook the gravy for 10 minutes.
  • Alternatively you may pressure cook for 2 whistles.
  • Once done, the oil will separate from the gravy as shown.
  • Once done, add the coconut paste, throw in the coriander leaves and curry leaves. Simmer for few minutes and switch off!
  • Serve as a side for chapati or have it with plain rice and curry!

    Common questions

    Is Chicken Kurma healthy for kids?

    Chicken Kurma scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is Chicken Kurma best for?

    It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years and 16–18 years, and lowest, 80, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
