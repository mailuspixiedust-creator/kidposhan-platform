Lemon Rice Recipe (Chitranna)

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups raw rice - uncooked (or 4 cups cooked rice, use aged basmati or read notes for short grain)
  • 2 tablespoon oil (coconut, gingelly or any other)
  • ¼ cup peanuts (or cashews, raw or roasted)
  • ¾ teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1 ½ teaspoon chana dal (Bengal gram, optional)
  • 1 teaspoon urad dal (skinned black gram, optional)
  • 2 green chilies (slit or 2 dried red chilies broken or use both)
  • ½ tablespoon ginger (peeled and finely chopped)
  • 1 to 2 sprigs fresh curry leaves
  • ½ teaspoon organic turmeric powder (haldi)
  • ½ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 pinch hing ((asafoetida) (optional))
  • 2 to 3 tablespoons lemon juice (or lime juice as needed, 1 medium lemon)

Method

Stovetop Lemon Rice

  1. Omit this if you have precooked rice - Add raw rice to a strainer and rinse under running water.
  2. Transfer to a pot and pour water (read notes).
  3. Bring to a rolling boil and reduce the heat to medium.
  4. Cook covered until the rice is cooked through. (takes me 15 mins)
  5. Cool the rice completely & fluff with a fork.
  6. Pour ½ tablespoon oil to a pan and add the nuts.
  7. Fry them on a medium heat, until golden and aromatic.
  8. Remove to a plate and reserve aside to garnish. wipe down with a kitchen paper.
  9. Pour the remaining oil and add mustard, urad dal & chana dal. Fry on a medium flame until the dal turns golden & aromatic. (for 2 to 3 mins)
  10. Add ginger, green chilis, red chilies(optional) and curry leaves. Fry them until the leaves turn crisp for about 30 seconds.
  11. Stir in hing, turmeric and salt.
  12. Immediately pour 3 tbsps water & mix.
  13. Cook until the water evaporates and the lentils soften. (If you want the dal very soft, add 3 tbsps more and cook down).
  14. Turn off the stove.
  15. Add the cooled rice and squeeze the lemon juice. Give a good mix.
  16. Taste test to adjust salt and lemon. Garnish with roasted nuts. Serve lemon rice with plain yogurt, papad or pickle.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
