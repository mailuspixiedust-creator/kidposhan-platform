Chatpata Chicken Bites in Air Fryer

Ingredients

  • 450 grams boneless chicken thighs (cut into 2 inch piece)
  • 1 tablespoon curds or greek yogurt (whisked)
  • 8 garlic cloves (crushed)
  • ½ inch ginger (crushed)
  • 1 teaspoon red chilli flakes
  • 1 teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon meat masala (refer note 1)
  • 1 teaspoon kashmiri chilli powder
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • 1 teaspoon crushed pepper
  • 2 tablespoons mustard oil
  • ½ cup thinly sliced onions
  • 2 green chillies (slit into 4)
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 teaspoon chat masala

Method

Marinating chicken

  1. Take chicken and all the ingredients listed for marination in a bowl, mix well and set aside to marinate for 30 minutes.

Air frying chicken

  1. Add marinated chicken to airfryer basket, spray 2-3 teaspoons of oil, air fry for 7 minutes.
  2. Shake the basket well, brush with 2-3 teaspoons of oil and air fry for 5 minutes.

To serve

  1. When chicken is still hot, transfer to a bowl, add sliced onions, green chillies, finely chopped coriander leaves, sprinkle chat masala, toss well and serve hot.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
