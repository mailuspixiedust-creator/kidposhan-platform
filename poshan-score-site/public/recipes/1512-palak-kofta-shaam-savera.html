Method

Making The Paneer Stuffing

  1. Combine the grated paneer with rice flour, garam masala, nutmeg, saffron and salt.
  2. Divide the mixture into equal portions and shape them into small, smooth balls. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

Common questions

Is Palak Kofta (Shaam Savera) healthy for kids?

Palak Kofta (Shaam Savera) scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Palak Kofta (Shaam Savera) best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 10–12 years, and lowest, 50, for adults. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
