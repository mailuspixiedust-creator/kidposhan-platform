Kerala Egg Roast

Recipe by myfoodstory.com

Photo: myfoodstory.com
⏱ 27 minServes 4EggDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Kerala Egg Roast scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 5 boiled eggs
  • 3 tablespoons coconut oil
  • ½ teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 15 - 20 curry leaves
  • 2 red chilli broken into 2
  • 1 green chilli broken into 2
  • 1 ½ inches ginger crushed
  • 10 cloves garlic crushed
  • 1 ½ cups onions (sliced)
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1 ½ tablespoons kashmiri chilli powder
  • 1 tablespoon coriander powder
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder
  • ½ teaspoon pepper powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ¾ cup tomatoes (finely chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped coriander leaves

Method

  • Fry oil in a pan, add mustard seeds & once they crackle, add curry leaves, red & green chilli, crushed ginger and garlic, onions & fry till they turn translucent.
  • Add spice powders, salt and tomatoes. Cook on medium heat & saute for 3 minutes.
  • Add warm water & cook on low for 10 minutes, till it roasts & oil separates from the sides of the pan.
  • Add boiled eggs & roast on low for 5 minutes.
  • Garnish with coriander leaves and serve.
