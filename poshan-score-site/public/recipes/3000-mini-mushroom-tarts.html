Mini Mushroom Tarts

Ingredients

  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped garlic
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped celery
  • 500 grams mushrooms (chopped into ⅛ inch cubes)
  • 2 tablespoons all purpose flour (maida)
  • ½ cup + 3 tablespoons milk
  • 2 tablespoons cream cheese
  • ½ + ⅛ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon crushed pepper
  • ¾ teaspoon italian seasoning
  • 18-20 mini tart shells
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped spring onion greens

Method

  • Heat butter in a pan, add garlic, celery and saute on low for a minute till they turn fragrant. Take care not to brown the garlic.
  • Add mushrooms, saute for 3-4 minutes till the mushrooms are cooked well.
  • Add flour & stir continuously on low for 1-2 minutes till they get mildly roasted.
  • Add milk, while stirring continuously and cook on low for 1-2 minutes till the milk mixes well with the flour, cooks well and turns creamy. Refer note 1
  • Add cream cheese, salt, pepper, italian seasoning, mix well and cook for 1-2 minutes till the mushrooms absorb the seasoning.
  • At this stage, if the mixture gets too dry you may add 1 ½ - 2 tablespoons of milk to adjust the consistency to be creamy.
  • Add 1 ½ tablespoons of the creamy mushroom mix to each of the tart shells, garnish with spring onion greens and serve immediately.
