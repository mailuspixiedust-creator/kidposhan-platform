Method

Marinate the chicken

  1. Clean the chicken, especially the stomach cavity - remove any traces of blood, wash and allow to drain completely or pat it dry.
  2. Gently poke the chicken all over with a fork.
  3. Gently run your fingers between the flesh and the skin to form pockets.
  4. Take care not to tear the skin.
  5. In a large bowl mix the ingredients for the marinade and apply it all over the chicken, inside the stomach cavity and between the skin and the flesh (into the pockets).
  6. Cover the pan with aluminium foil or cling wrap and refrigerate for at least one hour or overnight for best results.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
