Roast Chicken with Bread & Giblet Stuffing

Recipe by ruchikrandhap.com

Photo: ruchikrandhap.com
⏱ 2 h 10 minServes 4Non-vegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

Ingredients

  • 1.5-1.7 kg whole chicken with skin * see notes
  • 1-1/2 tsp pepper powder
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala powder
  • juice of 1 lime
  • 1 tsp olive oil or ghee
  • 4 slices bread cubed (trim & discard the hard edges)
  • 1 medium sized potato boiled (peeled and cubed)
  • 1-2 small green chillies finely chopped (adjust to taste)
  • 1-1/2 " ginger finely chopped
  • 200 gms chicken liver & heart washed (boiled and cut into tiny cubes)
  • 25 gm (2 tbsp black currants, black kishmish)
  • 50 gm mixed nuts (cashewnuts & pistas or almonds roughly chopped)
  • 2 small onions (or 1 large finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp stew masala powder * see notes
  • 1 cup loosely packed mint leaves roughly chopped
  • 2-3 tbsp coriander leaves roughly chopped
  • 1 tsp sugar (or increase it upto 1 tbsp for a sweeter stuffing)
  • salt to taste (approx 1/2 tsp)
  • 3-4 tbsp ghee (or as required)
  • 2 large potatoes
  • 2 tbsp cheese spread
  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • freshly ground pepper to taste
  • 1/2 cucumber peeled and finely chopped
  • 2 small spring onion whites finely chopped
  • 1 tbsp coriander leaves finely chopped
  • 1/2 tbsp mint leaves finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp lime juice
  • olive oil
  • 4-5 baby carrots peeled (or 2 regular big carrots cut into finger sized pieces)
  • 12-15 french beans stringed
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • salt & pepper to taste
  • 1/2 a medium sized apple thinly sliced

Method

Marinate the chicken

  1. Clean the chicken, especially the stomach cavity - remove any traces of blood, wash and allow to drain completely or pat it dry.
  2. Gently poke the chicken all over with a fork.
  3. Gently run your fingers between the flesh and the skin to form pockets.
  4. Take care not to tear the skin.
  5. In a large bowl mix the ingredients for the marinade and apply it all over the chicken, inside the stomach cavity and between the skin and the flesh (into the pockets).
  6. Cover the pan with aluminium foil or cling wrap and refrigerate for at least one hour or overnight for best results.
