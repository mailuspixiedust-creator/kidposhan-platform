Sweet Corn Curry (No Onion No Garlic)

Ingredients

  • 3 tomatoes roughly chopped
  • 1 celery stick chopped (optional)
  • 8 cashews
  • 1 -inch ginger, peeled and chopped
  • 1 green chili chopped
  • 2 tsp oil
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds
  • ¼ tsp asafoetida (Hing)
  • Prepared tomato puree
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 cups sweet corn (fresh or frozen)
  • ½ cup water or as required
  • 2 tbsp cream or whisked yogurt
  • 1 tsp Kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 2 tbsp chopped cilantro

Method

Making The Tomato Puree

  1. Add chopped tomatoes, celery, cashews, ginger, and green chili into the blender jar.
  2. Blend everything into a smooth puree.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
