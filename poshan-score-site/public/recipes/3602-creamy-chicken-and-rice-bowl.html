Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 150 grams boneless chicken thighs (cut into ¾ inch cubes)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • ½ teaspoon oregano
  • 1 teaspoon cornflour
  • 2 pinches baking soda
  • 4 teaspoons butter (divided)
  • 2 teaspoons finely chopped garlic
  • 2 teaspoons all purpose flour (maida)
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 cup blanched mixed vegetables (beans, carrots, broccoli)
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 teaspoons butter
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped spring onions
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped coriander leaves

Method

Marinating chicken

  1. Add chicken to a bowl, add all ingredients for marination, give a good mix and set aside to marinate for 30 minutes.

Making creamy chicken

  1. Heat 2 teaspoons of butter in a pan, add marinated chicken and saute on medium to high for 2-3 minutes till the chicken is cooked well and turns pale golden.
  2. Add garlic and saute on high for a few seconds till fragrant.
  3. Add all purpose flour and saute on low for 1-2 minutes till the rawness of flour goes.
  4. Keep stirring continuously to avoid the flour getting burnt.
  5. Add milk gradually while stirring continuously to avoid any lumps being formed.
  6. Bring to a boil while stirring and simmer for 2-3 minutes till the sauce thickens and turns creamy.
  7. Add salt & oregano, mix well and take off the heat.

Making herb rice

  1. While the sauce is cooking, heat another pan with 2 teaspoons of butter, add garlic and saute on medium for a few seconds till lightly roasted.
  2. Add rice, salt, spring onions, coriander leaves, give a good toss, cook for 2-3 minutes till the rice absorbs the seasonings and take off the heat.

Serving

  1. For each serving, add 1 cup of herb rice to a bowl, add 2-3 ladles of creamy chicken and serve immediately.
