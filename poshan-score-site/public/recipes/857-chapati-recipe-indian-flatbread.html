Ingredients

  • 2 cups chakki atta (stone ground whole wheat flour)
  • ¾ cup warm water (or hot water, 2 to 4 tbsps more as required read notes)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (optional)
  • 1 to 1 ½ tablespoons oil (optional, read notes)
  • 2 to 2 ½ tablespoons ghee (or oil)

Method

Make Dough

  1. Add atta to a wide mixing bowl & make a well in the center.
  2. Add oil and salt if using.
  3. Pour warm or hot water and begin to mix.
  4. If using hot water use a spoon first and then your hand.
  5. Mix well to form a soft dough, splashing more water only as required. Knead the dough for a minute or 2. It has to be soft and not sticky or crumbly.
  6. Wet your hands and smear water all over the dough ball. Cover and rest for a minimum of 30 mins. You can leave up to 2 hours at room temperature.
