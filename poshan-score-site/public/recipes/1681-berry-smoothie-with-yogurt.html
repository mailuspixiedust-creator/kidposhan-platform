Ingredients

  • 1 cup Frozen Blueberries (or use 125 grams)
  • 1 ½ cup Frozen Strawberries
  • 2 Banana (medium size or 4 small)
  • 1 cup Yogurt (unsweetened)
  • 8-10 Dates (seedless and roughly chopped)
  • ½ cup Water (optional for blending the smoothie, read notes)

Method

  • To a blender jar, add frozen strawberries and blueberries, frozen banana, dates and yogurt.
  • Blitz well until thick and creamy. Adjust consistency by adding 1/2 cup of water (optional). Serve chilled.
