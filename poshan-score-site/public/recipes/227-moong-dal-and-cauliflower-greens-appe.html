Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup yellow moong dal (split yellow gram) , soaked and drained
  • 1/4 cup finely chopped cauliflower greens , washed and drained
  • 1 tsp green chilli paste
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1/8 tsp salt (namak) or as advised by doctor / dietitian
  • 1 tsp oil for greasing
