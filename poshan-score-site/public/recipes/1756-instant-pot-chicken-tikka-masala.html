Instant Pot Chicken Tikka Masala

Recipe by pipingpotcurry.com

Photo: pipingpotcurry.com
⏱ 30 minServes 4Non-vegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 1 years

Instant Pot Chicken Tikka Masala scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb Chicken breasts
  • 1/4 cup Yogurt (thick)
  • 3/4 tablespoon Ginger (grated)
  • 3/4 tablespoon Garlic (minced)
  • 1 tablespoon Lime juice
  • 2 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (mild, adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Ground Turmeric (Haldi powder)
  • 1 teaspoon Garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Garam masala
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • 3 tablespoon Ghee or Oil (divided)
  • 1 cup Onion (finely diced)
  • 1 teaspoon Ginger (grated)
  • 1 teaspoon Garlic (minced)
  • 1 cup Tomato Puree (canned, do not use tomato paste)
  • 3/4 cup Water
  • 1/2 cup Heavy cream
  • 1 tablespoon Dried Fenugreek leaves (Kasoori Methi)
  • Cilantro (to garnish)
  • 1 cup Basmati rice
  • 1 1/4 cup Water
  • 1 tablespoon Ghee or Oil (optional)

Method

Marinate the chicken

  1. Combine all ingredients for the marinade (except chicken) in a bowl and mix well.
  2. Add chicken and coat on each side with the marinade.
  3. Let it rest for anywhere between 30 minutes to 8 hours in the refrigerator.
