Gujarati dhebri

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 25 minServes 4VegetarianLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Gujarati dhebri scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup chopped methi leaves
  • ½ cup poha
  • 2-3 green chilli
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 4-5 garlic cloves
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • ½ cup coriander leaves
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • 1 medium size grated tomatoes
  • ½ tsp fennel seeds
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds
  • ½ tsp ajwain
  • Pinch of hing
  • 1 tsp red chilli flakes
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ¼ cup besan (gram flour)
  • ¼ cup bajra flour
  • ¼ cup wheat flour
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Oil for frying
  • ½ cup thick curd
  • Pinch of salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper powder
  • ¼ tsp red chilli flakes
  • 2 tbsp green chutney

Method

Making crispy dhebri

  1. Wash and chop the methi leaves, then set them aside.
  2. In a mixer jar, add 2-3 green chilies, 1-inch ginger, 4-5 garlic cloves, and 1 tsp cumin seeds. Grind it into a coarse paste without adding any water.
  3. Wash ½ cup poha, drain the water, and mash it.
  4. In a mixing bowl, combine the mashed poha with 1 cup chopped methi leaves, ½ cup coriander leaves, ½ cup chopped onion, 1 medium-sized grated tomato, the ground chili-ginger-garlic paste, ½ tsp fennel seeds, 1 tbsp white sesame seeds, ½ tsp ajwain, a pinch of hing, 1 tsp red chili flakes, ½ tsp turmeric powder, 1 tsp coriander powder, ½ tsp cumin powder, salt to taste, ½ tsp garam masala, ¼ cup besan, ¼ cup bajra flour, ¼ cup wheat flour, and 2 tbsp oil.
  5. Mix well and knead into a dough.
  6. Heat oil in a pan. Grease your hands with oil, pinch off small portions of the dough, and shape them into round balls.
  7. Deep fry the dhebri in hot oil over medium flame, stirring occasionally, until they turn slightly golden brown.
  8. Remove the dhebri from the oil. While still hot, press them slightly and fry them again in hot oil.
  9. Fry on medium-high flame until crispy and golden brown on the outside.
  10. Serve the crispy dhebri with spicy dahi chutney or tomato ketchup.

Common questions

Is Gujarati dhebri healthy for kids?

Gujarati dhebri scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

What age is Gujarati dhebri best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 60, for 13–15 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
