No bread sandwich

No bread sandwich scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup poha
  • ½ cup rava
  • ½ cup curd
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup water or as required
  • 2 chopped green chilli
  • ½ tsp ginger paste
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • ¼ cup chopped capsicum
  • ¼ cup chopped carrot
  • ¼ cup boiled green peas
  • ¼ cup boiled sweet corn
  • Some coriander leaves
  • ½ tsp red chilli flakes
  • 1 tsp chaat masala
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • Pinch of hing
  • ¼ tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • Oil for greasing and roasting

Method

  • In a mixing bowl, add poha and wash it. Remove water, and poha become slightly soft.
  • Add soaked poha into a mixture jar, also add rava, curd, salt, and water. Grind it into a thick batter. Do not add more water while grinding the batter.
  • Take out the batter into the mixing bowl and whisk it slightly.
  • Cover and rest the batter for 15 minutes.
  • Now add chopped green chili, ginger paste, chopped onion, capsicum, carrot, boiled corn and peas, some coriander leaves, red chili flakes, chaat masala, black pepper powder, and salt. mix well.
  • Then in a tadka pan, oil, mustard seeds, urad dal, curry leaves, and hing. Saute it and add tadka to batter. Mix well.
  • Now add ¼ tsp baking soda and some lemon juice. Mix well.
  • Then grease hand sandwich toaster with oil and add 2 ladle of batter into it.
  • Close the toaster and roast it on medium flame till it is slightly golden brown from both sides.
  • Also, you can cook nasta in a regular cooking pan or an appe pan. (Refer to video)
  • Serve nasta with tomato ketch up or chutney.

    Common questions

    Is No bread sandwich healthy for kids?

    No bread sandwich scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, fibre, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is No bread sandwich best for?

    It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 60, for 13–15 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
