Potato Green Pea Tikki Recipe (Baby And Toddlers Tikki)

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup boiled and mashed potatoes
  • 1/4 cup boiled and coarsely crushed green peas
  • 2 tbsp grated paneer (cottage cheese)
  • 2 tbsp grated processed cheese
  • 1 tbsp butter
  • 2 tbsp roasted and crushed peanuts
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • a pinch of freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch)
  • oil for greasing and cooking

Method

  • To make Potato Green Pea Tikki, Baby and Toddlers Tikki, In a deep bowl put 1/2 cup boiled and mashed potatoes.
  • Add 1/4 cup boiled and coarsely crushed green peas.
  • Add 2 tbsp grated paneer (cottage cheese).
  • Add 2 tbsp grated processed cheese, add 2 tbsp roasted and crushed peanuts, add 1 tbsp butter and mix well.
  • Divide the mixture into 20 equal portions, shape each portion into a round and flatten slightly to make coin sized tikkis.
  • Heat a non-stick tava (griddle) and grease it lightly using a little oil.
  • Place the tikkis on it and cook them using a little oil, till they turn light brown in colour from both the sides.
  • Serve Potato Green Pea Tikki, Baby and Toddlers Tikki warm.
