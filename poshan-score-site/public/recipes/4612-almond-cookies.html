Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 3/4 cup wheat flour
  • 1/8 heaped cup almonds (yields 1/4 cup powder)
  • 1/3 cup powdered sugar
  • 1/4 cup butter (at room temperature)
  • 1/4 teaspoon cardamom powder
  • 1/8 cup + 1 tablespoon milk
  • 2 tablespoon sliced almonds
  • 1/8 teaspoon salt

Method

  • Take almonds in a grinder and grind to a fine powder, set aside.
  • To a mixing bowl add wheat flour.
  • Add powdered almond flour, powdered sugar and cardamom powder.
  • You can blanch almond peel the skin then powder it too.
  • But it needs time for drying up so took this short route.
  • Add required salt. Mix well.
  • Add butter to it. Mix well to form crumbles.
  • Add milk to it and mix well with your finger tips, do not knead. Form a dough.
  • Using a tablespoon take the dough (for equal sized cookies). Roll between your palms, slightly flatten it.
  • Preheat oven at 180 deg C for 10 minutes.
  • Dip the top in silvered almond.
  • Bake in preheated oven at 180 deg C for 12-15 minutes or until the top turns golden.
  • Enjoy Almond Cookies!
