Mushroom Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop)

Mushroom Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 2 cups white mushroom (about 6 oz) (add more if desired)
  • 1 medium onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 medium tomato (finely chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste
  • 2 green chillies (slit lengthwise)
  • 1/4 cup yogurt (curd) (OR coconut milk, vegans can use dairy free yogurt)
  • 1/4 cup mint (pudina)
  • 2 tablespoons cilantro (coriander leaves)
  • 1.5 cups water (depends on the quality of the rice)
  • 2 tablespoon oil (or ghee)
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1/4 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon coriander powder (optional)
  • 1.5 teaspoon biryani masala (or garam masala powder)
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 inch cinnamon stick
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 2 cardamom
  • 1 star anise (optional)

Method

Stovetop Pot:

  1. Follow all the steps as Instant Pot, Then close the glass lid and cook on medium-low flame for 10 to 15 minutes or until the rice is cooked through.
  2. Remove the lid and fluff the rice with a fork.
  3. NOTE: Add an extra ½ cup of water when cooking in a Pot.

Common questions

Is Mushroom Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) healthy for kids?

Mushroom Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, saturated fat, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

What age is Mushroom Biryani (Instant Pot & Stovetop) best for?

It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
