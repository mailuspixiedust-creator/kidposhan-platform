Yeasted Doughnuts

Ingredients

  • 4 1/2 cups (22½ ounces/638 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup (3½ ounces/99 grams) granulated sugar
  • 1 teaspoon instant or rapid-rise yeast
  • 1 1/2 cups (368 grams) milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons table salt
  • 8 tablespoons (114 grams) unsalted butter, cut into ½-inch pieces and softened
  • 2 quarts (1680 grams) vegetable oil, for frying
  • 3 1/4 cups (13 ounces/369 grams) confectioners' sugar
  • 1/2 cup (114 grams) hot water
  • Pinch table salt

Method

FOR THE DOUGHNUTS:

  1. Stir flour, sugar, and yeast together in bowl of stand mixer.
  2. Add milk and egg and mix with rubber spatula until all ingredients are moistened.
  3. Fit stand mixer with dough hook and mix on medium-low speed until cohesive mass forms, about 2 minutes, scraping down bowl if necessary.
  4. Cover bowl with plastic wrap and let stand for 20 minutes.
  5. Add salt and mix on medium-low speed until dough is smooth and elastic and clears sides of bowl, 5 to 7 minutes.
  6. With mixer running, add butter, a few pieces at a time, and continue to mix until butter is fully incorporated and dough is smooth and elastic and clears sides of bowl, 7 to 13 minutes longer, scraping down bowl halfway through mixing.
  7. Transfer dough to lightly greased large bowl, flip dough, and form into ball.
  8. Cover bowl with plastic.
  9. Let sit at room temperature for 1 hour.
  10. Transfer to refrigerator and chill overnight (or up to 48 hours).
  11. Adjust oven racks to lowest and middle positions.
  12. Place loaf pan on lower rack.
  13. Line rimmed baking sheet with parchment paper and grease parchment.
  14. Transfer dough to lightly floured counter.
  15. Press into 8-inch square of even thickness, expelling as much air as possible.
  16. Roll dough into 10 by 13-inch rectangle, about ½ inch thick.
  17. Using 3-inch round cutter dipped in flour, cut 12 rounds.
  18. Using 1-inch cutter dipped in flour, cut hole out of center of each round.
  19. Transfer doughnuts and holes to prepared sheet. (If desired, use 1-inch cutter to cut small rounds from remaining dough.
  20. Transfer to sheet with doughnuts.) Bring kettle or small saucepan of water to boil.
  21. Pour 1 cup boiling water into loaf pan.
  22. Place sheet on upper rack, uncovered.
  23. Close oven and allow doughnuts to rise until dough increases in height by 50 percent and springs back very slowly when pressed with your knuckle, 45 minutes to 1 hour.

Common questions

Is Yeasted Doughnuts healthy for kids?

Yeasted Doughnuts scores 50 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 5 of the 10 checks and loses points on added sugar, saturated fat, fibre, base-ingredient integrity.

What age is Yeasted Doughnuts best for?

It scores highest, 60 out of 100, for 10–12 years and 13–15 years, and lowest, 40, for adults. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
