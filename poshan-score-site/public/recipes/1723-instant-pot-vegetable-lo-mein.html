Instant Pot Vegetable Lo Mein

Recipe by pipingpotcurry.com

Instant Pot Vegetable Lo Mein scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Ingredients

  • 8 ounces Noodles (Spaghetti works best, you can also use thicker chow mein or hakka noodles)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Black Pepper (adjust to taste)
  • Sesame seeds (for garnish, if desired)
  • 2 cups Vegetable Broth (or water)
  • 2 teaspoon Sesame oil
  • 1 teaspoon Ginger (paste)
  • 1 teaspoon Garlic (minced)
  • 3 tablespoon Soy Sauce
  • 1 tablespoon Vinegar (I used rice vinegar)
  • 1-2 teaspoon Red chili paste (adjust to taste, I used sambal oelek)
  • 1 teaspoon Brown sugar
  • 1/2 cup Cabbage (thinly sliced)
  • 1 Carrot (peeled and sliced into matchsticks)
  • 1/2 cup Bell Pepper (thinly sliced)
  • 1/4 cup Scallion or Green onion (white and green part divided)
  • 1/2 cup Broccoli (cut into very small florets)

Method

  • In a bowl, add all the sauce ingredients. Whisk until evenly combined. Taste and adjust as needed.
  • Add the noodles to the instant pot (break spaghetti in half).
  • Pour the above sauce into the pot above the noodles. Add vegetables on top except broccoli. (check the video)
  • Seal your Instant Pot and set to manual, high pressure, 4 minutes. (see note for cooking time)
  • When Instant Pot is done cooking, do a quick pressure release.
  • Open the lid and add the broccoli florets (make sure they are cut into small pieces).
  • Stir the noodles with tongs several times, breaking up any that may have clumped together during cooking.
  • Add salt and black pepper to taste.
  • Close the lid for 5 minute so the broccoli get cooked (do not pressure cook in this step).
  • Open the lid. Sprinkle some green onions and sesame seeds and serve.

    Common questions

    Is Instant Pot Vegetable Lo Mein healthy for kids?

    Instant Pot Vegetable Lo Mein scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, hfss status.

    What age is Instant Pot Vegetable Lo Mein best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 70, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
