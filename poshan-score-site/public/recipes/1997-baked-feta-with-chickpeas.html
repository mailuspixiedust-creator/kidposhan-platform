Baked Feta with Chickpeas

Ingredients

  • 1 bunch Swiss chard, stems and leaves divided
  • 1 onion, cut into wedges
  • 6 garlic cloves, cut into slices
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil, divided
  • 1/4 cup harissa paste (make your own or use Mina brand- adjust to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 2 teaspoons maple syrup
  • 1/2 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 3 cups chickpeas (or 2 15 ounce cans)
  • 8 ounces feta, cut into large squares
  • salt and black pepper to taste

Method

  • Preheat oven to 425° F.
  • Roughly chop the chard leaves and set them aside for later.
  • Cut chard stems into 1-inch pieces and place on a sheet pan (or in a large baking dish) with onions and sliced garlic.
  • Drizzle with olive oil and a sprinkle of salt and pepper if desired.
  • Bake stems & garlic for 10 minutes.
  • In a bowl, stir together harissa paste, tomato paste, water, lemon juice, maple syrup, and smoked paprika. Add chickpeas and toss to combine.
  • Remove the sheet pan from the oven and add the chard leaves on top, drizzle with olive oil and sprinkle with salt and pepper.
  • Add the chickpea mixture evenly over top of the chard.
  • Nestle the feta and coat it in the sauce by flipping it over.
  • You can broil for a few minutes, but watch carefully.
  • with Aleppo pepper or red pepper flakes and fresh chopped parsley.
  • over basmati rice or quinoa as a main dish, or place in a serving dish with pita bread, pita chips, or crusty bread as an appetizer (best warm).
