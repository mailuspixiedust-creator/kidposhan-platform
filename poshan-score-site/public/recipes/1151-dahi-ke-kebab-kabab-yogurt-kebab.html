Dahi Ke Kebab (Kabab) / Yogurt Kebab

Ingredients

  • 350 g hung curd or yogurt (about 1&¾ cups)
  • 100 g grated paneer or Chhena (about ½ cup)
  • 5 tbsp roasted besan (chickpea flour)
  • 1 tsp green chili paste
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander leaves
  • ½ tbsp finely chopped mint leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ tsp black salt (optional)
  • Black pepper powder to taste
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • 1 cup breadcrumbs (or as required)
  • Oil as needed for deep or shallow frying kebabs

Method

Making Dahi Ke Kebab Mixture

  1. Take hung curd into a mixing bowl.
  2. To this, add Paneer, chickpea flour, green chili, ginger, coriander leaves, mint leaves, salt, black salt, black pepper powder, roasted cumin powder, and garam masala.
  3. Using a spoon or spatula, mix until everything is well combined.
  4. If the mixture is still wet and not thick, you may add more chickpea flour.
  5. However, add only a tbsp of extra flour, and do not add excessively.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
