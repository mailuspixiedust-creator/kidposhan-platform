Best Creamy Tomato Soup

Ingredients

  • 1 tsp Butter
  • 1/2 Onion
  • 2 Clove Garlic
  • 1 Bayleaf
  • 3 Tomato
  • 1/2 Carrot
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 1 cup Water
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1/2 tsp Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Cream

Method

  • In a kadhai, heat butter and saute onion, garlic, and bay leaf.
  • Add tomatoes, carrots, and salt. Saute for a minute or until the tomatoes change color.
  • Add water to cover vegetables, then bring to a boil for 10 minutes.
  • Boil the tomatoes until they are soft and mushy.
  • Remove the bay leaf when they are done.
  • Blend them into a smooth paste without adding any water.
  • Then sieve the tomato paste to get rid of the residue in it.
  • You can add half a cup of water or more to make the soup thinner.
  • Then, make the soup boil and add one teaspoon of sugar, one-half teaspoon of pepper, and 1/4 teaspoon salt.
  • Mix well, making sure that all the spices are mixed in.
  • Turn off the flame and add two tablespoons of cream. Mix well
  • Finally, you should serve the soup with cream and a mint leaf.
