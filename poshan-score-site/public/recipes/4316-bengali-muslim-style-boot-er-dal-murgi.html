Method

Boiling the Dal

  1. Wash the dal under running water 2-3 times.
  2. Transfer the washed dal to a pressure cooker and add 2 cups of water to cover the dal.
  3. Now add 1/2 Tsp Turmeric Powder and 1/2 Tsp Salt as well.
  4. Cover the pressure cooker with the lid and put the vent weight.
  5. Cook on medium flame till the pressure cooker releases pressure 3 times (3 whistles).
  6. After switching the flame off, open the lid when the pressure drops completely.
  7. The dal should be properly cooked but in shape.
