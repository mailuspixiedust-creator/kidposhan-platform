Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 white onion (medium in size, roughly chopped)
  • 3 garlic cloves (peeled and crushed)
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 1/4 cup red wine (pick a red that you would drink)
  • 2 bay leaves
  • 1 28oz can crushed or whole peeled tomatoes
  • parmesan rind (optional)
  • 1 tsp salt (plus more to taste)
  • tsp tsp salt (divided)
  • 8 oz mushrooms (diced into very small pieces - shiitake, baby bella, or most any other mushroom will work)
  • 10 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 16 oz rigatoni (you may have leftover rigatoni - save them for another use)
  • 1/4 cup parmesan (grated)
  • 1/4 cup fresh basil (roughly chopped or torn)

Method

Ricotta & Mushroom Filling

  1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil over medium heat.
  2. Add mushrooms and 1/2 tsp salt and cook, stirring occasionally, until liquid from mushrooms has evaporated completely - about 10-12 minutes.
  3. Add cooked mushrooms to a bowl with ricotta, beaten egg and remaining 1 tsp salt. Allow mixture to cool in the refrigerator while you prepare the marinara.
  4. After filling has cooled for an hour, add filling to a sandwich-sized ziplock bag.
  5. Cut a very small opening into one corner of the bag (smaller than the size of the opening of the rigatoni).

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
