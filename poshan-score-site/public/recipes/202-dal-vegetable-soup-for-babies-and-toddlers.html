Ingredients

  • 1 tbsp green moong dal (split green gram) , washed and drained
  • 1/4 cup chopped tomato
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped cabbage
  • 1 tbsp chopped spinach (palak)
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)
  • 1 tbsp crumbled paneer (cottage cheese)

Method

  • If your baby like dal vegetable soup for kids and toddlers, also try other kid-friendly soups like Mixed Vegetable Soup for babies and toddlers Carrot moong dal soup for babies and toddler Apple and carrot soup with potatoes for babies and toddlers
  • Dal vegetable soup of kids and toddlers is a protein enriched soup for babies above 1 year.
  • Vitamin A and C along with calcium, magnesium and phosphorus are some other nutrients your baby will gain by way of this soup.
  • Do not add too much salt in the soup, as the habit laid in the beginning years remain with them forever.
  • Remember to sterilize all the utensils and equipments well before use.
  • Always serve small portions of any new dish tried for first time. Look for their reaction. If they show a dislike try again after a week.
  • Feed your baby healthy vegetable dal soup for kids in an upright position and not sleeping position.
  • Drain the excess water using a strainer and discard it. Keep the washed moong dal aside.
  • Meanwhile, wash and clean the tomatoes. Use bright, red, firm tomatoes for making moong dal mixed vegetable soup.
  • Place the tomatoes on a chopping board and chop them into medium sized pieces.
  • For healthy dal vegetable soup for kids, you also need to buy the perfect cabbage.
  • Light green and white in color, cabbage is a really simple vegetable to cook.
  • Choose cabbage heads that are firm and dense with shiny, crisp, colorful leaves free of cracks, bruises and blemishes.
  • Severe damage to the outer leaves is suggestive of worm damage or decay that may reside in the inner core as well, so do not buy it.
  • Wash the cabbage very well with clean water to make it free of dirt if any. Wash it under running water to prevent any sort of contamination.
  • Wipe it with a clean kitchen towel and pat dry it.
  • Using a sterilized knife chop the cabbage very finely. Keep the chopped cabbage aside.
  • Then also choose spinach that has vibrant deep green leaves and stems with no signs of yellowing.
  • The leaves should look fresh and tender, and not be wilted or bruised.
  • Avoid those that have a slimy coating as this is an indication of decay.
  • Spinach is rich in thiamin, folic acid, magnesium, fiber, etc.
  • To read in detail about the nutritional benefits of spinach, refer our article about health benefits of spinach.
  • Then wash the spinach leaves with water to get rid of any dirt.
  • Place the spinach on a chopping board and chop it and keep aside.
  • Once all the ingredients for dal vegetable soup for babies and toddlers are ready assemble them in a pressure cooker.
  • First add the washed and drained moong dal.
  • Then add the chopped tomatoes.
  • Follow it by adding cabbage.
  • Also add the spinach.
  • Add restricted quantity of salt.
  • Add 1 cup of water for cooking.
  • Mix well using a ladle.
  • Close the lid of the pressure cooker and cook healthy dal vegetable soup for kids for 2 to 3 whistles.
  • Allow the steam to escape before opening the lid.
  • Keep aside to cool slightly.
  • When the pressure cooker cools down, this is how the cooked mixture looks like.
  • Transfer the mixture of moong dal mixed vegetable soup into a mixer jar and blend it to a smooth pur&eacute;e. This is how the mixture looks upon blending.
  • Transfer the pur&eacute;e into a broad non-stick pan.
  • Add little salt if required.
  • Mix well and bring to boil.
  • Serve dal vegetable soup for babies and toddlers lukewarm. If your child likes a different mouthfeel in the soup, then garnish it with crumbled paneer.
  • If your baby loves dal vegetable soup for babies and toddlers, then also try other textured foods like Jowar Golpapdi for babies and toddlers and Mutligrain Palak Paneer Roti for Toddlers.
