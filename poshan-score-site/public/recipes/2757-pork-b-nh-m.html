Ingredients

  • 1 (12-ounce) pork tenderloin, trimmed and halved crosswise
  • 3 tablespoons fish sauce
  • 1 teaspoon grated lime zest plus 2 tablespoons juice
  • 2 tablespoons packed dark brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 1 ½ teaspoons fish sauce
  • 1 ½ teaspoons packed dark brown sugar
  • ½ teaspoon grated lime zest plus ¼ cup juice (2 limes)
  • ¼ teaspoon table salt
  • 8 ounces daikon radish, peeled and cut into 2-inch matchsticks
  • 1 small carrot, peeled and cut into 2-inch matchsticks
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • ⅓ cup mayonnaise
  • 4 teaspoons sriracha
  • 6 ounces chicken liver pâté
  • 1 (18-inch) baguette, cut into 4 equal lengths and halved lengthwise
  • ½ English cucumber, halved lengthwise and sliced thin
  • 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves and stems, trimmed and cut into 2-inch lengths

Method

FOR THE PORK:

  1. Place pork pieces between 2 layers of plastic wrap and pound to ¼ inch thickness.
  2. Whisk fish sauce, lime juice, sugar, and pepper flakes in large bowl until sugar has dissolved.
  3. Add pork and toss to coat.
  4. Cover and refrigerate for 30 minutes.

FOR THE PICKLES:

  1. Meanwhile, whisk fish sauce, sugar, lime zest and juice, and salt in medium bowl until sugar and salt have dissolved.
  2. Stir in radish and carrot and refrigerate for 15 to 30 minutes.
  3. Drain vegetables and set aside for serving.

FOR THE BANH MI:

  1. Remove pork from marinade and pat dry with paper towels.
  2. Heat oil in 12-inch nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  3. Add pork, reduce heat to medium, and cook until pork is well browned and registers 140 degrees, about 4 minutes per side.
  4. Transfer pork to cutting board and let rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
  5. While the pork rests, whisk mayonnaise, sriracha, and lime zest together in small bowl.
  6. Slice pork ¼ inch thick.
  7. Spread pâté evenly over cut sides of baguette, followed by mayonnaise mixture.
  8. Layer pickled vegetables, pork, cucumber, and cilantro evenly over bottom halves.
  9. Top with baguette tops and serve.

What age is Pork Bánh Mì best for?

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
