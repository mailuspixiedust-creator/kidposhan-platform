Beetroot Rice Recipe (Beetroot Rice)

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Photo: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Ingredients

  • 4 cups Cooked Rice
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Split Urad dal
  • ¼ tsp Asafoetida (Hing)
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 2 large Beetroots (peeled and grated)
  • 2 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • Salt to taste

Method

  • Heat ghee in a pan, add in tempering spices like mustard, urad dal, curry leaves and asafoetida and let them fry for a minute. .
  • Add in ginger garlic paste and cook for a minute until the raw smell leaves. You can add in some chopped onions as well.
  • Now add in the peeled and grated beetroots and saute with the spices and ginger garlic paste.
  • Let this cook down for 3 to 4 minutes.
  • The raw smell will leave from the beets and the vegetable will be cooked.
  • Now add in salt, red chilli powder and turmeric powder and mix well with the beetroots.
  • Add in cooked rice and mix well so the rice is coated with the beetroot and heated through. You can taste and adjust the salt at this point.
  • Serve beetroot rice hot, or pack in your kids lunch box.
