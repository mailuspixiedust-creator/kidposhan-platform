Thai Pumpkin Chicken Curry

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 cups Pumpkin or Kabocha squash (bite-sized pieces, 1 lb)
  • 1 can Coconut Milk (14oz)
  • 3 tablespoon Red Curry Paste (adjust to taste)
  • 1 pound Chicken thighs (boneless, skinless, cut into 1-inch cubes)
  • 1 tablespoon Fish Sauce (or soy sauce)
  • 1 tablespoon Brown sugar (or palm sugar)
  • 5-6 Kaffir lime leaves (crushed)
  • 8-10 Thai basil leaves
  • 1 cup Red Bell Pepper (sliced, or a mix of red and green.)
  • Salt (to taste)
  • 1/2 Lime or Lemon (juiced)
  • Roasted Peanuts (optional, for garnish)

Method

  • Rinse the kabocha squash/pumpkin with water.
  • Dry it and remove the skin (you can leave the skin on if you prefer).
  • Cut and remove the seeds by scraping them off with a spoon.
  • Then, cut the pumpkin into bite-sized cubes (about 1-inch).
  • In a large pot or Dutch oven, bring 1/2 cup of coconut milk to a boil on medium-high heat.
  • Then add the curry paste and stir it well.
  • Continue cooking for 1-2 minutes (the oil may start to separate).
  • Add the chicken thighs, stir, and cook for a minute. Add the fish sauce, brown sugar, and lime leaves.
  • Then add the pumpkin and the rest of the coconut milk.
  • Stir and simmer for about 3-4 minutes until you see a boil.
  • Cover and cook for about 8-10 minutes or until the pumpkin is cooked through.
  • Remove the lid and stir in Thai basil and red bell peppers. Stir and cook for a minute, then take off the heat.
  • Taste and add salt if needed. Sprinkle some lime or lemon juice. Optionally, garnish with crushed peanuts. Serve immediately over jasmine rice.

    • Instant Pot Method

    1. Start the Instant Pot on saute mode.
    2. Add 1/2 cup of coconut milk and bring it to a boil.
    3. Then add the curry paste and stir it well.
    4. Continue cooking for 1-2 minutes (the oil may start to separate).
    5. Add the chicken thighs, stir, and cook for a minute.
    6. Add the fish sauce, brown sugar, lime leaves, and pumpkin.
    7. Give it all a good stir and let it cook for 3-4 minutes.
    8. Pressure cook for 4 minutes on high pressure. When the cooking time is done, quick release the pressure manually.
    9. Open the lid and set to sauté mode. Add remaining coconut milk, Thai basil, and red bell peppers. Cook for 2-3 minutes, then turn off.
    10. Sprinkle some lime or lemon juice. Optionally, garnish with crushed peanuts. Serve immediately over jasmine rice.
