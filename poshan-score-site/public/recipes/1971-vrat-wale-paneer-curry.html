Ingredients

  • 8 to 10 Cashews, broken
  • 1 tbsp Ginger
  • 2 to 3 Green chilies (adjust to taste)
  • 3 Red chilies (for color)
  • 3 Tomatoes, medium-sized, chopped
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds or Jeera
  • 200 grams Paneer
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves or cilantro chopped
  • Salt, to taste

Method

MAKING OF PANEER MASALA

  1. In a pan on medium heat, add oil.
  2. Once the oil becomes hot, add cumin seeds and saute for few seconds.
  3. Add the ground masala, bring it to a boil, and simmer it for 5 minutes or until oil releases.
  4. You may add little water to adjust the consistency only if required.
  5. To this, add paneer cubes, salt and continue to cook for another 2 to 3 minutes.
  6. Garnish it with coriander leaves and switch off the flame. It is super easy, right? Try this out today, and do share your comments.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
