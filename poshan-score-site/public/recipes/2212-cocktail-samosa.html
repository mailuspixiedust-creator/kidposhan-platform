cocktail samosa

Recipe by hebbarskitchen.com

Photo: hebbarskitchen.com
⏱ 55 minServes 8Vegetarian4 PM snackFrom 2 years

cocktail samosa scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup maida / plain flour / all-purpose flour
  • ½ cup wheat flour / atta
  • salt to taste
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp oil (hot)
  • water (as required to knead)
  • 2 tsp oil
  • ½ tsp coriander seeds / dhaniya seeds (crushed)
  • 1 onion (roughly chopped)
  • ½ tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 green chilli (finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup peas / matar
  • ½ tsp kashmiri chilli powder / lal mirch powder
  • ½ tsp aamchur powder / dry mango powder
  • 2 medium sized potatoes (boiled & peeled)
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • 2 tsp coriander leaves (finely chopped)
  • oil for deep frying
  • 2 tbsp maida / plain flour / all-purpose flour
  • 4 tbsp water

Method

party samosa stuffing recipe:

  1. firstly, heat oil and saute crushed coriander seeds.
  2. further add onions and ginger garlic paste and chili. saute well.
  3. also add peans and saute.
  4. additionally add spices and salt.
  5. furthermore add boiled potatoes and mix well.
  6. also add garam masala and coriander leaves. good mix.

Common questions

Is cocktail samosa healthy for kids?

cocktail samosa scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, fibre, hfss status.

What age is cocktail samosa best for?

It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years, and lowest, 60, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
