One Pan Salmon Fajitas

Recipe by Easy Peasy Foodie

Photo: Easy Peasy Foodie
⏱ 30 minServes 4Non-vegetarianDinnerLunchFrom 2 years

One Pan Salmon Fajitas scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried chilli flakes (See Note 2)
  • 3 teaspoons smoked paprika
  • 2 teaspoons dried oregano
  • Juice of 1 lime
  • 2 cloves garlic (grated or crushed)
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoon salt (or to taste)
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper (or to taste)
  • 4 boneless, skinless salmon fillets (approx. 130g/4½oz each - see Notes 3+4)
  • 2 large red onions (cut into thick wedges)
  • 3 (bell) peppers (sliced into strips, I use 1 red, 1 green and 1 yellow)
  • 2 tablespoons fresh coriander (AKA cilantro) (chopped)
  • 12 medium tortillas (or 8 large ones – See Note 5)
  • Your choice of guacamole / sour cream / salsa / extra limes & coriander / tortilla chips - any or all of them!

Method

  • Preheat your oven to 220C fan.
  • Wrap the tortillas in foil. (See Note 6.)
  • Place all the marinade ingredients in a large bowl and mix together thoroughly.
  • Place the peppers and onions onto a large oven tray.
  • Add half the marinade to the tray and use your hands to turn the veggies in the marinade until they are evenly coated in the marinade.
  • Then spread the veggies out so they are in a single layer. (Tip: use two oven trays if you haven’t got one big enough.)
  • When the oven has heated up, place the baking tray in the oven and roast the veggies for 12 minutes.
  • Meanwhile, cut each salmon fillet into 6 even-sized pieces and place the salmon in the bowl with the remaining marinade.
  • Use your hands to turn the salmon pieces in the marinade until they are evenly coated in the marinade.
  • After the 12 minutes are up, remove the tray from the oven and Place the salmon pieces on top of the veggies.
  • Return the tray to the oven and roast for a further 8 minutes, by which time the salmon will be just cooked through and the vegetables will be lightly charred.
  • Place the foil-wrapped tortillas in the oven at the same time, so they get 8 minutes in the oven too.
  • After the 8 minutes are up, remove the tortillas from the oven but keep wrapped in foil until needed (so they stay warm).
  • Remove the salmon/veggies tray from the oven too and sprinkle the salmon and veggies with the chopped coriander.
  • Serve in the pan on the table, together with the warm tortilla wraps and whatever extras you have chosen for people to make up their own fajitas.
