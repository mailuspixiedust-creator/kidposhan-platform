Lemon Rice

Recipe by Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar

Photo: Awesome Cuisine / Praveen Kumar
⏱ 30 minVegetarianBreakfastDinnerLunch4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Lemon Rice scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Pachai Arisi (Raw Rice / Extra Long-Grained Rice) (cooked and set aside to cool)
  • 1/4 cup Lemon Juice
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1/4 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 2 Green Chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Fresh Ginger (minced)
  • 3 tbsp Peanuts (roasted)
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 Dry Red Chilli
  • 1/4 tsp Asafoetida Powder
  • 3 Curry Leaves
  • 1 tsp Black Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Urad Dal
  • 1 tsp Chana Dal

Method

  • Combine the lemon juice, salt and turmeric powder in a bowl.
  • Set aside.
  • Put the oil in a frying pan over medium heat.
  • When the oil is hot add the dry red chilli, asafoetida powder and curry leaves.
  • Stir in the mustard seeds, urad dal and chana dal.
  • Fry till mustard seeds splutter and the dals are golden for about 30 seconds.
  • Add the cooked rice.
  • Stir in the lemon juice mix.
  • Let it simmer for a few minutes.
  • Remove from heat.
  • Add green chillies, ginger and peanuts.
  • Toss gently for the ingredients to blend and serve.
