Layered Banana Chocolate Cake Recipe (Two Flavoured Cake)

Ingredients

  • 1 ¾ cup All Purpose Flour (Plain Flour | Maida) (210 grams)
  • ¾ tsp Baking Soda
  • ¾ tsp Salt
  • ½ cup Unsalted Butter (100 grams)
  • 1 cup Granulated Sugar (200 grams)
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract
  • 1 cup Ripe Bananas (mashed)
  • 2 large Eggs
  • ½ cup Plain Unsweetened Yogurt (Curd) (120 ml)
  • ½ cup Dark Chocolate
  • 2 tbsp Unsweetened Cocoa

Method

  • Preheat oven to 170 degree C (350 degree F). Line a standard 9x5 loaf pan with parchment paper, grease with oil and set aside.
  • Now lets melt the chocolate for the chocolate flavour cake.
  • Take chocolate in a bowl and melt it gently either in microwave for 10 to 20 seconds or in a double boiler.
  • Make sure the chocolate is completely melted.
  • Once it is smooth, add cocoa powder and mix well.
  • Set this aside.
  • Mix Dry Ingredients. Take flour, baking soda and salt in a bowl and mix well using a whisk. You can sieve this mixture too.
  • Take butter, sugar and vanilla in a bowl and use an electric beater or a spatula to and cream this together until light and fluffy.
  • Add in eggs and cream again until combined. Don't over mix the batter. Now Add banana puree, yogurt and mix well.
  • Now add in the dry ingredients and fold gently. Use a spatula to combine the batter well. Once it is combined, divide it into two bowl.
  • To one bowl, add in melted and cooled cocoa chocolate mixture and fold gently. This is your chocolate flavoured batter.
  • Now take the loaf pan, spoon banana batter and then chocolate batter.
  • Spread evenly, You can use a knife to swirl the cake lightly.
  • Now place this into the preheated oven and bake for 1 hour and 15 mins.
  • Insert a toothpick to check whether the cake is done, if it comes out clean then it is done.
  • Remove it from oven, cool it down for 15 mins in the cake pan. Now gently remove the cake from the pan. Place on a cooling rack and let it cool completely.
  • Once cooled, Slice and serve.
