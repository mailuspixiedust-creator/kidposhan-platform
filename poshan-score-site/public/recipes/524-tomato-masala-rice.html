TOMATO MASALA RICE

Recipe by Madhu's Everyday Indian / Madhavi Kadaba

Photo: Madhu's Everyday Indian / Madhavi Kadaba
⏱ 40 minServes 2VegetarianDinnerLunchSchool lunchboxFrom 3 years

TOMATO MASALA RICE scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • cooked rice - 3 cups
  • very ripe tomato-4, pureed
  • medium onion-1, sliced
  • green peas - ¼ cup (boiled or frozen thawed)
  • green chilly-1. slit
  • ginger garlic paste- 1 tsp
  • garam masala (or pulav masala) - 1 tsp
  • coriander powder- ¾ tsp
  • chilly powder- ¾ tsp
  • turmeric powder -1/4 tsp
  • chopped mint- ¼ cup
  • chopped coriander leaves- 1/4 cupchopped coriander leaves- 1/2 cup
  • salt - 1 tsp or to taste
  • sugar- ¼ tsp (optional),use if the tomato is too acidic

Method

  • Pour oil in a large pan. add the cumin seeds and the green chili. saute for 30 seconds.
  • Add the onion and cook till the onion becomes slightly soft.
  • Add the turmeric powder and ginger garlic paste. saute for a minute.
  • Add the pureed tomato and mix well. Cook uncovered for about 12-15 minutes.
  • The tomato mixture needs to become thick.
  • Add the green peas, garam masala, coriander powder, chilly powder, salt, mint and coriander leaves.
  • Mix well and cook for five minutes.
  • Add sugar is desired and mix.
  • Add the rice, stir thoroughly till the rice is completely quoted with onion tomato mixture.Serve with some raita.
