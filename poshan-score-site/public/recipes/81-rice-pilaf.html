Rice Pilaf

Recipe by Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

Ingredients

  • 1 cup Basmati rice-1 cup
  • 1 large Onion (peeled and sliced thinly)
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • 3 tbsp Coriander leaves (for garnishing)
  • 2 tsp Salt (or to taste)
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 4 no Cloves
  • 4 no Cardamom (crushed slightly)
  • 1 tsp Whole Black Pepper
  • 1 no Bay leaf

Method

  • Wash rice in lots of water and soak it for 30 minutes. Strain in a colander and set aside.
  • In a thick bottom pan add ghee and heat it up.
  • Add in cloves, cardamom, bay leaf, pepper, cinnamon and fry for 30 seconds.
  • Add in onions and cook till golden.
  • Add in the rice and fry for 2 min.
  • Pour in 2 cups of water and bring that to boil.
  • Once it starts to boil, cover it with a tight fitting lid and let it cook on a very low flame for 10-15 mins.
  • Once it has absorbed all the water, fluff it with a fork and let it stand for 10 min covered.
  • Add in lots of coriander and mix well. Serve hot with any curry.

    Common questions

    Is Rice Pilaf healthy for kids?

    Rice Pilaf scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years. It passes 7 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, fibre, hfss status.

    What age is Rice Pilaf best for?

    It scores highest, 70 out of 100, for 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 60, for 1–3 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
