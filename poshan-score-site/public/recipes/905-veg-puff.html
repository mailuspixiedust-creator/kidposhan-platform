veg puff

Recipe by hebbarskitchen.com

Photo: hebbarskitchen.com
⏱ 4 h 25 minServes 14VegetarianDinnerLunch4 PM snackFrom 2 years

veg puff scores 60 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups maida / all-purpose flour / plain flour
  • salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp butter (unsalted)
  • cold water (as required)
  • 400 grams butter (cold)
  • 2 tsp lemon juice / vinegar
  • ½ cup maida / all-purpose flour / plain flour
  • 2 tsp oil
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds / jeera
  • ½ medium sized onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 green chilli (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • ½ cup peas / matar
  • ½ cup beetroot (finely chopped)
  • 1 carrot (finely chopped)
  • 5 beans (finely chopped)
  • ½ tsp turmeric / haldi
  • ½ tsp kashmiri red chili powder / lal mirch powder
  • ½ tsp garam masala
  • ½ tsp aamchur powder / dry mango powder
  • 2 medium sized potato (boiled, peeled & mashed)
  • 2 tbsp coriander leaves (finely chopped)
  • ¼ cup maida / all-purpose flour / plain flour (for dusting)

Method

  • firstly, take the pastry sheet and roll to thin and uniform sheet.
  • cut the sides and cut to rectangle pieces.
  • place the prepared stuffing in the centre.
  • and fold half, press and seal the sides.
  • now place in the baking tray.
  • brush the puff with butter for more golden colour.
  • bake the puffs in preheated oven at 140 degrees for 25 minutes.
  • finally, veg puffs or veg patties is ready to serve with tomato sauce.

    What age is veg puff best for?

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
