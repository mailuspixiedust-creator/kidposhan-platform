Ingredients

  • 1 cup toor dal (split pigeon peas) (or ¾ cup of toor dal and ¼ cup of moong or masoor dal)
  • 3 cups water (for cooking)
  • salt (to taste)
  • 2 tablespoons oil (or ghee)
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 dried red chili (optional)
  • 1 pinch asafoetida (hing)
  • 1 large onion (finely chopped, about 1 cup)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic (minced)
  • 1-2 green chili (finely chopped)
  • 2 large tomatoes (finely chopped, about 1 cup)
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2-1 cup water (to adjust the dal consistency)
  • 1 tablespoon lime juice
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves

Method

Cook the Dal (Lentils)

  1. Rinse the dal 2-3 times until the water runs clear.
  2. Pressure cook 1 cup of rinsed toor dal with 3 cups of water in the Instant Pot or traditional stovetop pressure cooker.Stovetop Pressure Cooker: Pressure cook on high heat for 2-3 whistles and wait for natural pressure release.Instant Pot: Pressure cook on Manual (High Pressure) for 8 minutes with natural pressure release.
  3. When the pressure comes down, open the lid. Using a wire whisk (or immersion blender), mash the dal until you get a smooth texture. Keep it aside.
