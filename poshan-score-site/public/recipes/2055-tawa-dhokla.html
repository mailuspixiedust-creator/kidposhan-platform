Tawa dhokla

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 1 hServes 3VegetarianBreakfastLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Tawa dhokla scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • ½ cup small grain rice
  • ¼ cup urad dal
  • ¼ cup chana dal
  • ¾ cup rava (fine semolina)
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 cup dhokla mix
  • 2 cup sour buttermilk or 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • 3-4 chopped green chilli
  • ¼ cup coriander leaves
  • 2 tsp oil
  • Water as required
  • ½ tsp eno into half of batter.
  • 1 tsp oil
  • ½ tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
  • Some curry leaves
  • Dhokla batter
  • Some red chilli powder

Method

Making instant tawa dhokla

  1. In a mixing bowl, add dhokla flour and buttermilk. Mix well and make a smooth and flowing batter.
  2. Whisk batter with hand for 3-4 minutes and rest batter for 30-35 minutes.
  3. Then add chopped green chili, ginger paste, coriander leaves, and oil. Mix well.
  4. Divide batter into two parts.
  5. In one part, add Eno fruit salt & mix in one direction.
  6. Heat tawa on the pan, grease streel ring with oil and place it on tawa.
  7. Add oil to tawa and spread it.
  8. Then add mustard seeds, sesame seeds, and chopped curry leaves.
  9. Now add 2 to 2.5 ladle dhokla batter into the ring. Spread it and sprinkle some red chili powder on it.
  10. Cover and cook dhokla on the lowest flame for 12-15 minutes.
  11. Once done dhokla puff up and a knife comes out clean, then switch off the flame and take out a ring from dhokla.
  12. Make all dhokla in the same way and Serve with chutney.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
