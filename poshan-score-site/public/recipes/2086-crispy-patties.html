Crispy patties

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 35 minServes 8VegetarianLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Crispy patties scores 50 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • ¾ cup green peas
  • 1 small size potatoes
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ½ tsp jeera
  • 3 spicy green chilli
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 5-6 garlic cloves
  • ½ cup chopped onion
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • ¼ tsp garam masala
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp dry mango powder
  • Pinch of sugar
  • Little water
  • Some coriander leaves
  • 1.5 cup maida (all-purpose flour)
  • 3 tbsp melted hot ghee
  • ½ tsp ajwain
  • ½ cup water or as required
  • 2 tbsp ghee (for paste)
  • 2 tbsp corn flour (maida or rice flour)
  • Oil for deep frying

Method

Making patties stuffing

  1. In a mixture jar, add green chilli, ginger, and garlic cloves. Grind it into a coarse paste. keep it aside.
  2. Now in a mixture jar, add green peas and potatoes. Without adding water grind it into a coarse mixture on pulse grinding. Keep it aside.
  3. Then in a pan, add oil, cumin seeds, and grinded chili-ginger-garlic paste. Saute it.
  4. Now add coarsely grinded peas-potato mixture and salt. Saute it.
  5. Then add turmeric powder, garam masala, coriander powder, dry mango powder, and a pinch of sugar. Mix well.
  6. Now sprinkle some water and cover and cook stuffing on low flame for 3-4 minutes.
  7. Then switch-off gas and add coriander leaves. Mix well.
  8. Patties stuffing is ready.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
