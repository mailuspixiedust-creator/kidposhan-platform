Methi Thepla / Fenugreek Flatbread

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups (180g) whole wheat flour (chapati flour)
  • 1 cup (40g) fresh fenugreek (methi) leaves
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander leaves
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp green chili paste or to taste
  • 1 tsp ginger paste
  • ½ tsp turmeric powder
  • ½ tsp roasted cumin powder
  • ½ tsp coriander powder
  • ½ tsp cumin seeds (unroasted)
  • ½ cup (120g) plain yogurt
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tsp jaggery powder (optional)
  • 1-2 tbsp water or as needed to make the dough
  • Oil or ghee as required for roasting Thepla

Method

Making The Dough

  1. Rinse fenugreek leaves thoroughly, drain and chop them finely.
  2. Take flour, methi leaves, coriander leaves, oil, chili and ginger paste, turmeric powder, coriander powder, roasted cumin powder, cumin seeds, salt, and jaggery powder in a large bowl and mix well.
  3. Add yogurt and mix with your hands until the dough comes together. If you can not make the dough, you may need to add 1-2 tbsp of water.
  4. Instead of water, you can add more yogurt until the soft dough is formed.
  5. Knead the dough for about 4-5 minutes until it is smooth and soft. Then divide the dough into medium-sized balls.

What age is Methi Thepla / Fenugreek Flatbread best for?

It scores highest, 90 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years, and lowest, 80, for 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
