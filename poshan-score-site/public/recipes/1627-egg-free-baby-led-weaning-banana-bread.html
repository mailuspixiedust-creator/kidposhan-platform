Egg-Free Baby Led Weaning Banana Bread

Ingredients

  • 6 medium bananas
  • Zest 1 lemon
  • 1 tsp apple cider vinegar
  • 125 ml melted butter
  • 125 ml milk
  • 125 ml smooth peanut butter
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 2 cups self-raising flour
  • 3 tsp smooth peanut butter

Method

  • Preheat oven to 180ºC/350ºF.
  • Add the bananas to a bowl, mash until smooth, then add the lemon zest, apple cider vinegar, melted butter and milk, stir well until smooth, then mix in the peanut butter.
  • Fold in the cinnamon, baking soda and flour until it turns into a smooth batter, then pour into a loaf tin lined with parchment paper.
  • Use a spoon to spread the batter evenly.
  • Top the batter with the teaspoons of peanut butter and swirl with a toothpick.
  • Bake for 40 minutes or until a skewer comes out clean.
  • Slice into soldiers for babies, along with a few raspberries.
