Besan Chilla

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 1 cup besan (120 grams)
  • 1 teaspoon kosher salt
  • ¼ teaspoon ground turmeric
  • ½ teaspoon ajwain carom seeds
  • 1 small red onion finely diced
  • ½ tomato finely diced
  • 1 teaspoon green chili (finely chopped)
  • 2 teaspoons ginger grated
  • 1 tablespoon cilantro finely chopped
  • ¾ cup water
  • 2 tablespoons oil

Method

  • Add besan to a mixing bowl.
  • Add salt and turmeric.
  • Lightly crush carom seeds on the palm of your hands and add to the bowl.
  • Add onion, tomato, green chili, ginger and cilantro.
  • Add water little bit at a time mixing the batter well. The batter should be smooth with a good pouring consistency.
  • Heat a griddle and apply oil.
  • Pour two small ladle full of batter, about ½ cup on the pan, and spread it gently in a circular motion using the back of the ladle or a spoon to make a 6-inch round chilla.
  • Cook for 3 to 4 minutes on medium heat. Drizzle ½ teaspoon of oil on top and around the edges.
  • Carefully flip over the chilla. Cook until the other side becomes lightly brown and the edges start to crisp. Take out the chilla.
  • Repeat the above steps to make Chillas with the remaining batter.
  • Serve hot with green chutney and pickles.

    Common questions

    Is Besan Chilla healthy for kids?

    Besan Chilla scores 80 out of 100 (OK, held by a risk check) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on sodium, being high in fat, sugar or salt.

    What age is Besan Chilla best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years, and lowest, 60, for 16–18 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
