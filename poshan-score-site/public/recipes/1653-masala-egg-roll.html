Masala Egg Roll

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 2 tablespoons groundnut or any neutral cooking oil
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds (jeera)
  • 1 teaspoon finely chopped ginger
  • 2 teaspoons finely chopped garlic
  • 2 sprigs curry leaves (finely chopped)
  • 2 green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1 cup finely chopped onions
  • 2 medium tomatoes (pureed)
  • 2 ½ teaspoons sambar powder
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • ½ cup water
  • 4 boiled eggs (chopped into ¼ inch cubes)
  • 2 tablespoons finely chopped coriander leaves
  • 3 frozen laccha parathas
  • 3 tablespoons mint chutney (MFS link)
  • ½ cup pickled onion rings (refer notes)
  • 2 teaspoons finely chopped coriander leaves

Method

  • Heat oil in a pan, add cumin seeds and once they crackle, add ginger, garlic, curry leaves, green chillies and saute on medium for a few seconds till fragrant.
  • Add onions and fry till they turn golden.
  • Add tomato puree, sambar powder, salt and cook on low for 2-3 minutes till the rawness of tomatoes goes.
  • Add water, mix well and cook for 1-2 minutes on low till the gravy thickens.
  • Add chopped eggs, coriander leaves, mix well and cook for 1-2 minutes till the eggs absorb the masalas.
  • Heat a tawa or pan and fry all the parathas on either side with a few drops of ghee or oil till they are light golden and set aside.
  • Just before serving, take one fried paratha on a plate, spread mint chutney all over the paratha, add egg masala to the center, add a few onion rings, garnish with coriander leaves, wrap into a roll and serve.
  • Follow the same steps to make rest of the egg masala rolls.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
