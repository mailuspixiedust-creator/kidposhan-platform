Paneer Patties

Recipe by SouthIndianRecipes.in

Photo: SouthIndianRecipes.in
⏱ 40 minServes 2Egg4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Paneer Patties scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 kg Potato
  • 1 tbsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1/2 tbsp Aniseeds
  • 1/2 tbsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 Onion
  • 1 Ginger
  • 300 g Cottage Cheese
  • 1 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tbsp Cumin Seeds Powder
  • 1/2 tbsp Garam Masala
  • 2 Green Chillies
  • 1 tsp Fresh Coriander
  • 3 tbsp Gram Flour
  • 2 Eggs
  • 1 cup Bread Crumbs
  • Salt
  • Oil

Method

  • Peel potatoes. Mix half the red chilli powder and half the salt. Mash and make a paste.
  • Heat one tbsp oil and sauté cumin seeds.
  • Add onion and ginger and fry till the ingredients soften.
  • Add grated paneer, cumin powder, coriander powder, garam masala, green chillies, aniseed, fresh coriander, remaining salt and red chilli powder.
  • Mix well and remove from the stove.
  • Make eight portions of the potato paste and shape them into balls.
  • Make equal portions of the paneer stuffing.
  • Slightly flatten the potato balls and place the stuffing in each of the balls.
  • Cover them and roll them again into balls so that the stuffing is secured.
  • Heat oil in a kadai and them reduce the heat.
  • First roll the potato balls in dry besan, then in whipped eggs, wrap in bread crumbs and fry them in hot oil.
  • When patties become brownish and crisp, remove.
  • Prepare the remaining patties the same way. Serve hot.
