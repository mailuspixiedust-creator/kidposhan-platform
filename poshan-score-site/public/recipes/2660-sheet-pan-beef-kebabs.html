Ingredients

  • 1 kg beef mince
  • 1 red onion (grated)
  • 3 garlic cloves (grated or crushed)
  • 2 teaspoons cumin
  • 2 teaspoons coriander
  • 2 teaspoons smoked paprika
  • ½ teaspoon chilli flakes (or to taste)
  • 2 teaspoons oregano
  • ½ teaspoon salt (or to taste)
  • ¼ teaspoon black pepper (or to taste)

Method

  • Preheat your oven to 220C / 200C fan / gas mark7 / 425F.
  • Place all the kebab ingredients in a large bowl and mix together thoroughly with your hands.
  • Line a 35x25cm (14x10inch) sheet pan (aka baking sheet) with baking paper (optional).
  • Place the beef mince mixture onto the sheet pan and press down until the mince reaches the sides of the baking sheet and is an even thickness all over.
  • Use a knife to cut the mince into 8 kebabs widthways, then indent the mince all over with shallow lines, using a teaspoon. (This helps the kebabs cook more evenly.)
  • Cook for 15-20 minutes, until the meat is nicely browned and fully cooked.
  • Remove from the oven, cut into individual kebabs and serve with flatbreads, red onions, tomatoes and yogurt, garlic sauce or tzatziki.
