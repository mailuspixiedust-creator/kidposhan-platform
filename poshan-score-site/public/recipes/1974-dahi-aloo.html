Dahi Aloo

Recipe by cookwithkushi.com

Photo: cookwithkushi.com
⏱ 30 minServes 6VegetarianDinnerLunch4 PM snackFrom 2 years

Dahi Aloo scores 100 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 600 grams Potatoes
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 2 cups Yogurt or Dahi
  • 2 to 3 Green chilies, finely chopped
  • Salt or Rock Salt or Sendha Namak, to taste
  • 1/2 tsp Roasted Coriander Powder (Can use curry powder or garam masala powder or chaat masala)
  • 1 tbsp Coriander leaves

Method

  • Peel off the potato skin and cut them into cubes. Then cook the potatoes using your preferred method (stovetop, pressure cooker, instant pot, or microwave).
  • I like to cook the potatoes in the microwave for 4 minutes.
  • Heat oil in a pan on medium flame, add cumin seeds and saute for a few seconds.
  • Add the boiled potatoes and ½ teaspoon salt, and saute it for 3 to 4 minutes until it becomes light in color.
  • Now slightly mash a few potatoes and switch off the flame.
  • Check out the video here.
  • In another bowl, add fresh yogurt or dahi and nicely whisk until it is smooth.
  • To this, add green chilies, ¼ teaspoon salt, coriander powder, and coriander leaves, and mix.
  • Now add the cooked potatoes and mix until everything is combined.
  • Garnish it with more coriander leaves and coriander powder if desired.
