Microwave Khandvi

Recipe by Tarla Dalal

Microwave Khandvi scores 80 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

Tick what you have at home and what you need to buy. We keep your ticks on this device.
  • 3/4 cup besan (gram flour)
  • 3/4 cup dahi (curd) mixed with 3/4 cup water
  • 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste
  • 1/4 tsp haldi powder (turmeric powder)
  • a pinch of asafoetida (hing)
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds (rai / sarson)
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds (til)
  • 4 to 5 curry leaves (kadi patta)
  • 3 tbsp grated coconut
  • 2 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania)

Method

  • Combine the 3/4 cup besan (Bengal gram flour), 3/4 cup curd (dahi) mixed with 3/4 cup water, 1 tsp ginger-green chilli paste 1/4 tsp turmeric powder (haldi), a pinch of asafoetida (hing) and salt to taste in a microwave safe bowl, whisk well and microwave on high for 4½ minutes, stirring twice in between with help of a whisk after every 1½ minutes.
  • Spread the mixture on a smooth greased kitchen platform or surface. Allow to cool for 2 to 3 minutes.
  • Cut the khandvi lengthwise at a distance of 37 mm. (1½”) in width to make equal sized strips.
  • Carefully roll out each strip from one end to another end to form cylindrical rolls. Keep aside.
  • Combine the 2 tbsp oil, 1 tsp mustard seeds ( rai / sarson) and a pinch of asafoetida (hing) in a microwave safe bowl and microwave on high for 2 minutes.
  • Pour the tempering on top of the khandvi.
  • Garnished with 3 tbsp grated coconut and 2 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania).
  • Serve microwave khandvi recipe | Gujarati khandvi recipe in microwave immediately.

    Common questions

    Is Microwave Khandvi healthy for kids?

    Microwave Khandvi scores 80 out of 100 (Good) for 4–6 years. It passes 8 of the 10 checks and loses points on fibre, protein.

    What age is Microwave Khandvi best for?

    It scores highest, 80 out of 100, for 1–3 years and 4–6 years and 7–9 years. The same dish scores differently as a child grows, because the daily limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat grow with them.

    How is the Poshan Score worked out?

    Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
