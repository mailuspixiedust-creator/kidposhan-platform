Murmura nashta

Recipe by Nehas Cook Book

Photo: Nehas Cook Book
⏱ 20 minServes 12VegetarianBreakfastLunchSchool lunchbox4 PM snackFrom 3 years

Murmura nashta scores 70 out of 100 (OK) for 4–6 years, worked out for one serving. The same dish scores differently for different ages, because a younger child's daily limit for sugar, salt and saturated fat is smaller.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

Ingredients

  • 2 cup murmura
  • 1 cup idli rava or semolina
  • 1 cup curd
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 cup + ¼ cup water or as required
  • 1 tsp eno
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp mustard seeds
  • 1 tsp chana dal
  • 1 tsp urad dal
  • 5-6 chopped curry leaves
  • Pinch of asafetida
  • 2 chopped green chilli
  • ¼ cup chopped onion
  • ¼ cup chopped carrot
  • ¼ cup chopped yellow capsicum
  • ¼ cup chopped red capsicum
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • ¼ cup chana dal
  • 3-4 curry leaves
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1 inch ginger
  • 4 dry red chilli
  • ¼ cup water or as required
  • 1 tsp oil
  • ¼ tsp mustard seeds
  • Some curry leaves
  • Pinch of hing

Method

For murmura idli

  1. Grind 2 cups of murmura into a coarse powder.
  2. In a mixing bowl, add murmura powder, idli rava, salt and curd. Mix well.
  3. Add water gradually and make thick idli-like batter.
  4. Cover and rest the batter for 15-20 minutes.
  5. In a pan, add oil, mustard seeds, chana dal, urad dal, chopped curry leaves, asafoetida, and chopped green chilli. Sauté till dal turns slightly golden brown.
  6. Then add onion and sauté till onion become translucent.
  7. Now add chopped carrot, yellow capsicum, red capsicum, and salt. Sauté it on high flame till it is slightly soft.
  8. tadka is ready. keep it aside.
  9. After 20 minutes add water as required, to form an idli batter consistency batter.
  10. Then add prepared tadka to the batter and mix well.
  11. Steam water in a pan for 5 minutes and also grease idli stand with oil.
  12. just before steaming add 1 tsp eno (fruit salt) and mix well till it turns frothy. alternatively, add a pinch of baking soda.
  13. pour the batter immediately into idli plate. do not rest the batter.
  14. steam the idli for 15-20 minutes on medium flame.
  15. finally, serve instant murmura idli with chana dal chutney
