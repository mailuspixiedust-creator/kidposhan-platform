What to check on the label

The checks breakfast cereal most often fail:

  • Fibre. Look for fibre. A pack passes when one serving gives at least 15% of a 4 to 6 year old's day, about 3 g.
  • Added sugar. Look at added sugar per serving. For a child of 4 to 6 the daily ideal is 17 g, and a pack fails this check when one serving takes 30% of that (15% for snacks and sweets).
  • Artificial additives. Check the ingredient list for artificial colours, flavour enhancers (INS 627, 631, 635), artificial sweeteners and flavours.

Common questions

What are the best breakfast cereal for kids?

Of the 41 breakfast cereal we have scored, Bagrry's Corn Flakes Plus Original & Healthier (with Fibre Power) scores highest: 90 out of 100 for a child aged 4 to 6 (Good). The lowest is Tata Soulfull Choco Fills Ragi Bites Cereal, at 50.

Are breakfast cereal healthy for kids?

It depends on the pack. Of the 41 we have scored, 16 are Good (80 or more), 25 OK (50 to 79) and 0 Do not use (under 50). The most common reason a pack loses points is the fibre check (36 of 41).

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one realistic serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether the pack is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each check passed is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI, and they change with the child's age, so the same pack can score differently for a toddler and a teenager.
