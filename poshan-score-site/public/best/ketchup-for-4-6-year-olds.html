What is the best ketchup and sauce for 4 to 6 year olds?

Of the 13 ketchup and sauces we have scored for 4 to 6 year olds, Heinz Tomato Ketchup scores highest: 80 out of 100 (Good). This covers a 5 year old.

Is ketchup and sauces healthy for 4 to 6 year olds?

It depends on the pack. For 4 to 6 year olds, 4 of the 13 are Good (80 or more), 9 OK (50 to 79) and 0 Do not use (under 50). The checks they most often fail are fibre, protein, preservatives. The score is for one serving as the label gives it, and the limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat are smaller for younger children.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
