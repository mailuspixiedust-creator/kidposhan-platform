What to check on the label

The checks namkeen and mixture most often fail:

  • Saturated fat. Look at saturated fat per serving. For a child of 4 to 6 the daily limit is about 15 g, and a pack fails when one serving takes 30% of it (15% for snacks).
  • Fibre. Look for fibre. A pack passes when one serving gives at least 15% of a 4 to 6 year old's day, about 3 g.
  • Sodium. Look at sodium per serving. For a child of 4 to 6 the daily limit is about 1,360 mg, and a pack fails when one serving takes 30% of it (15% for snacks).

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one realistic serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether the pack is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each check passed is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI, and they change with the child's age, so the same pack can score differently for a toddler and a teenager.
