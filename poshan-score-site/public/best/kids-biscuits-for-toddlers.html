Common questions

What is the best kids biscuit for toddlers?

Of the 45 kids biscuits we have scored for toddlers, Britannia Nutri choice zero scores highest: 80 out of 100 (Good).

Is kids biscuits healthy for toddlers?

It depends on the pack. For toddlers, 2 of the 45 are Good (80 or more), 26 OK (50 to 79) and 17 Do not use (under 50). The score is for one serving as the label gives it, and the limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat are smaller for younger children.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
