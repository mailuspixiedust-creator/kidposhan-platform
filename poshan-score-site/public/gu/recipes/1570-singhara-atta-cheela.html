સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup Singhara atta (water chestnut flour)
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger (finely chopped, optional)
  • 3 Green chilies (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds
  • 1 tsp Salt (sendha namak)
  • 1 cup Water (adjust as needed)
  • Oil for cooking

રીત

Prepare the batter

  1. In a bowl, mix singhara atta, coriander leaves, ginger, green chilies, cumin seeds, and salt well.
  2. Now add water little by little, whisking continuously to form a medium-thick batter. (The batter should be slightly thicker than regular dosa batter, but you should be able to spread it.)
  3. Let the batter sit for 5 minutes.
