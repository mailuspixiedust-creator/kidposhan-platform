સામગ્રી

  • 250 g Broken Rice / Khud
  • 2 Potato
  • 1/2 Cup Green Peas
  • 3 Green Chilli
  • 1/2 Tsp Garlic Paste
  • 3 Tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 Tbsp Ghee
  • 1 Tsp Sugar
  • 2/3 Tsp Salt
  • 1 Bay leaf
  • 1 Dry Red chili
  • 1 Cinnamon Stick

રીત

Bou Khuda Recipe

  1. Soak broken rice in water for 15 minutes.
  2. Peel the potato skin and cut it into matchstick-like pieces.
  3. Soak the potatoes in water as well.
  4. Chop Onion roughly.
  5. Now heat oil in a deep bottom pan and fry the potatoes in medium flame.
  6. Strain fried potatoes from oil.
  7. Temper remaining oil with Bay leaf, Dry Red Chili, and Cinamon stick.
  8. Add chopped onion and fry till it turns light pink in color.
  9. Now add garlic paste, green peas, and also green chilies and cook over low flame till the raw aroma of garlic goes away.
  10. At this point discard the water and add soaked rice to the pan and fry for five minutes over the low flame.
  11. Add salt and also sugar and mix lightly.
  12. Pour boiling water over the rice. The water must cover the rice and the water must reach 1" above the rice as shown in the picture.
  13. Lightly mix and cover the pan with a lid and cook over low flame for 7-8 minutes.
  14. Open the lid and check in between.
  15. Once the water evaporated completely add fried potatoes and lightly mix.
  16. Now add ghee and mix it.
  17. Switch the flame off and serve Bou Khuda hot.
  18. I prefer to add a handful of cashew nuts which is completely optional.
