Chingri Shutki Bhorta

70ઠીક

Chingri Shutki Bhorta ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 250 g Chingri Shutki/ Dried Prawn (small size)
  • 4 Onion
  • 1.5 Tbsp Garlic Paste
  • 1/2 Tsp Ginger Paste
  • 3-4 Green Chili
  • 3 Dried Red Chilli
  • 1 Tsp Salt or to taste
  • 2.5 Tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 Tbsp Lemon juice

રીત

  • Clean dried prawns properly before cooking.
  • Wash under running water 2-3 times, followed by washing the shrimps in a bowl full of water.
  • Now strain water from the prawns using your hand, as much as you can.
  • Chop onions roughly.
  • Heat oil in a wok and fry half of the onion crisply to make "Piyaj Beresta" (fried crisp Onion). Leave those aside.
  • Now in the same oil fry, two dried Red chilies with the stem on for a minute or till the chilies change color.
  • Strain fried chilies from the oil and leave aside.
  • Now add washed, dried prawns to the remaining oil.
  • Start sauteing till the water evaporates completely and the prawns start emitting aroma.
  • Add the remaining chopped onion, ginger, and garlic paste, and 2-3 green chilies.
  • Cook for 8-10 minutes on a low flame.
  • Adjust Salt if needed.
  • Now switch the flame off and give the fried prawn a rest of at least 15 minutes.
  • Take cooked prawns along with fried onion, fried red chili, and a dash of lemon juice, along with a little mustard oil, in a mixer grinder.
  • Make a smooth paste of it.
  • You can serve the Shutki Chingri Bhorta or Dried Prawn Paste as it is with rice. Else you can make small balls out of the paste as I did.
  • Serve it with steamed rice with a side of green chili and a piece of lemon.
