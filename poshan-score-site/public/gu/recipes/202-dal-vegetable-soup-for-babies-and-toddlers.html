સામગ્રી

  • 1 tbsp green moong dal (split green gram) , washed and drained
  • 1/4 cup chopped tomato
  • 1/2 cup finely chopped cabbage
  • 1 tbsp chopped spinach (palak)
  • restricted quantity of salt (namak)
  • 1 tbsp crumbled paneer (cottage cheese)

  • For healthy dal vegetable soup for kids, you also need to buy the perfect cabbage.
  • Light green and white in color, cabbage is a really simple vegetable to cook.
  • Choose cabbage heads that are firm and dense with shiny, crisp, colorful leaves free of cracks, bruises and blemishes.
  • Severe damage to the outer leaves is suggestive of worm damage or decay that may reside in the inner core as well, so do not buy it.
  • Wash the cabbage very well with clean water to make it free of dirt if any. Wash it under running water to prevent any sort of contamination.
  • Wipe it with a clean kitchen towel and pat dry it.
  • Using a sterilized knife chop the cabbage very finely. Keep the chopped cabbage aside.
  • Then also choose spinach that has vibrant deep green leaves and stems with no signs of yellowing.
  • The leaves should look fresh and tender, and not be wilted or bruised.

  • Place the spinach on a chopping board and chop it and keep aside.
  • Once all the ingredients for dal vegetable soup for babies and toddlers are ready assemble them in a pressure cooker.
  • First add the washed and drained moong dal.
  • Then add the chopped tomatoes.
  • Follow it by adding cabbage.
  • Also add the spinach.
  • Add restricted quantity of salt.
  • Add 1 cup of water for cooking.
  • Mix well using a ladle.
  • Close the lid of the pressure cooker and cook healthy dal vegetable soup for kids for 2 to 3 whistles.
  • Allow the steam to escape before opening the lid.
  • Keep aside to cool slightly.

  • Mix well and bring to boil.
