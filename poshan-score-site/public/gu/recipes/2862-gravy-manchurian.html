રીત

Making Manchurian balls

  1. Cut cabbage into rough pieces.
  2. Now in a mixture jar, add cabbage pieces, and on pulses grind it coarse.
  3. Take out the coarse cabbage mixture into the cotton cloth and squeeze water from it. Do not discard its water.
  4. Now in a mixing bowl, add grated cabbage, ginger garlic paste, red chilli powder, salt, and pepper powder. Mix well.
  5. Now add maida and corn flour and form a soft dough.
  6. Make small-size Manchurian balls.
  7. deep fry in hot oil keeping the flame on medium. Stir occasionally, until the balls turn golden brown. Drain off the Manchurian balls and keep them aside.
  8. Now again heat oil on high flame, add all fried Manchurian balls and fry it for 2 minutes for the crispy outer layer.
  9. Manchurian balls are ready.
