  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds or Jeera
  • 200 grams Paneer
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves or cilantro chopped
  • Salt, to taste

MAKING OF PANEER MASALA

  1. In a pan on medium heat, add oil.
  2. Once the oil becomes hot, add cumin seeds and saute for few seconds.
  3. Add the ground masala, bring it to a boil, and simmer it for 5 minutes or until oil releases.
  4. You may add little water to adjust the consistency only if required.
  5. To this, add paneer cubes, salt and continue to cook for another 2 to 3 minutes.
