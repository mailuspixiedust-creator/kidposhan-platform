સામગ્રી

  • 16 oz spinach (using baby spinach, or use chopped frozen spinach)
  • 360 grams paneer
  • 1-2 tbsp ghee (or oil)
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1-2 green chillies (chopped, adjust to taste)
  • 5 cloves garlic (minced, or 1 tablespoon garlic minced)
  • 2 inch ginger (chopped or grated, Or use 1 tablespoon ginger minced)
  • 1 medium yellow onion (finely chopped)
  • 1 medium roma tomato (finely chopped)
  • ¼ teaspoon turmeric powder (optional)
  • ½ teaspoon red chilli powder (adjust to taste)
  • ½ teaspoon cumin powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • ½-1 teaspoon Punjabi garam masala (or to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon Kasuri methi (dry fenugreek leaves)
  • 1 to 1.5 teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tablespoon lemon juice
  • 1-2 tablespoons heavy cream (optional)
  • ½ teaspoon sugar (optional)

રીત

Making Instant Pot Palak Paneer

  1. Start the Instant Pot on saute mode. Heat oil and add the bay leaf and cumin seeds.
  2. When the seeds splutter add the onion and saute till they turn translucent.
  3. Next, add ginger garlic green chilies and saute for a few seconds till the raw garlic smell disappears.
  4. Add the tomatoes and saute for a couple of mins.
  5. Next add spices- salt, turmeric, red chili powder, cumin powder, coriander powder, dry mango (amchur) powder, garam masala, and sugar (optional).
  6. Mix well and saute for a minute more.
  7. Deglaze with ¼-½ cup of water depending on how thick you like your gravy. Give it a quick stir to ensure nothing sticks to the bottom. Press cancel.
  8. Add the washed and drained palak leaves. These might feel like a lot but they shrink considerably.
  9. Close the Instant pot with the lid and vent in the sealing position.
  10. Pressure cook on manual or pressure cook at High pressure for "0" mins. (or 1 minute for frozen spinach).
  11. Once the instant pot beeps, wait for 5 mins and then do a pressure release.
  12. Open the lid, transfer the inner pot (be careful its hot) to a hot plate trivet.
  13. Remove the bay leaf, and using an immersion blender to blend it coarse or into a smooth gravy.
  14. You can also blend it in a blender.
  15. Transfer the inner pot back to the Instant Pot.
  16. Crush the fenugreek leaves between your palms and then add them to the gravy along with the soft paneer cubes and cilantro.
  17. Switch the instant pot back to Sauté mode, mix well, and cook for about 2 mins stirring intermittently. (Be careful as it splutters).
  18. I prefer thicker gravy but you can add water and adjust consistency if required.
  19. Turn off the saute.
  20. Transfer the inner pot back to the hot plate trivet to prevent the curry from cooking further.At this stage, you can drizzle over some cream (optional).
  21. Palak paneer is ready.
  22. Serve with naan, paratha or jeera rice.
