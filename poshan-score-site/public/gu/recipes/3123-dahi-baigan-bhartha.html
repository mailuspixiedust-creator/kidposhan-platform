Dahi baigan bhartha

70ઠીક

Dahi baigan bhartha ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup curd
  • 250 grm or 1 medium size green baigan (brinjal)
  • 4-5 garlic cloves
  • ¼ cup chopped green onion
  • ¼ cup chopped green garlic
  • ¼ cup chopped coriander leaves
  • ½ tsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp green chilli paste
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp cumin powder
  • ½ tsp sugar
  • 325 grm green-red chillies
  • 5 tbsp salt
  • 2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 6 tbsp slightly coarse mustard seeds (rai kuria)
  • ¼ tsp hing
  • 4 tbsp oil
  • 1.5 tbsp fennel seeds powder
  • 5 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1 cup bajra flour
  • ½ cup water
  • Wheat flour for dusting

રીત

For dahi baigan bhartha

  1. Add curd to a muslin cloth and hang curd for 20-25 minutes, to remove extra water from it.
  2. Wash the baigan (aubergine), make a slit on it, and add garlic cloves to it.
  3. Apply a few drops of oil over it. Roast the baigan on an open flame, till it has charred and wilted, takes around 15 minutes to do it on all sides.
  4. Cool, peel, and cut into small pieces.
  5. Now in a mixing bowl, add the cooled and mashed baigan, spring onions, green garlic, coriander, ginger paste, chilli paste, salt, cumin powder and sugar.
  6. Mix well
  7. Add yogurt and give it a gentle mix.
  8. Serve dahi baigan bhartha with bajra roti.
