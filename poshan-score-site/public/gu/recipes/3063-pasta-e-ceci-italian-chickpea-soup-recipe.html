સામગ્રી

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 onion (1 1/2 cups)
  • 2 tablespoons garlic, minced
  • pinch of red pepper flakes
  • 2 large carrots, diced into small pieces
  • 4 cups vegetable broth (or chicken broth)
  • 14 -ounce can of whole tomatoes
  • 1 tablespoon fresh rosemary, minced (or 1 teaspoon dried)
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
  • 15 -ounce can chickpeas, drained -1 1/2 cups
  • 3 ounces ditalini pasta (or orzo, or other small quick cooking pasta)
  • 2 cups Swiss chard leaves, chopped small (optional-saute chopped stems in with the onions)
  • 1/2 cup fresh parsley or basil (or both)
  • Serve with 2 ounces of grated parmesan or pecorino cheese (see notes for the rind) or a drizzle of good olive oil (or both).

રીત

  • In a large pot over medium heat, add olive oil, onions, garlic, carrots and red pepper flakes.
  • Cook for 5 minutes, stirring frequently, until onions are softened and fragrant.
  • Add broth and tomatoes; break up the tomatoes with a spoon or mash with a fork (your hands work well too), some chunks are nice.
  • Stir in rosemary, Italian seasoning, salt, pepper, and chickpeas.
  • Bring soup to a simmer.
  • Add pasta and simmer for 10 minutes or until the pasta is al dente. Add chard greens.
  • Add salt, pepper flakes Aleppo flakes for mild spice), or a splash of vinegar as needed to bring balance to the soup. Stir in half of the herbs. Turn off heat.
  • with plenty of freshly grated Parmesan or Pecorino Romano and fresh, chopped parsley or basil. A drizzle of olive oil is nice too.
