Paneer Fingers Recipe (Kids Friendly Paneer Fingers)

Paneer Fingers Recipe (Kids Friendly Paneer Fingers) ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 60 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 200 grams Paneer (cut into fingers like shape)
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala Powder
  • ½ tsp Salt (or to taste)
  • ¼ cup All Purpose Flour
  • ¼ cup Cornstarch
  • 1 tsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 1 tsp Salt (or to taste)
  • ½ cup Water (or as needed)
  • 1 ½ cups Bread Crumbs (I used Panko)
  • Sunflower Oil (for Deep frying)

રીત

  • Cut paneer into fingers like shape. Add in red chilli powder, salt, garam masala powder and mix well. Set aside.
  • Take plain flour, cornstarch, ginger garlic paste, chilli powder, salt and water in a bowl and make it into a thick batter. Set aside.
  • Take breadcrumbs in a bowl and set aside.
  • Now to assemble the paneer.
  • Take each paneer piece, dip it into the batter, roll it in the breadcrumbs really well.
  • Place this in a plate and chill in the fridge for 30 mins.
  • Heat oil for deep frying, drop the paneer pieces one by one till golden brown and crispy.
  • Strain using a slotted spoon and keep it on some paper towel to remove excess oil.
  • Serve hot with ketchup.
