Prawn Curry Recipe (without Coconut)

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 2 tbsp Gingelly Oil (Indian Sesame Oil)
  • 1 tsp Fennel Seeds
  • 1 stick Cinnamon
  • 1 sprig Curry Leaves
  • 1 large Onion (peeled and sliced thinly)
  • 2 no Green Chillies (slit)
  • 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • 2 medium Tomatoes (chopped finely)
  • 1 tbsp Kashmiri Red Chilli Powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric powder
  • 2 tbsp Coriander Powder
  • 1 tsp Garam Masala Powder
  • 1 tsp Salt to taste
  • 1 cup Water (or as needed)
  • 1 medium Onion (peeled and chopped)
  • 1 medium Tomatoes (chopped)
  • 500 grams Prawns (cleaned, deviened)
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • 2 tsp Ginger Garlic Paste
  • Lemon Juice (to taste)
  • 3 tbsp Coriander leaves (finely chopped)

રીત

  • Mix all the ingredients given for marination except oil. Let it sit for 10 to 15 minutes. Grind onion and tomato to a fine paste and set aside.
  • First sear the prawns. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a cooking pan in which we are making the curry. Let the oil get hot.
  • Place the prawns in the oil and cook for 1 min on each side. Flip the prawns over and cook for another 1 minute. Remove the prawns to a bowl and set aside.
  • In the same pan, add 2 tbsp of oil and add in cinnamon, fennel seeds and curry leaves. Let them sizzle in the oil.
  • Add in onions, green chillies and salt. Cook till golden. Add in ginger garlic paste and mix well. Cook for 2 more minutes so the raw smell leaves from it.
  • Add in chopped tomatoes and ground onion tomato paste and cook till raw smell leaves.
  • It will take around 4 to 5 minutes.
  • The mixture will get thick at this point.
  • Now add in spice powders and mix well. Stir fry the spices in the oil for 2 more minutes until it gets aromatic and toasted.
  • Pour in required amount of water.
  • Don't add too much water else the curry may get very thin.
  • Add water little by little and mix well, see the consistency and add more.
  • Now bring the curry mixture to a full boil. Once it comes to a boil, taste and adjust the salt at this point.
  • Now add in the seared prawns and mix well. Simmer the curry for 5 to 10 minutes on medium high heat.
  • Add lemon juice and coriander leaves and mix well. Serve hot with rice.

    • રીત: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi. મૂળ રેસીપી જુઓ.
