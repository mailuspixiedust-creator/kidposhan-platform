Tandoori chicken wings ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 2 pounds raw chicken wings (**)
  • ¾ cup plain yogurt
  • 2 tablespoons lemon juice
  • 1 to 2 teaspoons garam masala (**)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons Kashmiri red chili powder
  • 1 tablespoon kasoori methi (dried fenugreek leaves) (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon ginger paste
  • 1 tablespoon garlic paste
  • ½ teaspoon ground turmeric
  • 2 teaspoons kosher salt
  • 2 tablespoons cilantro (chopped, optional for garnish)

રીત

  • Trim as much skin off of the wings as possible. I use kitchen sheers for this.
  • Combine all ingredients (except cilantro) in a bowl and marinate wings for at least 1 hour or longer.

    • Oven Method

    1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F. Line up wings on a greased grilling rack. Place the rack on top of the baking tray to catch all the drippings.
    2. Bake the wings for 20-25 minutes (in a convection oven) or up to 40 minutes otherwise.
    3. Broil on high for the last 2 minutes to get the char marks.
    4. Note: Wings can also be cooked on a preheated outdoor grill.
    5. Garnish with chopped cilantro and serve hot.
