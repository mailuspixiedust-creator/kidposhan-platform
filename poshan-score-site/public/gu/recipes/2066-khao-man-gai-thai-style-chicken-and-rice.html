સામગ્રી

  • 12 cups water
  • 1 (2-inch) piece ginger, peeled and sliced into ¼-inch-thick rounds
  • 2 tablespoons table salt
  • 6 garlic cloves, smashed and peeled
  • 1 (3½- to 4-pound) whole chicken, giblets discarded
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 1 shallot, chopped fine
  • 1 (2-inch) piece ginger, peeled and cut in half lengthwise
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/4 teaspoon table salt
  • 2 cups jasmine rice, rinsed
  • 1 cup fresh cilantro leaves and stems
  • 1/2 English cucumber, sliced into thin rounds
  • 1/4 cup Thai soybean paste
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup distilled white vinegar
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 Thai chiles, stemmed and minced
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 scallions, sliced thin

રીત

FOR THE CHICKEN AND BROTH:

  1. Combine water, ginger, salt, and garlic in large Dutch oven.
  2. Add chicken to pot, breast side up, and bring to simmer over high heat.
  3. Place large sheet of aluminum foil over pot, then cover with lid.
  4. Reduce heat to low and simmer until breast registers 160 degrees and thighs register at least 175 degrees, 25 to 35 minutes.
  5. Transfer chicken to bowl, tent with foil, and let rest while making rice.
  6. Using slotted spoon, skim foamy residue from surface of chicken broth.
  7. Set aside 3 cups broth for cooking rice.
  8. Cover remaining broth.
