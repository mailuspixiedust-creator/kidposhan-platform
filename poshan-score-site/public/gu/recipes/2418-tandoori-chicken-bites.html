સામગ્રી

  • 450 grams chicken thigh boneless (cut into 1 ½ inch cubes)
  • 1/3 cup whisked curds
  • 1 tablespoon ginger garlic paste
  • 2 tablespoon kashmiri chilli powder (can be adjusted to taste)
  • 1/2 teaspoon garam masala powder
  • 1 tablespoon roasted besan (roasted gram flour)
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • ½ teaspoon roasted & crushed kasuri methi
  • 4 teaspoons lemon juice
  • 2 tablespoons mustard oil
  • 1 - 2 teaspoons cooking oil
  • 1 cup thick curds (whisked)
  • 3 tablespoons finely chopped spring onion greens
  • 1 onion (cut into rings)

રીત

  • Marinating chicken: Add all ingredients for chicken marinade to a bowl, whisk them to combine all the ingredients.
  • Add chicken cubes, toss well in the marinade till the chicken gets coated well.
  • Set aside to marinate for 30 mins to 1 hour.
  • Preheat the airfryer to 180C. Brush the airfryer tray with oil.
  • Place a portion of the marinated chicken cubes on the tray with some gap between each other.
  • Try not to overcrowd the basket to ensure uniform frying.
  • Air fry for 10 minutes at 180C & then for 2 minutes at 200C till the chicken is roasted well.
  • Repeat the same process for the rest of the chicken cubes.
  • Add 1 cup of whisked curds to a serving bowl, add spring onion greens and mix well. Top with air fried chicken, onion rings and serve.
