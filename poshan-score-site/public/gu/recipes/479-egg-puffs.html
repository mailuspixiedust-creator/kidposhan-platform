Egg Puffs ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 90 મળ્યા (સારું). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 4 square Puff Pastry sheets (each about 4 x 4 inch)
  • 2 each Hard Boiled eggs (cut into half)
  • 3 teaspoon Oil
  • 1/2 cup Onion (thinly sliced)
  • 1 teaspoon Ginger (julienned)
  • 1 teaspoon Garlic (julienned)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Red Chilli powder (hot)
  • 1/2 teaspoon Black Pepper powder
  • 1/2 teapoon Salt
  • 2 tablespoon Water (for brushing & sealing)

રીત

  • Thaw the frozen puff pastry sheets.
  • Meanwhile heat oil in a pan and saute onions, ginger and garlic until it becomes lightly soft.
  • Add in turmeric powder, red chilli powder,black pepper powder and salt.
  • Fry till the masala gets crispy a bit and switch off to cool completely.
  • Pre heat oven to 400F
  • Place each puff pastry sheet in an oven proof tray. Divide the onion mixture into 4 equal parts and place them in each sheet.
  • Place the egg on top with cut side facing down and seal applying little water.
  • Bake for 15 to 20 minutes or until the pastry turns golden crisp.
  • Once down, rest it for 5 minutes in the tray and then carefully remove.Serve hot with choice of sauce and a cup of tea!
