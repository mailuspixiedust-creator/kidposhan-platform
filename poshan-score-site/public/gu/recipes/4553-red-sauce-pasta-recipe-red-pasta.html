સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup pasta
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 tablespoon garlic finely chopped
  • 1/2 cup onion finely chopped
  • 1 cup tomato puree
  • 1/2 teaspoon chilli flakes
  • 1/2 teaspoon mixed herbs
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed pepper
  • 1 tablespoon tomato sauce
  • salt to taste
  • cheese for garnish

રીત

  • Boil 4 cups water in a sauce pan. Let it boil - add required salt to it.
  • Add 1 cup pasta to it. I used whole wheat pasta.
  • Cook in medium flame.
  • Reserve 3/4 cup cooked pasta water and set aside.
  • Cook until aldente (fork tender). The timing may differ for whole wheat and regular pasta.
  • Drain the pasta, rinse with cold water and set aside.
  • Heat 1 tablespoon oil - add 1 tablespoon garlic finely chopped.
  • Add 1/2 cup onion, add required salt saute until golden.
  • Add 1 cup tomato puree - blend 4 big tomatoes in a mixer jar without adding water, tomato puree is ready.Cook until raw smell leaves it will take atleast 5-7 mins.
  • Add 1/2 teaspoon chilli flakes, 1/2 teaspoon mixed herbs, 1/2 teaspoon pepper powder and 1 tablespoon tomato sauce.
  • Add reserved pasta cooked water.
  • Give a quick mix.
  • Let the sauce boil for a minute.
  • Add cooked pasta.
  • Toss well until the sauce coats the pasta evenly.
  • Serve hot with a sprinkle of cheese grated.
