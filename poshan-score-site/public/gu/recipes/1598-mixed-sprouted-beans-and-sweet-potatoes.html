Mixed Sprouted Beans and Sweet Potatoes

સામગ્રી

  • 1 tbsp Coconut oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard seeds
  • 2 Red chilies
  • 1 sprig Curry leaves
  • 1 Sweet potato (cubed)
  • 4 cups Sprouted mix beans
  • 3 to 4 Green chilies (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Jaggery (optional)
  • 1/2 cup Coconut (grated)
  • 3/4 tsp Salt (adjust to taste)
  • 2 cups Water

રીત

  • Heat oil in a pressure cooker. Add mustard seeds, red chilies, and curry leaves.
  • When the mustard seeds splutter, add sweet potatoes, sprouted mixed beans, green chilies, jaggery, coconut, salt, and water, and mix well.
  • Cover the cooker and pressure-cook for 1-2 whistles on medium flame.
  • Let the pressure release naturally.
  • Remove the lid and stir everything once.
  • Serve and enjoy.
