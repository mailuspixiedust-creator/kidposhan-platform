Vegetable Focaccia Sandwich

Vegetable Focaccia Sandwich ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

સામગ્રી

  • 4 pieces Whole Wheat Focaccia Bread
  • 3 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 1 large Onion (peeled and sliced thinly)
  • 4 cloves Garlic (peeled chopped finely)
  • 1 medium Bell Pepper (sliced thinly)
  • 1 medium Zucchini (sliced into thin slices)
  • 4 piece Lettuce
  • 2 tsp Dried Oregano
  • 2 no Green Chillies (chopped finely)
  • 1 tsp Salt (to taste)
  • 1 tbsp Lemon Juice (or to taste)
  • ½ cup Mayonnaise
  • 2 cloves Garlic (peeled and chopped finely)

રીત

  • Heat oil in a pan, add in onions, peppers, zucchini and saute till it is lightly golden and well caramelized.
  • Now add in garlic, oregano and salt and mix well. Add in lemon juice and mix well. This is your vegetable filling.
  • For Garlic Mayonnaise. Mix mayo with garlic in a small bowl until combined. Set aside.
  • Now lets assemble the sandwich. Take your whole wheat focaccia, and slit them in middle. You can toast them if you want too.
  • Now spread the mayo on both sides. Place a layer of lettuce, top with the grilled veggies. Place the other half on top and press down.
  • Now you can enjoy it immediately or wrap it in plastic wrap or foil for putting in lunch box.
