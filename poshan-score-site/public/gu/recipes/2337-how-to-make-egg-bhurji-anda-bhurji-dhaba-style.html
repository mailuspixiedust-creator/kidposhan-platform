How to make Egg Bhurji/Anda bhurji (dhaba-style)

How to make Egg Bhurji/Anda bhurji (dhaba-style) ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી પરથી અંદાજિત, એક સર્વિંગ માટે (રેસીપી 3 માટે છે).

સામગ્રી

  • 4 eggs
  • 1 Medium sized onion (finely chopped)
  • 2 Small tomatoes (chopped)
  • 3 tablespoons coriander leaves (finely chopped)
  • 5-6 curry leaves
  • 2-3 green chillies (finely chopped)
  • 1/4 tsp. cumin seeds
  • 1/4 tsp. turmeric powder
  • 1/2 tsp. red chilli powder
  • 1/2 tsp. pepper powder
  • 2-3 tsp. fresh lime juice
  • Salt (to taste)

રીત

  • Add approximately 3 tablespoons of oil to a pan set on medium heat.
  • Add cumin seeds, curry leaves, and onions. Sprinkle some salt on the onion so it cooks faster.
  • Stir fry the onions till they turn translucent, it should take approximately 2 minutes or so
  • Next add chopped green chillies, turmeric powder and chopped tomatoes.
  • Stir fry the tomatoes till they are slightly soft and add red chilli powder
  • Give that a quick stir
  • Turn down the heat and add in the beaten eggs
  • When the eggs are almost solidified, add in black pepper powder and fresh lime juice
  • Continue to stir the eggs till all the liquid evaporates
  • Next add in half the amount of coriander leaves and stir it for some more time
  • Add salt as needed and break up the egg chunks into smaller pieces with your spatula (that's how I saw it served in most dhabas)
  • Garnish with coriander leaves
