70ઠીક

Soy Sauce Chicken ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 2 boneless chicken breasts (cut into 2 inch long strips, approx. 270gms)
  • 1 teaspoon light soy sauce
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • 1 teaspoon corn flour
  • A pinch baking soda
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 teaspoon toasted sesame oil
  • ½ tablespoon rice vinegar
  • ½ tablespoon oyster sauce
  • ½ tablespoon honey
  • 2 tablespoons water
  • 2 teaspoons sesame or any neutral flavoured cooking oil
  • 2 teaspoons finely chopped garlic
  • 2 green chillies (sliced diagonally)
  • 1 onion (cut into thin petals)
  • ½ cup green capsicum cut into 1 inch long strips
  • 1 tablespoon finely chopped coriander leaves

રીત

  • Add chicken strips to a bowl, add all the ingredients for marination, give a good toss and set aside to marinate for 20-25 minutes.
  • Add all the ingredients listed for sauce to a bowl or jar, whisk well and set aside.
  • Heat oil in a pan, add garlic, green chillies and saute on high for about a minute till fragrant.
  • Add chicken & stir continuously on medium to high for 2-3 minutes till they get roasted well.
  • Add onions, capsicums and saute on high for 1-2 minutes till they get roasted well.
  • Add sauce and mix well on high, once it starts boiling, simmer for 2-3 minutes till the chicken gets coated well with the sauce.
  • Garnish with coriander leaves and serve.
