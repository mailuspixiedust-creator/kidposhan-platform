Air Fryer Croutons ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

સામગ્રી

  • 4 slices sourdough bread (or bread of your choice)
  • 2 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil (or butter)
  • ¼ teaspoon garlic powder
  • 1 -2 tablespoons parmesan (finely grated optional)
  • ⅛ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
  • ⅛ teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon dry parsley flakes

રીત

  • Preheat the air fryer to 370°F. Cut the bread of your choice into bite-sized cubes. You can keep or remove the edges, it's your choice.
  • In a large mixing bowl, add olive oil, garlic powder, dried parsley (or oregano), salt, black pepper and finely grated parmesan cheese. Mix well.
  • Add the bread cubes to the olive oil and spices and mix until the bread cubes are well coated.
  • Add the seasoned bread cubes to the air fryer basket. Spread them out in an even layer without overlapping.
  • Air fry for 3 minutes.
  • Then open and shake the air fryer basket to toss the croutons around.
  • Cook for another 3-4 minutes depending on how golden you would like them to be
  • Remove from the air fryer and allow to cool slightly which will make them more crunchy and air fryer croutons are ready!
  • Serve with your favorite salad or soup.
  • Enjoy!
