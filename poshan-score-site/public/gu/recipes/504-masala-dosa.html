Masala Dosa

સામગ્રી

  • 1 1/2 cups sona masoori rice
  • 1/2 cup urad dal gota
  • 1 tablespoon toor dal
  • 1 tablespoon chana dal
  • 1/2 teaspoon methi seeds
  • 1/4 cup medium poha
  • salt (to taste)
  • 1 teaspoon sugar
  • oil (or ghee) (for cooking dosa)

રીત

  • SoakingSoak raw white rice (Sona Masuri or dosa rice), whole urad dal, chana dal, and toor dal, methi seeds together in water.
  • Separately, soak medium poha in water for about 20 minutes.
  • Grinding:Drain the soaked ingredients and transfer them to a blender.
  • Add ½ cup water and grind into a smooth batter.
  • Add more water, if needed, to achieve a medium-thick, pourable consistency.
  • Fermentation:Add salt to taste, then gently mix the batter well with your hand.
  • Mixing by hand helps incorporate air into the batter, making the dosa softer and lighter.
  • Cover the batter and let it ferment in a warm place for 8-12 hours.
  • In cold climates, use an oven with the light on or the Instant Pot Yogurt mode to help maintain a warm temperature.
  • Fermentation is done when the batter doubles in volume and develops a slightly sour smell.
  • Add sugar, stir the fermented batter gently.
  • Mix well before making the dosa.
  • Cooking:Heat a non-stick dosa tawa and pour a ladleful of batter onto it.
  • Spread the batter in a circular motion to form a thin dosa.
  • Drizzle a little ghee or oil around the edges and on top for extra crispiness.
  • Cook until the bottom turns golden brown, You dont have to cook both sides.
  • Spread a tasty potato masala mixture over the dosa.
  • Serve hot with coconut chutney or sambar.
