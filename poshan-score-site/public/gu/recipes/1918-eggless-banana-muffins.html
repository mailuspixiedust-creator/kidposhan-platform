સામગ્રી

  • 1 ½ cups mashed very ripe banana (about 2 medium; be sure the banana is very ripe with a lot of brown spots)
  • ¼ cup plain Greek yogurt (whole-milk style preferred)
  • ¼ cup unsalted butter (melted and slightly cooled)
  • 1 teaspoon pure vanilla extract
  • 1 ½ cups all-purpose flour
  • ⅓ cup granulated sugar (optional)
  • 1 teaspoon cinnamon
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • ½ teaspoon baking soda
  • ¼ teaspoon salt

રીત

  • Preheat the oven to 375 degrees F. Grease a 12-cup muffin pan with nonstick spray.
  • In medium bowl, stir together the banana, yogurt, butter, and vanilla.
  • Add in the flour, sugar (if using), cinnamon, if using, baking powder, baking soda, and salt. Gently stir to combine into a thick batter.
  • Divide batter among the prepared muffin tin, using just under 1/4 cup batter in each. (I use an ice cream scoop to make this easy.)
  • Bake for 14-16 minutes, or until the edges are lightly golden brown and a cake tester inserted into the center comes out cleanly.
  • Remove from oven, then transfer muffins to a wire rack to cool fully.
