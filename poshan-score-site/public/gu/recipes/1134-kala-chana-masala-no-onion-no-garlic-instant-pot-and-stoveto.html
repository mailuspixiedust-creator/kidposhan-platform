સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup Kala Chana (black chickpeas)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1 tbsp ginger paste
  • 1 tsp green chili paste
  • 2 celery stalks finely chopped
  • 1 cup tomatoes finely chopped
  • 2 tbsp chickpea flour
  • Salt to taste
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp Garam masala or Chana masala
  • 2 cups water
  • ¼ tsp sugar (optional)
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves finely chopped
  • Lime or lemon juice to taste

રીત

Stovetop Pressure Cooker Method

  1. Wash Kala Chana (Black Chickpeas) and soak in 3 cups of water for 6-8 hours or overnight.
  2. When you are ready to make Kala Chana Masala, drain the water from Chana and set it aside.
  3. Heat oil in a pressure cooker and add cumin seeds. Once cumin seeds start to sizzle, add chili and ginger paste and saute for a few seconds.
  4. Add chopped celery and cook for a minute.
  5. Add chickpea flour, mix, and right away, add chopped tomatoes. Mix well and cook for a minute.
  6. Add salt, turmeric powder, red chili powder, cumin powder, and Garam masala powder or Chana masala.
  7. Continue cooking until tomatoes are soft and mushy and the oil separates from the sides.
  8. Add Kala Chana and 2 cups of water. Mix well.
  9. Cover the pressure cooker lid and cook for 8-9 whistles or until chana is cooked thoroughly.
  10. Once pressure is released from the cooker, open the lid. Add lime juice, sugar, and coriander leaves. Mix well and transfer the curry to the serving bowl.
