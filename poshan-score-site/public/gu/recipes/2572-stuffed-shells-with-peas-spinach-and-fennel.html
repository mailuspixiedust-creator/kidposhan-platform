સામગ્રી

  • ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 (28-ounce) can crushed tomatoes
  • 1 cup passata
  • ½ teaspoon red pepper flakes
  • 3 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
  • ¾ teaspoon table salt
  • ¼ teaspoon sugar
  • 1 tablespoon extra-virgin olive oil
  • 1 large fennel bulb, stalks discarded, bulb halved, cored, and chopped fine
  • 1 large shallot, halved and sliced thin
  • 2 ¼ teaspoons table salt, divided
  • 1 pound frozen peas, thawed
  • 8 ounces Taleggio cheese, rind removed, divided
  • 2 teaspoons grated lemon zest
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • 1 pound frozen chopped spinach, thawed and squeezed dry
  • 12 ounces jumbo pasta shells
  • Table salt for cooking pasta
  • ¼ cup grated Parmesan cheese

રીત

FOR THE SAUCE:

  1. Heat ¼ cup extra-virgin olive oil and 2 minced garlic cloves in medium saucepan over medium heat, stirring frequently, until garlic is fragrant but not browned, about 2 minutes.
  2. Stir in 1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 cup passata, and ½ teaspoon pepper flakes and simmer until slightly thickened, about 10 minutes.
  3. Stir in 3 tablespoons chopped basil, ¾ teaspoon table salt, and ¼ teaspoon sugar.
  4. Cover and set aside.
