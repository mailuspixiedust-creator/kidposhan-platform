સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup (200 grams) urad dal (Indian black lentils/ whole black gram)
  • ¼ cup (55 grams) rajma (red kidney beans, or sub with urad dal)
  • 4 cups (960 ml) water to pressure cook the dal (more to cook in pot)
  • 2 tablespoons (30 ml) ghee (or use oil)
  • 3 tablespoons (42 grams) unsalted butter (cultured butter if you can)
  • ½ cup (65 grams) onions (fine chopped 1 small to medium)
  • 1 tablespoon (18 grams) ginger garlic paste (or ¾ tbsp., 9 grams, each grated)
  • 1 cup (220 grams) tomato puree (bottled or 2 to 3 medium tomatoes pureed in a blender)
  • ¾ to 1 ½ teaspoon Kashmiri red chili powder (adjust to preference, I use 1 tsp)
  • 1 teaspoon garam masala (+ ½ tsp more if you want at the finishing stage)
  • 1 teaspoon sea salt (adjust as needed)
  • 2 cups (480 ml) hot water (to add later)
  • 1 tablespoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves, optional)
  • ¼ to ⅓ cup (60 to 80 ml) heavy cream (cooking cream or whipping cream, more to garnish if you want)
  • 1 bay leaf, 2 green cardamoms, 1 black cardamom

રીત

How to make Dal Makhani (stovetop)

  1. Pour 4 cups fresh water to the dal and pressure cook for 10 whistles on a medium heat or in the instant pot for 20 mins (high pressure).
  2. If you don’t have a cooker, cook in a heavy bottom pot until soft and tender, adding more hot water as needed.
  3. Both urad dal and rajma must cook down very soft.
  4. When mashed with a spoon the dal and rajma must be soft and get mashed well easily.
  5. If not then pour another half cup hot water and continue to cook for another 3 to 4 whistles on a medium heat.
  6. Heat a heavy bottom pot with ghee. Add the whole spices if using and let them sizzle for a minute.
  7. Stir in the onions and saute until golden, for 7 mins. Stir in ginger garlic paste and saute for 1 minute until aromatic.
  8. Pour the tomato puree and cook for 3 to 4 minutes.
  9. Stir in salt, chili powder & garam masala.
  10. Saute well until the masala smells good and turns thick, for 4 to 5 mins.
  11. Mash the cooked urad dal slightly and pour it along with the stock (lentil cooked liquid). Pour 1 cup hot water & mix.
  12. Cook on a low flame until the dal becomes thick, stir often in between to prevent burning the bottom. After 60 mins, the dal thickens.
  13. Add another ½ to 1 cup hot boiling water and continue to cook until really thick. (This takes another 30 mins.
  14. Totally cook for about 90 mins from the time you added the cooked dal to the pot.
  15. The cook time may vary depending on your pot and stove)
  16. Crush kasuri methi in your palm and add it.
  17. Also pour the cream.
  18. Stir and cook again for 6 to 8 mins until it becomes thick and creamy.
  19. Taste and add more salt or garam masala if needed.
  20. Add butter and mix well.
