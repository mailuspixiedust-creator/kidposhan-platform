સામગ્રી

  • 1 Avocado
  • 1/4 cup Cilantro
  • 1/4 Jalapeno (seeds and membrane reserved)
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1/4 cup Sour cream (or plain greek yogurt)
  • 1/2 Lime (juiced)
  • 2 tablespoon Olive Oil
  • 1/4 teaspoon Salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon Black Pepper

રીત

  • Place all the ingredients In a food processor or blender.
  • Process until smooth, stopping to scrape down the sides a few times. You can also keep it a bit chunky based on your preference.
  • Enjoy right away or keep in an airtight container for 3-5 days.
