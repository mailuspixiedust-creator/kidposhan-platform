સામગ્રી

  • 4 cups Cooked Basmati rice
  • 1 cup Onion (sliced)
  • 4 each Green Chilli
  • 1 teaspoon Ginger Garlic paste
  • 1/4 cup Tomato
  • 1 tablespoon Yogurt
  • 1/2 teaspoon Biryani Masala / Garam Masala powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon Turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Red Chilli powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon Cumin seeds
  • 3/4 teaspoon Salt (or to taste)
  • 1/4 cup Coriander leaves
  • 1/4 cup Mint leaves
  • 1 teaspoon Ghee
  • 2 tablespoons Oil
  • 15 florets Cauliflower
  • 1 teaspoon Tandoori Masala powder (store bought, used kissin masala)
  • 1/4 cup Bhajji Bonda mix (store bought, used aachi masala)
  • 1/4 teaspoon Ginger Garlic paste
  • 1/4 teaspoon Red Chilli powder
  • 1/4 teaspoon Salt (only if needed) (store bought mix includes salt)
  • 3 tablespoon Water
  • 1/2 cup Oil (for deep frying)

રીત

  • Make a paste combining tandoori masala, bhajji bonda mix, corn flour, ginger garlic paste, red chilli powder, salt and water.
  • Coat the cauliflower with this paste.
  • Heat 1/4 cup oil in a fry pan and shallow fry the coated cauliflower until lightly crisp.
  • Remove from and pan keep it aside.
  • Now heat oil in another pan, season with cumin seeds.
  • Fry the onions & green chillies till brown and crisp.
  • Throw in few mint and coriander leaves at this stage.
  • Add in ginger garlic paste and saute well.
  • Add in the tomato along with yogurt and saute until it becomes mushy.
  • Add in the turmeric powder, red chilli powder, garam masala powder and salt.
  • You will be sauteing this until the mixture turns dry... more like a dry mixture.served.
  • Now add the cooked basmati rice to this and mix to combine.
  • Alternately you may layer the rice with masala.
  • Add in the shallow fried cauliflower, a little mint and coriander leaves along with a drizzle of ghee.
  • Cover the pan with a lid and bring the flame to the lowest setting...cook for 10 minutes and switch off.
  • Drizzle ghee again if you like and its all ready to be served.
