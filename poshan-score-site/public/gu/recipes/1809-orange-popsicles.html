સામગ્રી

  • 4 cups fresh orange juice (from about 10 -12 oranges)
  • ⅓ cup sugar
  • 1-2 tablespoons fresh lemon juice (adjust to taste)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)

રીત

  • Peel the oranges.
  • Use a knife to slice off the peel and remove as much white pith and any seeds as possible.
  • You can further cut them into chunks and remove any seeds (if any).
  • Run the peeled orange through a slow juicer until you have 4 cups of fresh orange juice.
  • Alternatively, juice them by hand or with a citrus juicer.
  • If using a blender, blend until smooth, then strain through a fine-mesh strainer.
  • Add the orange juice, sugar, lemon juice, and salt, to a large bowl or pitcher. Stir or blend until the sugar is completely dissolved.
  • Taste the mixture and adjust the sugar if needed. It should taste slightly sweeter than you want the finished popsicles to taste.
  • Pour the mixture into popsicle molds, leaving a little space at the top for expansion. Insert the popsicle sticks.
  • Freeze for at least 4-6 hours or overnight, until completely firm.
  • To unmold, run the outside of the popsicle mold under warm water for a few seconds, then gently pull out the popsicles and serve.
