રીત

Hot Honey

  1. Combine honey, jalapeno and crushed red pepper in a small pot.
  2. Place the pot over medium heat.
  3. As soon as the pot comes to a gentle simmer, remove the pot from the heat and allow the chilis to infuse the honey for 15 minutes.
  4. Strain the honey mixture through a fine mesh sieve.
  5. Set aside until chicken is complete.
  6. Leftover honey can be stored in a container in the refrigerator for one week.
