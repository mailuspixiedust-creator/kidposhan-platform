Vegetable Biryani Recipe (Pressure Cooker Veg Biryani)

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 2 cups Basmati Rice
  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tbsp Ghee
  • 3 piece Pandan Leaves / Ramba Ilai / Biryani leaf
  • 1 large Onions (sliced thinly)
  • 2 no Green Chillies (slit)
  • 1 large Tomatoes (chopped finely)
  • 3 tbsp Biryani Masala Powder
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 3 cups Water
  • 2 tsp Lemon Juice
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 large Carrots (chopped)
  • 1 cup Cauliflower (chopped)
  • 10 no Beans (chopped)
  • 1 cup Green Peas
  • ½ cup Coriander Leaves (chopped)
  • ½ cup Mint Leaves (chopped)
  • 2 inch Ginger
  • 6 cloves Garlic (peeled)
  • 2 tbsp Ghee
  • 10 no Cashews

રીત

  • Wash and soak rice in lots of water for 30 mins. Take the grinding ingredients in a blender and make it into a smooth paste.
  • Heat oil and 1 tblspn of ghee in a pressure cooker.
  • Add in pandan leaf and fry for 30 seconds.
  • Now add in sliced onions and saute till it is brown.
  • Add in green chillies and ground masala and saute for 2 mins till raw smell leaves from it.
  • Add in tomatoes and cook for 2 mins or so.
  • Sprinkle in biryani spice mix and salt.
  • Mix well.
  • Add in chopped veggies and toss well in the masala.
  • Add in soaked drained rice and mix well.
  • Pour in water and some sugar.
  • Taste and adjust for seasoning.
  • Bring this to boil, simmer the stove and pressure cook for 1 whistle.
  • Simmer for 5 minutes and turn off the flame.
  • Switch off the heat and let the steam go all by itself.
  • Open the cooker.
  • Now heat 1 tblspn of ghee and fry cashews till it golden, pour this ghee and cashews over the rice and mix well.
  • Serve.

    • રીત: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi. મૂળ રેસીપી જુઓ.
