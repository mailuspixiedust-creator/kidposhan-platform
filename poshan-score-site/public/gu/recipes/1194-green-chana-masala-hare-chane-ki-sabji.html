સામગ્રી

  • 2 cups (300g) fresh green chickpeas (Choliya)
  • 4 cups water
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 1 tsp Cumin seeds
  • ¼ tsp Hing
  • 2 green chilis slitted
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • ½ cup finely chopped tomatoes
  • ¼ tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • ½ cup whisked yogurt
  • Boiled green chickpeas
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ tsp garam masala or chana masala
  • ½ tsp Kasoori methi
  • ½ cup water or as required
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander leaves

રીત

Cooking The Green Chickpeas

  1. Add water to a medium saucepan and bring to a boil.
  2. Add green chickpeas, salt, and sugar, and stir well.
  3. Boil the chickpeas until they are thoroughly cooked.
  4. It will take about 10-12 minutes.
  5. Once they are cooked, drain the water and set the boiled chickpeas aside.
