Chicken Scramble

100સારું

Chicken Scramble ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 100 મળ્યા (સારું). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 1 tbsp Oil
  • 2 large Chicken Breast
  • 1 tbsp Cornstarch
  • ½ tsp Baking Soda
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • ½ tsp Black Pepper Powder
  • 2 tsp Oil
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 medium Onion (chopped finely)
  • 2 no Green Chillies (chopped finely)
  • 1 small Tomato (chopped finely)
  • 4 large Egg Whites
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • ½ tsp Black Pepper
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves (finely chopped)

રીત

  • Slice chicken breast into thin slices.
  • Take chicken in a bowl, add in cornstarch, salt, pepper, baking soda and 1 tbsp oil.
  • Mix this really well and let it sit for 5 minutes.
  • Now heat 1 tsp of oil in a pan, add chicken in the pan and cook for 3 minutes stiring often till cooked.
  • Remove it to a bowl.
  • Heat oil in the pan, add garlic, chillies and onions. Saute for 2 minutes till translucent. Add in tomatoes and cook till mushy. Season with salt and pepper.
  • Add in egg whites and mix, add in the cooked chicken and mix through and scrambled. Add in coriander leaves and mix well. Serve.

    • રીત: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi. મૂળ રેસીપી જુઓ.
