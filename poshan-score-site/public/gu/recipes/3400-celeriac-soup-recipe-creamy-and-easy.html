સામગ્રી

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • 1 white or yellow onion-diced
  • 1 large fennel bulb, diced (about 1 ½ cups) Or sub 1 1/2 cups celery.
  • 2 garlic cloves, rough chopped
  • ½ teaspoon salt
  • 2 grapefruit-sized celeriac, peeled and diced (about 5 cups, diced)
  • 8 Cups Chicken stock or Veggie stock
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 teaspoon dried thyme, or 1 tablespoon fresh thyme
  • ¼ teaspoon white pepper
  • ¼ cup creme fraise or sour cream for garnish (optional)
  • 1 cup packed Italian parsley (stems ok)
  • ½ cup olive oil
  • ½ clove garlic
  • ¼ teaspoon kosher salt
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice

રીત

  • Slice off the top and bottom of the celery root to create a stable base.
  • Stand it upright and use a sharp knife to cut away the thick, knobby skin, following its natural shape.
  • Cut into evenly sized, small cubes.
  • Heat olive oil in a large, heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat.
  • Add the diced onion and sauté until tender, about 5 minutes.
  • Add the fennel, garlic, and salt.
  • Reduce heat slightly and continue sautéing for 7 minutes, stirring occasionally, until softened and fragrant.
  • Add the diced celeriac, pepper, bay leaf, thyme, and stock.
  • Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat to low, cover, and cook for 15–20 minutes, until the celeriac is very tender.
  • Remove the bay leaf.
  • Using an immersion blender, puree the soup until silky smooth. (Alternatively, carefully blend in batches in a regular blender, filling it no more than halfway.
  • Hold the lid securely with a kitchen towel and start on the lowest speed) Return the soup to the pot.
  • Gently reheat if needed. Taste and adjust the salt and pepper. Add a splash of stock for a thinner consistency.
  • Ladle into bowls and finish with a drizzle of parsley oil and an optional swirl of crème fraîche or sour cream.

    • Optional Parsley Oil

    1. Combine all ingredients in a blender or food processor and pulse until well blended. Drizzle over the soup just before serving.
