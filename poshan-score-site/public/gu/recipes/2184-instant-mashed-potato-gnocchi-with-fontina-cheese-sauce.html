સામગ્રી

  • 2 cups (4 ounces/113 grams) plain instant mashed potato flakes
  • 1 cup (5 ounces/142 grams) all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons table salt, plus salt for cooking gnocchi
  • 1 ½ cups water
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons unsalted butter
  • 1 garlic clove, minced
  • 2 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 1 ¼ cups whole milk
  • 4 ounces (113 grams) fontina cheese, shredded (1 cup)
  • ½ teaspoon table salt
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • Grated Parmesan cheese

રીત

FOR THE GNOCCHI:

  1. Whisk potato flakes, flour, and salt together in large bowl.
  2. Whisk water and egg together in separate bowl.
  3. Add water mixture to potato flake mixture and stir with wooden spoon until fully combined and mixture forms dough ball.
  4. Let sit for 3 minutes for potato flakes and flour to hydrate.
  5. Turn out dough onto lightly floured counter and knead until dough has texture of Play-Doh and springs back halfway when poked with your finger, about 3 minutes.
  6. Lightly dust dough with flour and let rest on counter for 5 minutes.
  7. Divide dough into 6 equal pieces.
  8. On very lightly floured counter, roll 1 piece into ¾-inch-thick rope.
  9. Lightly dust rope with flour.
  10. Using floured bench scraper, cut rope crosswise into ¾-inch pieces; transfer gnocchi to lightly floured rimmed baking sheet.
  11. Repeat with remaining dough pieces.
  12. Press gnocchi, cut side down, on tines of fork, then roll downward to create grooves.
  13. If dough sticks, dust your thumb and fork with flour.
  14. Return gnocchi to sheet.
