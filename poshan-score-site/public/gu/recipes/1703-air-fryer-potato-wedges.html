How to Cut and Prep Potatoes for making crispy wedges

  1. First, scrub the potatoes well and wipe dry using a kitchen towel.
  2. Cut the Potato in half (lengthwise)
  3. Further, cut each half of the potato into two parts.
  4. Each of the 4 quarters can be divided into 2-3 thick wedges, depending on the size of the potatoes.
  5. Soak the potatoes in ice cold water for 20 mins (to get rid of excess starch).
  6. Drain the water completely after 20 mins, pat dry using a dry kitchen towel. Air dry it for a few minutes to ensure there is no moisture.
