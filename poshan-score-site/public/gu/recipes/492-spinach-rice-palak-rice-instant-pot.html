રીત

Stovetop Pressure Cooker Method:

  1. Follow the same process mentioned above for Instant Pot.
  2. Give 2 whistles on medium flame and let the pressure release naturally.
  3. All the other steps remain the same.
  4. After 10 mins fluff up the rice gently.
