Urad Dal And Vegetable Appe Recipe (South Indian Dal Appe)

80સારું

Urad Dal And Vegetable Appe Recipe (South Indian Dal Appe) ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 80 મળ્યા (સારું). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 1/2 cup urad dal (safed urad dal)
  • 1/4 cup grated carrot
  • 1/4 cup grated cabbage
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped coriander (dhania)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • oil for greasing and cooking

રીત

  • what is urad dal and vegetable appe made of ?
  • South Indian dal appeis made from1/2 cupurad dal (split black lentils),1/4 cupgrated carrot,1/4 cupgrated cabbage,2 tbspfinelychopped coriander (dhania),1/2 tspchilli powder andsaltto taste.
  • See below image of list of ingredients for urad dal and vegetable appe.
  • For making theurad dal and vegetable appe, clean and wash the urad dal 2-3 times in water.
  • Soak it in enough water for atleast 2 hours.
  • Urad dal after soaking for 2 hours.
  • Drain the urad dal. The urad dal will almost double in volume and become soft.
  • Keep aside.
  • In a mixer put the soaked and drained urad dal.
  • Add&frac12; cup of water.
  • Blend till smooth.
  • In a mixer put the soaked and drained urad dal.
  • Add1/4 cupgrated carrot.
  • Add1/4 cupgrated cabbage.
  • Add2 tbspfinelychopped coriander (dhania).
  • Add1/2 tspchilli powder.
  • Add salt to taste. We added 1/2tsp salt.
  • Mix well.
  • Pour 1 tbsp of the batter into each mould.
  • Cook on medium heat for 1 minute till the lower surface becomes golden brown.
  • Brush little oil on the top of the appe.
  • Then turn each appe upside down using a fork and spoon or 2 spoonsso as to cook them from the other side using a little oil.
  • Appes are turned over.
  • Then brush the top of the appe with oil.
  • Keep cooking and turning till appes are done.
  • Then turn each appe upside down using a fork and spoon or 2 spoonsso as to cook them from the other side using a little oil.
  • You can also add chopped onions in appe.
  • Instead of red chilli powder you can also add finely chopped green chillies.
