Pork Curry

70ઠીક

Pork Curry ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 500 grams pork (with or without skin & fat)
  • 1/2 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 2 medium sized onions (finely sliced)
  • 1 " ginger (minced)
  • 7-8 fat flakes of garlic (minced)
  • 2 medium tomatoes (finely chopped)
  • 2 sprigs curry leaves
  • 1/4 cup fresh coriander (chopped)
  • 1-2 teaspoons red chilli powder (adjust to taste)
  • 2 teaspoons coriander powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1 cup water
  • salt to taste
  • 2 tablespoons oil
  • 1 teaspoon aniseed
  • 1 cardamom
  • 1 " stick cassia bark or cinnamon
  • 3 cloves

રીત

  • Cut the pork into medium sized pieces, wash & allow to drain
  • Heat oil in a heavy-based wok/pan and add the mustard seeds.
  • When they stop spluttering, add the curry leaves and fry for a couple of seconds.
  • Add the chopped garlic & ginger and fry till the raw smell vanishes.
  • Add the sliced onions and fry till they turn golden.
  • Then reduce the heat and add the red chilli powder and fry for a couple of seconds before adding the coriander & turmeric powders.
  • Add a cup of water (approx) and bring the mixture to a boil.
  • Add the pork, garam masala powder & salt to taste.
  • Mix well and cover and cook on a low heat till the pork is tender and the gravy thickens.
  • Add the tomatoes and cook for another 5-7 minutes or till they turn mushy.
  • Garnish with chopped coriander & take off the heat. Serve hot with rice or neer dosa, chapathis or appams
