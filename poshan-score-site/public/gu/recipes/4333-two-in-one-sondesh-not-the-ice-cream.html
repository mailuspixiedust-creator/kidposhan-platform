સામગ્રી

  • 2 Liter Full Cream Milk
  • 2 Lemon
  • 600 g Chana/ Cottage Cheese: (prepared from two liters Milk)
  • 5 tbsp Powdered Sugar
  • 3 drops Vanilla Essence/ Extract
  • 4 tbsp Strawberry Jam/ Strawberry Compote
  • 6 tbsp Condensed Milk
  • 1 tsp Ghee (to grease the mold)

રીત

To make Chana or Cottage Cheese:

  1. Boil milk in a deep-bottom pan and reduce to 1/3.
  2. When the milk is reduced in quantity, simmer the flame and add lemon juice to the milk to curdle it.
  3. Once the milk starts curdling, switch the flame off.
  4. Cover the pan with a lid and leave that for 10 minutes.
  5. Strain the water from Chana/ cottage cheese using a strainer.
  6. Put the Chana in the middle of a muslin cloth and tie the edges of the cloth to close it.
  7. Place something heavy (I have used my “Shil Nora / Shil batta”) for this over the Chana-filled cloth.
  8. Leave it for 30 minutes to drain away excess water from it.
  9. After thirty minutes, remove the cloth and there should be a block of cheese as the end product.
