Creamy Chicken Afritada with Mushrooms

સામગ્રી

  • 1 1/2 lbs chicken (cut into serving pieces)
  • 2 pieces potatoes (cubed)
  • 1 piece carrot (sliced)
  • 8 oz tomato sauce
  • 3 pieces dried bay leaves
  • 1 piece red onion (chopped)
  • 2 teaspoons garlic (minced)
  • 2 cups chicken broth
  • 1/2 teaspoon sugar
  • 1 cup fresh button mushrooms (sliced)
  • 1 pack NESTLÉ All-Purpose Cream (250 ml)
  • 4 tablespoons cooking oil (divided)
  • 8 grams Maggi Magic Sarap
  • ground black pepper (to taste)

રીત

  • Heat 1 tablespoon of cooking oil in a pan over medium-high heat.
  • Add the mushrooms and sauté until lightly browned and the moisture they release has evaporated.
  • Remove from the pan and set aside.
  • Heat the remaining 3 tablespoons of cooking oil in a cooking pot. Sauté the onion and garlic until the onion softens.
  • Add the chicken. Cook for 30 seconds, then turn each piece over and cook the other side for another 30 seconds.
  • Pour in the tomato sauce and chicken broth. Cover the pot and bring to a boil.
  • Add the dried bay leaves. Cover the pot and simmer over medium heat for 30 minutes, or until the chicken is tender.
  • Add the carrot and cook for 3 minutes.
  • Add the potatoes. Cover the pot and continue cooking for 8 minutes, or until almost tender.
  • Add the sautéed mushrooms. Stir gently and cook for 2 minutes.
  • Pour in the NESTLÉ All-Purpose Cream.
  • Stir gently until fully incorporated into the tomato sauce.
  • Continue cooking over low heat for 3 to 5 minutes without allowing the sauce to boil vigorously.
  • Add the sugar. Season with Maggi Magic Sarap and ground black pepper to taste.
  • Transfer to a serving bowl. Serve hot with steamed rice. Share and enjoy!
