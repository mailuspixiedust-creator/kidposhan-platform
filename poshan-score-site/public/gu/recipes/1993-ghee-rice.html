Ghee Rice

70ઠીક

Ghee Rice ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 1 cups basmati rice
  • 2 tablespoons ghee (or clarified butter)
  • 1 medium onion (thinly sliced, optional)
  • 1 ¾ cups water for Indian cooker (1¼ cup Instant pot, 2 cups for normal pot)
  • 1 green chilies (slit or chopped)
  • ½ teaspoon Salt (adjust to taste)
  • 14 cashewnuts (kaju, 28 split)
  • 14 raisins
  • 2 inch cinnamon (dalchini)
  • 4 cloves (laung)
  • 3 to 4 green cardamoms (elaichi)
  • 1 strand mace (javitri)
  • 1 star anise (chakri phool)
  • 1 bay leaf (tej patta)

રીત

Preparation

  1. Add rice to a bowl and rinse well at least thrice.
  2. Soak it for at least 20 to 30 minutes.
  3. Drain and set aside.
  4. While the rice soaks, slice onion thinly & slit green chilies.
  5. Heat a pressure cooker or a pot with ghee. Fry cashews till lightly golden, add raisins and stir up. When they turn plump set them aside in a plate.
  6. To the same pan, add onions.
  7. You can also skip onions from the recipe.
  8. Fry them till golden, about to caramelize but not burnt.
  9. Remove them to a plate and keep aside to garnish. (These are the fried onions / barista. )
