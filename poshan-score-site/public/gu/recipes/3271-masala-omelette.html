Masala Omelette

60ઠીક

Masala Omelette ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 60 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

સામગ્રી

  • 2 large Eggs
  • 3 tbsp Onion (peeled, chopped finely)
  • 3 tbsp Tomato (deseeded, chopped finely)
  • 1 no Green Chillies (chopped finely)
  • 2 tbsp Coriander leaves (chopped finely)
  • ½ tsp Black Pepper Powder
  • ½ tsp Salt (to taste)
  • 1 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 2 no Mushrooms (stalks removed)
  • 1 small Tomato (halved)
  • 2 tsp Butter
  • Salt and Black Pepper Powder (to taste)

રીત

  • Peel and chop onions into fine dice. Cut tomatoes in half, remove seeds from it and chop it fine. Chop green chillies in to fine dice.
  • Take eggs in a bowl, add in salt and black pepper and mix until combined. Set aside.
  • Heat oil in a pan. Add in onions, tomatoes, green chillies, salt, pepper and saute for 30 seconds.
  • Now pour the mixed eggs over and mix gently.
  • Gently pull the egg and scramble gently.
  • Cook on low heat until top is set.
  • Sprinkle coriander leaves on top of the egg.
  • Once the top is set, fold gently. Serve hot on a plate.
  • Heat butter on the same pan.
  • Place mushrooms, tomato halves on the butter.
  • Season with salt and pepper and toss well.
  • Let the mushrooms and tomatoes cook till seared.
  • Serve with omelette.

    • રીત: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi. મૂળ રેસીપી જુઓ.
