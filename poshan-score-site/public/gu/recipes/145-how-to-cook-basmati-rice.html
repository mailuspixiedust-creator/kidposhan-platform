સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup Basmati Rice
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 6 cups Water
  • ½ tsp Salt
  • 1 ½ cups Water

રીત

Draining Method

  1. Take rice in a bowl and wash it really well with different changes of water.
  2. Now take it in a bowl and cover with water.
  3. Let it soak for a minimum of 30 minutes.
  4. Strain through a sieve and set aside.
  5. Bring lots of water to full boil in a sauce pan.
  6. Add salt to it.
  7. Add in the strained rice and boil this until the rice is cooked to your liking.
  8. It will take around 8 to 10 minutes.
  9. Keep tasting the rice to check whether it is cooked or not.
  10. Once cooked, strain the rice through a strainer.
  11. Keep covered for 5 minutes.
  12. Once it is strained, fluff the rice and serve.
