સામગ્રી

  • 750 g Goat Meat (medium pieces; use Baby goat meat)
  • 4 Potato
  • 2 Onion
  • 1 Tbsp Ginger Paste
  • 1 Tbsp Garlic Paste
  • 2 Tomato
  • 100 g Curd
  • 1 Tsp Bengali Garam Masala
  • 1 Tsp Jeera /Cumin Powder
  • 1 Tsp Dhane/ Dhania / Coriander powder
  • 1 Tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 2 Dried Red Chilies
  • 2 Bay leaves
  • 1 Tbsp Sugar
  • 2 Tsp Red Chili Powder
  • 4 Tbsp Mustard Oil
  • 1 Tsp Ghee

રીત

Prep Work

  1. First, wash the baby goat meat and pat it dry.
  2. Next, prepare a marinade with half the turmeric powder, red chili powder, ginger-garlic paste, and yogurt.
  3. Let the meat soak in this marinade for about an hour and a half, or even better, leave it in the fridge overnight.
  4. While the meat is marinating, peel and cut the potatoes in half, and roughly chop the onions. Then, either blend the tomatoes into a paste or chop them finely.
  5. Now, heat some oil in a deep pan.
  6. Fry the potatoes until they turn a light golden color, but don't cook them all the way through.
  7. Take them out and set them aside.
  8. In the same oil, toss in a bay leaf, a dried red chili, the chopped onions, and a pinch of sugar. Cook until the onions become light brown.
  9. Add the remaining ginger-garlic paste, along with salt, red chili powder, garam masala powder, cumin powder, and coriander powder.
  10. Stir in the chopped or pureed tomatoes and cook over medium heat for about 5-7 minutes, until the oil starts to separate from the mixture.
