સામગ્રી

  • 200 grams Instant Rice Sevai (Rice vermicelli)
  • 2 liter Water
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • ½ tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Split Urad Dal (Ulundu Paruppu)
  • ¼ tsp Asafoetida
  • 2 no Dry Red Chillies (broken into small pieces)
  • 1 sprig Curry leaves
  • 1 tsp Turmeric Powder
  • 2 tbsp Lemon Juice

રીત

  • You can use leftover idiyappam for this recipe.
  • Or use instant rice sevai.
  • Bring water to a full boil.
  • Add in salt and mix well.
  • Now add in the rice sevai and switch off the heat.
  • Let the sevai sit in the hot water for 1 minute.
  • Now strain this completely and set aside to cool.
  • Now make tempering. Heat oil in a pan, add mustard seeds, urad dal, dry red chillies, asafoetida, curry leaves and allow them to splatter.
  • Now turn off the heat and add in turmeric powder, lemon juice and salt and mix well.
  • Finally add in the cooked strained sevai and toss to coat. Serve hot.
