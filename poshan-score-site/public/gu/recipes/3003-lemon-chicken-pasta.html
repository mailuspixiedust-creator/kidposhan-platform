સામગ્રી

  • 2 Chicken Breasts (boneless, skinless)
  • 125 g Pasta
  • 1/4 cup Plain Flour (Maida)
  • 1 1/2 tbsp Olive Oil
  • 5 to 6 Garlic Cloves (peeled, sliced)
  • 1/2 cup White Wine
  • 3 tsp Lemon Juice
  • 1/4 cup Cheese (grated)
  • handful Coriander Leaves or Basil (chopped)
  • 1/4 tsp Black Pepper Powder
  • 1/4 tsp Oregano
  • 1/4 tsp Red Chilli Flakes
  • as per taste Salt

રીત

  • Using a rolling pin, pound the chicken breasts to 1/4 inch thickness.
  • Sprinkle some salt and pepper powder on top.
  • Dredge it in the flour on both sides and shake off any excess.
  • Cook the pasta as per packet instructions for al dente.
  • Heat 3 tsp of oil in a pan over medium flame.
  • Cook the chicken until it is no longer pink, about 2 to 3 minutes on each side.
  • Remove and keep it warm.
  • To the same pan, add the remaining oil.
  • Stir-fry the garlic over medium flame for 30 seconds.
  • Add wine and cook until the liquid is reduced by half, stirring to loosen the browned bits from the pan.
  • Add lemon juice and stir.
  • Drain the pasta and transfer to a large bowl. Reserve some of the pasta water.
  • Add the oregano, chilli flakes, grated cheese, coriander leaves, garlic mixture, salt and pepper powder.
  • Toss well and add a little of the pasta water if it is too dry.
  • Transfer the chicken and the pasta to a serving plate.
  • Drizzle some of the remaining garlic mixture and garnish with coriander leavs.
  • Garnish with cheese if desired and serve.
