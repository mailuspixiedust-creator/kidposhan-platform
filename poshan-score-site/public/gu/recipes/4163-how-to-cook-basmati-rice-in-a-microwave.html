રીત

Prep Work

  1. To prepare the rice, begin by placing one cup (about 200g) of Basmati rice into a bowl.
  2. While I prefer using whole grain fragrant rice, feel free to use any other variety you like.
  3. Next, wash the rice thoroughly to remove extra starch.
  4. The easiest way is to rinse it under a running tap.
  5. If that's not possible, add water to the bowl, stir the grains, and then drain and replace the water two or three times until the water runs clear.
  6. Be careful not to break the rice grains during washing.
  7. Once washed, soak the rice in fresh water for 20 to 30 minutes, then drain the water completely.
