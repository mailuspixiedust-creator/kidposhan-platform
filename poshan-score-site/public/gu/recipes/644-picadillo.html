Picadillo

Picadillo ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 70 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

સામગ્રી

  • 1 1/2 lbs ground beef
  • 1 piece yellow onion (minced)
  • 4 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 14 ounces diced tomatoes (one can, with the juice)
  • 1/2 cup raisins
  • 1 cup beef broth
  • 3/4 cup Manzanilla olives (drained)
  • 1 piece potato (cubed)
  • 2 pieces carrots (cubed)
  • 3/4 cup frozen green peas
  • 3 tablespoons olive oil
  • Salt and ground black pepper (to taste)

રીત

  • Heat the olive oil in a cooking pot.
  • Sauté the onion and garlic until the onion softens and turns translucent.
  • Add the ground beef. Cook while stirring until the beef turns light brown.
  • Pour in the diced tomatoes and beef broth, then add the raisins. Stir and bring the mixture to a boil. Cover the pot and cook for 30 minutes.
  • Add the Manzanilla olives, carrots, potato, and green peas.
  • Stir to combine.
  • Cover the pot and continue cooking for 8 minutes, or until the vegetables are tender.
  • Season with salt and ground black pepper to taste. Continue cooking for 3 minutes.
  • Transfer the picadillo to a serving plate and serve with warm rice. Share and enjoy!

    • રીત: Panlasang Pinoy. મૂળ રેસીપી જુઓ.
