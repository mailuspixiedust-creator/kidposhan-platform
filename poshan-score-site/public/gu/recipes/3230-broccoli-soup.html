સામગ્રી

  • 500 gms Broccoli
  • 1 tblsp Oil
  • 1 tblsp Cornflour
  • 2 cups Vegetable Stock
  • 1 tsp Salt
  • 1/2 tsp , freshly ground Black Pepper
  • 1/2 cup Cream
  • 4 tblsp, grated Cheddar Cheese

રીત

  • Cut the broccol into small florets.
  • Heat the oil in a pan over moderate heat and saute the broccoli lightly.
  • Mix the cornflour with a little stock in a bowl until well blended.
  • Pour in the remaining stock and mix well.
  • Add the blended cornflour to the broccoli and cook for about 10 minutes.
  • Add the salt and pepper. Remove from heat.
  • Puree the soup and return it to the pan.
  • Place the pan over moderate heat and heat through.
  • Transfer the soup to individual serving bowls and garnish with cream.
  • Sprinkle the grated cheese on top.
  • Serve hot.
