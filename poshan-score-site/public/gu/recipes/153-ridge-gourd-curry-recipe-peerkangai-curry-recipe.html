Ridge Gourd Curry Recipe (Peerkangai Curry Recipe)

Ridge Gourd Curry Recipe (Peerkangai Curry Recipe) ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 60 મળ્યા (ઠીક). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 1 medium Ridge Gourd (peeled and sliced lengthwise)
  • 1 medium Onion (peeled and sliced thinly)
  • 2 no Green Chillies (slit lengthwise)
  • 1 tsp Red Chilli Powder
  • ½ tsp Turmeric Powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 1 tbsp Coconut Oil
  • 1 tsp Mustard Seeds
  • 1 tsp Split White Urad dal
  • ½ tsp Cumin Seeds
  • 1 sprig Curry leaves
  • ½ cup Fresh Coconut (grated)

રીત

  • Wash, peel and chop ridge gourd into medium size pieces. You can reserve the ridge gourd peel for making chutney.
  • Heat oil in a pan, add in the tempering ingredients and let them fry in the oil for a minute. Let it crackle.
  • Add in onions, green chillies, salt and turmeric and saute this in the oil for couple of minutes until onions get translucent.
  • Now add in ridge gourd and toss well in the onions.
  • Cover and simmer on a low heat for 10 mins till it is cooked.
  • No need to add any water.
  • Ridge gourd has water content in them.
  • So they will cook in their own juices.
  • Now add in red chilli powder and coconut. Toss this into the cooked ridge gourd for 1 to 2 minutes.
  • Thats it ridge gourd curry is ready. Serve with rice or chapati.
