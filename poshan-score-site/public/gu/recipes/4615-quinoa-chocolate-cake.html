સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup cooked quinoa
  • 1/2 cup + 2 tablespoon cane sugar
  • 1/4 cup coco powder
  • 2 nos eggs
  • 1/3 cup milk
  • 1/4 cup oil
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
  • 1/4 teaspoon salt

રીત

  • Preheat at 175 deg C for 10 mins. To a mixer jar add 2 eggs, milk and oil.
  • Add vanilla essence to it. Blend well for 5 seconds.
  • Now add cooked quinoa, cane sugar.
  • Blend until smooth(it will still be slightly coarse). After grinding I tasted and felt the sugar less so added little and gave a quick blend.
  • Prepare the baking mold by laying butter paper. Now pour this mixture into the mould.Tap few times to level the top.
  • Bake in preheated oven at 175 deg C for 20-25 mins.
  • Insert a tooth pick in the center to check if it comes out clean then its cooked, if its still gooey then bake for few more mins and adjust accordingly.
  • Cool down the gently invert to demould. Remove the butter paper, cut and serve after completely cool.
  • Serve Quinoa Chocolate Cake with chocolate sauce!
