સામગ્રી

  • 3 cups All Purpose Flour (360 grams)
  • 1 ½ tsp Salt (7.5 grams)
  • 1 large Egg (reserve 1 tbsp for egg wash)
  • ¾ cup Warm Milk (180 ml)
  • 3 tbsp Melted Butter (45 ml)
  • ¼ cup Warm Water (or as needed) (60 ml)
  • 3 tbsp Sugar (45 grams)
  • 2 tsp Dry Yeast

રીત

  • Start by take warm water in a stand mixer. Sprinkle in sugar and dry yeast. Set aside for 5 mins till it looks foamy.
  • Now add in milk, well beaten egg (reserving one tbsp for egg wash), melted butter, flour, salt in the same and start kneading.
  • While the mixing process stop in-between and scrape the bowl of the stand mixer for even mixing.
  • Make sure you knead the dough for at least 10 mins. Add more flour if needed.
  • Once the kneading is done, remove the dough from the strand mixer bowl and place it in a well oiled bowl.
  • Cover and set aside for 1 to 2 hours for proofing.
  • It will get double in size.
  • Now take dough out of the bowl on a silicon mat.
  • Divide it into equal portion.
  • For larger hot dog rolls divide the dough into 8 pieces.
  • For small hot dog rolls divide them into 12 pieces.
  • Shape the divided dough into a smooth ball.
  • Roll each ball into a long rectangle like and start rolling into a log like.
  • Seal the edges and place seam side down on the baking tray.
  • Cover and let it proof for 30 mins.
  • During the last 10 mins of proofing, Preheat oven to 200 degree C (400 degree F).
  • Now once the rolls are proofed, brush the top with the reserved beaten egg mixed with little water or milk and Bake at 200 degree C for 15 mins.
  • Remove the buns from oven and let it cool for 10 mins.
  • Serve.
