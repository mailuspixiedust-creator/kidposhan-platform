Jain Pizza Sauce

80સારું

Jain Pizza Sauce ને 4–6 વર્ષ માટે એક સર્વિંગ મુજબ 100 માંથી 80 મળ્યા (સારું). એક જ વાનગીનો સ્કોર ઉંમર મુજબ અલગ હોય છે, કારણ કે નાના બાળકની ખાંડ, મીઠું અને સેચ્યુરેટેડ ફેટની દૈનિક મર્યાદા ઓછી હોય છે.

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

સામગ્રી

  • 2 tsp olive oil
  • 2 1/2 cups chopped tomato
  • 5 tbsp chopped basil
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp dry red chilli flakes (paprika)
  • 1 tsp jain tomato ketchup
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch)
  • 1/2 tsp red chilli powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • salt (namak) to taste

રીત

  • To make Jain pizza sauce, Heat 2 tsp olive oil in a deep non-stick pan.
  • Olive oil is a strong antioxidant and good for heart.
  • Also it has anti inflammation properties.
  • Add 2 1/2 cups chopped tomatoes. Tomatoes provide a tangy and slightly sweet base for the sauce, which is essential for a well-balanced pizza.
  • Mix well.
  • Cover with a lid and cook on a medium flame for 3 to 4 minutes, while stirring occasionally.
  • After cooking.
  • Add 5 tbsp chopped basil.
  • The aromatic qualities of basil enhance the overall appeal of the pizza sauce, making it more inviting.
  • Basil helps to balance the acidity of the tomatoes, creating a more harmonious flavor profile.
  • Mix well.
  • Cook on a medium flame for 1 minute.
  • Add 1 tsp dried oregano.
  • Oregano adds a distinct herbal, slightly bitter, and aromatic flavor to the sauce.
  • It complements the sweetness of the tomatoes and other spices used in the Jain pizza sauce.
  • Add 1 tsp dry red chilli flakes (paprika).
  • Jain cuisine excludes onion and garlic, so chilli flakes help to compensate for the missing depth of flavor.
  • Chilli flakes add a subtle heat and complexity to the sauce, elevating its overall taste.
  • Add 2 tsp jain tomato ketchup.
  • The most crucial aspect is that Jain tomato ketchup is completely free from onion and garlic, which are strictly prohibited in Jain cuisine.
  • It provides the essential tomato base for the pizza sauce, offering a tangy and sweet flavor profile.
  • Add 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch).
  • Add 1/2 tsp chilli powder.
  • Add 1 tsp sugar.
  • Tomatoes, a primary ingredient in pizza sauce, can be quite acidic.
  • A small amount of sugar can help to balance out this acidity and create a more pleasant taste.
  • Add salt (namak) to taste.
  • Mix well.
  • Cook on a medium flame for 2 to 3 minutes, while stirring occasionally.
  • Switch off the flame, let it cool completely.
  • Transfer to a mixer.
  • Blend till coarse.
  • Store Jain pizza sauce in an air- tight container in the freezer. Use as required. Jain pizza sauce will last for 3 to 4 months in the freezer.
  • Serve Jain pizza sauce.
