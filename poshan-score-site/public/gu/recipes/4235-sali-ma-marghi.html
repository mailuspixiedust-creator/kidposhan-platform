સામગ્રી

  • 1 kg Chicken (preferably leg and breast pieces)
  • 4 Onion medium sized (finely chopped)
  • 1 Onion paste (optional)
  • 1 Tbsp Ginger paste (half for marination and half during cooking)
  • 2 Tbsp Garlic Paste (half for marination and half during cooking)
  • 200 g Yogurt
  • 4 Green chilies (paste)
  • 2 Tomato (Pureed/chopped)
  • 1 Tsp Chilli powder
  • 1 Tsp Turmeric powder
  • 1 Tsp Cumin Powder
  • ½ Tsp Coriander Powder
  • ½ Tsp Garam Masala
  • 1 Tsp Jaggery
  • 2 Tbsp Vinegar
  • 4 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 2 Tsp Black Pepper Powder
  • 2 Bay Leave
  • 1 - inch Cinnamon Stick
  • 1 Black Cardamom
  • 3 green Cardamom
  • 1 Tsp Salt or to taste
  • 2 cups Water
  • 5 Potatoes
  • 1 Tbsp Salt
  • 100 ml Vegetable Oil for Deep Frying

રીત

How to cook Chicken Curry:

  1. First, wash the chicken well and then mix it with ginger paste, garlic paste, onion paste, green chili paste, yogurt, salt, and turmeric powder.
  2. Let it sit overnight, or for at least 2-3 hours.
  3. I like to let it sit overnight (8 hours).
  4. Next, heat oil in a heavy pan. Add bay leaves, cinnamon, and green and black cardamom to the hot oil.
  5. Add chopped onions and cook until they are golden or light brown.
  6. Now, add chopped or pureed tomatoes and cook for another 2-3 minutes over medium heat.
  7. Add ginger and garlic paste and cook until the oil separates from the mixture.
  8. Add cumin powder, coriander powder, turmeric powder, and jaggery. Cook for 5 more minutes.
  9. Add vinegar and jaggery and stir well.
  10. Add the chicken and cook for 10 minutes on low heat.
  11. Add hot water (about 2 cups), cover the pan, and cook for about 10 minutes.
  12. Add salt to taste.
  13. Cook until the sauce gets thick and turns red.
