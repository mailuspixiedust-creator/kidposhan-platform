Hot and Sour Soup

સામગ્રી

  • 1 tbsp corn flour
  • ¼ cup water
  • 2 tsp oil
  • 1 inch ginger (finely chopped)
  • 4 cloves garlic (finely chopped)
  • 2 green chilli (finely chopped)
  • ½ onion (finely chopped)
  • ½ cup mushroom (finely chopped)
  • 1 carrot (finely chopped)
  • ½ cup cabbage (finely chopped)
  • 5 beans (finely chopped)
  • 3 tbsp sweet corn
  • ½ capsicum (finely chopped)
  • 1 tsp salt (or as required)
  • 5 cups water or vegetable stock
  • 2 tbsp chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp vinegar
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • ½ tsp black pepper powder
  • 2 tbsp coriander (finely chopped)
  • 2 tbsp spring onion greens (finely chopped)

રીત

  • Firstly, wash all the vegetables thoroughly and finely chop them. Finely chopped vegetables cook quickly and give the soup a restaurant-style texture.
  • To prepare the corn flour slurry, in a small bowl, take corn flour and water.
  • Mix well until the corn flour dissolves completely and forms a smooth, lump-free slurry. Keep it aside.
  • Heat oil in a large kadai. Add finely chopped ginger, garlic, green chilli, and onion.
  • Sauté on a high flame until the onion softens slightly and the ginger and garlic turn aromatic. Do not brown the onion.
  • Add the finely chopped mushrooms and sauté well. Cook until the mushrooms release their moisture and shrink slightly. Adding mushrooms is optional.
  • Now add carrot, cabbage, beans, sweet corn, and capsicum.
  • Add salt and stir-fry the vegetables over high heat for about 2 minutes.
  • Do not overcook the vegetables. They should retain a light crunch for the best texture.
  • Pour in 5 cups of water and mix well. For a richer flavour and added nutrition, you can replace the water with vegetable stock.
  • Add chilli sauce, vinegar, soy sauce, and black pepper powder.
  • Mix everything well and taste the soup. Adjust the chilli sauce, vinegar, salt, and pepper to taste.
  • Mix the prepared corn flour slurry once again, as the corn flour may settle at the bottom.
  • Slowly pour the slurry into the soup while stirring continuously. Continue stirring to prevent lumps from forming.
  • Bring the soup to a boil and cook for about 2 minutes, or until it thickens slightly.
  • Do not make the soup too thick, as it will continue to thicken slightly after cooling.
  • Finally, add finely chopped coriander and spring onion greens. Mix well and turn off the flame.
  • Serve the Hot and Sour Soup immediately while it is hot.
