સામગ્રી

  • 2 pounds boneless, skinless chicken thighs, trimmed
  • 3 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 2 tablespoons minced canned chipotle chiles in adobo sauce
  • 2 tablespoons lime juice, divided
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 teaspoons table salt, divided
  • 1 ½ teaspoons ground cumin
  • 1 teaspoon dried oregano
  • 1 ½ cups long-grain white rice
  • 12 ounces (3 cups) frozen sliced peppers and onions
  • ⅓ cup chopped fresh cilantro

રીત

  • Adjust oven rack 6 inches from broiler element and heat broiler.
  • Line rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil.
  • Combine 2 pounds chicken thighs, trimmed; 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil; 2 tablespoons minced canned chipotle; 1 tablespoon lime juice; 3 minced garlic cloves; 1 teaspoon table salt; 1½ teaspoons cumin; and 1 teaspoon dried oregano in large bowl; set aside.
  • Rinse 1½ cups long-grain white rice in fine-mesh strainer until water runs clear.
  • Bring rice and 2¼ cups water to boil in large saucepan over medium-high heat.
  • Cook, uncovered, until water level drops below surface of rice and small holes form, about 5 minutes.
  • Reduce heat to low, cover, and cook until rice is tender and water is fully absorbed, about 15 minutes.
  • Keep warm until ready to serve.
  • Meanwhile, toss 3 cups frozen sliced peppers and onions, ½ teaspoon salt, and remaining 1 tablespoon oil together on prepared sheet and spread to even layer.
  • Arrange chicken over vegetables.
  • Broil until chicken registers 175 degrees, 16 to 20 minutes.
  • Transfer chicken to cutting board and let rest for 5 minutes.
  • Fluff rice with fork, then stir in ⅓ cup chopped cilantro, remaining 1 tablespoon lime juice, and remaining ½ teaspoon salt.
  • Chop chicken into bite-size pieces, then stir into vegetables on sheet.
  • Top individual portions of rice with chicken mixture and serve.
