સામગ્રી

  • 1 cup or (190 grams) basmati rice
  • 3 tablespoons ghee (clarified butter)
  • 15 to 18 cashews
  • 1 tablespoon raisins (without seeds)
  • ⅓ cup or (50 grams or 1 medium-sized) onion (- thinly sliced)
  • 1.75 to 2 cups water (for stove-top pan and 1.25 cups for instant pot)
  • ⅓ teaspoon pink salt (or add as required)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons coriander leaves (- chopped, cilantro, for garnish)
  • 1.5 inches cinnamon
  • 3 green cardamoms
  • 3 cloves
  • 2 single strands of mace
  • 4 to 5 black peppercorns
  • 1 tej patta (Indian bay leaf)

રીત

Making ghee rice (stove-top)

  1. Cover the pan with a tight fitting lid.
  2. Simmer until the rice is tender and all the water is absorbed.
  3. Time take will be about 8 to 9 minutes or more depending on the quality, age of rice and the kind of pan.
  4. You can check once or twice while the rice is cooking.
  5. If the grains are under cooked and the water has dried up, add some tablespoons of water all over.
  6. Gently stir with a fork.
  7. Cover and continue to cook.
  8. Once the rice grains are fluffy and tender, rest for 5 to 6 minutes. Later remove the lid. Fluff the rice gently with a fork.
  9. Serve ghee rice hot or warm garnished with fried cashews & raisins and some chopped coriander leaves.
  10. It tastes so good, that you can even have it as it is without any accompanying curry or dal or kurma.
