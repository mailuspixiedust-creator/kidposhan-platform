19 real products, scored on 10 metrics.

Each product is scored out of 20 — ten metrics, two points each, equal weight. Six move with the age band you pick (sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fiber, protein); three don't (preservatives, additives, base-ingredient integrity); the tenth is a composite HFSS flag modelled on FSSAI's own pending proposal. Any risk metric that fails outright caps the tier at Caution — fiber and protein are credit metrics, so scoring low there costs points but can't trigger the cap on its own. Full citations for every threshold are on the Age & Metrics page.

pass (2 pts) partial (1 pt) fail (0 pts; caps tier at Caution for the 8 risk metrics) Order: Sugar · Sodium · Sat Fat · Trans Fat · Fiber · Protein · Preservatives · Additives · Base Integrity · HFSS

Where the numbers come from: all ten thresholds and their sources are detailed on the Age & Metrics page. Sugar, sodium, saturated fat, fiber and protein are per-serving figures read off each label; preservatives, additives, trans fat and base-ingredient integrity are read from the ingredient list. Products marked est. have one or more figures estimated from category norms or a brand's public statement rather than read directly off that exact photographed pack.

Sources used in this scoring: ICMR-NIN 2020 (protein RDA, energy reference) · WHO free-sugar guideline, 2015 · WHO saturated-fat guidance (<10% of energy) · NASEM/IOM Dietary Reference Intakes (sodium and fiber, used as proxy where ICMR has no pediatric table) · FSSAI trans-fat regulation (≤2% of total fat) · FSSAI Food Products Standards and Food Additives Regulations, 2011 (preservatives, additives) · FSSAI compliance affidavit to the Supreme Court of India, W.P.(C) No. 437/2024, 29 Aug 2026 (proposed HFSS front-of-pack warning). Full citations with numbering on Age & Metrics.
