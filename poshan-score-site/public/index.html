How we rate

Ten things we check, every time.

The same rubric for every product on the shelf. Ten metrics, each marked 1 to 10, for your child's age and one realistic serving. Six are numbers off the label, measured against the daily reference for that age. Three are checks on the ingredient list. The tenth is the worst of the sugar, sodium and saturated-fat marks. Full citations and thresholds on the Age & Metrics page.

Added Sugar

Checked against a WHO-derived, age-wise free-sugar limit, not just "no added sugar" claims on the front.

Sodium

Measured against age-appropriate daily limits — not adult RDAs relabelled for kids.

Saturated Fat

Scored separately from trans fat, against WHO's under-10%-of-energy guidance.

Trans Fat

Hydrogenated oils flagged per FSSAI's ≤2%-of-total-fat regulation — the same ceiling for every age.

Fiber

A credit metric — rewards a serving for actually contributing to a child's daily fiber need.

Protein

Scored against real ICMR-NIN 2020 age-wise protein RDAs, not a generic adult number.

Preservatives

Class II preservatives called out by name — the ones parents recognise least, checked most.

Artificial Additives

Colours, flavour enhancers and emulsifiers flagged by INS number, not hidden behind "natural flavours."

Base-Ingredient Integrity

Whether the first ingredient on the pack is actually the main ingredient in the bowl.

HFSS Status

A composite flag modelled on FSSAI's own pending Supreme Court proposal for a front-of-pack warning.
