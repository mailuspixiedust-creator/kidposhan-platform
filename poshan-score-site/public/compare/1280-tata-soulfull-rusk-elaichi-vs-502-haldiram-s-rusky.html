Common questions

Which is healthier for kids, Tata Soulfull Rusk Elaichi or Haldiram's Rusky?

Tata Soulfull Rusk Elaichi scores higher for a child aged 4 to 6: 70 against 60 out of 100. It passes added sugar, which Haldiram's Rusky does not. The score is for one serving as the label gives it; the limits for sugar, salt and saturated fat are smaller for younger children.

How is the Poshan Score worked out?

Ten yes-or-no checks on one serving: added sugar, sodium, saturated fat, trans fat, fibre, protein, preservatives, artificial additives, the first ingredient, and whether it is high in fat, sugar or salt. Each passed check is worth 10 points. The limits come from ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO and FSSAI and change with the age.
