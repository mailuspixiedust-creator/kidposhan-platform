French Onion and Bacon Tart

सामग्री

  • 1 1/4 cups unbleached all-purpose flour (6 1/4 ounces/177 grams)
  • 1 tablespoon sugar
  • 1/2 teaspoon table salt
  • 8 tablespoons unsalted butter (1 stick), cut into 1/2-inch cubes and well chilled
  • 2 - 3 tablespoons ice water
  • 4 ounces (113 grams) bacon (about 4 slices), halved lengthwise, then cut crosswise into 1/4-inch pieces
  • Vegetable oil, if needed
  • 1 1/2 pounds onions, halved through root end, peeled, and cut crosswise into 1/4-inch slices (about 6 cups), see photo below
  • 1 sprig fresh thyme
  • 3/4 teaspoon table salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1/2 cup half-and-half
  • 1/4 teaspoon ground black pepper

विधि

FOR THE CRUST:

  1. Spray 9-inch tart pan with nonstick cooking spray.
  2. Combine flour, sugar, and salt in food processor with four 1-second pulses.
  3. Scatter butter pieces over flour mixture; pulse to cut butter into flour until mixture resembles coarse sand, about fifteen 1-second pulses.
  4. Add 2 tablespoons ice water and process until large clumps form and no powdery bits remain, about 5 seconds, adding up to 1 tablespoon more water if dough will not form clumps.
  5. Transfer dough to greased tart pan.
  6. Following illustrations above, pat dough into prepared pan.
  7. Lay plastic wrap over dough and smooth out any bumps or shallow areas.
  8. Place tart shell on plate and transfer to freezer for 30 minutes.
  9. Adjust oven rack to middle position and heat oven to 375 degrees.
  10. Place frozen tart shell on baking sheet; lightly spray one side of 18-inch square extra-wide heavy-duty aluminum foil with nonstick cooking spray.
  11. Press foil greased side down inside tart shell, folding excess foil over edge of pan.
  12. Fill with pie weights and bake until top edge just starts to color and surface of dough under foil no longer looks wet, about 30 minutes.
  13. Remove from oven and carefully remove foil and weights by gathering edges of foil and pulling up and out.
  14. Return baking sheet with tart shell to oven and bake until golden brown, 5 to 10 minutes.
  15. Set baking sheet with tart shell on wire rack.
  16. Do not turn off oven.
