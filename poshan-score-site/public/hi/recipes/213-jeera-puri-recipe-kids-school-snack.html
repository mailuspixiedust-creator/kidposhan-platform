अंग्रेज़ी मूल देखें

  • 1/4 cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)
  • 1/4 cup maida (plain flour)
  • 1 tbsp rava (suji)
  • 1/2 tbsp oil
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • oil for deep-frying

    1. If you like jeera puri, then also try other jar snack recipes like palak pudina sev verki puri butter murukku
    2. Then add maida. Also called as refined flour, this has to be free of lumps. Sieve it if necessary.
    3. To this add semolina. This is called sooji or rava and it adds the necessary crunch to the puris.
    4. Next add coarsely crushed cumin seeds (jeera).
    5. You can coarsely crush it the whole jeera in a mortar-pestle (khalbhatta).
    6. You can substitute jeera with coarsely crushed black pepper also if you wish to.
    7. Add little oil.
    8. Add salt as per taste.
    9. Mix all the ingredients well.
    10. Add enough water gradually.
    11. Knead into a semi-stiff dough using enough water. If the dough is too soft, then rolling puris will be difficult.
    12. Divide the dough into 30 equal portions.
    13. Take one portion of the dough and roll it in between your palms to smoothen it.
    14. Place it on the rolling board.
    15. Roll it into a 50 mm. (2&rdquo;) diameter circle without using flour for rolling.
    16. Prick it using a fork at regular intervals. This is to avoid the puri from puffing while deep frying.
    17. Repeat step 11 to 14 to roll 29 more jeera poori Indian snack.
    18. Heat the oil in a deep non-stick pan and add a few puris at a time.
    19. Deep fry them on a medium flame till they are crisp and turn golden brown in colour from all the sides.
    20. If the flame is high, the puri will burn quickly since they are thinly rolled.
    21. So ensure you fry on slow to medium flame.
    22. Drain on an absorbent paper.
    23. Cool crispy fried Gujarati puri and serve of store in an air-tight container. They stay fresh for a week.
