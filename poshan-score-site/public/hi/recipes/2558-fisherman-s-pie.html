Fisherman's Pie

कितनी बार: कभी-कभी · सैचुरेटेड फैट ज़्यादा हफ़्ते में लगभग एक बार। Poshan Score बताता है कि एक सर्विंग बच्चे की दैनिक सीमाओं के हिसाब से कैसी है; यह पंक्ति बताती है कि खाना कैसे बना है।

70ठीक

Fisherman's Pie को 4–6 साल के लिए एक सर्विंग के हिसाब से 100 में से 70 मिले (ठीक)। एक ही व्यंजन अलग उम्र के लिए अलग स्कोर पाता है, क्योंकि छोटे बच्चे की चीनी, नमक और सैचुरेटेड फैट की दैनिक सीमा कम होती है।

सामग्री

  • 2 pounds russet potatoes, peeled and cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 1 tablespoon table salt for cooking potatoes
  • 3 tablespoons unsalted butter, cut into 3 pieces
  • ⅓ cup heavy cream
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 12 ounces jumbo shrimp (16 to 20 per pound), peeled, deveined, tails removed, and cut in half crosswise
  • ¾ teaspoon table salt, divided
  • ⅛ teaspoon baking soda
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter, divided
  • 1 leek, white and light-green parts only, halved lengthwise, sliced thin, and washed thoroughly
  • 1 teaspoon minced fresh thyme
  • ⅓ cup dry white wine or dry vermouth
  • 3 tablespoons all-purpose flour
  • 2 (8-ounce) bottles clam juice
  • ⅔ cup heavy cream
  • ¼ teaspoon pepper
  • 1 pound skinless cod fillets, cut into 1-inch chunks
  • 4 ounces cold-smoked salmon, cut into ½-inch pieces
  • 1/4 cup minced fresh parsley

विधि

FOR THE TOPPING:

  1. Place potatoes in large saucepan and add water to just cover.
  2. Add salt and bring to boil over high heat.
  3. Reduce heat to maintain simmer and cook until tip of paring knife inserted into potato meets no resistance, 8 to 10 minutes.
  4. Drain potatoes and return to saucepan over low heat.
  5. Cook, shaking saucepan occasionally, until any surface moisture on potatoes has evaporated, about 1 minute.
  6. Off heat, mash potatoes well.
  7. Stir in butter until melted.
  8. Whisk cream and egg yolk together in bowl; stir into potatoes.
  9. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  10. Cover to keep warm and set aside.

FOR THE FILLING:

  1. Set 8-inch square broiler-safe baking dish on rimmed baking sheet.
  2. Sprinkle shrimp with ¼ teaspoon salt and baking soda in bowl and toss to combine.
  3. Refrigerate until needed.
  4. Melt 3 tablespoons butter in medium saucepan over medium-low heat.
  5. Add leek and thyme and cook, stirring occasionally, until leek is softened, 6 to 7 minutes.
  6. Add wine and cook, stirring occasionally, until wine has evaporated, about 5 minutes.
  7. Add flour and cook, stirring constantly, for 1 minute.
  8. Add clam juice and stir until mixture is smooth.
  9. Stir in cream, pepper, and remaining ½ teaspoon salt.
  10. Increase heat to medium-high and bring to simmer.
  11. Lower heat to maintain simmer and cook, stirring frequently, until mixture resembles thick chowder, 10 to 13 minutes.
  12. Stir cod, salmon, and shrimp into sauce and return to simmer.
  13. Cover and cook, stirring every 2 minutes and adjusting heat if needed to maintain simmer, until shrimp are opaque and just cooked through, 4 to 6 minutes.
  14. Off heat, stir in parsley.
  15. Transfer filling to prepared dish.
  16. Adjust oven rack 8 inches from broiler element and heat broiler.
  17. Spoon topping over filling, starting at edges and working toward center.
  18. Smooth with rubber spatula, making sure to seal around edges of dish so no seafood or sauce is exposed.
  19. Using back of spoon or tines of fork, make pattern on topping.
  20. Melt remaining 1 tablespoon butter and drizzle over topping.
  21. Broil pie, still on sheet, until topping is golden brown and crusty and filling is bubbly, 6 to 7 minutes (watch closely).
  22. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.

आम सवाल

क्या Fisherman's Pie बच्चों के लिए सेहतमंद है?

Fisherman's Pie को 4–6 साल के लिए 100 में से 70 मिले (ठीक)। यह 10 में से 7 जाँच पास करती है और सोडियम, सैचुरेटेड फैट, ज़्यादा वसा, चीनी या नमक पर अंक खोती है।

Fisherman's Pie किस उम्र के लिए सबसे अच्छी है?

सबसे ऊँचा स्कोर, 70, 1–3 साल, 4–6 साल, 7–9 साल के लिए है, और सबसे कम, 60, 13–15 साल के लिए। बच्चे के बढ़ने के साथ एक ही व्यंजन का स्कोर बदलता है, क्योंकि चीनी, नमक और सैचुरेटेड फैट की दैनिक सीमाएँ बढ़ती हैं।
