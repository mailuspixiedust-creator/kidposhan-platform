Vegetarian Lasagna Soup

सामग्री

  • 10 strips lasagna noodles (1/2 pound, lb, broken into pieces)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 cup yellow onion (diced/chopped)
  • 1 tbsp garlic (minced)
  • 1 15oz can diced tomatoes
  • 1 15oz can tomato sauce (or marinara sauce)
  • 4 cups vegetable broth (or water)
  • 1 cup carrot (chopped)
  • 1 cup zucchini (chopped)
  • ½ cup red bell pepper (chopped)
  • ¾ cup broccoli florets
  • ½ cup spinach leaves (chopped)
  • 1 tsp Italian seasoning
  • ½ tsp dry oregano
  • ½ tsp red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp Salt (adjust to taste)
  • Freshly cracked black pepper (adjust to taste)
  • ½ cup Ricotta cheese
  • ½ cup mozzarella cheese
  • ¼ cup parmesan cheese
  • Three Cheese mixture (as per above)
  • Maldron Sea Salt (optional)
  • Freshly cracked black pepper to taste (adjust to taste)
  • Red pepper flakes (adjust to taste)
  • few Basil leaves

विधि

Stovetop Instructions:

  1. In a large pot heat the olive oil over medium-high heat.
  2. Once the oil is hot, add the onions, carrots, bell pepper, zucchini and garlic.
  3. Mix and cook until the onions turn translucent and veggies are half cooked.
  4. Add in the rest of the ingredients like diced tomatoes, tomato sauce, spices (salt, oregano, Italian seasoning, freshly cracked black pepper, red pepper flakes) with 4-5 cups water or broth.
  5. Mix well.
  6. Bring it to a boil.
  7. Stir in broken lasagna noodles and simmer until noodles are aldente.
  8. When the noodles are almosrt done add in the broccol and spinach, mix well.
  9. Simmer for 5 mins and then switch off the heat.
  10. Make the cheese topping. Add and mix all the cheeses Ricotta, mozzarella, and parmesan.
  11. Easy vegetable Lasagna soup is ready! Serve immediately!!
