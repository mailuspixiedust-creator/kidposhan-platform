Quinoa Fried Rice with Tofu

सामग्री

  • 1 cup Quinoa
  • 1 3/4 cups Water
  • 2 tbsp Oil
  • 2 tbsp Spring onions
  • 1 tbsp Garlic cloves (chopped)
  • 1 Green chili (chopped)
  • 1/3 cup Green beans (chopped)
  • 1/3 cup Carrot (chopped)
  • 1/3 cup Capsicum (bell pepper chopped)
  • 7 oz Extra-firm tofu (chopped)
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce (tamari for gluten free version.)
  • 1 tsp Vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp Pepper powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp Salt
  • 1/4 cup Spring onions (green onions)

विधि

Cook the quinoa

  1. Rinse the quinoa thoroughly under running water.
  2. Add the quinoa, water, and a pinch of salt (if needed), then bring to a boil.
  3. Cover the pan and simmer for 15-20 minutes, or until the water is completely absorbed and the quinoa is cooked.
  4. Avoid overcooking quinoa.
  5. Grains should be fluffy.
  6. Gently fluff the quinoa and let it cool.
  7. Meal prep and Tip.
  8. Cook the quinoa and let it cool completely.
  9. Ideally, refrigerate it for a couple of hours, overnight, or even 2-3 days.
  10. Warm, freshly cooked quinoa will turn mushy when fried, while cold quinoa fries up light and separate, just like restaurant-style fried rice.
