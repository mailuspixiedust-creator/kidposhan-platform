Oven Risotto with Roasted Mushrooms & Tomatoes

सामग्री

  • 1 cup cherry tomatoes (cut in half)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1/2 tsp salt (plus more to taste)
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 lb mushrooms (cut or torn into 1-2" pieces - use any mushrooms like shiitake, oyster, king trumpet or whatever you can find)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 4 garlic cloves (minced)
  • 6 thyme sprigs
  • 1 white onion (medium in size - diced)
  • 1 cup arborio rice
  • 1/2 cup white wine (dry vermouth will also work)
  • 1/4 tsp black pepper (cracked)
  • 3 cups vegetable broth
  • 1 cup parmesan (grated)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1/4 cup fresh dill (chopped)
  • lemon wedges (optional)

विधि

Tomatoes

  1. Make the tomatoes first.
  2. Heat the oven to 400F and line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  3. Toss all tomato ingredients together in a bowl.
  4. Place the tomatoes, cut side up, on the prepared baking sheet.
  5. Roast tomatoes for 30-35 minutes or until the edges start to take on a little color.
  6. Add tomatoes to a plate and set aside until ready to plate.
  7. Reserve baking sheet for mushrooms.
