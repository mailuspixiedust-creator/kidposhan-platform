Sesame Chikki Recipe (Til Chikki

रेसिपी: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

फ़ोटो: Aarthi / Yummy Tummy Aarthi

प्रति सर्विंग

प्रोटीन2 g
फाइबर1 g
सैचुरेटेड फैट1 g
एडेड शुगर12.7 g
सोडियम1 mg

रेसिपी पेज से, एक सर्विंग के लिए (रेसिपी 15 के लिए है)।

सामग्री

  • 1 cup Sesame Seeds
  • 1 cup Jaggery
  • ¼ cup Water
  • 1 tsp Ghee

विधि

  • Take sesame seeds in a dry nonstick pan and toast till it gets nutty and pops. Remove to a plate.
  • Now in the same pan, add jaggery and water and mix well till it is completely melted.
  • Now cook till it gets thick and starts to bubble up nicely. It will take around 3 to 5 mins. Add in ghee and mix well.
  • Now add in sesame seeds and mix well. Cook till it leaves the sides of the pan and forms a mass like.
  • Pour it in a greased parchment paper and Let it sit for just 30 seconds for the mixture to set.
  • Now place another greased parchment paper on top.
  • Roll using a rolling pin till it is evenly spread and little thin.
  • Now use a sharp knife to cut into bars. Cool completely and store in an air tight container for upto a week.
