Moong Dal Dhokla

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

    1. What are Dhoklas?
    2. Dhokla is a soft and fluffy steamed snack from the Gujarati repertoire.
    3. This all-time favourite is enjoyed as a starter, as a tea-time snack, or even for breakfast.
    4. Basically, something you can have any time you are hungry!
    5. There are innumerable dhokla recipes falling under different types, so you can make this snack multiple times a week and still not get bored.
    6. Some dhokla batters like the healthy Moong Dal Dhokla require grinding and fermentation, while others like the Rava Dhokla are quick-fix options that you can simply mix and steam.
    7. If you have just a few minutes, not to worry, you can put together yummy Khaman Dhokla in the microwave - Microwave Mug Dhokla!
    8. You can even make Khatta Dhokla with handy idli batter.
    9. So, depending on the availability of time and ingredients, you can pick a dhokla recipe.
    10. The best part is that you can always reinforce your dhokla batter with delicious and tasty veggies and greens, like grated carrots, crushed green peas or fenugreek leaves.
    11. This makes the dhokla colourful, tasty and healthy too.
    12. Once you know the different types of dhokla batters, you can go it on your own, by varying the added veggies, tempering, and so on, according to preferences and availability.
    13. For making the healthy Moong Dal Dhokla batter, clean and wash the 3/4 cup yellow moong dal (split yellow gram).
    14. Refer our glossary to read about nutritional benefits of moong dal and other ways to use.
    15. Add enough water and soak for 30 mins.
    16. Drain the soaked dal using a strainer.
    17. Transfer the washed yellow moong dal (split yellow gram) to a big mixer jar. You can also make use of green moong dal and make Green Moong Dal Dhokla.
    18. Add the 1 tsp roughly chopped green chillies. You can even add ginger for an added flavor
    19. Add a approx 1/2 cup water that would help to make a paste of pouring consistency.
    20. Blend the moong dal to a smooth paste
    21. Transfer the paste to a deep bowl Add salt to taste.
    22. Add 1 1/2 tsp sugar.
    23. Sugar is totally optional but, we want the moong dal dhokla to be like the Nylon khaman dhokla which are sweet hence, we are adding the sugar.
    24. Add the a pinch of asafoetida (hing). Dals and pulses are a bit difficult to digest, hence a little addition of hing will help in aiding digestion.
    25. Add the 1 tbsp oil, 1/2 tsp turmeric powder (haldi). Oil helps in making soft moong dhokla.
    26. Also, add the 1 tbsp besan (Bengal gram flour). Besan is another healthy flour which is loaded with good fats and proteins.
    27. Add the 2 tbsp curd (dahi). We have used fresh homemade curd which will make the dhoklas fluffy and spongy.
    28. For steaming the no-fermentation moong dal dhokla, grease the dhokla plate with little oil and even put water for steaming in a steamer.
    29. Just before steaming, sprinkle 1 1/2 tsp fruit salt. After adding the fruit salt, do not let the batter sit idle or else you will get hard Moong Dal Dhokla.
    30. Pour 1 tsp water over the fruit salt to activate it. Do not let the Moong Dal Dhokla batter sit for too long after adding the fruit salt.
    31. Do not mix the batter vigourously to maintain the aeration.
    32. For the tempering of Moong Dal Dhokla, heat the 1 tbsp oil in a small non-stick pan.
    33. Add the 1/2 tsp mustard seeds ( rai / sarson).
    34. When the mustard seeds crackle, add the 1/2 tsp sesame seeds (til).
    35. Add the a pinch asafoetida (hing) and sauté on a medium flame for 30 seconds.
    36. Add the 2 tsp finely chopped green chillies.
    37. Sauté on a medium flame for 30 seconds.
    38. Pour the tempering over the prepared Moong Dal Dhokla and spread it evenly. Sprinkle the 1 tbsp finely chopped coriander (dhania) evenly on top.
    39. Also, sprinkle some freshly 1 tbsp grated coconut on the Moong Dal Dhokla.
    40. Cut Moong Dal Dhokla into even pieces.
    41. Mix well to make a dhokla consistency batter. The best part about the moong dal dhokla is the batter does not require any soaking or fermenting.
    42. Pour the batter into a greased 175 mm. (7”) diameter thali.
    43. Steam in a steamer for 10 to 12 minutes or till the moong dal dhokla
    44. Are cooked. Keep aside.
    45. Serve the quick and easy Moong Dal Dhokla hot with green chutney.

    आम सवाल

    क्या Moong Dal Dhokla बच्चों के लिए सेहतमंद है?

    Moong Dal Dhokla को 4–6 साल के लिए 100 में से 90 मिले (अच्छा)। यह 10 में से 9 जाँच पास करती है और फाइबर पर अंक खोती है।

    Moong Dal Dhokla किस उम्र के लिए सबसे अच्छी है?

    सबसे ऊँचा स्कोर, 100, 1–3 साल के लिए है, और सबसे कम, 80, 16–18 साल के लिए। बच्चे के बढ़ने के साथ एक ही व्यंजन का स्कोर बदलता है, क्योंकि चीनी, नमक और सैचुरेटेड फैट की दैनिक सीमाएँ बढ़ती हैं।

    Poshan Score कैसे निकाला जाता है?

    एक सर्विंग पर दस हाँ-या-नहीं जाँच: एडेड शुगर, सोडियम, सैचुरेटेड फैट, ट्रांस फैट, फाइबर, प्रोटीन, प्रिज़र्वेटिव, कृत्रिम एडिटिव, पहली सामग्री, और क्या यह ज़्यादा वसा, चीनी या नमक वाला है। हर पास हुई जाँच के 10 अंक हैं। सीमाएँ ICMR-NIN 2020, WHO और FSSAI से ली गई हैं और उम्र के साथ बदलती हैं।
