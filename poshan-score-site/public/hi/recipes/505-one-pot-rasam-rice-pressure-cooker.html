One Pot Rasam Rice (Pressure Cooker)

सामग्री

  • 1/2 cup toor dal
  • 3/4 cup basmati rice
  • 1 tablespoon oil
  • 1 tablespoon ghee
  • 1 teaspoon mustard seeds
  • 1/2 teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 stalk curry leaves
  • 1 pinch hing(asafoetida)
  • 1/2 teaspoon crushed pepper
  • 2-3 shallots (or 1/4 cup onion)
  • 2-3 garlic cloves (crushed)
  • 1 green chili (optional)
  • 1 medium tomato (finely chopped)
  • 1-1.5 tablespoons rasam powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon turmeric powder
  • 1/2 teaspoon red chili powder
  • 1 teaspoon coriander powder
  • salt (to taste)
  • 2-3 tablespoons tamarind extract
  • 4 cups water
  • 2 tablespoons coriander leaves

विधि

  • Soak the rinsed rice and toor dal together in water for at least 15-20 minutes.
  • In a pressure cooker or instant pot, heat oil and ghee. Then add mustard seeds, cumin seeds, curry leaves, hing, and black peppercorns. Let them sizzle.
  • Next, add chopped shallots (or red onions), garlic, and green chili. Sauté until the onions turn soft and translucent.
  • Next, add the chopped tomatoes along with rasam powder, turmeric, red chili powder, and coriander powder.
  • Sauté for a few minutes until the tomatoes soften and the spices are fragrant.
  • Then, add the tamarind water (or tamarind paste), soaked dal, and rice. Pour in 3 cups of water and mix well.
  • Pressure Cook for 4 whistles on a medium-high flame, and turn off the flame. Allow the pressure to release naturally.
  • Add about 1 cup of boiling water, or as needed, to bring the rasam rice to a flowy, pourable consistency.
  • Lastly, garnish with coriander leaves.
  • One-pot rasam sadham is ready to serve.

    • Instant Pot Instructions

    1. Follow the same steps as the stovetop pot pressure cooker, PRESSURE COOK/MANUAL (High Pressure) setting, and set the cooking time to 8 minutes, followed by natural pressure release.
