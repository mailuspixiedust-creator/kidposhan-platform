सामग्री

  • 1 cup sabudana (sago or tapioca pearls)
  • 4 potatoes (- medium-sized)
  • ½ cup peanuts
  • 1 teaspoon cumin seeds (- optional)
  • 1 to 2 green chillies (- finely chopped or ½ to 1 teaspoon)
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (- finely chopped)
  • 2 teaspoons lemon juice (optional)
  • 2 tablespoons chopped coriander leaves (- optional)
  • 1.5 teaspoon sugar (or as required)
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons buckwheat flour (or amaranth flour - optional)
  • rock salt (edible and food grade, or sendha namak)
  • oil (as required, for deep frying)

विधि

Soaking Sabudana

  1. You need to plan ahead and soak the sago pearls.
  2. It is very important to soak the sago really well.Important: Please refrain from using un-soaked or under-soaked sabudana to avoid scalding or burns as they will burst and shatter in oil when frying.
  3. First optionally, using a colander or mesh strainer rinse 1 cup sabudana lightly twice draining all the water.
  4. Soak the sabudana or sago in water for 5 hours at least or overnight. I soaked the sago pearls overnight and by morning they had become soft.
  5. When properly soaked, the sabudana pearls will be tender without any hard center.
  6. When you press the pearls between your forefinger and thumb, they should get mashed easily with a floury moist texture on your fingers.
  7. If you feel some hardness in the center, then soak for some more time. Drain all the water really well from the soaked sabudana.
  8. There should not be any water logged in the soaked pearls.
  9. Water will make the patties moist and sticky.
  10. The patties will soak up a good amount of oil while frying making them loaded with oil.
  11. So drain the water really well.
  12. You can opt to gently and lightly press the soaked pearls in the strainer with a spoon or spatula.
  13. Or set aside the pearls in a strainer (keeping a bowl beneath) for some 20 to 30 minutes dripping of any extra water.
  14. After draining the water thoroughly transfer the sabudana in a mixing bowl and set aside.
