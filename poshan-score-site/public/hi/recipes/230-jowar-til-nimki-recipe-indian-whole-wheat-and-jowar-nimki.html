सामग्री

  • 1/2 cup jowar atta (jowar flour)
  • 1/2 cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta)
  • 1 tbsp sesame seeds (til)
  • 1/2 tsp freshly ground black pepper (kalimirch)
  • 1 tsp green chilli paste
  • 2 tsp oil
  • salt (namak) to taste
  • whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta) for rolling

  • In a deep bowl put1/2 cupjowar (white millet) flour.
  • Add1/2 cupwhole wheat flour (gehun ka atta).
  • Add1 tbspsesame seeds (til).
  • Add1/2 tspfreshly ground black pepper (kalimirch).
  • Add1 tspgreenchilli paste.
  • Add 1 tsp oil.
  • Add salt to taste. We added 1/2tsp salt.
  • Use enough water to make a semi stiff dough. We added 1/4th cup plus 4 tablespoons more of water.
  • Knead into semi-stiff dough.
  • Divide the dough into 6 equal portions
  • Lightly dust the rolling board using little whole wheat flour.
  • Place a portion of the dough on it and flatten it.
  • Roll out each portion into a thin round circle of 150 mm. (6&rdquo;) in diameter using a little whole wheat flour for rolling.
  • Cut it into 8equal sized strips and keep aside. Note that the sides of the doughcircle will not give you equal sized strips.
  • Heat a non-stick tava (griddle) and grease it with 1 tsp of oil.
  • Place the 8 uncooked strips on the tava.
  • Cook the 8 stripson the hot tava (griddle) on low heat for one minute.
  • Grease the top with oil.
  • Flip over with a pair of tongs.
  • Cook the strips while pressing them using folded muslin cloth.
  • Keep cooking and flipping till they turn crisp and golden brown in colour from both the sides.
  • Cool and serve.
