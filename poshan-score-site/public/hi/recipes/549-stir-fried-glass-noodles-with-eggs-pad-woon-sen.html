Stir-Fried Glass Noodles with Eggs ผัดวุ้นเส้น (Pad Woon Sen)

सामग्री

  • 5 garlic clove(s)
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 2 egg(s)
  • 40 g glass noodles
  • 50 g white cabbage
  • 1 tomato(es)
  • 0.5 onion
  • 2 tbsp fish sauce
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 0.25 tsp dark soy sauce
  • 2 Chinese celery
  • 2 Spring onions

विधि

  • Heat up oil in a wok on medium-high heat. Then toss in chopped garlic and stir-fry till they turn golden brown.
  • Crack in the eggs and gently stir them so that they are not completely combined. You can still see the yellow and the whites.
  • Add in the softened glass noodles and stir-fry. If the noodles are not soft enough, you may add in some water or stock to help soften the noodles.
  • Once the noodles has softened, add in the veggies.
  • Starting with the harder textured ones, the cabbage and onions, followed by the softer ones, tomatoes.
  • Stir till they're slightly soft.
  • Stir in the seasoning. Mix till evenly combined.
  • Right before turning off the heat, add in the spring onions and celery. Stir-fry for 10 secs and transfer to a serving plate. Enjoy!
