  • 2 tbsp grated cabbage
  • 2 tbsp finely chopped onion
  • 1 tbsp grated coconut
  • 1 tbsp chopped coriander (dhania)
  • 1/2 tsp chaat masala
  • 2 1/4 tsp oil for greasing and cooking
