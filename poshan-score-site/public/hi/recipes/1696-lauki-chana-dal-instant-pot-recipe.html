Lauki Chana Dal (Instant Pot Recipe)

सामग्री

  • 450 grams Lauki (bottle gourd) (1 & ½ cups diced thick)
  • ¾ cup Chana Dal or Bengal Gram Dal
  • 3 Onions (small, finely chopped or use 1 large)
  • 2 Tomatoes (small, finely chopped)
  • 1 ½ tablespoon Peanut oil (or use avocado oil)
  • ½ teaspoon Cumin Seeds or Jeera
  • 2 teaspoon ginger garlic paste
  • 1 teaspoon Turmeric
  • 1 teaspoon Kashmiri Red chili powder
  • ½ teaspoon Garam Masala
  • ½ teaspoon Aamchur Powder (Dry Mango Powder) (substitute with juice of ½ a lime - check notes)
  • 1 ½ cups Water
  • Salt to taste

विधि

  • Prep Work – Wash and Soak Chana Dal (overnight or for 2-3 hours in hot water). Drain the water and rinse once before proceeding with cooking.
  • Wash and peel lauki. Cut the top /stem and ends, dice them into thick chunks. If the seeds are big, scoop out using a spoon or knife.
  • Switch on Sauté Mode, Once it displays “HOT” or “ON” add oil, cumin seeds.
  • Once cumin toasts, add finely chopped onions and ginger garlic paste.
  • Sauté for 1-2 mins until the onions are translucent.
  • Next add chopped tomatoes and sauté for a couple of seconds.
  • Now add the diced lauki, soaked and drained chana dal, spices and salt to taste. Cancel Sauté Mode.
  • Add Water, mix once, scrape the sides and close the Instant Pot lid.
  • Pressure cook on high for 8 mins.
  • Do a natural pressure release(approximately 15 mins).
  • Press Cancel.
  • Open the lid, add the finely chopped coriander leaves and serve.
