White Chicken Chili

70ठीक

White Chicken Chili को 4–6 साल के लिए एक सर्विंग के हिसाब से 100 में से 70 मिले (ठीक)। एक ही व्यंजन अलग उम्र के लिए अलग स्कोर पाता है, क्योंकि छोटे बच्चे की चीनी, नमक और सैचुरेटेड फैट की दैनिक सीमा कम होती है।

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

सामग्री

  • 3 pounds bone-in, skin-on chicken breast halves, trimmed of excess fat and skin
  • 1 tablespoon vegetable oil
  • 3 medium jalapeño chiles
  • 3 poblano chiles (medium), stemmed, seeded, and cut into large pieces
  • 3 Anaheim chile peppers (medium), stemmed, seeded, and cut into large pieces
  • 2 medium onions, cut into large pieces (2 cups)
  • 6 medium cloves garlic, minced or pressed through garlic press (about 2 tablespoons)
  • 1 tablespoon ground cumin
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons ground coriander
  • 2 (14.5-ounce) cans cannellini beans, drained and rinsed
  • 3 cups low-sodium chicken broth
  • 3 tablespoons fresh lime juice (from 2 to 3 limes)
  • 1/4 cup minced fresh cilantro leaves
  • 4 scallions, white and light green parts sliced thin

विधि

  • Season 3 pounds chicken breast halves liberally with salt and pepper.
  • Heat 1 tablespoon vegetable oil in large Dutch oven over medium-high heat until just smoking.
  • Add chicken, skin side down, and cook without moving until skin is golden brown, about 4 minutes.
  • Using tongs, turn chicken and lightly brown on other side, about 2 minutes.
  • Transfer chicken to plate; remove and discard skin.
  • While chicken is browning, remove and discard ribs and seeds from 2 jalapeños chiles; mince flesh.
  • Seed 3 poblano chiles and 3 Anaheim chile peppers and cut into large pieces.
  • In food processor, process half of poblano chiles, half of Anaheim chiles, and 1 onion, cut into large pieces until consistency of chunky salsa, ten to twelve 1-second pulses, scraping down sides of workbowl halfway through.
  • Transfer mixture to medium bowl.
  • Repeat with remaining poblano chiles, remaining Anaheim chiles, and 1 onion, cut into large pieces; combine with first batch (do not wash food processor blade or workbowl).
  • Pour off all but 1 tablespoon fat from Dutch oven and reduce heat to medium.
  • Add minced jalapeños, chile-onion mixture, 6 minced garlic cloves, 1 tablespoon ground cumin, 1½ teaspoons ground coriander, and ¼ teaspoon table salt.
  • Cover and cook, stirring occasionally, until vegetables soften, about 10 minutes.
  • Remove pot from heat.
  • Transfer 1 cup cooked vegetable mixture to now-empty food processor.
  • Rinse 2 (14.5-ounce) cans cannellini beans.
  • Add 1 cup beans and 1 cup chicken broth and process until smooth, about 20 seconds.
  • Add vegetable-bean mixture, remaining 2 cups chicken broth, and chicken breasts to Dutch oven and bring to boil over medium-high heat.
  • Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, covered, stirring occasionally, until chicken registers 160 degrees (175 degrees if using thighs) on instant-read thermometer, 15 to 20 minutes (40 minutes if using thighs).
  • Using tongs, transfer chicken to large plate.
  • Stir in remaining beans and continue to simmer, uncovered, until beans are heated through and chili has thickened slightly, about 10 minutes.
  • Mince remaining 1 jalapeño chile, reserving and mincing ribs and seeds and set aside.
  • When cool enough to handle, shred chicken into bite-sized pieces, discarding bones.
  • Stir shredded chicken, 3 tablespoons lime juice, ¼ cup minced cilantro, 4 thinly sliced scallions, and remaining minced jalapeño (with seeds if desired) into chili and return to simmer.
  • Adjust seasonings with salt and pepper and serve.

    क्या White Chicken Chili बच्चों के लिए सेहतमंद है?

    White Chicken Chili को 4–6 साल के लिए 100 में से 70 मिले (ठीक)। यह 10 में से 7 जाँच पास करती है और सोडियम, फाइबर, ज़्यादा वसा, चीनी या नमक पर अंक खोती है।

    White Chicken Chili किस उम्र के लिए सबसे अच्छी है?

    सबसे ऊँचा स्कोर, 90, 7–9 साल, 10–12 साल, 13–15 साल के लिए है, और सबसे कम, 70, 4–6 साल के लिए। बच्चे के बढ़ने के साथ एक ही व्यंजन का स्कोर बदलता है, क्योंकि चीनी, नमक और सैचुरेटेड फैट की दैनिक सीमाएँ बढ़ती हैं।
