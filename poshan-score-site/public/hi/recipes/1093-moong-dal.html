Moong Dal

Go easy on the chilli for under-5s: use less, or leave it out.

सामग्री

  • ½ cup moong dal (yellow mung lentils)
  • ¼ cup onions/ shallots (optional, 1 small fine chopped)
  • ½ cup (1 medium) tomatoes (deseeded & chopped)
  • 1 tablespoon oil (or ghee)
  • ½ teaspoon cumin seeds
  • 1 teaspoon ginger (peeled & fine chopped)
  • 1 green chili (slit or chopped, deseeded if required)
  • 4 to 5 curry leaves (or 1 bay leaf)
  • ⅛ teaspoon turmeric
  • ¼ teaspoon Kashmiri red chilli powder
  • ½ teaspoon garam masala
  • 1 ¾ cups water
  • 1 teaspoon kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves, optional)
  • ½ teaspoon amchur (optional, dried mango powder or 1 tbsp lemon juice)
  • 1 tablespoon ghee (or oil or butter)
  • 2 to 3 garlic cloves (crushed)
  • 1 dried red chilli
  • ¼ teaspoon mustard seeds (optional)
  • 6 to 8 curry leaves (optional)
  • ⅛ teaspoon hing (asafoetida, optional)
  • ¼ teaspoon red chilli powder

विधि

  • Add moong dal to a bowl and rinse them thrice.
  • Soak in 1¾ to 2 cups water until you chop and prepare the rest. (If you don't have a cooker, soak them in hot boiling water for 30 mins before you proceed with the below steps)

    • How to Make Moong Dal (Stovetop)

    1. Heat oil in a cooker or pot and add cumin seeds.
    2. When the seeds splutter, add ginger, green chilies and 4 to 5 curry leaves.
    3. Fry until aromatic for about a minute and add onions if using.
    4. Saute until transparent for 2 to 3 mins.
    5. Add tomatoes and salt. Saute for 1 to 2 mins. Stir in turmeric, red chilli powder and garam masala. To bring out the flavors, optionally saute for 1 to 2 mins.
    6. Add the moong dal along with the soaked water. Mix well to deglaze the bottom of the cooker. Cover and pressure cook for 4 to 5 whistles on a medium flame.
    7. When the pressure drops, open the lid and add kasuri methi and amchur. If you prefer you can mash the moong dal a bit. Taste test and add more salt if required.
    8. Moong dal is ready at this stage and you can serve it as is. For extra flavour you may proceed to the next steps.

    आम सवाल

    क्या Moong Dal बच्चों के लिए सेहतमंद है?

    Moong Dal को 4–6 साल के लिए 100 में से 100 मिले (अच्छा)। यह 10 में से 10 जाँच पास करती है।

    Moong Dal किस उम्र के लिए सबसे अच्छी है?

    सबसे ऊँचा स्कोर, 100, 4–6 साल, 7–9 साल, 10–12 साल के लिए है, और सबसे कम, 80, 1–3 साल के लिए। बच्चे के बढ़ने के साथ एक ही व्यंजन का स्कोर बदलता है, क्योंकि चीनी, नमक और सैचुरेटेड फैट की दैनिक सीमाएँ बढ़ती हैं।
