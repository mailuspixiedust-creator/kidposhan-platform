Creamy Chicken Noodle Soup

सामग्री

  • 2 tablespoons olive oil or butter
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 large celery ribs, chopped small
  • 2 large carrots, chopped small
  • 4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1/3 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning- see notes
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt, more to taste
  • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper, more to taste
  • 6 cups chicken broth
  • 2 cups cooked chicken, chopped or shredded (see notes)
  • 2 cups dried egg noodles (4-5 ounces)
  • 1/2 cup heavy cream or half-and-half, more to taste
  • 3/4 cups fresh spinach, chopped, optional
  • 1/2 cup parsley, chopped
  • Garnish: chives (optional), tarragon, or more parsley, a squeeze of lemon juice

विधि

  • See notes below.
  • In a large Dutch oven or heavy soup pot over medium-high heat, add olive oil and onion, and sauté for a few minutes.
  • Lower heat to medium.
  • Add celery, carrots, and garlic, stirring often for 8-10 minutes, until the veggies are softened.
  • Stir in the flour, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper, coating the veggies evenly.
  • Add the chicken broth, turn up to medium-high heat, stir and bring to a boil.
  • Turn the heat down to a simmer. Add cooked chicken and noodles. Cook for 10 minutes, or until the noodles are cooked and the soup thickens.
  • Stir in heavy cream, spinach, and fresh parsley.
  • Adjust salt and pepper to taste; add a pinch of cayenne or chili flakes.
  • in bowls, garnished with herbs and a squeeze of lemon.
