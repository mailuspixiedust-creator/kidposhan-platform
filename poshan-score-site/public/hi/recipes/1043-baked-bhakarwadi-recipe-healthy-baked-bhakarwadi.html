    1. What is baked bhakarwadi made of ? See below image of list of ingredients for baked bhakarwadi.
    2. In a deep bowl put 1/2 cup whole wheat flour (gehun ka atta).
    3. Add 1/4 cup besan (bengal gram flour).
    4. Add 1/8 tsp turmeric powder (haldi).
    5. Add 1/4 tsp chilli powder.
    6. Add salt to taste. We added 1/8th tsp salt.
    7. Add 3/4 tbsp oil.
    8. Gradually add enough water to make a semi stiff dough. We added 1 tablespoon water and then added gradually 3 1/2 tablespoons water while making the dough.
    9. Knead into a semi-stiff dough.
    10. Cover with a lid and keep aside.
    11. Heat a broad non-stick pan.
    12. Add 1/4 cup grated dry coconut (kopra).
    13. Dry roast on a medium flame for 1 minute or till the coconut turns light brown in colour.
    14. Remove it in a deep bowl and keep aside.
    15. Add 2 tbsp sesame seeds (til) into the same pan.
    16. Dry roast on a medium flame for 1 minute or till it turns light brown in colour.
    17. Remove it in the bowl containing roasted dry coconut and keep aside.
    18. Add 1 tbsp poppy seeds (khus-khus) into the same pan.
    19. Dry roast on a medium flame for 30 seconds or till they turn light brown in colour.
    20. Remove it in the bowl containing roasted dry coconut and sesame seeds. Keep aside to cool for 1 to 2 minutes.
    21. Add 3/4 tsp coriander (dhania) powder.
    22. Add 3/4 tsp cumin seeds (jeera) powder.
    23. Add 1/2 tbsp coarsely powdered fennel seeds (saunf).
    24. Add 1/2 tsp grated ginger (adrak).
    25. Add 1/2 tbsp chilli powder.
    26. Add 1/8 tsp turmeric powder (haldi).
    27. Add 3/4 tsp dried mango powder (amchur).
    28. Add 1/8 tsp asafoetida (hing).
    29. Add 1/2 tbsp sugar.
    30. Add salt to taste. We added 1/3rd tsp salt.
    31. Mix well and put in a mixer.
    32. Blend it in a mixer to a coarse powder.
    33. Transfer blended powder to a bowl.
    34. Add 1/2 tbsp besan (bengal gram flour).
    35. Mix masala for bhakarawadi well. Keep aside.
    36. Divide the masala into 4 equal portions.
    37. Flatten the dough.
    38. Roll a portion of the dough into 200 mm. (8”) diameter circle using very little flour for rolling if required.
    39. Place a portion of the masala over it.
    40. Pat masala lightly while leaving a little space at the circumference. Use a spoon to help you do that.
    41. Gently roll over the masala evenly using a rolling pin, to ensure that the masala sticks well to the dough circle.
    42. Spread a little water at the circumference of the dough circle which doesn’t have any masala.
    43. Immediately start rolling very tightly from one end of the circle towards the other end to form a cylindrical roll while pinching the masala inwards simultaneously.
    44. Roll stuffed dough from one end to another end to form a cylindrical roll.
    45. Gently press all over the cylindrical roll evenly so that the masala further sticks to the dough circle very well.
    46. Trim a small portion from both the sides of the cylindrical roll and discard them.
    47. Now cut it into 16 to 18 equal roundels using a knife and separate out each roundel very well.
    48. Press each rolled bhakarwadi in a slanting manner using your palm.
    49. Bhakarwadi pressed in a slanting way.
    50. Place them on a baking tray lined with parchment paper.
    51. Bake them in a pre-heated oven at 180°c (360°f) for 10 minutes.
    52. Turn them over with your fingers.
    53. Bake again for 10 minutes at the same temperature.
    54. Roll a portion of the dough into 200 mm. (8”) diameter circle using very little flour for rolling if required.
    55. Pat masala lightly while leaving a little space at the circumference. Use a spoon to help you do that.
    56. Gently roll over the masala evenly using a rolling pin, to ensure that the masala sticks well to the dough circle.
    57. Immediately start rolling very tightly from one end of the circle towards the other end to form a cylindrical roll while pinching the masala inwards simultaneously.
