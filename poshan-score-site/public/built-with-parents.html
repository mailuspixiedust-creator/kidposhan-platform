What "beta" means here

  • We are still adding packs. If yours is missing, scan it and we will score it from your photos.
  • Some scores come from open data that our team has not checked yet. Those are marked not yet checked. The numbers are the pack's own label, but we can still have read or filed one wrongly.
  • When we are wrong, we want to hear it. Every pack has a Report a mistake button.

Where the numbers come from

  • The pack's own label: the nutrition table and the ingredient list, per 100 g, as printed.
  • Open Food Facts, a free database of food products made by people around the world (openfoodfacts.org, Open Database Licence). We credit it on every page that uses it.
  • Photos from parents: the front and back of a pack. Our label reader reads the table, and our team checks the numbers against the photo before a pack is marked checked.

No brand pays to be here, and no brand can pay to change a score. How a score is worked out is open for anyone to read on the Age & Metrics page.

How you can help

Scan a packLook for a mistake

  • Scan a pack we do not have, and photograph its front and back.
  • Is this your pack? On a pack that is not yet checked, tell us if the name on your pack is different. Then photograph yours, and it is scored from your photos.
  • Report a mistake on any pack: a wrong name, numbers that do not match the label, or a wrong photo.

About your photos and your email

  • When you send photos you tick a box to say they are of a pack you have and that KidPoshan may show them and use them to score the pack. Please photograph only the pack, not people.
  • Your email is optional. If you leave it, we use it once, to thank you when your pack is checked, or to tell you what we did about a report. We use it for nothing else, and we do not share it.
  • We keep no name or phone number with a scan or a report. More is on the privacy page.
